# Proposed integration plan (suggestions, not a work order)

*The spike agent's proposals, revised 2026-10-06 ~21:00 MDT after three verifications and three strengthening rounds. The findings they rest on are in `FINDINGS.md`.*

1. **Planner: no disagreement term.** Give its view of a context the context's look (chart class, blocks, length), which carries far more about how a context will be answered than anything per mind. A flat split's Sonnet side is settled; a third mind is the more informative question.
2. **Catalog combination rule (§9).** Replace Abbott with a logit model with a criterion and slope per mind and kind, with shuffled controls as heterogeneous items (about 640 nats on cohere, reproduced twice). For continue evidence, include glyph covariates and per-mind responses to them; contiguity is where the families differ most clearly.
3. **The per-sequence quantity.** Its measurable form is a per-(Unicode block, mind) lean beyond the context's look and beyond the other minds' rate on the block: worth about +900 nats held out in total, carried mostly by Grok, Haiku without thinking and Gemini Flash. With a median of two contexts per group, its interval matters more than its point value. Whether it holds per sequence within a block is untested. Report alongside it each mind's chart-walk coefficient and the content splits read off the chart.
4. **Universality** is read on content and on these model terms, never on raw yes or stop counts.
5. **Sheet hygiene.** Keeping same-block contexts off one sheet is cheap and removes a selection confound from every later reading. No format effect is shown.
6. **Pre-registrations:** pairwise likelihood ratios as the verdict, a stated reading for every band, and a naive reference model.
7. **On Saturday:** Gemini questions that test `▁` outside triads.
