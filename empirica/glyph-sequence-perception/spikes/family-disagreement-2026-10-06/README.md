# Spike: family disagreement (2026-10-06)

**The question.** Where do the model families agree about which runs are sequences, and where don't they? Which sequences are model-sensitive, and which are (at least among frontier families) model-agnostic? And what does the disagreement rest on? Asked by Joseph before any planner term was built on disagreement: *"look at the data, and then we'll probably need to have a model-sensitive vs agnostic finding / parameter on found sequences, or possibly something else depending on what the data is actually showing, if anything"*.

**What happened.** Disagreement between families turned out to be mostly about whether to commit, at thresholds that belong to a mind and a question kind, not about what the sequence is. Frontier minds that commit agree on content 93–99% of the time. The family-shaped effects are few and nameable. See `FINDINGS.md`.

**Strengthened** (`strengthening-1.md`, 17:15–17:35 by the clock, same snapshot, now rebuildable from `snapshot/`): a calibrated threshold model (a mixture with a criterion and a slope per mind and a familiarity term; single-answer disagreement, predicted out of sample, matches observation within 0.87–1.07). Under it, flat splits are borderline contexts, not settled ones, which withdraws the debrief's "asking again won't settle anything". Logit beats the catalog's Abbott model on cohere by about 640 nats held out. Gemini Pro's `▁` holds on the scan itself, and the familiarity effect is confirmed. **Pre-registered:** the flat-split reading predicts what the 43 Sonnet-continues / Grok-stops contexts do when asked again (`prereg-flat-splits.json`, committed before the test existed). The test is round `p001` (`prereg_round.py` builds it, `prereg_check.py` scores it). **Result** (`prereg-result-1.md`): Grok stopped again at 29/42 = 0.69, inside its band near the top; Sonnet continued again at 42/43 = 0.98, *above* its band [0.70–0.91]. So the model's "borderline, partly chance" reading holds for Grok's side, and Sonnet's continuing at these contexts is steadier than the model allows. Sheet size (one question per sheet in the test) moves stop rates by a few points in that direction, far less than Sonnet's departure. **Not yet independently verified:** a second, fresh verification (`de-novo-feedback-2.md`) is under way.
