## What it studies

Which glyph sequences LLM minds perceive. Sequences are unoriented (direction-free) linear orders over glyphs, within and across glyph families, and need no named axis. The study also asks how widely each sequence is shared across LLM families. Magnitude and direction are no longer central (Joseph, 2026-10-04).

The co-equal object is unchanged: how the measurement format and context manufacture or suppress the order a mind reports.
