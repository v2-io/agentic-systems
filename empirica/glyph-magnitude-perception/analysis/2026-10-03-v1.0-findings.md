*Synthesis written by the agent that built and ran the campaign. Two independent audits checked it, and this version incorporates their corrections; the history is at the end. Every number is in `analysis/v1.0-results.md`, generated by `harness/runner/analyze_v1.py` (parser p1.3) from the ledgers in `data/runs-v1/`; the inventory is `data/runs-v1/INVENTORY.md`.*

### Isolation

Each judge was configured to see only the study prompt. Tools, MCP, instruction files and agent profiles were stripped, and memory where the CLI allows. The residue is recorded per adapter in `harness/runner/judges-v1.json`:

- Claude judges see the account email;
- agy runs with a non-isolated HOME;
- grok and codex keep their built-in preambles.

The per-call record bears this out: every Claude call reports no tools, every agy call has one turn, and no grok call after the a2 adapter shows a tool event.

### Instruments

All run under protocol `gmp-v1.0`:

- 300 held-out triads, 1,800 presentations per judge;
- the format experiment: 320 pairs × both orders × {forced, tie, perp};
- a deconfounded axis-conflict battery: 38 items × both orders, × 3 reps for API judges;
- holistic pairs (13 sets) and gestalt reconstruction (21 sets × 3 shuffles).

Not registered: a SIGNA consumer probe, and the top-40 battery (`analysis/top40.md`).

### Not equalized

- **Reasoning effort** was set to low wherever settable. Even so, claude-haiku-4-5 spends about 400 thinking tokens on every item, claude-opus-5-5 about 29, claude-sonnet-5-5 about 60 in single mode, and claude-sonnet-5 none.
- **Seed coverage.** The seed stratum draws only on Anthropic-family surveys (fable-1, sonnet5-1, sonnet-survey-1/2), because of a field-name defect. grok-1, the only non-Anthropic surveyor, was left out.

## Claim by claim

### Claim 3 — the format manufactures order (demand characteristics)

- **[v1.0] Offering ⟂ dissolves a substantial share of tie-format directed pairs in every frontier judge.** The share ranges from 0.28 (Sonnet-5.5 single) to 0.72 (Gemini-3.1-pro). The survivors are axis-coherent: within-locus pairs survive 0.31–0.74 more often than random pairs (P3).
- **[v1.0-exploratory] Under the forced format, judges commit to edges they decline once ⟂ is offered, and those edges are the less accurate ones.**
  - On pairs committed under both formats, accuracy does not change (P4 note).
  - The pairs committed only under the forced format score 0.40–0.92 on denoted value. Those are the pairs ⟂ removes.
- **[v1.0-exploratory] How much order a judge reports depends on its context.**
  - The cleaner comparison is the share of ⟂ answers on the 160 pilot-replication pairs. The two tie formats are not the same instrument: the pilot's walk2 judges used ≈ on 51% of these pairs, while every v1.0 Claude run used ≈ on none of them.
  - On the ⟂ measure (from the audit, `verification/audit_v1.py`):
    - pilot walk3: 0.88;
    - pilot-condition arm: 0.76;
    - isolated-CLI Claude judges: 0.41–0.56.
  - The pilot's dissolution on these same pairs was 0.77.
  - The pilot-condition arm reproduces the pilot setup: claude-sonnet-5-5 in Claude Code's subagent harness, pilot wording, 80-item sheets read from a file. The same model through the isolated CLI gives 0.41 (single) and 0.47 (sheet).
  - The arm bundles five differences, so which one moves the ⟂ rate is open: the harness, the wording, the sheet size, the user's global instruction context (which the subagents loaded), and agent-default rather than low effort.
- **What can be claimed now:**
  - Offering ⟂ removes a large share of cross-domain directed edges in every mind tested.
  - The pilot's "~80%" sits at the top of a range whose position depends on the judge and on the measurement context.
  - The measurement context is itself a lever on how much order a mind reports.

### Claim 4 — transitivity, discipline, and what capability buys

- **[v1.0-exploratory] Every judge is locally transitive where it is consistent.**
  - In this triad design, a within-orientation 3-cycle occurs exactly when a judge picks the same screen position for all three pairs. The registered cycle rate therefore measures position habit as much as intransitivity.
  - Restricted to triads whose three pairs are consistent-directed across both orientations, the cycle count is zero for every judge with any such triads:
    - frontier runs: 0 of 32–94 each;
    - llama3.2: 0 of 33;
    - gemma3: 0 of 8.
  - Sonnet-5's registered cycle rate (0.17) is its second-position habit: 67% of its directed answers pick the second-shown glyph, and all 31 of its cycles are all-second sets.
- **[v1.0] Denoted number is a substrate-invariant axis among frontier minds.**
  - The value correlate (winner has the larger Unicode numeric value) is 0.95–1.00 for all eight frontier judges.
  - The small models sit at chance: llama3.2 0.51, gemma3 0.46, hermes3 0.50, mistral 0.58.
- **[v1.0] Ink is a secondary frontier correlate.**
  - Among non-numeric edges the denser glyph wins 0.64–0.83 for frontier judges.
  - The small models are at chance or below: llama3.2 0.51, gemma3 0.55, hermes3 0.52, mistral 0.28.
- **[v1.0] What separates the tiers is discipline and consistency, not transitivity.**
  - Use of ⟂ in triads: 0.07–0.35 for small models, 0.45–0.66 for frontier.
  - Agreement across the two presentation orientations: 0.35–0.60 for small models, 0.66–0.91 for frontier.
  - Position habits of the size that manufacture cycles appear in both tiers: Sonnet-5 favours the second-shown glyph, mistral 73% second, qwen2.5 the first.
- **What can be claimed now:** capability buys ⟂ use, consistency, and the axes. It does not buy transitivity, which every judge has where it is consistent. MANIFEST claim 4's "the same axes survive at both tiers" does not hold at 3–7B.

### Claim 5 — what arbitrates axis conflicts (deconfounded battery)

- **[v1.0] Compiled numeric decode wins over ink and element count, pooled per frontier judge** (P9: 0.81–1.00). The pooled items:
  - Roman numerals, upper and lower case;
  - seven-segment 0 against 1 and 7;
  - fractions with large denominators;
  - small-form digits against larger full digits.
- **[v1.0] Two item-level exceptions:**
  - The Sonnet models choose the full-size **3 over ₈**. claude-sonnet-5-5 single answered 3 on 6 of 6 committed answers, sheet on 3 of 5, and claude-sonnet-5 on 4 of 5. claude-sonnet-5 also splits ⁹ vs 2.
  - For the Sonnet models, size beats value on subscripts.
- **[v1.0] Grams: line count leads.**
  - ☷ beats ⚌ in every committed frontier answer (43 of 43). Measured ink favors ⚌.
  - On equal-line items, the yang (solid) side wins 0.81 pooled.
  - Yang also carries a compiled cultural "more" (☰ = heaven), so this is either fill or semantic decode. The data cannot separate the two.
- **[v1.0] Per-mille splits by model family.**
  - ‱ beats % and ‰ for the Sonnet models: 0.58–1.00 across the three Sonnet runs.
  - The other frontier judges choose by value: 0.00–0.33.
  - ‱ is per *ten thousand*, so a judge that attends to the denominator can call it "more" while decoding. This item cannot show that weak decode splits toward the visual reading.
- **[v1.0-exploratory] Size and equal values.**
  - Equal-value probes: pooled, judges answer ≈ or ⟂ 0.68 of the time (P12 passes). Several judges still call the full-size digit larger (⁹ vs 9: Haiku 6/6, Opus 5/6, Grok 5/6).
  - Size against count: a single big disc beats clustered small dots for most judges. claude-sonnet-5 prefers ⁖ over ● (4:2), and GPT splits.
  - █ beats ⣿, except for GPT, which calls them equal.
- **What can be claimed now:**
  - Where a compiled numeric reading exists, it usually arbitrates. The Sonnet family's size preference on subscripts is the clear exception.
  - Without such a reading, judges resolve by counts of like elements (lines) before measured ink.
  - Which reading wins on per-mille signs differs by model family, and the item does not separate visual from decoded readings.

### Claim 2 — holistic sequences

Gestalt τ is computed only over the glyphs a judge places, so coverage is reported beside it.

- **[v1.0] Controls.** Dice reconstructs perfectly for every frontier judge. Noise foils drew ⟂ on 0.64 of frontier presentations, short of the 0.80 threshold. Three judges declined consistently: Gemini-3.1-pro, Haiku and Opus. Others imposed orders, so whole-set honesty is not universal.
- **[v1.0] The pilot's flagship holistic specimen `-=>})|` does not replicate broadly.**
  - Sonnet-5.5 reaches τ 0.73.
  - Opus scores τ 1.00, but only on a sub-order: it placed `-` and `=` as extras on every shuffle (coverage 0.67).
  - Every other frontier judge is at 0.20–0.47.
- **[v1.0] risebar `_₋-⁻¯` reconstructs** at τ 0.73–1.00 for every Claude judge.
  - Its pairwise directed share is 0.20–0.70. The pairwise-blind half of the holistic signature therefore holds only for Haiku and Sonnet-5.
- **[v1.0] Grok's elaboration ladders** (the pilot's own registered prediction, P15) pass in 14 of 24 cells.
  - They are pairwise-⟂-heavy for Haiku, Sonnet-5.5, GPT and Gemini-flash.
  - Opus and Grok order their rungs pairwise (0.33–0.70). That fits the appendage-count reading the pilot named as the alternative.
  - A weakness of P15's criterion: τ ≥ 0.60 is weak for 4-rung ladders. On elab-n, seven of eight frontier runs agree with each other on `nɳɲŋ`, which is one swap from Grok's written reference. That says as much about the reference as about the judges.
- **What can be claimed now:**
  - Set-level presentation recovers some orders that some judges withhold pairwise, but the effect is judge-dependent.
  - "Holistic" is a relation between a sequence and a mind, not a property of the sequence alone.

## Other observations

- **[v1.0-exploratory] Seed retest.** When a frontier judge sees an order in a seed-local triad, it agrees with the surveyor's written order in 0.90–1.00 of cases. Chance would be 0.29–0.36, and coverage runs from 54 to 104 of 120 triads.
  - The surveyed sequences are all from Anthropic-family surveyors. Claude, Grok, GPT and Gemini judges confirm them, but no non-Claude surveyor's sequences were retested.
  - Small models agree at 0.58–0.67, slightly above their chance of 0.44–0.50.
- **[v1.0-exploratory] Mode.** For claude-sonnet-5-5, single vs sheet: triad ⟂ 0.45 vs 0.54, value correlate 1.00 vs 0.97. This confounds mode with thinking: the judge averages about 60 thinking tokens per item in single mode and 0 in sheet mode.
- **[v1.0-exploratory] Tool reach.** Offered tools, grok-4.6 tried to use them mid-sheet. Its tool calls are recorded as events (seven in the aborted run), and the adapter was rebuilt to remove tools. The specific query quoted in the PROTOCOL notes came from a debugging call outside the ledger.
- **Held-out leakage.** Four pilot salt glyphs appear in 20 triad presentations (1.1%).

## Consumer probe — the SIGNA ladder `·╶╌╍━═⚬○◎◉⬤` (not registered)

- **[v1.0-exploratory] Where frontier judges commit, they commit in the SIGNA direction** (0.94–1.00 of committed pairs).
- **No judge confirms the seam where the line family hands over to the circle family.** Each one reverses ═ → ⚬, declines it (⟂), or answers it inconsistently.
- **Where ⚬ (4 hours) lands, by Copeland score:**
  - every frontier judge except Haiku places it below ╍ (1 minute);
  - both Geminis place it below ╌ (10 seconds) as well;
  - Haiku places ⚬ above ○ and puts ═ at the very top.
- **The perceived single-glyph order interleaves the two families by visual weight.** The typical order is `·╶╌⚬╍○━═◎◉⬤`.
- **━ vs ═ is unconfirmed for about half the judges.** The two glyphs measure the same ink (0.0811).
- **Whole-set reconstruction.** Gemini, Grok and Opus recover the written SIGNA order perfectly; Haiku reaches 0.66 and Sonnet-5.5 0.62. Seeing the whole set lets family grouping carry the order, where single glyphs do not.
- **This probe says nothing about run length in a column.**

## Top-40

`analysis/top40.md` ranks 117 survey sequences, chosen by concordance over all seven surveys. The ranking columns are:

- **stability:** frontier rung confirmation;
- **universality:** the share of judges confirming ≥ 80% of rungs;
- **floor:** the weakest judge's rung share;
- **gestalt:** whole-set reconstruction τ.

The frontier saturates. The floor and the small-model column discriminate more than the rank does.

Two weaknesses of the step score:

- It takes each judge's dominant direction after the fact, so a judge that reads a ladder backwards still scores full.
- Top-ranked items with three or four rungs reach 1.00 more easily than long ladders.
