**Columns**

- **dir** — the sequence's direction, fixed *once per sequence* by majority across the six frontier judges' confirmed steps: ↑ = the later glyph as written is "more", ↓ = the earlier one is. (The builder's top-40 score instead lets each judge take its own dominant direction; this view does not.)

- **against** — adjacent steps, summed over those six judges, confirmed in the *opposite* direction.
- **builder #** — the sequence's rank in `../top40.md` (the primed builder's ranking, for comparison).
- **judge cells** — the whole-set task: each judge arranged the scrambled set twice (two shuffles), shown as `a·b`. Each number is how many glyphs must be moved or restored to recover the written order, reading it in either direction: *n* − the longest common subsequence with the written order or its reverse. A glyph the judge left out of its order counts as one to restore. `0` = exact. `ʳ` = the best match was the reversed order. `⟂` = the judge said there is no ordering. `?` = unparsed answer. `–` = no answer recorded (quota or omitted). One number alone = only one shuffle recorded.
- **local models** — only llama32-3b and qwen25-3b answered the full set; the other small models were not run on these sequences.

**Reading it:** a low *strict* with distances of 0 means judges rebuild the whole set but won't commit pair by pair (the trigram row is the clearest case); a high *strict* with non-zero distances means pairs agree but whole-set arrangements leave some glyphs out or move them.
