## Position & Lineage

ASF integrates four mature disciplines under one formalism for adaptive, purposeful agents: control theory's stability machinery (Lyapunov, contraction analysis, monotone operators), causal inference's interventional reasoning (Pearl's hierarchy and identifiability theory), information theory's compression and channel-capacity arguments (Shannon, the information bottleneck), and agent architecture's structural decomposition (modular vs coupled processing topologies). The machinery is classical and used as such. What the integration makes provable is the contribution: exact and conditional bounds on when an adaptive, purposeful agent can correct, plan, and persist — results expressible only over objects the framework had to construct (the complete agent state $X_t = (M_t, G_t)$, the sector-persistence template, the directed-separation architecture classes). The scope conditions are not caveats on a general theory; they are among its sharpest results — disciplining the richest channels (language, goal-coupled cognition) rather than carving exceptions out of a clean case. Three cross-cutting meta-patterns, recognitions carried at discussion grade rather than theorems, name a shape the theory's results share:

- A *separability pattern* — where problems decompose cleanly, where partial repair exists, where the general case is open.
- An *identifiability-floor pattern* — structural no-go results from observational data, and the capabilities that escape them: interventional access, observation of a latent variable, or shared structure across components.
- An *additive-coordinate-forcing pattern* — places where AAT-internal additivity axioms force logarithmic / Fisher-Rao coordinates at multiple layers.

Volume 1's nine headline results, stated at the strength their derivations support (the volume introduction, [`01-aat-core/INTRODUCTION.md`](01-aat-core/INTRODUCTION.md), carries the same nine in full, each with the segment that proves it). *Part I.* The **persistence condition** $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ — correction efficiency against disturbance rate, relative to how wrong the model can afford to be — is sufficient for every corrector that points inward at least in proportion to its error inside a region (the sector condition, the premise the whole chain rests on) and necessary for that class as a whole; it is derived where the correction is a Bayes update or a strongly convex gradient step, so the Kalman filter's stability margin is a theorem's instance, and it is a posit the inequality makes testable per system where it is not, which is where the organizational viability test and the software maintainability threshold sit ([`result-persistence-condition`](01-aat-core/src/result-persistence-condition.md), [`form-sector-condition`](01-aat-core/src/form-sector-condition.md)). The **containment dichotomy**: bounded disturbance is contained forever, stochastic disturbance is left with probability one at every correction strength, and correction buys an expected stay exponential in the barrier height, bounded on a fixed noisy channel below the Kalman gain ([`deriv-sector-condition`](01-aat-core/src/deriv-sector-condition.md)). *Part II.* **Directed separation** sorts real systems into three architectural classes by whether the goal is upstream of the belief update — separated by construction (Kalman + LQR), coupled by construction (a transformer whose prompt carries the goal), partial between — with humans unplaced and the coupling measure run on no system yet; the norm it carries is that goal-dependent *distortion* of the update worsens reliability while goal-dependent *allocation* of attention does not ([`der-directed-separation`](01-aat-core/src/der-directed-separation.md)). The **loop supplies data of interventional character** (Pearl Level 2) that a passive observer cannot obtain; identifying causal effects from it is a further step, gated on positivity, ignorability and consistency ([`der-loop-interventional-access`](01-aat-core/src/der-loop-interventional-access.md)). The **satisfaction-gap / control-regret split** partitions a single shortfall exactly into "the world doesn't permit it" ($\delta_{\text{sat}}$) and "you're not doing it well enough" ($\delta_{\text{regret}}$), each relative to a planning convention; that routing repairs by the two does better than routing by their sum is a hypothesis not yet tested ([`def-satisfaction-gap`](01-aat-core/src/def-satisfaction-gap.md), [`def-control-regret`](01-aat-core/src/def-control-regret.md), [`def-value-object`](01-aat-core/src/def-value-object.md)). The **orient cascade** orders belief before anything that reads belief and attainability before regret as an exact dependency, and puts objective revision last as a recommendation ([`der-orient-cascade`](01-aat-core/src/der-orient-cascade.md)). The **strategy graph**: temporal order, probabilistic edges and testable components suffice for a DAG with the Markov property (sufficiency, not uniqueness), and its AND/OR success-probability pass is exact iff the graph is a tree ([`deriv-graph-structure`](01-aat-core/src/deriv-graph-structure.md)). *Part III.* **Composition closure**, a conditional formulation of when a group is one agent, with a closure defect and three tiers ([`form-composition-closure`](01-aat-core/src/form-composition-closure.md)); and the **adversarial tempo advantage**, a power law in the tempo ratio, exact in the coupling-dominant limit under the stated coupling model, capped for an attacker that must itself persist on a noisy channel ([`result-adversarial-tempo-advantage`](01-aat-core/src/result-adversarial-tempo-advantage.md)). **Software is treated as the high-identifiability calibration laboratory** of Volume 2 — tests, deploys, and `git bisect` are literal interventions on declared causal structure — and other domains inherit the machinery under transfer assumptions that are stated rather than assumed. <!-- headlines: H1–H9 · restates the volume introduction's nine items -->

For practitioners already working with active inference or standard RL framings, the divergence is precise. Active inference begins from a single optimization principle (minimize variational free energy) and recovers perception, action, and learning as cases; ASF begins from operational requirements on the feedback loop and uses information-theoretic compression as one modeling move rather than the master objective. The two objectives share a shape and not a content: Expected Free Energy splits into pragmatic and epistemic value, and ASF's unified objective into a value term and a causal-information term. ASF's information term is causal, the distinguishability of actions under intervention, rather than entropy reduction over hidden states; and ASF encodes preferences as a value functional on trajectories rather than as priors over outcomes, which the satisfaction-gap / control-regret diagnostic needs ([`disc-ciy-unified-objective`](01-aat-core/src/disc-ciy-unified-objective.md)). Whether Expected Free Energy is recoverable from ASF as a special case is an open strengthening, not a result. With Hafez 2026 (*A Mathematical Theory of Agency and Intelligence*), the relationship is complementary: bi-predictability $P$ supplies a substrate-independent diagnostic whose dynamics ASF predicts, while ASF supplies the goal-and-strategy machinery Hafez explicitly does not address. With Miller 2022 (Santa Fe coevolving automata), similarly complementary on composition mechanics. With Miehling et al.'s 2025 ICML position paper *"Agentic AI Needs a Systems Theory"* — which renewed the field-level call — ASF reads as a substantive, independently-developed answer (the formal apparatus was in place as Temporal Feedback Theory before that paper was encountered).

The maturity gradient is explicit, so it can be relied on selectively rather than wholesale. Part I (adaptive systems under uncertainty — mismatch dynamics, gain structure, the persistence condition and the containment dichotomy) has its central chain derived, exact under named disturbance models, with its open edges named and simulations of its own equations beside it. Part II (actuated agents) has a strong diagnostic core and a maturing operational layer; the bias bound for Coupled (GUC Class 3) agents is conditional under named sub-scopes. Part III (composition and adversarial dynamics) has its bridge lemma and a contraction-template generalization, with latent structural diversity, endogenous coupling, and composition transition dynamics still open. Software (TST) is a working draft grounded in AAT; logogenic agents are framework-stage with directed separation failing by construction for goal-conditioned LLMs (handled as architectural scope, not approximation); Emergent Logozoetic Intelligences (ELIs) are largely future work. The expected arc is exact core, principled architecture in the middle, open formulation at the edges.
