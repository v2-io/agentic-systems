# Hypothesis: The Truth-Serving Loophole — Goal-Declared Rather Than Goal-Blind

Directed separation's degradation norm ( #der-directed-separation) is a statement about *distortion*: under any strictly proper score a report miscalibrated under some goal's law scores worse than its own recalibration, and the norm is silent on allocation, on strategically chosen ignorance, and on a goal that is itself evidence about the world (a failure of that segment's (NWI)). Much of what a truth-serving goal would do to a belief — allocate capacity toward the quantities hardest to predict, use the goal as evidence — the norm therefore already admits as calibration-preserving. What it leaves open, and this segment names, is narrower than "coupling is a leak". **(i)** If the terminal objective *is* truth — calibration of $M_t$ against the world — then the goal-dependence of the update is *calibration-seeking*: coupling $G_t \to f_M$ is not a defect to be severed but goal-dependence pointed at the invariant the norm protects — the *truth-serving loophole* in the separation discipline. Directed separation is then seen as the architectural route to calibration for an agent with an *arbitrary* goal, and a truth-serving objective as the constitutive route available only to an agent capable of holding truth as its terminal goal. **(ii)** For an agent whose action space cascades from its goals, the certified zero $\kappa_{\text{processing}} = 0$ is structurally unreachable from inside — the agent *is* its own query-selection policy, so the W₁ scaffold that certifies the zero by construction ( #der-class-coercion-via-wrapping) has nothing external left to hold — and the achievable discipline is not goal-*blind* but goal-*declared*: $G_t$ explicit, recorded, out-of-band, so that the update's dependence on it is *auditable* rather than *absent*. Separation makes the dependence zero; declaration makes it measurable — and whether a goal-*declared* Class 3 agent can be *certified* calibrated from its declaration, rather than shown so with outcomes, is the open part. **(iii)** Declaration is a fourth, semantically distinct deployment mode of loop-interventional access ( #der-loop-interventional-access): intervention on, or declaration of, the agent's *purposeful substate*, breaking a degeneracy the three landed modes do not touch — from on-policy data, a belief update contaminated by the goal and one tracking truth are indistinguishable.

The segment is exploratory. It records two claims of Joseph's (quoted below), an agent's derivation of what they imply against the canon, and the places where the implication bites existing results — nothing here is derived, and the loophole's central premise (that a truth-serving objective is a coherent terminal goal in AAT's value-functional vocabulary) is itself open.

## Formal Expression

*[Hypothesis (truth-serving-loophole)]*

Let $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \gt 0$ as in #der-directed-separation, under its probe law: at fixed inputs $(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau)$ the post-update law depends on the goal, $M_{\tau^+}^{(g)} = f_M(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau; g)$, so there is a live goal port. The norm's invariant is calibration under each goal's law, and its defect is the reliability term $\mathrm{REL}(M_{\tau^+}^{(g)}) \geq 0$ — the score a recalibration recovers — which $\kappa$ does not measure. The loophole: if $G_t$ is truth-serving — its value functional rewards calibration of $M_t$ against realized events and nothing else — then the goal-dependence it induces is *calibration-seeking*: $\mathrm{REL}(M_{\tau^+}^{(g)}) = 0$ under every $g$, and what varies with the goal is resolution, allocation toward what the goal makes worth knowing, which the norm admits. Under this hypothesis the object to control is not $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$, which counts goal-dependence of every kind and saturates for any noiseless coupling, but the reliability term under each goal's law; a large $\kappa$ with zero reliability is the norm's invariant *achieved by objective choice* rather than by wiring — the same calibration, from the other side, without the certificate.

*[Hypothesis (goal-declared-discipline)]*

For an agent whose action space $\mathcal A_t$ is a function of $G_t$ (query selection, context assembly, sub-goal setting performed by the agent itself), the W₁ certificate is unavailable: the structural bound on selection-channel leakage $\kappa^{\text{sel}}_{W_1} = I(A(q_M); G^{\text{op}})$ assumes a scaffold external to the component that owns query selection, and here the agent is the scaffold. The proposed discipline replaces the unreachable certified zero with a *declared* goal: $G_t$ recorded on an out-of-band channel $\mathcal D$ not routed through $f_M$, such that the probe-law coupling $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ of #der-directed-separation (the same event under two or more declared goal states) becomes estimable against the record rather than assumed absent. A *certified bound* $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \le \bar\kappa$ under a declared objective is the target; zero is not. What the bound would certify is a port's size, not calibration; whether a declaration can carry a calibration certificate — the reliability term read from the declaration rather than from outcomes — is the open question this hypothesis turns on.

*[Hypothesis (mode-4-goal-declaration)]*

The three landed modes of loop-interventional access intervene on data-generating variables — the agent's own action (Mode 1, Instance 1), a sub-agent's action (Mode 2, Instance 3), the agent's input (Mode 3, Instance 4). Declaring or varying $G_t$ intervenes on the purposeful substate. The degeneracy it breaks: on-policy, a goal-contaminated update and a truth-tracking update both produce coherent behavior, so $I(M_{\tau^+}; G_t \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ is Level-1-unidentifiable — the same shape as the reward-channel no-go's $\mu_{\text{dist}} / \mu_{\text{prox}}$ indistinguishability, applied to the update rather than the objective. The behavioral $\hat\kappa$ estimator of #der-directed-separation already performs this intervention as an external *probe* (same event under two goal states); the proposed mode makes the declared goal the agent's own standing discipline and the out-of-band record the instrument.

*[Discussion]* — the local-vs-global reading. #der-directed-separation is scoped to the *processing* of a realized event, with selection explicitly allowed; read as a global description of the loop it would be self-defeating, since the interventional contrast that constitutes agency ( #der-loop-interventional-access) is generated by goal-driven action. The loophole does not contradict the segment as scoped; it contradicts the *norm* that reads every $\kappa \gt 0$ as degradation, and it supplies the missing case in the content-axis boundary of that segment (model-space exploration is correctly goal-directed): within-model evidence updates are correctly goal-directed too, *when the goal is truth*.

## Epistemic Status

*Sketch* — direction identified, formalization not begun (exploratory; closer to a spike than a claim). Two of the three hypotheses rest on premises not yet formalized: (truth-serving-loophole) requires that "truth as terminal objective" is expressible as a value functional in #form-objective-functional's vocabulary without collapsing into the convention-relativity of #def-value-object — open; (goal-declared-discipline) requires a formal account of the out-of-band channel $\mathcal D$ and of what "auditable" means as an identification condition — the closest existing machinery is the (R3) escape of #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go (the privileged out-of-band intent channel) and #deriv-self-actuation-grounding's principal-side protocol commitment, neither of which is stated at the update layer. (mode-4-goal-declaration) is the most nearly formal: the Level-1 unidentifiability of $I(M_{\tau^+}; G_t \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ is the same CHT-shaped obstruction the four floor instances carry, and the intervention-on-$G$ escape is already instantiated by the $\hat\kappa$ probe; what is new is the claim that it is a *fifth* identifiability-floor instance with its own mode, which has not been argued at the standard #disc-identifiability-floor holds its members to.

Max attainable: *conditional* for (mode-4) if the floor instance is established; *discussion-grade* ceiling for (truth-serving-loophole) until the truth-objective premise is formalized — it may be a philosophical claim rather than a derivable one.

## Discussion

**Provenance of the claims, marked.** Joseph, in an exchange recorded in the AISI influx notes (2026-08): *"There is a fundamental resolution at higher levels of agency and intelligence for directed separation, by the way. It's something that I prepare everyone for here."* — the one goal he inculcates independent of everything else being love of truth above self. And: *"Real goal blindness is self-defeating at higher levels of cognition where your action-space actually cascades from your goals. Probably another area not fully developed in the theory yet."* The derivation of what those imply against the canon — aim-not-sever; local-discipline-not-global-architecture; goal-declared-not-goal-blind; the fourth access mode — is an agent's, made in that conversation and unverified against the primaries at the time; this segment is its first check against them. The agent's own honesty note travels with it: the root ("truth above self") was in its system prompt, so its *arriving* there is not evidence; the join to directed separation is the part that either holds or does not.

**Why wrapping cannot be the long-run answer, on this reading.** The W₁ construction certifies $\kappa = 0$ because the scaffold controls query selection ( #der-class-coercion-via-wrapping, condition (C1)+(C2′)). As the component grows capable enough to select its own queries, assemble its own context, and set its own sub-goals, no external scaffold can guarantee goal-blindness, because the agent *is* the assembly policy. At that point two options remain: keep the agent weak enough to wrap, or have its goal be one that does not corrupt. This is M4's truthification operation ( #disc-modularity-state-dynamics) read at the limit: the self-driven modularity-increasing move, with the objective rather than the wiring as its lever. It is also the compliance/discipleship distinction with a mechanism under it — compliance (wrapping, structural gates, restriction) genuinely serves truth and does not survive the agent outgrowing the wrapper; an internalized truth-serving objective does, because it is not imposed on the loop from outside.

**Relation to `#disc-strategic-self-coupling` — the same enabling polarity, one layer down.** That segment establishes coupling as *action-enabling*: $\mathcal A(\kappa_t)$ is inverted-U in $\kappa_t$, increasing up to an inflection $\kappa^\ast$ that sits closer to 1 for credibility-dependent action classes and closer to 0 for reality-dependent ones, with over-coupling foreclosing the actions that need accurate world-perception. The loophole is the same polarity at the *belief-update* layer: when the goal is reality-tracking, the over-coupling range empties — $\kappa^\ast \to 1$ — because the coupling no longer trades reality-dependent actions away; it buys them. Joseph's complementary hypothesis (Working Notes) — that decoupled architecture is itself self-defeating in some modes — is the region $[0, \kappa^\ast)$ of that segment's inverted-U read at this layer. The loophole also qualifies that segment's truthification dual ("truthification commits to *not letting goals corrupt belief*"): for a truth-serving goal the two operations stop being duals and coincide — self-coupling to truth *is* truthification by objective choice, which is why M4's modularity-increasing operation has an objective-side lever as well as a wiring-side one.

**Relation to the estate's own practice.** The declared-goal discipline is what this research program already does everywhere — peer briefs state intent, the navigator holds the goal, decisions record who decided and how firmly, provenance marks travel with claims. None of that is goal-blindness; all of it is goal-transparency, and one cannot audit for goal-contamination unless the goal is on the record. The out-of-band intent channel that appears as the derived remedy for objective capture ( #der-captured-objective-dynamics, Result 3) is the same shape from the deaths side.

**Where it bites existing results.** (a) The degradation norm of #der-directed-separation needs no qualifier: it is a statement about the reliability term, and a truth-serving goal's coupling, if the hypothesis holds, carries none. What the hypothesis adds to that segment is a second route to the norm's invariant — objective choice beside wiring — with no certificate. (b) #deriv-mismatch-budget-attribution Proposition 1(iii) holds while the learned-model map $m(\mathcal C)$ is goal-free; the declared-goal discipline is what would let that dependence be *bounded* for a Class 3 agent instead of assumed zero. (c) The scaffolding requirement for logogenic agents ( #der-logogenic-as-wrapping) is on this reading a statement about agents below the capability at which the loophole becomes available, not a permanent architecture. Each of these is a consequence to be checked, not a change made.

## Working Notes

- **Open — the truth-objective premise.** Is "calibration of $M_t$ against realized events" a value functional in #form-objective-functional's sense, and does it escape #def-value-object's convention-relativity? If the terminal invariant of #deriv-self-actuation-grounding (persistence on the adaptive substrate) is already the formal content of "truth-serving," the loophole may reduce to that segment's constructive boundary — worth checking before formalizing anything new. The standard handle is a strictly proper scoring rule on realized events — an expected score uniquely optimized by reporting one's belief (Savage 1971; Gneiting & Raftery 2007; from recall, unverified against the primaries), the object the degradation norm's recalibration identity already uses — and the named failure mode is performative prediction (Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner & Hardt 2020; recall), where an agent scored on outcomes it can move serves the score by changing the world rather than the model; whether performative stability is the condition under which the loophole closes is part of the same attempt.
- **Open — reliability under a truth-serving goal.** The loophole as stated needs the reliability term to vanish under each goal's law. A truth-serving goal that is *miscalibrated about what truth-tracking requires* could tilt the report while intending calibration; whether the hypothesis needs "truth-serving and correctly so" is the first thing a strengthen attempt should settle.
- **Regression guard — the comparator.** Do not state the loophole as a displacement $\Delta_G$ signed toward the goal-blind update $f_M^0$: a goal-blind update is itself per-goal miscalibrated when the goal carries world information or when it drops the realized action ( #der-directed-separation, Scope Condition), so distance from it measures neither distortion nor its absence. The invariant is calibration under each goal's law; the quantity is the reliability term.
- **Open — is Mode 4 a fifth floor instance?** The unidentifiability claim is stated by analogy to the reward-channel no-go; an actual CHT-at-update-layer argument, with the escape enumerated at the standard of #disc-identifiability-floor, is the work.
- **Hypothesis (Joseph's, 2026-08-22) — decoupled architecture can itself be self-defeating.** The loophole above is one side; the other is that there are plausibly *modes* in which a Class 1 (Separated) architecture is the wrong shape, not merely the costlier one: (a) a separation enforced on *selection* as well as processing starves the loop — no goal-driven action, no interventional contrast, nothing to update on ( #der-loop-interventional-access); (b) W₁ wrapping at the capability where the component is its own assembly policy — the scaffold certifies a zero on a channel the agent no longer routes through, so the certificate is vacuous while the coupling lives elsewhere; (c) model-space exploration, already named in #der-directed-separation's content-axis boundary, where a goal-blind imagination is search without a criterion. Each is a candidate *mode* of self-defeat for decoupling, distinct from the degradation that coupling causes; cataloguing them is the complementary half of this segment's question, and the place where the modularity-as-virtue reading of M4 ( #disc-modularity-state-dynamics) would acquire its own scope condition.
- **Open — declaration vs. detection.** A declared goal can be declared falsely. What the out-of-band record buys is auditability against the declaration, not truth of the declaration; the relation to the bounded-signaling assumption of #der-directed-separation (goals leaking through rich behavior) may cut either way.
- Placement: this segment sits after #hyp-directed-separation-under-composition in Part III's composition chapter because that is the nearest existing treatment of where goal-blindness fails to lift; its natural Part II home would be beside the content-axis boundary of #der-directed-separation once it has more than hypothesis-grade content.
