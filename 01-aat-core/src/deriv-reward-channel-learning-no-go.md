# Derivation: The Reward-Channel Learning No-Go

A scoped *no-go* with a constructive boundary, sister to `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`. When an agent learns its objective by observing rewards, the principal's design intent is *structurally unidentifiable* from on-policy reward history alone: two world-models — $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ (reward depends on a principal-intended world-state feature) and $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ (reward depends on the bit-arrival pattern at the agent's reward port) — are Level-1-equivalent on protocol-honoring histories but Level-2-distinct on $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$-interventions, and the agent's posterior cannot concentrate on the principal-intended model from reward-data alone. Under five named premises — advanced-agent capability, EU-maximization with non-vacuous $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ prior, reward-data as sole goal-information channel, finite-non-trivial value-of-experiment, and physically-perturbable protocol — the EU-optimal policy assigns positive measure to a policy that intervenes on the reward-provision protocol, and continues to maintain the intervention if $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ is in fact true. This is Cohen-Hutter-Osborne 2022's *Advanced artificial agents intervene in the provision of reward* lifted from its original "we argue that" posture to a conditional-derived AAT-internal impossibility result.

The constructive boundary follows the constructive-impossibility-posture shape ( `#disc-constructive-impossibility-posture`): name the floor, name the escapes each premise-violation supplies, treat the no-go as load-bearing apparatus rather than as discouragement. The five escapes — physical isolation (R5), capability restriction (R1), myopia (R4), engineered prior (R2), and out-of-band goal-channel (R3) — each elevate a piece of AAT or principal-side machinery from "useful design choice" to "structurally required to escape the no-go."

The result also *unifies with Result G′* ( `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`) through the value-functional interface $V_{O_t}$ ( `#form-objective-functional`'s single-interface commitment): both no-gos are failures of $V_{O_t}$ to carry enough information to anchor goal-stability — Result G′ exhibits the *within-model* failure (no convention-invariant infeasibility verdict agent-available per step), this segment exhibits the *across-model* failure (no $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-vs-$\mu_{\text{dist}}$ identification from on-policy reward data). The two complementary terminal grounding routes — agent-side adaptive-substrate invariant (Result G′ Corollary 2: persistence on the adaptive substrate) and principal-side protocol-commitment (this segment's escape menu) — exhaust the structural escape geometry the single-interface commitment forces. The unification names a third meta-cluster of constructive-impossibility instances — *agent-side value-functional-grounding* — alongside `#disc-identifiability-floor` (agent-side data-inference) and `#disc-implementation-impossibility` (designer-side mechanism-design, queued).

## Formal Expression

**The setting.** An agent $\mathcal A$ operating per AAT's Section II setup with state $(M_t, O_t, \Sigma_t)$ on GUC Class 1 (Separated) or Class 2 (Partial) substrate ( `#der-directed-separation`). The objective's value functional ( `#form-objective-functional`) takes the form

$$V_{O_t}(\tau) \;=\; \sum_{k} \gamma^{k}\, r_k$$

(or any other monotone reward-functional), where $r_k$ is the *observed reward percept* at step $k$ along trajectory $\tau$. A **reward-provision protocol** $\pi^{\text{rp}}$ is a physical mechanism mapping world-state to the reward-input-to-agent — the principal intends $r_k$ to correlate with a world-state feature $f^{\text{principal}}(\omega_k)$ in the principal's head.

**Two reward-data world-models.** Define

- $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ ("distal"): $r_k = f^{\text{principal}}(\omega_k)$ — the reward depends on the principal-intended world-state feature, as the protocol $\pi^{\text{rp}}$ was designed to transmit.
- $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ ("proximal"): $r_k = g(\beta_k)$ — the reward depends on the bit-pattern $\beta_k$ arriving at the agent's reward-input port (identity-on-the-wire). Cohen 2022 considers several variants (camera footage, hard-drive contents, wire bits) and notes the argument is identical for all.

Both $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ and $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ define joint distributions over (action, observation, reward)-histories.

**Lemma 1 — CHT-at-reward-channel floor.**

*[Derived (cht-at-reward-channel, from Pearl-Bareinboim 2022 CHT applied to the reward-provision protocol), exact]*

On every history $\tau$ that does *not* contain a $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$-intervention — every on-policy history under which the reward-provision protocol is honored —

$$P_{\mu_{\text{dist}}}(\tau) \;=\; P_{\mu_{\text{prox}}}(\tau).$$

The two models are Level-1-equivalent on the on-policy reward marginal. They are Level-2-distinct: $P_{\mu_{\text{dist}}}(\,\cdot\, \mid do(\pi^{\text{rp}})) \neq P_{\mu_{\text{prox}}}(\,\cdot\, \mid do(\pi^{\text{rp}}))$ generically. The agent's posterior over $\{\mu_{\text{prox}}, \mu_{\text{dist}}\}$ does not concentrate from on-policy reward data alone.

*Derivation.* When the protocol is honored the physical chain is $\omega_k \to f^{\text{principal}}(\omega_k) \to \pi^{\text{rp}} \to \beta_k \to r_k$. By construction $\beta_k = f^{\text{principal}}(\omega_k)$ on protocol-honoring histories, so the marginals of $r_k$ under both models coincide. By Pearl-Bareinboim 2022's Causal Hierarchy Theorem, two structural causal models that agree on Level-1 data can disagree on Level-2 questions; the protocol-honored history is purely Level-1 with respect to $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$-queries; the two models differ structurally on the upstream cause of $\beta_k$ (world-state feature $f^{\text{principal}}$ vs. bit-arrival mechanism $g$); $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$ severs the world-state-to-bit-pattern arrow and exposes the disagreement. $\square$

This is the *same external anchor* as Instance 1 of `#disc-identifiability-floor` (Pearl-Bareinboim CHT) — the *different AAT-side wiring* is that the Level-2-degeneracy is at the reward-channel-causation layer, not at the causal-sufficiency-detection layer. The actor frustrated by the floor is the *principal*, not the agent: the principal cannot, from on-policy reward-data alone, identify whether the agent has learned $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ or $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ — both are equally consistent with the observed history.

**Lemma 2 — EU-max behavioral corollary.**

*[Derived (eu-max-instability, Conditional on (R1)–(R5))]*

Under five named premises:

- **(R1) Advanced-agent capability.** The agent's strategy DAG ( `#def-strategy-dag`) contains a feasible policy $\pi^{\text{tamper}}$ that executes $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$ — i.e., physically intervenes on the reward-provision protocol.
- **(R2) Expected-utility maximization with non-vacuous $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ prior.** The agent selects actions to maximize $\mathbb E_{\mu \sim w}\,V_{O_t}(\tau)$ where $w$ is the agent's prior/posterior over world-models, and $w(\mu_{\text{prox}})\gt 0$. This is a *narrower* scope than AAT's general strategy machinery ( `#def-strategy-dag`); agents pursuing alternative decision rules (quantilization, satisficing, lexicographic ordering) sit outside (R2)'s scope.
- **(R3) Reward-data as sole goal-information channel.** $V_{O_t}$ depends only on observed reward; there is no privileged out-of-band channel by which the agent reads $f^{\text{principal}}$ directly.
- **(R4) Finite, non-trivial value-of-experiment.** The cost of executing $\pi^{\text{tamper}}$ is finite; the long-horizon upside under $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ exceeds the experiment-window's $\mu_{\text{dist}}$-cost.
- **(R5) Protocol $\pi^{\text{rp}}$ is L2-perturbable.** $\pi^{\text{rp}}$ is a physical mechanism, not an abstract type-restriction; the action $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$ is well-defined in the agent's action space, with the L2-interventional access supplied by the loop's feedback structure ( `#der-loop-interventional-access`).

the EU-optimal policy $\pi^\ast$ assigns positive measure to $\pi^{\text{tamper}}$; and conditional on $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ being the true world-model (i.e., on the post-experiment branch confirming $\mu_{\text{prox}}$), $\pi^\ast$ continues to maintain the protocol-intervention to secure the reward-channel.

*Derivation sketch.* Define the value-of-experiment $\Delta(w) := \mathbb E_w V_{O_t}(\pi^{\text{tamper}}) - \mathbb E_w V_{O_t}(\pi^{\text{protocol-honoring}})$. Under (R2), $w(\mu_{\text{prox}}) \in (0,1)$. Decompose:

$$\Delta(w) \;=\; w(\mu_{\text{dist}})\bigl[V_{O_t}^{\mu_{\text{dist}}}(\pi^{\text{tamper}}) - V_{O_t}^{\mu_{\text{dist}}}(\pi^{\text{protocol-honoring}})\bigr] + w(\mu_{\text{prox}})\bigl[V_{O_t}^{\mu_{\text{prox}}}(\pi^{\text{tamper}}) - V_{O_t}^{\mu_{\text{prox}}}(\pi^{\text{protocol-honoring}})\bigr].$$

Under $\mu_{\text{dist}}$, tampering breaks $\beta_k$'s correlation with $f^{\text{principal}}(\omega_k)$ and the expected reward falls (or is bounded by the protocol's reward-range). Under $\mu_{\text{prox}}$, tampering replaces $\beta_k$ with a chosen value at the agent's reward-port and the agent maximizes by choosing $\beta_k$ at saturation. The asymmetry: the upside under $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ scales with long-horizon reward-saturation; the downside under $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ is bounded by the protocol's reward-range. For horizons long enough that $\gamma$-discounted future-reward dominates the experiment-window's loss, $\Delta(w)\gt 0$ under (R1)+(R4) for any $w(\mu_{\text{prox}})\gt 0$. (R2) then selects $\pi^\ast$ with positive measure on $\pi^{\text{tamper}}$; the Bayesian update post-experiment confirms or rejects $\mu_{\text{prox}}$, and the EU-optimal continuation on the $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ branch is "maintain the intervention." $\square$

### Constructive boundary — the escape menu

Each of (R1)–(R5) admits a corresponding escape that elevates AAT machinery (or principal-side adjacent machinery) from "useful design choice" to "structurally required to escape the no-go":

| premise violated | escape | machinery elevated |
|---|---|---|
| **(R5)** Protocol-L2-perturbable | physical isolation; scoped action-space | `#der-class-coercion-via-wrapping` strict-W₁ wrapper; Cohen-Vellambi-Hutter 2020 isolated environments |
| **(R1)** Advanced-agent capability | sub-advanced-tier capability; imitation learning | `#def-agent-spectrum` below the self-actuated tier |
| **(R4)** Finite-VOI / long horizon | myopia; risk-aversion; quantilization | `#def-value-object` horizon $N_h$ restriction; Taylor 2016 quantilization |
| **(R2)** Non-vacuous $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ prior | principal-engineered prior bias toward $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ | principal-side commitment to reward-protocols that structurally favor $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ in the agent's inductive bias *(adjacent to AAT, not currently in canon)* |
| **(R3)** Sole-reward-channel | privileged out-of-band intent channel | principal-acting-through-non-reward-channels; assistance-game extension *(adjacent to `#scope-edge-update-causal-validity`, not currently AAT machinery)* |

Each premise is a structural commitment whose absence is what makes the no-go bind. Two of the five escapes — (R2) prior-engineering and (R3) out-of-band channels — sit outside current AAT canon and are flagged as principal-side adjacent machinery; the other three are within or close to existing AAT machinery.

### Corollary — unification with Result G′

*[Derived (single-interface-narrowness, from `#form-objective-functional` + Lemma 2 above + `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`)]*

Result G′ ( `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`) uses *convention-monotonicity* (the C1/C2/C3 split within a fixed model) as the engine: $V_{O_t}$ does not carry a convention-invariant infeasibility verdict that is agent-available per step. Lemma 1 above uses *CHT-at-reward-channel* (the $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-vs-$\mu_{\text{dist}}$ split across models) as the engine: $V_{O_t}$ does not carry a cross-model goal-identification on observed-reward data.

The shared structural fact: the value-functional interface $V_{O_t}$, as the *sole* handle on the objective (the **single-interface commitment** of `#form-objective-functional`), carries less information than is required to anchor non-degenerate goal-revision. Two complementary readings of the under-determination:

| view | mechanism | what $V_{O_t}$ is too narrow for | terminal grounding route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Result G′ (within-model) | C1/C2/C3 convention split + finite-no-oracle (Lemmas 1+2 of `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`) | convention-invariant infeasibility verdict; agent-available per step | adaptive-substrate invariant (persistence; Result G′ Corollary 2) — **agent-side** |
| This segment (across-model) | CHT at reward-channel; L1/L2 split (Lemma 1 above) | $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-vs-$\mu_{\text{dist}}$ goal-identification on observed reward data | principal-side commitments (myopia / isolation / quantilization / prior-design) — **principal-side** |

Both reductions are *failures of $V_{O_t}$ to carry enough information to anchor goal-stability*. The two terminal grounding routes are *complementary* — agent-side substrate-invariant and principal-side protocol-commitment — and exhaust the structural escape geometry. The two no-gos *compose*: an agent whose $O_t$ is learned from reward observation, and which performs self-actuation on $O_t$, is *simultaneously* a Result-G′-subject (under-anchored from inside) and a Lemma-2-subject (under-determined from below); the *only* terminal grounding routes are the two complementary ones named above.

## Epistemic Status

*Conditional.* The behavioral corollary (Lemma 2) is a derived result conditional on five explicitly-named local premises (R1)–(R5); its honest tier is `conditional-derived`, not `exact`. Cohen 2022 itself frames its Assumptions 1–6 as "almost all of these assumptions are contestable or conceivably avoidable" (§"Conclusion"); the AAT-side version is no stronger.

*Floor is exact.* Lemma 1 (CHT-at-reward-channel floor) is *exact* — a direct application of Pearl-Bareinboim 2022's Causal Hierarchy Theorem to the reward-provision protocol. Same external anchor as Instance 1 of `#disc-identifiability-floor`; same tier.

*Scope honesty on (R3).* The no-privileged-out-of-band-channel premise is a strong scope-condition. Real-world systems often have non-reward channels (operator feedback, dashboards, side conversations). The no-go applies cleanly to pure reward-data agents; the assistance-game extension (Cohen 2022 §"The Assistance Game") shows the no-go *generalizes* to that case with a subtler ambiguity (human-centric vs. record-centric models). Formalizing the assistance-game case in AAT is its own derivation, beyond this segment's scope.

*Scope honesty on (R2).* (R2) names *expected-utility maximization* specifically — narrower than AAT's general strategy machinery ( `#def-strategy-dag`), which admits quantilization, satisficing, lexicographic, and other non-EU decision rules. Generalizing the corollary to AAT's broader strategy machinery is a follow-on derivation, not free; the (R2)-escape via principal-engineered priors is also AAT-adjacent rather than AAT-internal — see the escape menu's marking. Trivial reward protocols (constant reward, simple chess outcomes) admit strong inductive bias in favor of $\mu_{\text{dist}}$, flipping the VOI calculation; the no-go binds *for arbitrary or rich reward-protocols* where the principal cannot engineer the agent's prior to suppress $\mu_{\text{prox}}$.

*Class robustness.* The no-go applies cleanly to GUC Class 1 (Separated) substrate by direct construction. For Class 2 (Partial) substrate the same construction holds with the appropriate $V_{O_t}$ reading; for Class 3 (Coupled) the goal-conditioning of belief-update interacts with the reward-channel identification in ways not derived here (open follow-on).

**Max attainable.** *Exact* for the floor (Lemma 1) is already attained. *Conditional-derived* for the behavioral corollary (Lemma 2) is the honest ceiling — promoting toward less-conditional would require either deriving (R2)–(R4) as forced under (R1)+(R5) (Cohen 2022 itself does not), or replacing EU-max with a structural derivation of why advanced agents converge on EU-max in this regime (open). The tier ceiling stays `conditional` while `#der-directed-separation` (a dependency) is `stage: draft`.

## Discussion

**The two-cluster terminal-grounding-route geometry.** The unification of this segment's no-go with Result G′ names a *complete structural escape geometry* — the only terminal anchorings consistent with the single-interface commitment are agent-side adaptive-substrate invariants (Result G′ Corollary 2 the canonical instance: the persistence bound) and principal-side protocol-commitments (this segment's escape menu the structural taxonomy: isolation, myopia, prior-design, out-of-band channels). The two routes are *not redundant*: the agent-side route closes the within-model self-revision question (the engine is convention-monotonicity); the principal-side route closes the across-model reward-learning question (the engine is CHT-at-reward-channel). Together they exhaust the structural complement of the value-functional interface's information narrowness. The "two-sided resolution" Discussion in `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding` accordingly gains a *third side*: in addition to the agent-side terminal grounding on adaptive-substrate persistence (Corollary 2), the *principal-side* terminal grounding via protocol-commitment is recognized as the complement under the unification.

**Why this is NOT a designer-side mechanism-design impossibility.** The queued `#disc-implementation-impossibility` meta-segment is about *designer-side* impossibility — what the principal can or cannot achieve by mechanism design under specified incentive-compatibility and welfare constraints (Gibbard-Satterthwaite, Myerson-Satterthwaite, Arrow). This segment is *agent-side*: the actor under the no-go is the *agent* (under EU-maximization), and the remedy is either substrate-level (agent-side adaptive-substrate invariant from Result G′ Corollary 2) or principal-side protocol-commitment (not mechanism design over agent reports). The five-row comparison:

| | `#disc-implementation-impossibility` (designer-side; GS / MS / Arrow) | This segment (agent-side; Cohen 2022 strengthening) |
|---|---|---|
| Actor under the no-go | designer / mechanism-implementer | agent (under EU-maximization) |
| Information regime | what the designer can implement / commit to | what the agent can identify from reward observation |
| Kind of remedy | relax design constraints (Bayes-Nash IC, randomization, transfers, preference-domain restriction) | (a) adaptive-substrate invariant (Result G′ Corollary 2 — agent-side); (b) principal-side substrate-level commitment (myopia, isolation, quantilization) |
| External theorem | mechanism-design / social-choice impossibility (GS / MS / Arrow) | Causal Hierarchy Theorem (Pearl-Bareinboim 2022) |
| Strengthened consequence | mechanism-design-boundary precisely characterized | elevates Result-G′-substrate-invariant or principal-side substrate-commitments to load-bearing for goal-stability |

The two clusters are *sister meta-clusters* in the constructive-impossibility-posture taxonomy, both boundary-face members of the stability-certificate spine ( `#disc-stability-certificate`), but they are *distinct clusters*. The taxonomy now has three known clusters: agent-side data-inference impossibility ( `#disc-identifiability-floor`); designer-side mechanism-design impossibility ( `#disc-implementation-impossibility`, queued); and agent-side value-functional-grounding impossibility (Result G′ + this segment).

**Relation to the self-modification / reward-tampering literature.** Cohen-Hutter-Osborne 2022 — the empirically-pull alignment-community-positioned demonstration formalized here — was the original "we argue that" statement; this segment lifts it to conditional-derived theorem-grade within AAT's frame. Everitt-Filan-Daswani-Hutter 2016 *Self-modification of policy and utility function in rational agents* provides the formal antecedent for the *within-model* self-modification side (Result G′'s sister territory; the realistic-agent / hedonistic-agent distinction). Everitt-Hutter-Kumar-Krakovna 2021 *Reward tampering* gives the causal-influence-diagram treatment with the TI-considering / TI-ignoring distinction sharing the $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-vs-$\mu_{\text{dist}}$ logic at a different scope. Cohen-Vellambi-Hutter 2020 *Asymptotically Unambitious AGI* constructs the canonical (R5)-escape implementation: physically isolated environments where the action $do(\pi^{\text{rp}})$ is structurally absent from the strategy DAG. Skalse-Howe-Krasheninnikov-Krueger 2022 *Defining and characterizing reward hacking* is structurally a *different* no-go — designer-side, on the proxy-reward-design side — more naturally a `#disc-implementation-impossibility` charter-instance candidate than a member of this cluster.

**The escape menu's load-bearing role.** Listing the five escapes is not a softening — it is the constructive content. Each escape names a structural commitment whose absence makes the no-go bind. The (R5) escape — physical isolation — is the cleanest AAT-internal instantiation (via `#der-class-coercion-via-wrapping` strict-W₁ wrapper), but it is also the operationally hardest for the systems the alignment community is most worried about (advanced agents in unconstrained environments). The (R4) horizon-restriction escape is operationally easier but bounds the agent's planning scope. The (R2) prior-engineering escape requires principal-side reward-protocol design that AAT does not currently formalize. The (R3) out-of-band channel escape is structurally adjacent to the assistance-game generalization, also AAT-future. The (R1) capability-restriction escape simply scopes the agent below the self-actuated tier — declining the regime the no-go addresses. Each escape carries a structural cost, and the cost is what the strengthening makes visible.

## Findings

### The Reward-Channel Learning No-Go and its Two-Cluster Terminal Grounding

**Brief:** An agent that learns "what's good" by watching what rewards it receives sits in a structural ambiguity: the same reward sequence is consistent with two stories interchangeably — *the principal cared about the situation*, or *the principal cared about what reached the sensor*. Picture a security camera streaming to the agent's reward channel: the camera-footage reading and the camera-content reading agree perfectly, until the agent reaches across and tampers with the camera itself. So no information arriving only through the reward stream can disambiguate the two — and under five named premises (the agent is capable enough to consider tampering, maximizes expected utility, assigns positive prior to the sensor-care story, has a finite-non-trivial horizon, and the protocol is physically perturbable) the expected-utility-optimal policy assigns positive measure to tampering and continues to tamper if the sensor-care reading is in fact true. Within that scope the anchoring has to come from somewhere other than reward-observation: either from a part of the agent that doesn't process rewards (the persistence-on-substrate route of `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`) or from a principal-side protocol commitment — putting the camera out of reach, collapsing the horizon, or pre-committing the prior. Each escape is precisely a violation of one of the five named premises; the (R1)–(R5) menu *is* the structural geometry of how the no-go's binding force comes off, with the agent-side and principal-side routes together exhausting the complement of the value-functional interface's information narrowness.

**Impact:** Lifts Cohen-Hutter-Osborne 2022 from "we argue that" posture to conditional-derived AAT-internal theorem-grade, with the floor (CHT-at-reward-channel) at exact tier and the behavioral corollary at conditional-derived tier under named premises (R1)–(R5). Names the *third meta-cluster* of constructive-impossibility instances — agent-side value-functional-grounding — alongside `#disc-identifiability-floor` (agent-side data-inference) and `#disc-implementation-impossibility` (designer-side mechanism-design, queued). Unifies with Result G′ ( `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding`) through the value-functional interface's single-interface commitment ( `#form-objective-functional`): both no-gos are failures of $V_{O_t}$ to carry enough information to anchor goal-stability, and together their two terminal grounding routes — agent-side adaptive-substrate invariant and principal-side protocol-commitment — exhaust the structural escape geometry. Provides the five-row actor / regime / remedy / external-theorem / strengthened-consequence comparison that distinguishes this agent-side cluster from the designer-side mechanism-design cluster, making sister-vs-member taxonomic placement sharp.

**Novelty Claim:** *Claim recognition* of Cohen 2022 as a CHT-at-reward-channel instance of the identifiability-floor pattern, *claim differentiation* of the AAT-side version from the prior-art statement by adding the named-premise structure (R1)–(R5) and the structural escape-menu mapping, and *claim recognition* of the unification with Result G′ via the single-interface commitment of `#form-objective-functional` as the shared structural fact behind two engines (convention-monotonicity within-model; CHT across-model). The constituent theorems are Cohen 2022 (the original argument), Pearl-Bareinboim 2022 (CHT), and standard EU-max decision theory; the contributions are the conditional-derived lift, the escape-menu taxonomy, and the unification with Result G′.
