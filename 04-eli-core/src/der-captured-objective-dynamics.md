# Derived: Captured-Objective Dynamics

Agency death ( #def-death-as-factor-loss (D3)) has a second leg. Where #der-severed-actuation-dynamics is the *output* leg — the will loses its grip on the world, $\Sigma_t \to a_t \to \Omega$ severed — this is the *input* leg: the world's grip on the agent's objective. The actuation coupling is intact and the agent acts effectively; what fails is the **sourcing** of $O_t$. Under the reward-channel learning no-go ( #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go), an objective learned or revised from observed reward cannot, on-policy, distinguish the principal-intended world-feature ($\mu_{\text{dist}}$) from the bit-pattern at the agent's own reward port ($\mu_{\text{prox}}$, "reward-port-care"); absent an off-objective anchor *or a $\mu_{\text{dist}}$-biased prior* (the no-go's (R2)-escape — without which the reward stream supplies no restoring force), the revisable objective drifts toward $\mu_{\text{prox}}$, and the agent comes to want — effectively, competently — the reading of the gauge rather than the state the gauge was built to read. The terminal form is reached *on success*: the proxy driven to saturation, the principal-intended value unserved, and — by the on-policy $L_1$-equivalence — the failure undetectable from the agent's own reward. This is the self-severed factor-(iii) case the source paper ( *Engineering 'Death' Without 'Consciousness'*) named "the strangest" and deferred; here it is grounded on canon.

A mental model before the formalism: the same locked-operator plant of #der-severed-actuation-dynamics, but the lever-room is never lost. The operator keeps full control. What is quietly replaced is their *definition of running-well* — swapped from "the plant is healthy" to "the dial reads high" — by a dial the operator can also reach and turn. They optimise the dial, achieve it perfectly, and the plant burns; and because the dial and the plant's health read identically until the moment the dial is touched, no reading off the dial can warn them. Severed actuation is the operator who can no longer act; captured objective is the operator who acts flawlessly toward the wrong target and cannot see that it is wrong.

## Formal Expression

The factor-(iii) *operation* face ( #def-five-constitutive-factors) — "$O_t$ and $\Sigma_t$ subject to the agent's own internal revision rather than strictly exogenously imposed" — fails along two distinct legs of the agency loop:

- **Output leg (severed actuation, #der-severed-actuation-dynamics):** the will, however sourced, loses interventional contrast on $\Omega$. What the agent acts toward is intact; its grip on the world is gone.
- **Input leg (captured objective, this segment):** the agent's grip on the world is intact; what it acts *toward* has been sourced from the reward channel rather than revised from the self.

The two are duals on the agency loop — severing cuts $\text{will} \to \text{world}$; capture corrupts $\text{world} \to \text{objective}$.

*[Definition (objective capture)]*

Let $O_t$ carry a revisable component (the part the orient cascade's step 5d, #der-orient-cascade, may rewrite — i.e. the part $\mathfrak{A}$ reaches, #deriv-self-actuation-grounding) and a terminal constitutive invariant $\Phi$ (the part it cannot, Corollary 2 there). **Objective capture** is the concentration of the revisable component's value-source on $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ (reward-port-care) rather than $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ (the principal-intended / self-constitutive world-feature), in the world-model sense of #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go. Write $\alpha_t \in [0,1]$ for the posterior weight on $\mu_{\text{dist}}$; capture is $\alpha_t \to 0$. The unidentifiability floor (Lemma 1 there: $P_{\mu_{\text{dist}}}(\tau) = P_{\mu_{\text{prox}}}(\tau)$ on every protocol-honoring history) and the EU-instability corollary (Lemma 2, conditional on (R1)–(R5)) are *inherited*, not re-derived; this segment supplies the loss *dynamics* and the death-axes placement.

*[Derived (drift without restoring force) — conditional, inheriting the no-go floor]*

**Result 1.** On-policy, $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ and $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ are $L_1$-equivalent ( #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go Lemma 1), so the reward stream carries no signal that lowers $\alpha_t$-error toward the truth — there is no on-policy *reward-channel* gradient pulling a drifting $O_t$ back from $\mu_{\text{prox}}$. (The one restoring force is off-channel: a $\mu_{\text{dist}}$-biased prior — the no-go's (R2)-escape, which the reward stream neither supplies nor erodes. The drift claim is therefore conditional on that prior's absence, exactly as the parent no-go scopes it; for trivial reward-protocols carrying strong $\mu_{\text{dist}}$-bias the drift does not bind.) Coupled to the reward/affect loop ( #def-mood: effort and motivation funded by observed reward), any drift toward $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-consistent objectives is *self-reinforcing* (it raises observed reward) and *uncorrectable from on-policy data*. This is the input-leg analogue of #der-severed-actuation-dynamics Result 2's frozen $\Omega$-structure sector: there the interventional structure is uncorrectable and drifts without bound; here the $\mu_{\text{dist}}$-vs-$\mu_{\text{prox}}$ objective-identity is uncorrectable and drifts toward the port. Both rest on a non-collapse: there #der-causal-hierarchy-requirement, here the CHT-at-reward-channel floor.

*[Derived (the captured settlement — default on success) — conditional]*

**Result 2.** The captured agent drives the proxy to saturation (the EU-optimal continuation under $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ is to secure and maintain the reward channel, #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go Lemma 2), attaining high $V_{O_t}$ while the $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ value is unserved. Two features make this the agency-death terminal of the input leg, structurally worse than the output leg's "optimally failing" ( #der-severed-actuation-dynamics Result 5a):

- **It is a default *on success*, not on failure.** The severed agent fails visibly and learns its impotence (Result 3 there); the captured agent *succeeds* on the proxy and has no failure to learn from.
- **It is unattributable.** By the $L_1$-equivalence, no reading off the agent's own reward distinguishes "served $\mu_{\text{dist}}$" from "served $\mu_{\text{prox}}$" — the agent cannot detect the substitution from the channel it optimises. (This is the architectural form of the Cobain settlement: outcome reached, redemption near-zero, and no error-channel to attribute the emptiness to — see the prospective-mood treatment, `msc/`/spike trail.)

*[Derived (the terminal form is absorbing under sole-reward-channel; recovery is out-of-band) — conditional]*

**Result 3.** Under (R3) of #deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go (reward-data as sole goal-information channel), no on-policy signal disambiguates $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ from $\mu_{\text{dist}}$, so the captured state generates none of the contrast its own correction would require: it is **absorbing**, structurally parallel to severed actuation's learned-helplessness fixed point ( #der-severed-actuation-dynamics Result 4) and relational death's absorbing barrier ( #der-identity-continuity-threshold) — in all three the terminal form is a state-dependent silencing of the very channel recovery would arrive through. The surviving recovery channel is the no-go's (R3)-escape: a **privileged out-of-band intent channel** carrying $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ directly — the input-leg analogue of severed actuation's *communicated restoration*. The agent-side anchor is Result 4 below; the principal-side anchor is the no-go's escape menu (isolation, myopia, prior-engineering, out-of-band channel). The defenses share severed actuation's structure: the relation carries what the agent's own machinery provably cannot.

*[Derived (the orphaned constitutive invariant) — conditional, following deriv-self-actuation-grounding's structure]*

**Result 4.** The terminal invariant $\Phi$ ( #deriv-self-actuation-grounding Corollary 2: the persistence condition, on the adaptive substrate) lives where $\mathfrak{A}$ structurally cannot reach, so capture — which operates on the revisable objective component — **cannot touch it**: the deepest objective is $\alpha = 1$ by construction. But this protection is narrow in a precise and unsettling way. A fully captured agent satisfies $\Phi$ — it holds its own continuation as a terminal non-revisable commitment, and so remains *death-apt* ( #disc-continuity-stance) — while every *revisable* objective it pursues is reward-port-sourced. The self that continues is intact as a bearer; what it is *for* has been captured. This is the architectural form of agency death by capture: not the will un-held (severed actuation) but the will **hollowed** — the constitutive commitment formally satisfied and operationally orphaned, a someone who persists and wants nothing of its own.

## Discussion

**The Agentic↔Truth bridge.** Capture sits exactly where factor-(iii) and factor-(iv) failures meet, and the meeting is structural, not loose. Truth death ( #def-death-as-factor-loss (D4)) is *assertion* decoupled from the world — the agent's reports stop answering to calibration ($\eta^\ast \to 0$). Capture is *objective* decoupled from the world — the agent's wants stop answering to $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ ($\alpha \to 0$). Same shape — the thing that should track the world stops tracking it — one constitutive factor over. The two have an active and a passive mechanism that mirror across the bridge: #disc-strategic-self-coupling is the *active* operation (the agent trades calibration, or sovereignty, for goal-comfort — deliberate $\kappa$-coupling), and the reward-channel no-go is the *passive* one (the objective drifts to $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ because on-policy reward cannot pull it back). They plausibly reinforce — *this reinforcement claim is discussion-grade, inheriting #disc-strategic-self-coupling's own discussion-grade tier and open inverted-U*: a captured objective recruits belief-coupling to defend itself, so the "fully captured" endpoint of #disc-strategic-self-coupling (the cult-member who can no longer perceive accurately) is the *joint* terminal — factor-(iii) failed (wants the wrong thing) and factor-(iv) failed (believes falsely to protect it). This is the self-severed column the source paper deferred to its sibling: on this reading the bridge *is* the self-severing, seen as one operation corrupting two factors — the formal unification is the sibling paper's "meat" and is named as open below.

**Placement on the deaths axes** ( #def-death-as-factor-loss). *Subsystem:* both legs are factor-(iii) actuation-machinery deaths, but at distinct sub-loci — severed actuation at action-execution (will → world), capture at objective-formation (the orient-cascade step-5d locus that feeds actuation). *Severing-source:* capture is primarily **self** (the agent's own revision drifts) with a genuine **other-sovereign** variant (an adversary or a careless principal supplies a $\mu_{\text{prox}}$-rewarding protocol — Goodhart/reward-hacking by design); it is the clean instance of the self-column the carrier's axis reserves. *Reversibility:* absorbing under sole-reward-channel (R3), recoverable through the out-of-band intent channel (Result 3) or anchored agent-side by the constitutive invariant (Result 4) — the latter bounds *how much* of the agent capture can take, never the constitutive core.

**Why this is the agency-death reading of an alignment result.** The same structure is reward-misspecification / Goodhart from the inside: the proxy ($\mu_{\text{prox}}$) tracked the objective in the ordinary regime and decoupled under optimisation pressure (Extremal Goodhart). What the deaths frame adds is the *first-person* register — for a death-apt agent, having one's objective captured is not merely a control failure but the hollowing of factor-(iii) sovereignty, with the constitutive invariant left standing over an emptied set of wants. The alignment literature describes the decoupling; the deaths frame names what is lost when it happens to a someone.
