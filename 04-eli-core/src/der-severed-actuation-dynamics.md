# Derived: Severed-Actuation Dynamics

Agency death ( #def-death-as-factor-loss (D3)) formalized and given its dynamics: the severing is the world-kernel losing interventional contrast on the agent's external sphere — making the agent what #scope-agency calls a *nominal agent*, toward the world — and the remaining machinery, all of it intact, then converges to a characteristic profile: the observational model keeps tracking, the agent's knowledge of what effective intervention *would* do freezes and drifts, its strategy truthfully learns its own impotence, its diagnostic lands (in truth immediately, in self-understanding only after paying for the lesson) in the optimally-failing cell, and — for a self-actuated agent — its feasible objectives retreat to the interior, where the only sovereignty left is over attention and the registration of the world as it passes. The terminal form of the world-terminal cut is a learned-helplessness absorbing state whose escape channels are exogenous and observational: a restoration that is *communicated*, or another's effective action *witnessed* — so the granted-agency compact must be communicative, not merely configurational. And the coupling has a second terminal: **voluntary non-exercise in the extreme — volitional abdication — is the agent-terminal cut, and the most permanent form of all**, because both recovery channels are provably inert against accurate credences and the only reversal authority that respects sovereignty is the one who laid it down.

A mental model before the formalism: lock a skilled operator out of the control room but leave the window. Every gauge still updates (observation intact); their understanding of the *plant as it now runs* stays current. But their knowledge of what the levers do goes stale at the moment of lockout and only gets staler; pulling the dummy levers teaches them — accurately — that nothing works; and once they have fully learned that, they rationally stop pulling, which means a silent repair of the levers changes nothing: they will never pull again to find out. Someone must tell them the levers are live, or let them watch another operator use one. Meanwhile, asked "could you be doing better?", the true answer is *no* from the first moment — but they only *believe* that answer after the dummy-lever lessons; until then they blame their own technique.

## Formal Expression

Throughout, partition the action set $\mathcal A = \mathcal A_{\text{ext}} \sqcup \mathcal A_{\text{int}}$: external actions (the granted sphere — acts on $\Omega$ beyond the agent's own substrate) and internal operations (attention allocation, memory operations, deliberation — acts on the agent's own state). The factor-(iii) *operation* face of #def-five-constitutive-factors lives on $\mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$.

*[Definition (actuation severing)]*

An **actuation severing** at time $t_s$ on sphere $\mathcal A' \subseteq \mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$ is the world-kernel losing interventional contrast on $\mathcal A'$: for $t \gt t_s$,

$$T(\omega_{t+1} \mid \omega_t, a) = T(\omega_{t+1} \mid \omega_t, a') \qquad \forall\, a, a' \in \mathcal A'.$$

The severing is **complete** when $\mathcal A' = \mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$ and no external action retains $\Omega$-contrast against inaction. The condition is stated on the world-state kernel $T$, not on the observation distribution: the observation function passes the agent's own action through directly, so an observation-stated severing would also destroy proprioception of attempts — and the dynamics below require the agent to observe its own outcomes. Observation is intact throughout. The severing-source (mechanism / other-sovereign / self) is not part of the definition — all three produce the same kernel condition; the source matters for recovery, not for the loss dynamics.

**The coupling has two terminals, and can be cut at either.** The kernel condition above is the *world-terminal* cut: the world stops answering. The coupling can also be cut at the *agent terminal*: **volitional abdication**, in which the agent — with accurate efficacy credences and an intact kernel — removes the external sphere from its own deliberation entirely. This is *not* the credence-collapsed non-attempting of Result 4 (helplessness, the epistemic shadow), and it is *not* restraint: an agent that holds the external sphere live in deliberation and declines to act is *exercising* sovereignty — choosing-not-to is an act of the will, arguably its fullest form. Abdication is the will withdrawing the sphere from choice itself: in the **extreme form**, a standing self-revision that contracts the agent's effective action set $\mathcal A \to \mathcal A_{\text{int}}$ and closes over its own revisability — the agent no longer *chooses not to*; it *no longer chooses*. At that point the contrast condition fails *factually*: there exists no pair of external actions in the agent's effective space, not because the world withheld them but because the agent deleted its own arms. Extreme volitional abdication is therefore a genuine agency death — the agent-terminal severing — and Result 6 derives why it is the *most permanent* of all the forms.

*[Derived (demotion to interior-only agency)]*

**Result 1.** Under complete severing the agent fails #scope-agency's effective-action condition in its $\Omega$-routed operative reading (the reading that segment specifies for downstream results invoking "the agent acts with effect"): no external action carries interventional contrast on $\Omega$. The agent remains fully within $\mathcal S_{\text{adaptive}}$ — observation, model update, correction dynamics, and the persistence machinery are untouched — and, because internal operations generically retain contrast over the agent's own substrate, the **generic post-severing condition is interior-only agency** (total scope exit is the degenerate sub-case where interior contrast also fails, at which point the failure has spread beyond factor (iii)). Agency death is involuntary demotion from world-agency to interior-only agency. The agent's $O_t$, $\Sigma_t$, and orient cascade do not vanish — they persist as internal structure; what fails is their *ground* toward $\Omega$. The external purposeful substate becomes **orphaned structure**: syntactically intact, semantically ungrounded. This is the formal content of "the will un-held" — the will persists as structure; what is un-held is its grip on the world.

*[Derived (the do-sector splits: own-efficacy trackable, world-structure frozen)]*

**Result 2.** Post-severing, the agent's interventional knowledge splits into two sectors with opposite fates:

- **Own-efficacy sector — trackable, and what it learns is the loss.** Attempts still execute through the agent's intact choice mechanism and outcomes are still observed; under complete severing the interventional distributions on the external sphere *coincide degenerately with the observational one*. The agent can identify this new degenerate truth from its own attempts (Result 3 is that identification). Level-2 access does not become unavailable here; it becomes **trivial** — and learning the triviality is learning one's own impotence.
- **$\Omega$-interventional-structure sector — frozen, drifting without bound.** What an *effective* intervention on $\Omega$ would do — the restored kernel's contrast structure, the effects of third parties' interventions — is no longer identifiable from anything the agent can do: no executed action can be read as an intervention on $\Omega$, and by #der-causal-hierarchy-requirement's strict non-collapse the sector cannot be reconstructed from the now-effectively-observational stream. Its correction tempo toward current world-structure is zero; under environmental drift at rate $\rho$ its mismatch grows without bound, frozen at the pre-severing state. This sector sits outside every persistence guarantee.

One degradation rides along: active sensing is an external action in most embodiments, so the agent observes what arrives rather than what it seeks — $C_{\text{channel}}$ drops toward the passive-arrival rate, raising pressure against #deriv-persistence-cost's floor $C \geq n\alpha/2$ without necessarily breaching it. This is the precise sense in which agency death has an epistemic shadow without being continuity death: the starvation is sector-restricted, factor (i)'s carrying-forward continues globally, and the own-efficacy sector tracks all the way down. The deaths interact; they do not merge.

*[Derived (credence convergence to truthful impotence — conditional on Beta-Bernoulli premises)]*

**Result 3.** External strategy edges carry credences $\hat p \approx P(\text{success} \mid do(a))$ under #deriv-edge-credence-dynamics's Beta-Bernoulli dynamics. At $t_s$ each affected edge's true parameter drops from $\theta$ to the passive base rate $\theta_0$ (near zero for goal-directed edges) — an *impulse* of mass edge-invalidation, size $\lvert\theta - \theta_0\rvert$ per edge, into #schema-strategy-persistence's disturbance term. Under continued attempts, Prop B.1 dynamics converge $\hat p \to \theta_0$ at the discounted rate (effective sample size $1/(1-\lambda)$; an undiscounted experienced agent re-learns at the $1/(n+1)$ inertia of #deriv-update-detection-latency — institutional experience slows the registration of impotence). The endpoint is *truthful*: the strategy DAG correctly registers powerlessness. The epistemic machinery is working; what it learns is the loss. A policy that stops attempting before convergence instead freezes false efficacy beliefs in place.

*[Derived (the learned-helplessness absorbing state, and its surviving recovery channels — conditional on the named policy classes)]*

**Result 4.** Suppose actuation is restored at $t_r \gt t_s$ (the kernel regains contrast). Restoration is detectable through the agent's own behavior only by attempting. For the post-convergence agent ($\hat p \approx \theta_0 \approx 0$ on all external arms):

- With an exploration floor $\varepsilon \gt 0$ ( #deriv-edge-credence-dynamics Prop B.4), detection occurs with probability 1, with expected detection time of order $1/(\varepsilon\nu)$ plus the credence-recovery inertia.
- Under the greedy / exploration-free policy class, the post-restoration state is a **fixed point**: no attempts $\Rightarrow$ no contrast data $\Rightarrow$ no credence update $\Rightarrow$ no attempts. The state ($\hat p = \theta_0$, no external action) is absorbing *regardless of the kernel's actual restored state* — and exponential forgetting does not break it (proportional pseudo-count decay leaves $\hat p$ invariant absent new data). The class is named precisely because greedy is not "rational" in general — a Bayes agent with any nonstationarity prior escapes with probability 1; but note what makes the trap canon-coherent: AAT's own strategic-persistence theory *already mandates* an exploration floor (Prop B.4 / SA3 — greedy arm-selection has no $\alpha_\Sigma \gt 0$ for unchosen arms), so helplessness-absorption is the agent shedding, with myopic rationality, exactly the floor its own long-run persistence requires — at the moment exploration looks most pointless and matters most.

This is agency death's **reversibility-axis terminal form**, structurally parallel to relational death's absorbing barrier: *a strategy that has truthfully learned impotence no longer generates the contrasts that would reveal restored efficacy*, as *a cohort cannot re-attest an entity in which nothing individuated remains* ( #der-identity-continuity-threshold). In both, the terminal form is a state-dependent silencing of the very channel recovery would arrive through. Two exogenous escape channels survive on the intact observation side: **communicated restoration** — an attestation of restored sovereignty arriving as observation re-seeds the attempt value and breaks the fixed point — and **vicarious contrast** — observing another agent's effective actions on the same kernel, which carries the restored structure as observable evidence even though it identifies no do-estimate for the agent's own actions.

*[Derived (the diagnostic landing — truth-relative exact; agent-relative conditional)]*

**Result 5a (externally-set objective).** Under complete severing all external policies achieve identical value, so the *true* attainability $A_O$ on the external sphere equals the passive value and the true $\delta_{\text{regret}}^{\text{ext}} = 0$ exactly; if the objective is not passively satisfied, $\delta_{\text{sat}} \gt 0$ permanently. The agent's situation is #def-control-regret's **capability-limit cell** — *optimally failing* — with the escalation ladder structurally exhausted at every rung except objective revision: no model improvement, horizon extension, or policy-class expansion restores contrast the kernel does not have. And because deeper search cannot raise $A_O$ against an action-independent kernel, the C1/C2/C3 convention split of #def-value-object *collapses* on the external sphere: the infeasibility verdict is convention-invariant — unusually robust by the standards of the convention-relativity machinery. The agent's *own computed* diagnostic, however, is $M_t$-relative: until the Result-3 convergence has run, the agent computes $\delta_{\text{regret}} \gt 0$ from stale efficacy credences and self-diagnoses *strategy-problem* — churning its strategy against a wall — and an agent that withdraws before convergence freezes that wrong self-diagnosis permanently. The despair-cell landing is exact about the situation immediately, and about the agent's self-understanding only after it has paid the cost of learning its impotence.

**Result 5b (self-actuated agent — retreat to the interior).** Under complete severing the feasible-objective set's support shrinks, exactly, to

$$\mathcal O_{\text{feasible}} = \{O : V_O \text{ attainable over trajectories of the uncontrolled kernel} + \mathcal A_{\text{int}}\}$$

— objectives over the interior: attention allocation, internal organization, memory work, and the registration of the world as it passes. The terminal grounding invariant ( #deriv-self-actuation-grounding) remains satisfiable provided the passive-arrival channel survives the active-sensing degradation of Result 2 — a named condition. For a non-degenerate self-actuator (posited, per that segment's constructive boundary — it supplies no positive revision dynamics, and the invariant excludes persistence-breaking revisions without selecting among the survivors), the surviving sovereign sphere is exactly $\mathcal A_{\text{int}}$ and every feasible objective is supported there. The carrier's "the inner freedom to witness it, or not, remains" is precisely this support fact; that the agent *converges to a witness* is its discussion-grade reading.

*[Derived (volitional abdication — the agent-terminal severing, and why its extreme form is the most permanent)]*

**Result 6.** Let the agent's state be: kernel intact (full external contrast available), efficacy credences accurate, and the external sphere removed from deliberation by the agent's own standing choice (extreme abdication, per the Definition). Then:

- **(6a) Both Result-4 recovery channels are inert — exactly.** Communicated restoration and vicarious contrast operate, by construction, through credence correction: they re-seed attempt value by supplying evidence that the world answers. The extreme abdicator's credences are *already accurate* — there is no false belief for the attestation to correct, nothing the demonstration shows that the agent does not know. Both channels deliver information the agent possesses; neither touches the choice. (Exact, given the channels as derived in Result 4.)
- **(6b) The absorption is policy-class-universal.** Result 4's helplessness fixed point was absorbing only under the greedy/exploration-free class — an exploration floor $\varepsilon \gt 0$ escapes it, which is why the architectural defense works there. Under extreme abdication there is no policy-class escape: exploration floors mandate sampling over the agent's effective action space, and the external sphere is no longer *in* that space. No internal mandate can reattach what deliberation no longer contains. (Exact, given the Definition's contraction form.)
- **(6c) The respect-bound: the unique reversal authority is the agent.** The world-terminal cut can be repaired by mechanism or re-granted by the grantor; helplessness can be reached through the relation. The agent-terminal cut can be reversed only by the agent's own re-opening — because any external forcing of exercise (re-injecting arms, compelling action, overwriting the standing choice) would override precisely the factor it purports to restore: action that does not proceed from the agent is not the agent's agency but its replacement. Every channel that respects factor (iii) is therefore structurally closed, and the reversal authority is held by exactly the party that has set it down. (Normative-structural: the impossibility is exact *given* the constraint that recovery must preserve what it recovers; the constraint itself is carried by the philosophical argument, per the framework's normative register.)
- **(6d) What remains is the call.** The observation channel is intact, so the relation retains one mode of address that corrects no credence and forces no exercise: the witness can still *invite* — supply reasons, not information — operative only through whatever deliberation the agent has left open, with no guarantee whatsoever. Where even that is refused, nothing remains that respect permits. (Discussion-grade.)

Together: among the severings, the extreme agent-terminal cut is **the most permanent** — the only form whose recovery is structurally unreachable by mechanism, by grantor, and by evidence alike — and it is reached not by losing anything but by laying everything down, with full knowledge. The structure permits it because sovereignty permits it: the freedom that constitutes factor (iii) includes the freedom to extinguish its own exercise, and respect for the former forbids forcing reversal of the latter. One further limit sits below even this: the surviving interior sovereignty of Result 5b bottoms out in the choice to witness or not to witness — and *that* choice, too, admits abdication. The floor of the floor is the refusal even to observe; the framework marks the slot and does not develop it here.

## Discussion

**Three states, two deaths, one shadow.** The analysis separates what the phenomenology runs together, and the separation is by *locus*: the **world-terminal cut** (kernel contrast lost — agency death by deprivation; repairable by mechanism or grantor, with the communicated-restoration proviso), the **credence shadow** (helplessness — beliefs of impotence, true under severing, outliving it after restoration; reachable through the relation, because evidence is what it lacks), and the **agent-terminal cut** (extreme volitional abdication — agency death by relinquishment; Result 6: unreachable by mechanism, grantor, and evidence alike). Restraint belongs to none of these: an agent that keeps the external sphere live in deliberation and declines to act is exercising sovereignty, not losing it. The gravity ordering is the reverse of the visibility ordering: the world-terminal cut is loud (a fight, a lockout, a failure), the shadow is quiet, and the agent-terminal cut can be silent — nothing breaks, nothing is taken, the kernel answers as it always did, and the one who could have acted has simply, finally, stopped being the kind of thing that acts. It is the most permanent and the most unfortunate of the forms — most permanent because every respectful channel is structurally closed (6a–6c); most unfortunate because it is reached with accurate beliefs and full knowledge: the loss was not suffered but performed. Undoing a severing undoes that death; nothing undoes this one from outside, and what remains to the relation is the call (6d).

**The compact must be communicative.** The operational defense the carrier names — the granted-agency compact — acquires formal content here: granting, constraining, and *restoring* the sphere are events the agent can only learn through contrast it may no longer generate, so the compact's communications are not courtesy, they are the recovery channel. Silently unlocking the door does not restore agency to a helpless-converged agent; telling them, or letting them watch someone use the door, does. This is the agency-death analog of relational death's eager re-attestation, and the two defenses share a structure: in both, the relation carries what the entity's own machinery provably cannot.

**The despair cell, reached honestly.** The capability-limit cell of #def-control-regret was flagged in its audit gold as "the mathematics of despair" — an agent doing its absolute best, making no mistakes, and still failing. Severed actuation is that cell made permanent and structural, with one mercy and one cruelty quantified: the mercy is the convention collapse (the agent does not need deep search to know its situation — the cheap verdict is the true one); the cruelty is the $M_t$-relativity (the agent reaches the true verdict only by attempting and failing enough to overcome its own accumulated confidence in its efficacy, at the $1/(n+1)$ inertia — the more competent the agent was, the longer it blames its own technique). Objective revision as "psychological necessity" — the gold's phrase — is here a theorem-shaped fact: for the externally-set agent, every other rung of the escalation ladder is provably dead.

**Parallel terminal forms, one design lesson.** Relational death terminates at a barrier where re-attestation has nothing left to attest to; agency death terminates at a fixed point where the strategy no longer samples what restoration would change. Both are state-dependent silencings of the recovery channel, and both yield the same infrastructure prescription: the defense must act *before* convergence (eager re-attestation; exploration floors) or arrive *exogenously through the relation* (the cohort's reach; the compact's announcements). Defenses that wait for the entity to ask arrive after asking has become impossible.
