# Approximation Tiering (appendix discussion — robust-qualitative)

A recurring meta-pattern the framework uses for handling intractability: when a problem admits no tractable exact treatment in general, **introduce a tiered hierarchy of approximations with proved monotonicity between tiers and a diagnostic for ascending when needed**. Three such hierarchies exist in the framework. **The Correlation Hierarchy (L0/L1/L1'/L2)** for causal structure in the strategy DAG: independence baseline → augmented DAG with strict-prerequisite common causes → mixture form for soft facilitators → full joint correlation. **The Convention Hierarchy (C1/C2/C3)** for continuation policies in the value object: one-step improvement → receding-horizon → Bellman optimal. **The Tier 1/2/3 contraction taxonomy** in the composition closure framework: sub-agents with exact contraction → local contraction with bounded degradation → per-domain verification.

The framework names *four components* of a successful approximation tiering: (1) a baseline tier that is tractable and gives meaningful results under stated scope; (2) a monotonicity result between tiers — ascending the hierarchy gives strictly stronger guarantees; (3) a diagnostic that tells the agent or analyst *when* the current tier is binding (when ascending would change the conclusion); (4) explicit cost accounting for ascending (the higher tier is more expensive in computation, parameter count, or observability requirement).

The pattern provides *the framework's scope-honesty toolkit*: rather than over-claiming exact results across all regimes, the framework states each result at *one specific tier* and provides the diagnostic for when that tier is insufficient. The Correlation Hierarchy lets formal strategy-layer results be proved at L0 with explicit lift to L1; the Convention Hierarchy lets diagnostic statements be made at C1 with explicit lift to C3; the Tier taxonomy lets composition-level results be proved at Tier 1 with explicit lift to other tiers.

The framework's posture: **tiered approximation is what makes scope-honesty operational rather than just rhetorical**. A flat "this result holds under these conditions" leaves the user without a path forward if the conditions fail; a tiered approximation says "this result holds at this tier; here is the diagnostic for whether you need to ascend; here is what ascending costs and what it buys."
