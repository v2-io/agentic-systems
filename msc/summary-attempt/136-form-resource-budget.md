# Resource Budget (appendix formulation — exploratory off-spine)

An exploratory off-spine extension of the framework, addressing what AAT's core machinery is *resource-blind* about. The formulation introduces a **depletable scalar reservoir** — a *resource budget* — whose drain rate rises with mismatch and whose level *gates correction capacity*. This is the minimal structure that makes "a degrading model is more expensive to run" a *dynamical statement* rather than an informal observation.

The construction is minimal. A scalar resource budget evolves under a drain-versus-replenishment equation: the drain rate is a non-decreasing function of mismatch magnitude (degrading models cost more to run — the **A-cost** assumption); replenishment is a separate rate (zero in the hard-budget regime; positive in the regenerative regime). A *resource-gating* assumption (**A-gate**) couples the sector parameter to the resource level: when the budget is depleted, the sector parameter heads to zero (the agent loses corrective capacity).

The framework names this segment as *off-spine* and *exploratory*: no Part I, II, or III result depends on it. The formulation is *conditional by nature* — the two posits (A-cost and A-gate) are assertions about an agent's *physical realization*, not derived from the framework's core machinery. The formulation is included in the appendices as an extension addressing a *named open scope* (resource-constrained agents — embodied controllers under torque/battery/episode-length limits; teams under headcount limits; any agent whose corrective actions spend an exhaustible pool).

The companion segment (the resource-bounded destabilization derivation) uses this formulation to derive a *self-depletion* result: under hard-budget plus the gating assumption, an agent facing even a *constant* adversarial coupling deterministically depletes its resource budget and crosses the destabilization threshold in finite time. The result *closes the effects-spiral mechanism* in the adversarial-destabilization treatment by making the previously-unspecified mismatch-amplification leg unnecessary — the resource-depletion route gives certain finite-time destabilization without invoking the spiral's non-linear coupling-strength leg.

The framework is honest about the formulation's status: it is a *named off-spine direction* with two operationally important posits and one downstream result that uses it. Whether the resource-budget axis becomes a first-class scope condition of the framework, or remains as an exploratory extension addressing a specific open scope, is treated as open.
