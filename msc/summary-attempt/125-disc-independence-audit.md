# Independence Audit (appendix discussion — robust-qualitative)

A *meta-architectural* enumeration of the framework's *independence assumptions* — modeling moves that treat one quantity as independent of another to obtain tractable mathematics. The framework names *six load-bearing independence assumptions* across its results, each with its *failure regime*, its *diagnostic signal*, and the *repair operation* the framework provides.

The six. **(1) Channel independence in adaptive tempo**: observations across distinct channels are non-redundant — fails when channels carry overlapping information; diagnostic is mutual information between channels; repair is mutual-information-discounted aggregation. **(2) Edge independence in strategy DAGs**: sibling edge outcomes are conditionally independent given parents — fails under latent common causes (causal insufficiency); diagnostic is positive sibling-covariance under exploration; repair is L1 augmentation (add common-cause node). **(3) Directed separation between model and goal update**: epistemic update is goal-blind — fails in Class-3 Coupled architectures; diagnostic is the processing-coupling scalar; repair is class coercion via wrapping. **(4) Independent dimensions in per-dimension persistence**: correction capacities are independent across dimensions — fails under cross-dimensional correction (off-diagonal tempo tensor); diagnostic is mismatch concentration on the dominant eigendirection of the tempo tensor; repair is matrix-Loewner persistence. **(5) Source independence in communication tempo**: ally communications are non-redundant — fails when allies report correlated information; diagnostic is communication mutual information; repair is redundancy-discounted communication tempo. **(6) Independence of unity dimensions**: the content axes (epistemic, teleological, strategic, perceptual) are independent of the structural axis — fails when content unity and structural unity covary; diagnostic is the heterogeneous-Kalman counterexample; repair is the two-axis decomposition itself.

The framework's *posture*: the enumeration makes visible *what is not an independence assumption* — acyclicity of the strategy DAG (proved from temporal ordering), Cox-derived probability (necessary-and-sufficient under desiderata), the Lyapunov machinery in the sector-persistence template (no independence assumption beyond bounded disturbance). The framework's results *survive* failure of any given independence assumption *within the corresponding repair scope*; the assumption is *not eliminable* in general but is *not a hidden conjecture* either — each assumption is named, its failure regime characterized, and a repair operation provided.

The framework names this audit as a *scope-honesty load-bearing apparatus*. Without the audit, downstream segments would silently rely on independence assumptions that may fail without diagnostic signal; with it, each assumption is *explicit* and each failure has a *named repair*. This is the framework's posture toward independence assumptions: *not avoidance, but structured visibility*.
