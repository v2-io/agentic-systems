# Graph Structure Uniqueness (appendix derivation — conditional)

The framework's proof that operational requirements on the agent's representation are *sufficient* for the strategy to be a directed acyclic graph with the Markov factorization property. Four postulates are stated: **P1 — Directed temporal ordering** (causes precede effects); **P2 — Probabilistic uncertainty** (edges carry credence; updates are Bayesian); **P3 — Causal sufficiency** (all common causes of any two strategy nodes are themselves represented as nodes); **P4 — Observable intermediates** (required for localized strategic diagnosis but not for the representation or persistence results themselves).

The argument proceeds in steps. **Acyclicity** is proved directly from temporal ordering: a finite set with a strict partial order over time is representable as a DAG (standard order theory). The apparent cycles of iteration are acyclic when time-indexed — each attempt is a distinct node at a distinct time. **Markov factorization** is proved under causal sufficiency via the *Causal Markov Condition theorem* (Spirtes-Glymour-Scheines 2000; Pearl 2009): causal sufficiency guarantees independent exogenous noise, which guarantees the Markov factorization.

The framework draws a careful parallel to Cox's theorem in *form* but not yet in *strength*. Cox's theorem is *necessary-and-sufficient*: the only measure satisfying the desiderata is probability. This result is *sufficient only*: the desiderata guarantee DAG+Markov, but it has not been shown that *no non-DAG structure* (factor graphs, junction trees with cyclic message schedules, chain graphs) could satisfy P1-P4 plus causal sufficiency. **The necessity direction is open**. The framework is honest about this: claims that the strategy *must* be a DAG should be read as "must-if-sufficient-via-this-route," not as a proved necessity. The placement of the DAG structure on a footing *comparable to probability* — a consequence of operational requirements rather than a modeling convenience — holds in the *sufficiency direction*. The full parallel with Cox awaits a uniqueness argument.

What this buys the framework: the strategy DAG is theorem-backed in its *graphical structure* (sufficient under postulates plus causal sufficiency); the *parameterization within* the structure (AND/OR combination with single-parameter edges) remains a formulation choice, treated as a *convergent representational choice* (every investigated alternative was rejected for documented reasons). This is what makes the framework's claim that downstream results "inherit the strength of Pearl-Bareinboim causal machinery" non-trivial: the inheritance flows through the Markov-factorization-as-theorem step.
