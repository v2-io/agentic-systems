# Survival-Imperative Exploration Drive (appendix derivation — conditional)

A striking structural derivation. The framework's unified policy objective combines pragmatic value with an epistemic exploration term weighted *proportionally to model uncertainty* — the intuitive "explore when uncertain" reading. **But the underlying Lyapunov dynamics force a second, structurally distinct exploration drive — the *Survival Imperative***.

The derivation: from the persistence condition's requirement that the agent's correction rate exceed the disturbance rate divided by tolerance, the *maximum tolerable observation-noise* scales *proportionally to model uncertainty*. As the agent becomes confident (model uncertainty heads to zero), the tolerable noise tightens, and *the agent is forced to actively seek pristine, low-noise observations to penetrate its own stubbornness*. The Lyapunov persistence constraint dictates an *immense shadow price* on exploration, with the weighting going to infinity as model uncertainty heads to zero in a drifting environment. **An agent with high confidence in a drifting world mathematically guarantees its own death by ignoring noisy observations**.

The framework therefore carries **two parallel exploration drives at opposite ends of the uncertainty spectrum**: the standard *epistemic drive* proportional to model uncertainty (explore when uncertain — dominant when model uncertainty is high), and the **survival drive** proportional to *inverse* model uncertainty (explore when confident in a drifting world, because the agent's own update gain has dropped below the rate at which reality is shifting — dominant when model uncertainty is low).

The result *bypasses the dark-room critique of active inference* entirely. The active-inference framework's expected-free-energy objective combines pragmatic value with epistemic value, and an EFE agent with strong priors can stop exploring (the dark-room critique of Sun-Firestone 2020). **An AAT agent in a drifting world physically cannot stop exploring**, because exploration is *forced by the same Lyapunov machinery* that grounds the rest of Part II. The bypass is *structural rather than parametric*: AAT's survival imperative is not a tunable knob but a *constraint* from the persistence condition.

The framework's prescription: in any drifting environment, *confident agents are not safe agents*. The agent's confidence in its current model is exactly when its correction-rate margin shrinks below the disturbance rate; *the framework predicts that confident agents in drifting environments must engineer exploration into their loop*, regardless of how well their current model is working. This is the formal grounding for *productive paranoia* and similar empirical heuristics about not resting on past success.
