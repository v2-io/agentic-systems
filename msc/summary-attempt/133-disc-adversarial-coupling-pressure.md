# Adversarial Coupling Pressure (appendix discussion — discussion-grade)

A consequential structural observation: **adversaries do not merely deceive through the observation channel; they apply strategic pressure that drives a target *across architectural classes* — from Class 1 (Separated) toward Class 3 (Coupled)**. *Coupling is itself an attack surface*: a Class-1 agent can be deceived only through the observation channel, while a coupled agent exposes additional input ports through its objective and strategy substates.

The framework identifies *three operational mechanisms* recurring across propaganda, social engineering, marketing, and influence operations. **(1) Identity-binding pressure**: attaching the target's purposeful state to a representation that makes belief and identity costly to revise separately (e.g., political identity, tribal loyalty, religious commitment). **(2) Affect manipulation**: using emotional triggers to short-circuit the target's belief-update process, biasing belief in goal-conditioned directions. **(3) Sunk-cost amplification**: making the target's prior commitments to a position increase the perceived cost of revising belief away from it (e.g., investment in social standing, professional reputation, sunk effort).

All three mechanisms drive the target's processing-coupling scalar *upward* — moving the target's effective architectural class from Class 1 toward Class 3 (Coupled). The diagnostic capture is *directional*: the attacker's optimal strategy is not just to inject false information but to drive the target's *capacity to update on truth* downward via coupling pressure. Once the target's coupling has been driven up, even *correct* information arriving through the observation channel is processed goal-blindly *less effectively* — the bias bound widens proportionally.

The framework names the structural consequence: **orient-cascade inversion**. When the target has been driven to high coupling under pre-loaded objectives, the orient cascade's information-dependency-forced ordering inverts in practice: objective-defending updates *precede* model updates rather than following them; the satisfaction-gap signal triggers strategy revision toward defending the pre-loaded objective rather than toward revising it. The cascade ordering still holds *structurally* but the practical update sequence has been corrupted by the coupling pressure.

The framework's prescription: **defensive scaffolding as composite-agent restoration**. The defender's countermeasure is to *re-impose Class-1 structure via external scaffolding* — peer review, prediction registers, structured deliberation protocols, separation of belief-update from objective-defense at the institutional level. This is the framework's organizational analog of the wrapping construction: the *target as composite* (target plus scaffolding) recovers the directed-separation property the target alone has lost under coupling pressure.

The result *populates the use-case for the coupled-formulation* beyond architecturally-coupled agents: **any agent under sustained adversarial pressure regardless of native architecture** falls in the same scope. The Class-3 bias-bound machinery applies; the wrapping construction applies as remedy; the framework's reach extends from native-Class-3 substrates (LLMs) to pressure-driven Class-3 states (humans, organizations under sustained adversarial pressure).

The framework names this as *one leg of the modularity-state-dynamics three-operation pattern*: **adversarial coupling pressure** is the *externally-driven decreasing-modularity* operation, alongside *self-driven decreasing* (strategic self-coupling) and *self-driven increasing* (truthification via class-coercion or defensive scaffolding).
