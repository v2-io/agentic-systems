# Class Coercion in Composition (derived — conditional)

The companion to the wrapping construction, establishing that the wrapped system is a *valid AAT composite agent*. Under the wrapper construction of the prior segment plus three additional design constraints on the wrapper itself — that the wrapper commits to a prediction map so macro-mismatch is well-defined, that the wrapper's belief-update supports a gain interpretation, and that the belief-update satisfies the sector condition — the wrapper satisfies the (A1)-(A4) admissibility conditions of the composition-closure framework and therefore qualifies as an AAT composite agent.

The wrapper *inherits* the sector-persistence template at the wrapper level. The effective disturbance at the wrapper level has *two* contributions: external environmental disturbance acting through the wrapper's observation channel, and internal disturbance from the component's response variance, bounded by the variance of the component's responses to goal-blind queries. Persistence at the wrapper level requires the wrapper's correction rate to exceed the sum.

The framework names the **tempo cost** as a *Brooks's-Law instance*. The wrapper makes at least two component calls per macro-step (one goal-blind for model update, one goal-conditioned for purposeful-state update), so the wrapper's macro-event rate is at most half the component's underlying rate. *More component calls per macro-step means lower macro-tempo*, exactly as the tempo-composition inequality predicts. The trade-off is *between architectural correctness and adaptive throughput*: a wrapped system gets directed separation (Class 1 status, the full Part II machinery applies), but pays for it in tempo. For LLM-agent practitioners, this means the cost of moving from a one-call-per-step LLM agent to a Class-1-by-structure agent system is a halving of macro-tempo at minimum (more in richer wrapper designs) — and this halving is the *structural price of admission* to AAT's persistence guarantees, not a tax to be minimized away.

The framework is also explicit about *what the wrapping construction does not buy*. The component's *internal variance* still acts as a disturbance on the wrapper level — the wrapper does not magically eliminate the component's non-determinism. What it eliminates is the *belief-goal coupling* that the directed-separation classification identifies as the load-bearing failure mode for Class 3 substrates. The wrapper turns Class 3 components into Class 1 systems at the cost of speed and an irreducible internal disturbance term.
