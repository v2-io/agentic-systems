# Fisher-Whitened Update Rule (appendix derivation — conditional)

A *constructive companion* to the L1' update-bias result. Under L1' / L2 correlated-evidence regimes, the framework derives that the default log-odds edge update *retains correct direction* — the angle between the log-odds gradient and the natural gradient never exceeds 45° at any finite correlation strength, so directional fidelity is *never actively violated*. But the *magnitude alignment degrades* by a factor proportional to the square root of (1 minus squared correlation). The **Fisher-whitened correction** restores sharp directional fidelity in the Fisher-weighted inner product.

Under the parameterization-invariance commitment and Čencov's uniqueness theorem (the framework's fourth additive-coordinate-forcing instance), the Fisher-whitened correction is **AAT-internally derivable rather than externally imported**. The result adds **sub-scope α₃** (correlated evidence plus Fisher-whitened update plus Bayesian coherence → sector condition derived) to the persistence-template sub-scope partition. The construction composes cleanly with the meta-gain machinery from the adaptive-gain dynamics derivation: *Fisher whitening is a special case of meta-gain with the gain operator being the inverse Fisher information matrix*.

The framework's posture: this is a *natural extension* of the gain-sector bridge to correlated-evidence regimes, available *AAT-internally* under the parameterization-invariance commitment. Without that commitment, the Fisher-whitened correction is an externally imported choice; with it, the same construction is *forced* by Čencov's uniqueness theorem operating on the (PI) axiom. The framework's overall coordinate-forcing posture extends naturally to this case: the *Fisher metric* is the canonical metric forced by parameterization-invariance, and *Fisher whitening* is the canonical correction that respects it.

The result *closes the magnitude degradation* under correlated evidence at the cost of one additional structural commitment (the Fisher metric for whitening). Under the original log-odds coordinate, magnitude degrades by the square-root-of-one-minus-squared-correlation factor; under Fisher whitening, magnitude alignment is restored. The framework's prescription: where correlated evidence is the dominant regime, Fisher whitening recovers sharp persistence guarantees at the cost of meta-gain machinery; where correlated evidence is mild or rare, the default log-odds update with degraded magnitude alignment suffices.
