# Edge-Credence Dynamics — Sector Condition Verification (appendix derivation — conditional)

Complete verification that specific strategy-edge update dynamics satisfy the sector condition, backing the strategy-persistence schema. Seven propositions are proved across distinct topologies. **B.1 (single edge)**: sector condition holds globally with sector parameter $1/(n+1)$. **B.2 (two-edge AND chain, observable intermediate)**: weakest-link result with sector parameter equal to the minimum of $1/(n_1+1)$ and $\theta_1/(n_2+1)$ — the *evidence-starvation* effect on the downstream edge attenuates by the upstream success probability. **B.3 (two-edge AND chain, unobservable intermediate)**: per-edge sector condition *fails* (marginal point estimate biased), but plan-level tracking recovers persistence with sector parameter $1/(n_\Phi+1)$ at the cost of per-edge diagnostics. **B.4 (two-arm OR, epsilon-greedy)**: *exploration-gated* weakest-link — pure greedy *fails* the sector condition; minimum exploration rate is required (the sufficient-exploration condition from the strategy-persistence schema).

**B.5 (sector transfer to plan-confidence error)**: the sector condition transfers from per-edge credence space to *strategy-plan-confidence error* via the Jacobian — and this transfer is *credit-assignment-free* (requires only status propagation). For linear correction the transfer is exact; for nonlinear correction the sector parameter degrades by a Jacobian-conditioning factor. **B.6 (L1 augmented DAG, strict-prerequisite common cause)**: the formal extension to causally-insufficient DAGs handled by augmentation. Persistence transfers with three-way gating: condition-testing rate, evidence-starvation factor, and exploration-gating factor. **B.7 (L1' mixture form, soft-facilitator common cause)**: extension to soft facilitators. Persistence holds under *observable* common cause via five-way gating; *refuted* under unobservable common cause by the Cramér-Rao floor (the framework's second identifiability-floor instance).

The framework's overall result: **strategic persistence has a complete verified instance set for the canonical topologies**. The strategy-persistence schema is no longer a sketch; specific topologies have been instantiated and the sector condition verified case-by-case. The strategic-tempo formula is consistent across cases. AND-chains exhibit depth-gated (geometric) attenuation; OR-nodes exhibit exploration-gated allocation; L1-augmented DAGs combine condition-testing, evidence-starvation, and exploration-gating. The chapter's claim that "strategic persistence is structurally identical to epistemic persistence at the sector-template level" is *verified* by these instantiations.
