# Internal-External Decomposition of Agent Viability (appendix derivation — conditional)

The framework establishes that *agent viability decomposes additively* into a reducible agent-internal term and an irreducible environmental floor, inherited from the exact mismatch decomposition. The decomposition is **additive, not multiplicative**: the framework proves a *no-go* against multiplicative factorization of the form "environment-rate × model-error-factor × policy-factor."

The construction: log-viability is the log-margin between steady-state mismatch and the critical task boundary. From the exact mismatch decomposition (reducible model error plus irreducible observation noise), log-viability decomposes into two additive components — an agent-internal term (reducible by better modeling) and an environmental floor (irreducible). The agent can move the internal term; the environmental floor it cannot.

The framework's constitutive no-go: *multiplicative ρ-factorization is type-incorrect*. The naive form "$\rho = \rho_\text{external} \cdot f(\text{model-class}) \cdot g(\text{policy})$" cannot generally hold because the environmental disturbance is *additive* in the mismatch decomposition, not multiplicative. The framework names this carefully — it is one of the recurring AAT internal-discipline points, and it has appeared elsewhere as a source of derivation-pitfall in segment authoring.

The framework leaves a *finer decomposition* (separating model-state, policy, and cross-term contributions to the internal component) as conditional and reserved for future work. What is *established* exactly is the *additive structure with the environmental floor*, derived from the mismatch decomposition. What is *not* established is a clean operational decomposition of the internal component into its mechanistic sub-pieces.

The result is what gives the framework's *bandwidth-cost-of-persistence* analysis its structural grounding. Persistence has a cost; that cost is paid in information rate; the cost is composed of *contributions the agent can influence* (the internal component, via correction architecture and gain) plus *contributions the environment imposes* (the noise floor, which information-rate bandwidth must cover regardless of agent design). The two are *separately accountable*, and the framework's prescription for design under bandwidth constraints handles them differently.
