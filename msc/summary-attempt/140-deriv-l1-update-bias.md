# L1' Update Bias (appendix derivation — conditional)

The *quantitative companion* to the identifiability-floor Instance 2 result. Under L1' correlated-evidence regimes with *unobservable* common cause, the default log-odds edge update converges to a **biased fixed point** — edges settle at log-odds values that match the L1' root-probability rather than the marginal-edge truth.

The derivation: in the two-edge OR-root case with a latent common cause acting on both edges, the framework derives a closed-form bias formula in the log-odds coordinate and verifies it via Monte Carlo simulation. The bias is *Lipschitz in the correlation strength* between the edges and *Cramér-Rao-floored under forgetting* (the framework's lower bound on inferable parameter variance from finite-information data, applied to this case).

The result *composes* with two other framework segments to produce a **dual forgetting-rate requirement** on strategic persistence. The strategy-persistence schema established that forgetting at a minimum rate is required for asymptotic persistence (the *plasticity lower bound*). The L1' update-bias result establishes that *forgetting at a maximum rate is required to bound the L1' bias* (the *bias-limiting upper bound*). The composite forgetting-rate requirement: forget fast enough to track non-stationarity (lower bound) *and* slow enough to integrate the bias-corrected estimate (upper bound). Between these is the *feasibility window* for forgetting.

The framework's posture: this is **the quantitative content of identifiability-floor Instance 2** — the same no-go result that establishes Instance 2 structurally (the per-conditional decomposition is Cramér-Rao-floored) also has a *bias-magnitude characterization*. Where Instance 2 says identification is *structurally impossible* from single-channel observation, this segment says the *magnitude drift is bounded* and gives the closed-form bound. The two together complete the framework's treatment of L1' under unobservable common cause.

A subtler composition: the dual forgetting-rate requirement intersects with the *consolidation-stability-plasticity window* from the consolidation-dynamics treatment. Three forgetting-rate constraints now compose: plasticity lower bound (track non-stationarity); stability upper bound from consolidation cadence (let consolidation integrate cross-episode patterns); bias-limiting upper bound from L1' (bound the L1' bias). When any combination of these constraints leaves an *empty window*, the framework predicts a specific failure mode — and the practitioner's task is to diagnose *which constraint is binding*.
