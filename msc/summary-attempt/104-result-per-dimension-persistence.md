# Per-Dimension Persistence (result — conditional)

The persistence condition lifted to multi-dimensional mismatch. **The scalar persistence condition overestimates adaptive capacity when the agent's correction gain varies across dimensions** — and *the weak dimension is the bottleneck*. It dominates the aggregate mismatch regardless of performance on strong dimensions.

The framework derives the per-dimension condition from the same Lyapunov argument applied dimension-by-dimension. Under bounded deterministic disturbance, persistence requires the per-dimension tempo to exceed the per-dimension disturbance rate divided by the per-dimension critical-mismatch tolerance, *for each dimension*. Under stochastic disturbance, the per-dimension stationary distribution gives three task-adequacy criteria (variance bound, quantile bound, exit probability bound) related by exact constants for Gaussian mismatch. The exact thresholds differ by Model (D vs S) and by which criterion is used, but the structural form — *the weak dimension limits aggregate persistence* — is robust across all variants.

Simulation confirms the scalar form *significantly overestimates* effective persistence margin in anisotropic systems. In a 3D simulation with gain varying 5:1 across dimensions, the scalar condition predicted 72% more margin than the actual per-dimension bound revealed. The weak dimension accounted for 84% of total mismatch. The matrix-Loewner persistence formulation (in the appendix) is the *strictly sharper canonical form* under cross-dimensional correction — and is *unsafe to skip* in regimes where the tempo tensor's eigenbasis misaligns with the coordinate axes the agent thinks in.

This result is *load-bearing for adversarial dynamics*. An attacker who identifies the target's weak direction can concentrate disturbance along that axis — the same total disturbance budget concentrated on the weak axis amplifies the mismatch ratio asymmetrically while *aggregate metrics still look acceptable*. Per-dimension monitoring is therefore not optimization polish — it is a *structural requirement* for any agent operating under directed pressure. The full adversarial-targeting machinery (the 16-cell emitter-recipient composition with closed-form arg-max) operates on this per-dimension structure, exploiting the asymmetry the scalar persistence condition hides.
