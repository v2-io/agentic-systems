# Linear ODE Approximation (appendix detail — exact)

The full pedagogical linear treatment of mismatch dynamics. Scalar and vector forms; steady-state solutions under both disturbance models (deterministic giving the inverse-tempo scaling; stochastic giving the inverse-root-tempo scaling that produces the distinctive Model D vs Model S split); transient behavior with exponential convergence from initial conditions toward steady state at the tempo rate; validity conditions for the linear approximation; breakdown modes outside the linear regime; the discrete-time connection via the fluid limit; and the adversarial-coupling case.

The detail serves as the *worked example* underlying the framework's hypothesis that mismatch dynamics follow a first-order linear ODE in the simplest case. Everything in this segment is *exactly* derivable from the linear assumption; the segment's purpose is to show, in detail, that the linear case gives clean closed-form results and to *delineate exactly where the linear approximation is valid and where it fails*. The validity range: bounded mismatch within the sector region; non-saturating correction; sufficiently fast event rate relative to the mismatch timescale. The breakdown modes: saturation at large mismatch (correction slower than linear at large errors); threshold effects (small mismatches uncorrected — a dead zone); structural breakdown beyond a critical mismatch where the correction collapses entirely because the model class is no longer appropriate.

The role of the segment in the framework's exposition is to give the reader the *cleanest possible* derivation of every chapter-level result that the linear case admits, so that the nonlinear sector-condition machinery developed elsewhere is read against this clean baseline. The chapter's central inequality (mismatch ratio scales as squared tempo ratio under adversarial coupling) is *first introduced* via this linear analysis; the appendix shows exactly which assumptions the simple form rests on and where the cleaner statement transitions into the sector-condition machinery as the assumptions relax.
