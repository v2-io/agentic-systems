# Update Detection Latency (appendix derivation — conditional)

A *structural lower bound* on regime-change detection latency. For a Beta-Bernoulli strategy-edge agent without forgetting, **the expected number of cycles required to detect a within-class regime change of observable footprint $\varepsilon$ scales linearly with accumulated experience**: $\Omega((n_{\min}+1)/\varepsilon)$ with $n_{\min}$ the minimum accumulated experience on load-bearing edges.

The framework's structural reading: this is *not a tunable rate*. The $1/(n+1)$ accumulation rate in the log-odds coordinate is **structurally forced** — it is the log-odds update magnitude per cycle under the Aczél-Cauchy uniqueness theorem (the third additive-coordinate-forcing instance from the prior segment), and no choice of coordinate escapes it. *No reparameterization escapes the rate without abandoning evidential additivity*, which would invalidate the update rule on AAT-internal grounds.

The result *sharpens the forgetting prerequisite* from the strategy-persistence schema. The prior statement was "forgetting required for asymptotic persistence." The detection-latency derivation sharpens this to **"forgetting required for detection-latency bounded independently of operating point"**: long-lived agents face detection latency that grows linearly with accumulated experience unless forgetting bounds the effective sample size. The framework names this **stability-induced myopia**: successful agents that have accumulated experience on their working plans *systematically take longer to notice regime changes than the same agents earlier in their lives*, with a *precise rate of slowdown*.

The cross-domain transfer is clear and concrete: successful organizations with long institutional memory take longer to detect regime changes than younger organizations; high-confidence ML models take longer to revise on encountering distribution drift than low-confidence ones; expert humans in stable domains take longer to update their craft heuristics after underlying-process changes than novices. All instantiate the same structural pattern: accumulated experience times slow-down rate. The framework's prescription: *short-circuit accumulated experience on critical edges* (forced forgetting) to maintain detection latency that does not grow without bound.

The result also composes with the adversarial-targeting machinery to produce the **OODA self-bound**: the most damaging cell of the 16-cell adversarial-targeting matrix is the one with maximal opacity *and minimal regime footprint given the defender's accumulated experience*. Adversarial targeting therefore concentrates *where the defender's response is slowest*, predicting the empirical patterns that *cybersecurity incidents cluster on long-trusted dependencies* and *adversarial-ML examples cluster on high-confidence model regions*.
