# Log-Odds as Unique Additive-Evidence Coordinate (appendix derivation — conditional)

The framework's *third instance* of the additive-coordinate-forcing meta-pattern (after the chain-confidence-decay layer and reverse-KL at the divergence layer). The **log-odds coordinate is the unique parameterization (up to positive affine transformation) on which independent Bernoulli evidence updates edge credences additively** — forced by an *evidential-additivity axiom* via Aczél's Cauchy functional equation uniqueness theorem.

The construction: posit that posterior updates from independent observations should compose *additively* in some smooth strictly-monotone reparameterization of the credence. This is the *evidential-additivity axiom*, motivated as the update-level analog of the chain-confidence-decay layer's log-additive identity. The standard Cauchy-functional-equation argument (Aczél 1966) then forces the reparameterization to be the *log-odds* function, up to positive affine transformation.

The framework lifts this to a *first-class coordinate commitment* at the update layer. Continuous-gradient edge-update machinery in the framework's credit-assignment treatment operates *natively in log-odds* and projects back to probability space via the sigmoid at the readout interface. The reason is mechanical: in probability space, the same update written as residual times gain can push the credence outside the unit interval when the Jacobian norm is small (a *mechanical break of the [0,1] probability domain*). The log-odds coordinate has domain over all of ℝ, so additive updates never escape the domain; the sigmoid projection guarantees boundedness by construction, not by clipping.

The framework explicitly compositions this with the additive-coordinate-forcing meta-pattern. Three layers now have their forced coordinates: log-probability at the chain layer (chain rule of probability); reverse-KL at the divergence layer (chain-rule additivity axiom via Hobson-Csiszár-Shore-Johnson); log-odds at the update layer (evidential-additivity axiom via Aczél). The Fisher information metric at the metric layer (Čencov's uniqueness theorem under parameterization-invariance) rounds out the four-layer structure. All four live on the *same geometric object* — the exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel duality. *AAT's coordinates are not chosen by convention; they are forced by AAT-internal axioms via classical uniqueness theorems, and they all live on the same geometric object.*

A *downstream load-bearing consequence*: the log-odds coordinate is what makes the detection-latency floor a structural rather than parametric result. The expected cycles to detect a regime change of given footprint scales linearly with accumulated experience precisely because the log-odds accumulation rate is *forced* — no reparameterization escapes the rate without abandoning evidential additivity, which would invalidate the update rule on AAT-internal grounds.
