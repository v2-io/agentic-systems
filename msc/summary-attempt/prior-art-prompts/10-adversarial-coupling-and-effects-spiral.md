## The Core Idea / Claim
Cooperation and adversariality are the same coupling dynamics with opposite signs. In adversarial coupling, a "tempo advantage" (faster loop speed) yields superlinear advantages: mismatch errors scale quadratically (exponent 2) against deterministic drift, and to the 3/2 power under stochastic noise. When pushed past adaptive reserve, an agent enters an "Effects Spiral" (mathematics of panic): degraded corrections make the agent more predictable (opaque), increasing adversarial coupling and driving it further into failure. Adversarial attacks classify into regimes: informative, magnitude shock (needs bandwidth), structural shock (needs new model), and ambient erosion (cognitive DDoS).
