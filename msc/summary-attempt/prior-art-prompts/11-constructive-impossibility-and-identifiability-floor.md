## The Core Idea / Claim
The framework recurrently uses external information-theoretic no-go theorems as *constructive scope-honesty apparatus*. Each instance follows the same pattern: (1) name an inferential task under a specific information regime; (2) invoke an external no-go to prove the task is impossible from the available information; (3) name the *unique broadly-available escape* via specific machinery the framework already provides. The posture is that impossibility, treated this way, *elevates* the load-bearing role of the machinery that supplies the escape rather than discouraging inquiry. Four formal instances span the framework: (a) on-policy L0-insufficiency detection (Bareinboim causal hierarchy theorem; escape via interventional access through the agent's own action loop); (b) per-conditional decomposition under unobservable common cause (Cramér-Rao floor; escape via instrument variable or multi-child joint observation); (c) composite contraction certification from component marginals (Liberzon common-Lyapunov-nonexistence; escape via observer-on-sub-agent intervention); (d) universal information-to-distance constant (heteroscedastic-Gaussian counterexample under Euclidean parameter norm; escape via the parameterization-invariance commitment). The Pearl-style intervention recurs as escape mechanism across all four, in different deployment modes.

## Boundaries of the Claim
- Domain: methodology of formal mathematical frameworks, causal inference, control theory, AI safety, information geometry.
- Focus: the *meta-methodological posture* toward impossibility results across multiple instances within a single framework, not the individual theorems.

## What Kind of Match Counts
- Methodological treatments of no-go theorems as *constructive* rather than discouraging.
- Explicit unification of multiple impossibility results across a single framework, each paired with named escape routes via the framework's own machinery.
- The pairing pattern: "the impossibility forbids X under regime R; the framework's machinery Y uniquely supplies the escape; Y is therefore load-bearing rather than incidental."
- Cross-disciplinary work that explicitly recognizes no-go theorems as scope-honesty apparatus.
- The Pearl-blanket vs Friston-blanket distinction extended to multi-instance scope-honesty.

## What Would NOT Count
- Standard citations of Pearl, Bareinboim, Cramér-Rao individually without the unification posture.
- Negative-results papers that treat impossibility as definitive without offering structural escapes.
- Philosophy-of-science papers about negative results without specific mathematical structure.
