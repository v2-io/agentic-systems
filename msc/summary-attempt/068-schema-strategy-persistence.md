# Strategy Persistence Schema (proposed-schema — sketch with verified instances)

The strategic instantiation of the sector-persistence template. If strategic update dynamics satisfy the template's preconditions for a strategic mismatch state — zero correction at zero strategic mismatch, a local sector condition on strategic correction, sufficient exploration (for OR-nodes), and bounded strategic disturbance — then the strategy persists exactly when the strategic sector parameter exceeds the strategic disturbance rate divided by the strategic reserve. The structural identity with the epistemic persistence condition from Part I is *not* an analogy; it is a *mathematical identity* at the sector-framework level. The strategic analog of "the environment changes faster than the agent can learn" is "the world invalidates plans faster than the agent can revise them" — and both produce the same catastrophic outcome.

The framework's most striking finding in this segment is a **forgetting prerequisite** that breaks the structural identity unless explicit memory-decay is imposed. The sector parameter for Beta-Bernoulli edge updates is $1/(n+1)$, which decays monotonically as experience accumulates. *Without forgetting, the strategic sector parameter heads to zero for every edge asymptotically* — and the persistence threshold is eventually violated for any positive disturbance rate, regardless of how comfortably the inequality holds at any given moment. With infinite memory, standard Bayesian updating guarantees eventual strategic failure.

The remedy is exponential forgetting: shrink the Beta pseudo-counts by a factor each step, stabilizing the effective sample size and producing an exact steady-state sector parameter of $(1-\lambda)/(2-\lambda)$. The forgetting prerequisite is then $(1-\lambda)/(2-\lambda) > \rho_\Sigma/R_\Sigma$. **The threshold has a hard ceiling**: when strategic disturbance reaches half the strategic reserve, *no* forgetting rate satisfies the prerequisite — the schema's trajectory guarantee fails regardless of forgetting design. This is structural, not heuristic.

The framework casts this as translating an organizational platitude into a quantitative survival inequality. The forgetting rate is the *strategic analog of adaptive tempo*: faster forgetting means faster tracking but noisier estimates; slower forgetting means stable estimates but slower tracking. The optimal forgetting rate balances bias and variance for the specific strategic disturbance the environment presents. The same dynamic underlies *institutional rigidity, RL value-function staleness, scientific-paradigm lock-in, and the loss-of-edge phenomenon in incumbent firms* — all instantiate the same structural pattern. The design choice is not whether to forget but at what rate. The framework also notes that the schema sharpens into a **structural-class theorem**: gain-decay update mechanisms (count-accumulating Bayesian without forgetting; observation-aggregating without restart; Robbins-Monro vanishing-step-size gradient methods) are *structurally disqualified regardless of tuning*. Finite-gain mechanisms (constant-step stochastic approximation, sliding windows, bounded memory, block restart) face *bidirectional* thresholds — both the forgetting prerequisite (lower bound on plasticity) and an upper bound from noise blow-up.

Five cases have been verified in detail: single-edge Beta-Bernoulli (sector condition globally satisfied); two-edge AND chain with observable intermediate (weakest-link result with evidence-starvation in the downstream edge); two-edge AND chain with unobservable intermediate (per-edge sector condition *fails*, but plan-level tracking recovers persistence at the cost of per-edge diagnostics); two-arm OR with epsilon-greedy exploration (sector condition holds with exploration-gated weakest-link; pure greedy *fails*); and mixed AND/OR with explicit common-cause node (the L1 augmented case). Persistence transfers from per-edge credence error to the *strategy-plan-confidence error* (the scalar gap between the plan-confidence score and the independence-model plan value) without requiring credit assignment. Persistence of the per-edge calibration residual (which would require credit assignment) remains open.
