## 2. What the re-checks say about the theory

These are in rough order of consequence. Each derivation is mine and has not been independently checked.

**2.1 `#obs-context-turnover`: the sufficiency-drop bound is false as stated** (C.1; finding F5; item `todo-loga-3-…-b462`). Lines 52 to 54 assert $\Delta S_{\text{turnover}} \geq 1 - I(M_{\tau_k^-}; M_{\text{rec}})/H(M_{\tau_k^-})$. `#def-model-sufficiency` normalizes by predictive information about future observations, but this bound normalizes by the state's entropy. Counterexample: $M_{\tau_k^-} = (X, Z)$, where the future depends only on $X$ and $Z$ is independent noise, and the agent externalizes $X$ only. Then $\Delta S = 0$, but the right side is $H(Z)/(H(X)+H(Z)) \gt 0$. The repair is an identity rather than a bound: when the reconstruction adds nothing, the drop is the normalized predictive information the externalize-and-reconstruct channel loses. The segment is typed `empirical`, which hides that it carries a formal claim. Today's A14 edit did not reach this passage.

**2.2 `#der-tempo-composition`: the closure-defect double count is live, and the segment states two persistence conditions** (C.1; finding F4). $\mathcal T_c$ is defined net of reconciliation ($C_{\text{coord}} := \mathcal T_{\text{joint}} - \mathcal T_c$, line 39).
