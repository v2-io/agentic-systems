## 2. What the re-checks say about the theory

In order of consequence. Where a claim is my derivation and not independently checked, I say so. The findings and checks are recorded in `reviews/items-recheck-t1.md` and taken into vsect (ee04fe32).

**2.1 The bias bound's $\kappa$ factor is asserted under a *[Derived]* tag, and the typology's one "earned" fact rests on it** (C.1; F4, F5; item TODO.md:161). `#deriv-observation-ambiguity-bias-bound` boxes both tracks as $\ldots \cdot \kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G;\Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$. The step that introduces $\kappa$ (§2 Step 4, §3 Step 3) is a sentence, and Track 2's own Step 2 ends at $2I$ with no $\kappa$ in it. $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ is defined in `#der-directed-separation` as a normalized information on the *output* $M_{\tau^+}$, not as a reweighting strength, so nothing licenses multiplying it onto an information about $\Omega$. One Working Note calls this "the landed $\varepsilon(\kappa)$ bound"; another, in the same segment, says the Class 2 case is open. The second is right. This matters beyond the segment: `#der-directed-separation`:113 says the typology's "one *earned* directional fact" (less coupling tracks reality strictly better) is "carried by the bound". A route that does follow from $\kappa$'s definition: $\mathbb E_G\,\mathrm{KL} = I(G;M_{\tau^+}\mid\cdot) = \kappa\,H(G\mid\cdot)$, hence $\mathbb E\,d_{FR}^2 \le 2\kappa H(G\mid\cdot)(1+o(1))$ for every class. It is $\kappa$-linear but against $H(G)$ rather than $I(G;\Omega)$. That is my derivation, and it treats $M_{\tau^+}$ as random given $G$, which the segment's posterior-as-point model may not. The item's frame ("derive the Class 2 bound") was wrong in a way that hides the problem: the bound is already stated, unearned.

**2.2 The causal-IB LMI's "if and only if" is false whenever the dynamics mix directions** (C.1; C2; item TODO.md:171). `#deriv-causal-ib-lmi` says survival ($\lambda_{\max}(\Sigma_\delta) \lt R^2$) holds iff $\mathbb E_\pi[\mathcal I_o(a)] \succeq \mathcal I_{\min}$ with $\mathcal I_{\min}$ symmetric positive-definite. A two-line Riccati iteration refutes necessity. With $A$ a $90^\circ$ rotation, $Q = 0.01 I$ and information in one direction only ($J = \mathrm{diag}(j,0)$), the steady state has $\lambda_{\max} = 0.046$ at $j = 10$, yet nothing positive-definite sits below a rank-one matrix. With $A = I$, the same $J$ fails, which is the case the segment's intuition pictures. The feasible information set is upward-closed with a frontier of minimal elements, not a single minimum. So "closed-form $\mathcal I_{\min}$ via DARE" presumes something that exists only when $A$, $Q_\rho$ and the observation directions align, and the blank-wall resolution needs that separability stated as a hypothesis. A consequence worth more than the repair: under mixing dynamics, information in a non-drifting direction *does* help survival, because the dynamics carry it into the drifting directions. The planned 2D test should include a mixing $A$, since that is the case that breaks the claim. The script is small and in my scratchpad; the recheck recipe in C2 reproduces it.

**2.3 `#form-composition-closure` carries three defects beyond the tracker's** (C.1; C1, F1, F2; items TODO.md:152, 154, 49). First, the "first genuine $\varepsilon^\ast \gt 0$" paragraph says the Beta-Bernoulli per-step strategy defect "grows without bound". It is bounded by $\max(1/2, \eta_c)$ and tends to $\eta_c\,\mathbb E\lvert\mathbb 1 - p\rvert$. The true obstruction is that the bridge-lemma ratio $\varepsilon_\Sigma\nu_c/\alpha_c \to \mathbb E\lvert\mathbb 1 - p\rvert$ independent of $\eta_c$. The accumulation-type spike flagged this in May and it never reached the segment. Second, the Tier-1 bridge-lemma contraction is proved in $\lVert H\cdot\rVert$, which is a norm only when $H$ has full column rank. With fewer observations than states, the null-space error is uncontracted (witness: $H = [1\ 0]$ gives eigenvalue $1$), so "Kalman filters" are Tier 1 only under full column rank or a multi-step detectability argument the segment does not make. Third, "No exponential regime exists" is labeled Resolved, but its case (II), the graph-Laplacian bound, is asserted in both canon and spike and derived in neither. Separately (item TODO.md:49): the body applies the closure criterion to every scope-satisfying set, while its own Working Note says (A2)+(A4) presume a unique attracting fixed point. **2.4 The communication gain trusts a known liar** (C.1; F7; item TODO.md:157). In $\eta_{ji}^\ast = U_{M_i}/(U_{M_i} + U_{o} + U_{\text{src}} + U_{\text{align}})$, $U_{\text{align}}$ is *uncertainty* about alignment. Certainty that $j$ is misaligned sends it to zero and raises the gain toward full trust. The Epistemic Status calls the additive form "conservative", which is true of uncertain alignment and false of known misalignment. A known-misaligned source is a bias, not a dispersion. The tracker item thought the scope sentence settled this; the scope sentence (present since March) asserts a safety property the formula lacks. The 2026-08-22 impl pass saw this and routed it to TODO, where it sat. **2.5 The meta-gain result does not use the timescale separation it cites** (C.1; F3; items TODO.md:208, 210 closed as ideas). `#deriv-adaptive-gain-dynamics` says completing the square "requires … (MG-3) timescale separation". Under (MG-3) that is $\mu = \alpha_K$, so slowing the gain *tightens* the condition.
