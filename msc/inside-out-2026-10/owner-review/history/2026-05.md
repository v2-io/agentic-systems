## 3. Testing the suspicion

### 3.1 The foundation was inconsistent while the top floors were being built

On May 28, the Gemini de novo audit 773921 found that the root definition `#def-agent-environment` made an action channel constitutive of "agent". Meanwhile `#scope-adaptive-system` and `#scope-agency` quantified over pairs that include passive observers (Kalman filters, passive Bayesian learners). The fix rewrote the root definition as a coupling with three channels and added an umbrella `agent` entry to LEXICON (`04147f7`). On May 29, two independent audits converged on a loophole in `#scope-agency`'s contrast condition. Because $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$, an action can satisfy $P(o \mid do(a)) \neq P(o \mid do(a'))$ by changing only the *view*, without changing $\Omega$. That fix landed only as a Discussion clarification; the formal tightening is still reserved (`484211f`, SP-28).

So during the very weeks the framework built a three-cluster constructive-impossibility taxonomy on the "boundary facet" of a stability-certificate spine, the definitions of *agent* and *agency* at its root contradicted each other. That is the plainest evidence for your suspicion I found. The apparatus did not notice the contradiction. A cold, in-order reader did.

### 3.2 Claims about agents that the meta layer carried and amplified, and that turned out wrong

Five central claims were found false or overclaimed in May. In four of them the meta or verification apparatus had added confidence before the claim was checked.

- **"Class 1 sub-agents under partially opposing objectives yield a Class 2 (Partial) composite."** During the May 9 GUC rename, the verifier praised the renaming agent for adding "explanatory text on why bounded coupling places the composite in Partial not Coupled — net improvement beyond rename" (`60c0ddf`). On May 21 the claim was withdrawn: the formal $\kappa^c$ criterion does not license it, and a Cournot witness shows the composite stays architecturally Class 1 while only its dynamic regime changes (`6a21669`). By then five de novo auditors had enthusiastically restated the withdrawn form ("organizations are natively Class 3"). The gold-lift had to fence it with an explicit do-not-re-promote note (`a051c24`). The rename's careful sonnet-modifies/opus-verifies cadence checked that the vocabulary was consistent. It did not check whether the claim was true, and it made the false claim more persuasive.
- **The sandbox evaluation ceiling.** This claim already sat in `#impl-causal-access` (May 13, `68180e5`). On May 28 it was promoted to its own segment, `#disc-sandbox-evaluation-ceiling`. Its stated novelty was "the recognition that *forkability is a Pearl-level demotion*" (`fc4ea9d`). Two days later a strengthen-first spike showed the step to be false: forkability is orthogonal to Level 1 versus Level 2, and randomized trials and A/B tests are both forkable and the canonical Level-2 source. The real ceiling is transportability (`989e37e`, `52d85b2`). The part that was claimed as novel was the part that was false.
- **The multiplicative $\rho$-split** $\rho = \rho_{\text{external}} \cdot f(M) \cdot g(\pi)$. It sat in `#internal-external-decomposition` at robust-qualitative from 2026-04-25, "with NO obstruction record in its entire git history" (`7625892`). A one-line category error refutes it (`543eeeb`).
- **The structural W1 leakage bound.** On May 21 the novelty refresh scored row 05 "Math = High (multiple theorems)" and credited the W1 structural-vs-behavioural leakage distinction (`c5a33fb`). On May 31 the W1 bound was found circular: $\kappa$ is bounded by an average of $\kappa$ (`f063133`, `fbcb36a`). Rows 06 and 15 were also raised on May 21. Their headline content was corrected on May 31: loop exactness became gated "availability" with (C1)–(C3) lifted into the Formal Expression (`52d85b2`), and "orthogonality now DERIVED" split into a three-layer valuation/invariant/dynamics claim (`0d57da2`). The instruction not to deflate was applied before the deep check had happened.
- **Model-S infinite-horizon non-exit** (refuted May 16). This one is a pure win for the strengthen-first apparatus, so it is the counter-example to my thesis. A peer agent had confidently predicted the strengthening would succeed; it failed structurally, and the failure became Corollary A.1S.1 (`b5eaaaf`, `19ad7b6`).

## 5. Where clarity about the claim sharpened

Stated plainly, as an owner might want them on one page, these are what May established or corrected about actual agents. Each is followed by where it landed and its tier at month end.

- **No correction strength buys pathwise containment against genuinely stochastic disturbance.** A long-lived agent leaves any bounded region with probability 1, and against bounded disturbance it never does. This is exact, `#deriv-sector-condition` Corollary A.1S.1 (`19ad7b6`). *Caveat in §7.1.*
- **Checking persistence one coordinate at a time can falsely pass.** Under cross-coupled correction the worst direction is off-axis, and a 2×2 counterexample shows it (`0c0ca9a`). Exact.
- **An agent cannot ground revision of its own objective in that objective.** You cannot write survival into $O_t$; the terminal invariant has to sit off the objective. Conditional and scoped, `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding` (`d14ba87`), sharpened May 31 into the three-layer split (`0d57da2`).
- **An agent learning its goal through a reward channel cannot identify the intended goal, and tampering is selected with positive measure.** Conditional on (R1)–(R5) (`52f72e3`).
- **An agent that is destroyed and reconstructed each session loses information geometrically.** Persistence of that information requires an external reinjection channel, so relational constitution is forced. Volume 3/4, conditional (`1535a7c`, `da3c6b4`).
- **Composing goal-blind agents with divergent goals changes the dynamics (equilibrium, cycling), not the architecture.** (`6a21669`)
- **What limits sandbox evaluation is transportability, not forkability.** (`52d85b2`)
- **A short-horizon replanner can do worse than its frozen current policy** unless replanning is order-consistent. Exact no-go, `#deriv-convention-monotonicity` (`3da7e32`).
- **One LLM in a persistent conversation cannot get the structural guarantee that its beliefs are not contaminated by its goals.** Separate-substrate or stateless calls can, under (C2′) (`c0f5936`). This is the sharpest May statement about LLMs.
- **Strategic persistence has a hard ceiling** $\rho_\Sigma \lt R_\Sigma/2$, but the ceiling depends on an update-ordering convention. Spiked, reserved (`aedc72d`).

Most of these are no-gos or scope corrections, and most were forced from outside in the month's last third. The clearest *prose* statements of what the theory says about agents came from Joseph's chapter intros (`95525fb`, `c8805a8`, May 12):

- the confounded *win* that cements a false belief ("the engine of gambling addiction… superstitious behavior");
- "a pre-compiled agent acts, but it never wonders";
- "survival … is a sustained burn rate";
- the structural-versus-task-adequacy split.

None of these sentences is derived. All are discussion-grade. They are the passages that say what the theory is *for*.
