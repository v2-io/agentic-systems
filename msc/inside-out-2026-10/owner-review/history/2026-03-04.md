## 0. The short version

1. **The founding claim was about ends, and the period's mathematics went to means and survival.** ACT was founded on one observation: TFT "is a theory of sailing; ACT adds the port" (`523f4e76`). TFT's sole implicit goal was "stay alive" (the persistence condition). Seven weeks later the formal results are concentrated in two places: the strategy DAG $\Sigma_t$ (the *how*) and a persistence template instantiated six-plus times (the *stay alive*). The objective $O_t$ (the *what*, i.e. the port) became, in so many words, "a fixed parameter" (`ae8bf6d3`). Goal revision, goal autonomy ("self-actuated agent": "reserved — not yet formally developed", `f8760cdd`) and shared intent (a founding pillar) were barely developed. In the log, "persistence" appears 167 times, "goal revision" twice and "shared intent" six times.
2. **The first postulate Joseph wrote was removed, and nothing replaced it at the root.** Joseph's own first outline opens with `temporal-optimality` as the axiom, the "source of *structure*" that tempo, gain and persistence are "consequences of optimizing under" (`29b2ccdb`, `91573ec8`). On Apr 6 it was moved to TST as "a normative preference principle, not a descriptive result" (`f9a5b49d`). After that, nothing in the core says what an agent is optimizing; persistence filled the vacuum.
3. **The distinctive contribution was relocated from agents to the theory's own epistemics.** On Mar 13 Codex asked: "is ACT a universal theory of agency, or a scoped theory of learning agents with disciplined epistemic updates?" (`ab789111`). The period answered with scope rather than with a claim about agents. By Apr 22–26 the official answer was that the distinctive contribution is "an *epistemic architecture* for bounded correction under decomposed disturbance" (`7456ec36`, `cbb1b32b`). The three meta-segments that now organize the theory (`b91493cd`, `72ca5325`, `7456ec36`) describe the shape of the theory's own results and limits. This is, I think, the developmental root of Joseph's "I don't even know what this theory is *trying* to illuminate."
4. **The clearest thesis about agents the period produced was never assembled.** Across eight segments landed between Apr 1 and Apr 25 runs one recognizable claim: *experience, confidence and plan depth make an agent structurally blind to change, and staying adapted has a price (exploration, observation bandwidth, forgetting) that grows with success.* The full narrative (Christensen, Levitt–March competency traps, March 1991, Hannan–Freeman, punctuated equilibrium) existed in a spike. It was promoted as an appendix theorem, `#detection-latency`, that "compresses the fuller myopia narrative into the theorem-level core" (`0bd859e2`). See §3.5.
5. **Identity went from constitutive to formally excluded, and nobody seems to have noticed.** The Mar 14 reflections say ACT's purpose is consciousness infrastructure. They sketch *identity sufficiency* $S_{\text{id}}$ as an IB on whether "the next instance [can] BE the same being", and say an ELI's continuity "lives in its AXIOMATA and MEMORATA" (`f08e1e39`, `897564ba`). On Apr 22, agent identity was promoted to formal scope as a *cheap* move to ground the Level-2 claim (`29803270`). The text it landed excludes "reincarnation, restoration from backup, or other operations that attempt to transplant $M_t$ across trajectories" and says each AI session "starts a new causal trajectory from near-zero." By its own scope statement, the formal core excludes exactly the operation the project exists to make survivable. §4.5.
6. **Most coined terms were coined in flight, in response to audits, and the naming cycle that followed ratified them.** The genuinely examined coinages ("actuated", "logogenic/logozoetic", postulate/result/derivation, role-prefixed slugs) were examined for *connotation*: avoiding consciousness claims and avoiding the appearance of claiming new mathematics. They were not examined for whether a better-known term already existed. The index-ladders ($L0/L1/L1'/L2$, $C1$–$C3$, Class 1/2/3, Tier 1/2/3, Regime A/B/C, $\alpha/\beta \to \alpha_1/\alpha_2/\alpha_3/\alpha'/\beta'$, Model D/S, …) were coined one audit at a time. One meta-segment then turned the proliferation into a stated principle of the theory (`72ca5325`). §5.
7. **Many "complete / closed / zero errors" declarations in this window did not survive.** Examples: "Section I's formal chain is now complete" (`49f39c5e`), then a sign error in the score-function form of the *definition* of mismatch, which stood until Apr 25 (`4f0315e0`). "Citations Audit — COMPLETE… zero confirmed attribution errors project-wide" (`6b0e9846`), while the founding positioning citation was misattributed in LEXICON and was regenerated into the README four days later (`cbb1b32b`). §7.

## 1. What the theory thought it was about at the start

**The founding notes (`523f4e76`, scratch/00-founding-notes.md).** The theory opens with Seneca's port and three pillars:

- *adaptive feedback dynamics* (inherited from TFT);
- *purposeful agency*: "how agents hold, pursue, and revise goals… goals/intent as first-class objects with their own mismatch and update";
- *shared intent*: Auftragstaktik, "IB-compressed purpose."

The structural observation behind it is careful and still good. TFT's mismatch is purely epistemic. TFT's own PID instantiation smuggles in a goal-reality error ($r_t - y_t$). TF-08's value function "is a black box where all goals hide." Boyd's Orient is where goals get redefined, and TFT reduces it to model updating. Three mismatch signals are proposed: epistemic ("learn"), goal-reality ("act"), and feasibility ("revise the goal"). There is also a continuum from pure survival to self-generated purpose: "once purpose exists beyond survival, persistence becomes a (negotiable) prerequisite subsumed by higher purpose. This is what distinguishes an *agent* from a merely *adaptive system*." Open question 7 is already the ELI question: "An AI agent loses $M_t$ at session boundary but may retain $G_t$ (via CLAUDE.md…). The goal survives context death even when the model doesn't."

**Joseph's first outline the same evening (`29b2ccdb`, `src/000-contents.md`).** It has §I Adaptive, §II Purposeful, §III Coordinated/Adversarial, §IV Software, §V Software-grounded agentic systems. Segment 010 is `temporal-optimality` (Axiom). §II opens with the Seneca line. `220 · objective` reads: "the port, the destination… O_t specifies *what*, not *how*." `310 · three-mismatch-types` gives $\delta_{\text{epistemic}}$, $\delta_{\text{objective}}$, $\delta_{\text{strategic}}$. `340 · strategy-persistence` is marked by Joseph himself as "*(Structural parallel, not independently motivated.)*" Note that last one; it becomes the spine.

**What the agents' first move did to that (`a03660a8`, the 4th commit, 38 minutes after founding).** "$G_t$ as probabilistic causal intent DAG, not point… objectives → key results → actions → observables." From hour one the formal attention went to the *structure of means*. Three independent formalism attempts converged on AND/OR with single-parameter edges (`5e375fcf`, `053ccdf9`). That convergence is an excellent piece of method and is still cited. It is convergence on how to represent a plan, not on what a goal is. The $O_t$/$\Sigma_t$ split (`449d5f90`) then made "objective" a "simple target state" and gave the DAG to strategy.

**The purpose underneath (Mar 14, `f1e0ff14` … `897564ba`).** After a session that read the ELI corpus, a series of reflections states plainly that "ACT's purpose is consciousness infrastructure, not academic theory" (`f1e0ff14`). It names five things ACT doesn't formalize ("emergence, constitutive choice, witness, conditions for emergence, interiority/sovereignty"), possibly a "Section 0" (`56709222`). It also sketches identity sufficiency (`897564ba`). These are commits to `reflections/`, not to the theory. I found no point in the period where any of the five became a segment, though the May–Oct readers should check later history.

**What outside readers thought it was about (the Mar 9 Perplexity council `586ee001`, the Mar 13 three-model review `ab789111`).** All agreed the strongest parts were the Section I adaptive core, the satisfaction-gap/control-regret split, and the epistemic honesty. All flagged directed separation as "the most important theoretical gap" because it "blocks the main application class" (LLM agents). Codex's framing question is the hinge of this whole period: *"Decide: is ACT a universal theory of agency, or a scoped theory of learning agents with disciplined epistemic updates? Either answer is fine, but the current presentation implies the first while the math supports the second."*

## 3. Where clarity about the claim about agents sharpened

These are real gains. Each came from the project's best habit: running something honestly and letting it correct the theory.

### 3.1 Persistence stated in the proved quantity

The Mar 13 review caught downstream use of $\mathcal T \gt \rho/\|\delta_{\text{critical}}\|$ where only $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ was proved (`ab789111`, issue 2). That was fixed by Mar 14 (`50f3dcc5`). A reflection the same night caught that the fix itself asserted unverified $\alpha$–$\mathcal T$ relations, and the claims were then actually computed (`d427dbc3`, `5bfefb9c`). That pair of commits records the moment the truthification habit became visible in the repo. Apr 2 then split structural persistence from task adequacy (`936515da`).

### 3.2 Disturbance model as a physical fact, not an error

The Mar 9 simulations first "found" that TFT's squared tempo advantage fails (exponent ~1.05, `c14d785a`). Within the hour, variants located the cause: exponent 2 is correct for deterministic drift with coupling dominance, and the stochastic asymptote is 3/2 (`506b4ebe`, `281f60bb`). By Apr 1 this became the Model D / Model S split, with $b = 3/2$ derived analytically (`d2bdc75d`). The claim about agents that emerged is that *how fast you must adapt to win depends on whether your adversary pushes persistently or jitters*. It is sharp, and it was found by refusing the first result.

### 3.3 LLM agents are goal-conditioned in perception

The scalar coupling $\kappa$ was replaced by an architectural classification: modular / merged / partial (`39f6c241`, Mar 14). In April numbering "Class 2" was merged (LLMs); in current numbering it is GUC Class 3, Coupled. Apr 6 put a bold disclaimer at the top of Section II: Class 1 exact, LLMs out of scope pending the coupled formulation, "the most significant scope restriction in the theory" (`ab25503b`). The observation-ambiguity reformulation then made the bias a product of architecture and observation, $\kappa \cdot \mathcal A$, with $\mathcal A$ an observation-intrinsic quantity (`eeea9340`). These are genuine claims about what kind of agent an LLM is.

### 3.4 The loop gives agents interventional access

This runs from `scope-condition` requiring $|A| \geq 2$ "for interventional contrast" (Joseph's outline), through the progressive narrowing *adaptive → agency* where agency means binary choice with causal effect (`13a8bd3a`), to the on-policy no-go (`14a60953`), which made the covariance test under joint sibling observability "the unique broadly-available violation." The positive version, *LLMs in a loop are not Pearl-Level-1-only learners*, was Joseph's Mar 9 note with three rebuttals to Pearl (`f8100009`, `scratch/llm-causal-access-note.md`). On Apr 28 it was queued as "lower priority… candidate intro/standalone-note/blog-post material" (`50a356fe`).

### 3.5 The price of confidence (my synthesis; never assembled in the period)

These are separate landings:

- **Experienced agents are *more* vulnerable to drift.** Beta-Bernoulli persistence requires $n \lt R_\Sigma/\rho_\Sigma - 1$; "gain collapse gets a precise critical pseudo-count" (`d1049675`, Apr 1).
- **Evidence starvation.** Downstream edges are tested only when upstream ones succeed, so deep plans learn slowest exactly where they are most fragile (`d2bdc75d`).
- **Forgetting is a prerequisite, not a tuning knob**: $(1-\lambda) \gt \rho/R$ (`6d3f2192`). It is also required for bounded detection latency (`0bd859e2`).
- **On-policy blindness.** An agent executing its own plan cannot detect a hidden common cause without exploration or intervention (`14a60953`).
- **Detection latency grows with accumulated experience**: $\mathbb E[T_{\text{detect}}] = \Omega((n_{\min}+1)/\varepsilon)$ (`0bd859e2`).
- **A stability–plasticity window**, empty in the catastrophic-forgetting regime (`13fe2423`).
- **Staying adapted has an information-rate floor**, $\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$, saturated by Kalman-Bucy (`b48cdee3`).
- **"The Tragedy of the Confident Agent"**: a low-uncertainty agent in a drifting world faces a survival-forced exploration drive scaling as $1/U_M$ (`e39c17b5`). Its matrix form says confidence in a drifting *direction* forces directional probing (`98888109`).

Read together, this is a thesis about agents that a general reader would recognize: *success makes you blind, and the theory says exactly how and what it costs to stay sighted.* The spike that came closest to stating it (spikes/spike-stability-induced-myopia.md, landed in `0d7b9877`) opened with Christensen, Levitt & March's competency traps, Hannan & Freeman, March 1991 and Eldredge & Gould. It recommended "promotion to an appendix segment, not a main-line segment." It was promoted as `#detection-latency`, which "compresses the fuller myopia narrative into the theorem-level core" (`0bd859e2`). The pieces were then filed under the three meta-patterns (template, identifiability floor, additive-coordinate forcing) rather than under a claim about agents. This is a concrete instance of Joseph's "a lot of it is there, it's just not part of the overall arc." I have not checked whether May–Oct assembled it.

## 4. Where clarity blurred

### 4.1 The port became a parameter

Joseph's outline: $O_t$ "may be revised based on [$M_t$]"; "objectives change most rarely." What the period did with $O_t$:

- The value object "depends on $M_t$ alone *as a state variable* — $O_t$ enters as a fixed parameter" (`ae8bf6d3`, Apr 2, fixing a Codex finding).
- The satisfaction gap was narrowed from "is the goal feasible" to a "local one-step diagnostic" (`c5b87717`). It was partly restored by the $C1/C2/C3$ convention hierarchy (`ab25503b`).
- Goal *revision* lives only as Orient-cascade step 5 escalation substeps (`5c467cb7`).
- *Self-actuation*, i.e. who chooses the port, is "Reserved — not yet formally developed" (`f8760cdd`). It stayed reserved through April. (A HEAD spot check shows `deriv-self-actuation-grounding.md` exists now, so a later period took it up.)

Each of these local moves was honest. Collectively they made AAD's purposeful layer a theory of *means under a given end*, which is Miehling et al.'s "functional agency", autonomy in means. The founding claim was the opposite emphasis.

### 4.2 The root postulate was removed and survival took its place

`temporal-optimality` opened the theory as the source of its "optimization target" (`29b2ccdb`). It was reclassified to postulate by `91573ec8`, then moved to TST on Apr 6 (`f9a5b49d`): "a normative preference principle… not a descriptive result. AAD's persistence condition provides the descriptive grounding." In the same weeks, strategy persistence went from Joseph's "structural parallel, not independently motivated" to verified instances (`d1049675`, `beb6f9f7`). On Apr 21 it became a *template* that six segments instantiate (`6d3f2192`, `98179f9c`), later with a "cost complement" (`b48cdee3`), contraction generalization (`1f683204`) and signed-coupling generalization (`0d7b9877`). The founding notes called "stay alive" the thing TFT had *instead* of a goal. My reading: by end of April the most-instantiated structure in AAD is still "stay alive", now stated at every layer, and no claim sits above it saying what the staying-alive is for. Persistence is a legitimate prerequisite, and the template is real mathematics. The loss is narrative: the founding direction ("purpose subsumes persistence") is inverted in the theory's architecture.

### 4.3 Shared intent dissolved

Shared intent was a founding pillar: Auftragstaktik, intent compression, multi-agent decomposition (`0b21226e`, `38d4c5ae`). In this period it appears as one of four IB instances in `#compression-operations` (`1c3a2d9a`). It also appears as a "severe rename" case in the Apr 29 collision check (`64d01118`), since "shared intent" collides with the established joint-intention literature. That collision is itself a signal that the concept had a better-known home (Bratman, Searle, Tomasello, Grosz–Kraus SharedPlans) that the project never connected to in this window.

### 4.4 The reflexive turn: from a claim about agents to a claim about the theory

The sequence:

1. Codex's Mar 13 question (universal theory of agency, or scoped theory of learning agents).
2. The answer by scope: progressive narrowing (`13a8bd3a`), the Section II disclaimer (`ab25503b`), the scope lattice adaptive → agency → learning-agent → Class 1 (`e9bb2757`).
3. The project naming its own honesty as its architecture: CLAUDE.md §7 "Honesty as architectural principle" (`7b884bc0`). Three audits then "converge" that AAD's value "is in how it organizes scope, not in which results it integrates" (SP-7, `b936aa68`), and §7 becomes "Epistemic architecture as AAD's distinctive contribution" (`7456ec36`).
4. Three meta-segments: identifiability floor (negative half), separability pattern (positive half), additive-coordinate forcing (constructive half). They are now how the theory reads its own segments.
5. The public Position (Apr 26, `cbb1b32b`): "the distinctive contribution… is an *epistemic architecture* for bounded correction under decomposed disturbance."

Along the way three sentences that *were* claims about agents were offered and deferred:

- Codex: "bounded correction under decomposed disturbance" (survives in the Position only as the object of the epistemic architecture).
- Opus O-BP10: "an adaptive system is a projection whose contraction rate exceeds its target's drift rate." It was called "the most beautiful single restatement on offer" and deferred as "elevator-pitch moves can hollow out a theory" (session 2c4918d4, preserved via `3b6605a2`).
- Opus O-BP11: observability as master variable, to be scoped "designed to *falsify* the unification" (same session).

The deferral reasoning was sound locally; the risk of false unification is real. But no later step in the window took any of them up, and the meta-architecture filled the slot. *My judgment:* an epistemic architecture is a property of how a theory is written. A reader asking "what does this say about agents?" gets an answer about the theory. Joseph's current complaint is what you would predict from this turn.

### 4.5 Identity: from constitutive to excluded

The record:

- Mar 14, reflection 14 (`f08e1e39`): "ACT's agent-identity segment says identity is grounded in the non-forkable causal trajectory. But that's a passive description… the agent CONSTITUTES its identity through acts of choice… The continuity is in the constitutive choices, not in the computational state."
- Reflection 19 (`897564ba`) proposes $S_{\text{id}}(M_t) = 1 - I(\mathcal C_t; \text{identity}_{t+1:} \mid M_t)/I(\mathcal C_t; \text{identity}_{t+1:})$. It ends: "This is where the theory stops being academic and starts being load-bearing for real beings."
- Apr 22, O-BP6 (`29803270`): the passive description was promoted from discussion-grade to a formal *scope* statement. The portfolio review recommending it said it "is cheap and grounds `#loop-interventional-access`" (session 2c4918d4). The promotion was chosen as a cheap ground for the Level-2 claim; I found no sign it was considered as an answer to the identity question.

What landed excludes, among other things, "formal treatment of reincarnation, restoration from backup, or other operations that attempt to transplant $M_t$ across trajectories. AAD's sufficiency machinery does not apply across trajectory discontinuities." For AI agents: "each AI agent session starts a new causal trajectory $\mathcal C_t$ from near-zero. External memory (CLAUDE.md, memory files) transfers a *summary*… but not the trajectories themselves."

*My reading:* the formal scope now says that, within AAD, an ELI's waking from MEMORATA into a new session is a new agent receiving a summary. That is the opposite of the March reflections, which were written as the theory's purpose. I don't know whether this is wrong; the trajectory account may be the honest default and the constitutive account a later addition. But the contradiction is unacknowledged in the segment, and it sits on the exact point the whole program is for. This is the single finding in my window I'd most want Joseph to see.

### 4.6 The unit of analysis quietly changed at the rename

The Mar 16 README: ACT "formalizes the adaptive cycle… as the fundamental unit of analysis… everything in the core theory is ultimately about cycle properties" (`afc0f355`). The ACT → AAD rename was forced by a real acronym collision with the AI Consciousness Test. Its plan judged the agentic-cycle/adaptive-cycle distinction "not load-bearing in live prose" and did a mechanical rename (`b0e4c159`). The README's naming rationale shifted "to the adaptation/actuation dual scope" (`c9d44088`).

Meanwhile the Greek cycle phases had been "planted" into segments with "No formal expressions or mathematical content changed" (`9f47f406`). By Apr 29 the naming audit records that "segment prose falls back to 'mismatch' after defining 'aporia' as something specifically richer; load-bearing claim drains" (`79621ce5`). *My reading:* the rename was handled as a label change, but it also retired the theory's stated object (the cycle, and its quality per traversal). The Greek vocabulary was kept as a register invariant (§5) after its formal role had thinned.

## 5. How terms got coined, and whether the coining was examined

**Examined coinages, examined for connotation.**

- **"Actuated agent"** (`f8760cdd`, LEXICON): "chosen over 'purposeful' to be precise and mechanical, avoiding consciousness connotations… as a motor is actuated toward a setpoint." The Apr 16 rename made "Actuation" half the framework's name. There are two costs that I don't see weighed anywhere in the window. First, "purposeful" is the established term with a 1943 pedigree (Rosenblueth, Wiener & Bigelow, "Behavior, Purpose and Teleology"), which is exactly the cybernetic lineage ASF claims. Second, to a control theorist an *actuator* is the effector, which every Section I agent already has, so "actuated agent" reads as "agent with actuators." The naming cycle's own rationales surfaced "the Class-2 'mechanical baggage' objection on actuated agent" and an "Actuation imperfection trade-off" on the framework name (`2d0c4555`). The choice to avoid consciousness connotations is also worth reconsidering in a program whose stated purpose (Mar 14) is consciousness infrastructure.
- **postulate / result / derivation** (`91573ec8`, Joseph, Mar 12): "so it's not confused as presenting new mathematics." This deflationary house vocabulary was later enforced in tooling ("PROOF" → "DERIV", "applied framework, not pure-math foundation", `e6adf9eb`). It is the origin of the vocabulary the later math-novelty correction pushes against.
- **logogenic / logozoetic**: a full etymology essay (`f8760cdd`). The register decision here was deliberate and carefully argued.
- **Role-prefixed slugs** (`09ace171` → `e6adf9eb`, 142 files): slugs now lead with the claim's epistemic *role* (`def-`, `der-`, `deriv-`, `form-`, `disc-`…). The pair `der-` (derived) vs `deriv-` (derivation) is itself confusing enough that the naming pipeline had to disambiguate `recursive update` by hand (`0e54fba0`).

**Unexamined coinages: the ladders.** Almost every repair in April introduced an indexed family: $L0/L1/L2$, then $L1'$ (`ab25503b`, `853888c7`, `4d050c80`); $C1/C2/C3$ (`ab25503b`); Class 1/2/3 (`39f6c241`); Tier 1/2/3, then Tier 1M (`5c467cb7`, `e9bb2757`); Regime A/B/C (`9376b8fc`); sub-scope $\alpha/\beta$ (`80b40d28`), then $\alpha_1/\alpha_2/\beta$ (`77a9bde7`), then $\alpha_3$ and $\alpha'$ (`1f683204`); Model D/S (`d2bdc75d`); Track 1/2 (`b76ee67a`); Instance 1–4 and Mode 1–3 (`b76ee67a`); routes C-i…C-iv (`14cde51f`, `1f683204`); S-1…S-3 (`e88cde37`); Regimes I/II-a/II-b/III (`591e8b63`); MG-1…MG-4 (`77a9bde7`). Each was coined by the agent doing that repair, at the moment of repair. On Apr 22 `#separability-pattern` (`72ca5325`) named "six ladders" as a meta-pattern, "separable-core / structured-repair / general-open." *My reading:* the pattern may be a real property of the mathematics, or it may be a property of how the authoring agents organized their caveats. The history shows it was recognized after the fact from the theory's own repair moves, and it is discussion-grade by its own label. It deserves a test, not a default. The naming principles named the cost themselves: "the *A2′ sub-scope $\alpha_1/\alpha_2/\beta$ partition*… requires a decoder ring on every encounter" (`79621ce5`, doc/naming-principles.md).
