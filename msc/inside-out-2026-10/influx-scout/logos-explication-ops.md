## 0. The finding that changes what "influx" means here

`~/src/arch/explication/sources-to-explication-2026-09-25.md` holds Joseph's 2026-09-25 statement of artifact layers and flow direction. It is unratified, and he calls it "still a little fuzzy". The primary is the verbatim dialog it indexes, `~/src/arch/logos/msc/dialog-2026-09-25-revision-orientation.md`, lines 125–154. The principle, verbatim:

> With the existing papers scattered all over the place there was a lot of development that happened at the time of the paper coming together that then (ostensibly) got fed *back* to ASF etc. and integrated back upstream. That hasn't worked out so well. I realize now that a much more principled and effective process would be to make sure that upstream is *always* the source of truth and that explication never asserts anything or derives anything or (possibly) argues anything that isn't already upstream.

The layers he sketched are SOURCES (theory = asf; data = vivarium, asf/msc, empirica; arguments = logos, "right now it doesn't exist and instead holds papers and pre-paper work"; cases = `_core`, longitudinal) → explication (all public output). Three consequences for KR3.3:

1. **Influx from papers is the backlog this principle exists to stop.** Everything below that a paper asserts or argues, and canon does not, is debt under the new rule. Integrating it is the precondition for revising any paper, not a courtesy to canon.
2. **Some of it has no upstream home yet.** The material is *argued*, not derived: positions of others (Belshaw, Parfit), objections and replies with a standing, instruments like the separator. ASF segments are the wrong home for most of it. Logos-as-argument-substrate is the intended home, and it does not exist yet. The 09-25 instance's case for it (dialog lines 207–222) is the best thinking on its shape. Its first caution matters for integrators: "in philosophy the phrasing often *is* the finding", so upstream-first should govern where an *assertion* is recorded, not strip the formulations. **KR3.3 needs a decision on where argued claims go before it can integrate this region honestly.** The alternatives are to leave them in papers or to force them into ASF segments at a register those segments don't hold. Neither is honest, and both repeat the failure the principle names.
3. **It revises the unratified `CHARTER-DRAFT.md`.** It overturns §6 ("develop in whichever register moves fastest… back-propagate") and changes §0's logos row and §3.2. That is scout 3's region; this is a pointer.

The dialog also shows the flow running *both* ways, and integrators should expect that. `def-death-as-factor-loss`'s own Epistemic Status says its factor-loss principle was "developed in the project's philosophical track (2026-05/06)", so the gate is a back-port from the paper. Joseph, same dialog: "some of what you're referencing there might be written as a *result* of or in anticipation of things discussed in the paper." So "canon says X, the paper says X" does not establish which one is primary.
