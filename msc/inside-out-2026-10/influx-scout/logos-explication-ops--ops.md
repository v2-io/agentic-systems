- **Process lesson, for O5.** Every claim-strength drift in §2.A follows one pattern. A result was stated outward at the strength it had *on the day of writing*, and when canon later moved (refuted, withdrawn, or never landed) nothing flowed back to the outward surface. Nothing in the program links an outward claim to the canon slug and tier it rests on, so a canon change can't find its outward copies. The 09-25 principle (papers assert nothing not upstream) fixes the direction of truth. It doesn't yet give a way to *find* the outward copies when upstream changes, which would mean slug citations in outward texts and a `relata`-style back-index. That is a candidate KR5.3 practice.
- **On the brief's framing.** The coordinator's guess, "less theory, more maps", holds for the documents. But the maps are useful mainly for *decoding* (old IDs, old slugs, paper → segment closures), and they are three to six months stale. The most useful thing in ops for this effort is the outward claim record (§2.A), followed by the two July documents that state the program's arc and telos (§2.F, §2.G). The `mtime 2026-07-09` trap in the brief is real and covers most of `papers/`. One more trap: `aad-consequential-findings` and `foundation-dependency` are dated the same day and **contradict each other** on strategic composition (→ Class 2 vs → Class 3).
