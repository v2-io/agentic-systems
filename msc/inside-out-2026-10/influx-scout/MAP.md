## 8. Method, and what to know about this map's reliability

- **Partition.** The drive was split into nine regions by detection method as well as by directory. Regions likely to hold renamed or developed material (the papers, the rewrites) got concept-level readers. The mechanical slug census (`data/`, explained in `README.md`) was used only to find the connected material and to separate out its noise. Joseph's named list was covered in full. Places beyond it that surfaced: context-saliency's uncommitted notes, overarching, udon's tooling corpus, verisectorium's theory, comproprium's term-reliability map, agent-core's `anatomy-of-an-agent/`, and the 43 `old-tst-*` legacy files counted in the census.
- **The "in canon?" column** was offered to every scout as a shared convention: grep of segments, OUTLINEs and `FINDINGS.md`; full-body asf git log; the `.integrated/` dirs; the source's own influx notes. Each file states which checks ran. Grep absence is weak evidence for renamed material, and the files say so where it matters.
- **What was not verified.** No scout re-derived the math it relays (the sandwich lemma, the August LMI results, the seed-sufficiency candidate and so on). Rungs such as "exact", "proved" and "verified" are the source's own tags, carried with the source's verification history. Only three aat-refactored results were re-derived by two readers. My own checks are the [coordinator-verified] rows: five canon texts re-read at HEAD.
- **Safeguard deaths.** This is system knowledge for later launches. The Opus `reasoning_extraction` safeguard killed:
  - **The region-7 scout (Opus), twice.** The first time was after it had mapped AISI-responses, on moving to `aisi-eoi`. The second was immediately on resume, which suggests that once the trigger sits in a transcript, resuming re-trips it.
  - **Its Sonnet replacement, once.** It had finished ai-risk-model and had `aisi-eoi` and `notability-extracts` in progress, so the safeguard is not Opus-specific.
  - **The region-9 scout (Opus), once.** It died after mapping `overarching` (Joseph's private notes, including notes on reasoning-trace interiority) and `_core/tst`. Resuming worked, and it finished.

  Independently, agent-core's middle-fold work records the same safeguard tripping stochastically on probes that ask a model about its own decision process (`agent-core.md` §8). Material *about* model reasoning seems to be the trigger class. `aisi-eoi` holds such material: its `spikes/briefs/` seeded asf's honest-activation spike, and its spikes include `who-is-the-reporter`. I did not retry a fourth time, nor read it myself, because losing the coordinating context would cost more than the gap. **`aisi-eoi` and `notability-extracts` remain unscouted.** For a route around this, a reader could work from file listings and commit bodies first, or Joseph could look directly.
