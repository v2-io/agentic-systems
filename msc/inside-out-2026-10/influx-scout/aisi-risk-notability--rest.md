### 3.0 What it is, and how much of it is ASF

*This is not an ASF repo.* It is a compilation of the frontier-AI risk field's own documents (212 sources: AISI, IASR/Bengio, SB 53, EU Code, company frameworks, MIT, NIST…) into a shared lexicon and claim model ("model gamma", plan in `MODEL-GAMMA-METHODOLOGY-AND-PLAN.md`, 124 KB). Its principles are compilation-not-adjudication and fidelity-to-source, so by design it carries few theory claims of its own. About 90% of the tree (`influx/source-atlas`, `source-models`, `verification`, `model-beta`, `thin-pass`, `reviews`, `search/`, `bin/`, `def/`, `lexicon/`, `catalog/`, `ref/`, `bib/`) is risk-literature infrastructure with nothing for ASF canon, and I did not read it beyond headings and a vocabulary grep (below). The dirty file (`MODEL-GAMMA-METHODOLOGY-AND-PLAN.md`, 5 lines changed) is table-formatting only (checked with `git diff`).

**The 12 `.claude/worktrees/*` dirs are checked-out copies of source-expert branches, all merged into main** (`git rev-list main..branch` = 0 for ten of them). The two exceptions (`expert/aisi-2025-frontier-held`, `expert/bengio-2026-international-held`) each hold one commit of reflections on the biological-section units, deliberately held back from main (safeguard caution). Nothing ASF-relevant is unique to them. One line so nobody re-walks them.
