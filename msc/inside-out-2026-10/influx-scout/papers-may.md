1. **Canon states a claim that `behavioral-floor` refuted.** `#der-loop-interventional-access` Working Notes (line 90) says that goal-conditioned LLM policies violate (C2) sequential ignorability "by construction", and `#impl-causal-access` (line 84) points to NeurIPS Paper 3 as carrying "the Coupled-class (C2) violation". The paper's own cycle-8 correction pins it to that channel and not to the whole response. 2. **`behavioral-floor` holds the clearest statement I found anywhere of what AAT claims about agents.** It is not mainly the math. `INTRO-SKETCH.md` (2026-05-15) is a dependency-ordered "installation spine" built from a walk of the asf canon. This is the "loss of clarity on what is being theorized" influx Joseph named.
3. **`causal-language` measured Joseph's foundational premise ("causal structure embedded in language"), and the measurement came back deflationary.** On the cleanest test (vocabulary-identical minimal pairs plus token-shuffle), the route that Theorem 1 licenses shows up in 3 of 12 embedding models, with no capacity trend. The premise appears only as a Working-Note listing (`04-eli-core/src/def-five-constitutive-factors.md:66`), and asf's spike INDEX still describes the empirical follow-on as future work. The paper also tested the "capacity asymmetry as asymmetric comprehension" hypothesis (raised in the 2026-05-14 morning reflection, an agent's reflection Joseph captured in `docs/reflections-2026-05-14-am.md`). It is not supported: every panel Spearman $\rho$ is non-significant at $n = 12$. That is a no-trend result, not a refutation of the conceptual hypothesis, and the paper says so.
4. **Canon cites `embeddings` with stale numbers, and leans on it harder than the paper supports.** The numbers are pre-trim draft values (details in §C1).
