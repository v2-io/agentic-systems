## Data (`data/`), taken 2026-10-30

- **`asf-slugs-2026-10-30.tsv`**: the 290 files under the four `*/src/` dirs (filename = slug), each with its component. 44 of them are legacy imports with no `slug:` frontmatter and no OUTLINE entry: 43 `old-tst-*` files (commit `da3d6e9f`, 2026-04-01) and `old-tf-appendix-f-multi-agent.md`. So canon proper is about 246 segments.
- **`slug-mentions-outside-asf-2026-10-30.txt`**: per-file counts of canon-slug string mentions under `~/src/`, excluding asf, `.git`, `node_modules`, JSON/JSONL, `ruby-community` and `_ref/rails-migrations-survey`. It was made with `rg -c -F -f <slugs of 10 or more chars>`. It mixes three populations:
  - generated projections of canon (e.g. `AISI-responses/extract/`);
  - tooling data that happens to contain slugs (e.g. udon's `udon-needs/.../recall-floor/*`, about 6,000 of `arch/firmatum`'s 7,176 hits, and md-press `model/*.tsv`);
  - theory-bearing documents.

  It finds connected material, not renamed material: the paper repos show single-digit hits even where they developed the math.
