## 1. The one picture

The right rung $A_O^{\text{RH}} \leq A_O^{\text{B}}$ compares two first-action suprema under different continuations. Two checkers found, by constructed instance, that the appendix's proof used two things nothing in canon delivers: that the receding-horizon replanner's stitched policy is a member of $\Pi$, and that state-wise optimality of $\pi^\ast$ makes the two readings of $A_O^{\text{B}}$ (best first action under $\pi^\ast$; class supremum) coincide. Both are false for restricted classes. What is true, and what canon now says in the appendix and in `#def-value-object` alike: the rung is exact under **(M)** the stitched replanner is a member, together with **(PC)** prefix-closure or **(SO)** state-wise optimality of $\pi^\ast$, read with $A_O^{\text{B}}$ as the first-action supremum; (PC) makes the two readings coincide, (SO) does so only with **(G)** greediness of $\pi^\ast$ at the decision point; a **rectangular** class (all history-dependent or time-indexed Markov policies with state-wise admissible action sets) has all of it, for every maximizer; a DAG-representable plan library generally has none of it. Three two-policy instances show each hypothesis is needed: $100$ against $10$ ((M) holds, (PC) and (SO) fail), $15$ against $6$ ((PC) and (SO) hold, (M) fails), $51$ against $12$ ((M) and (SO) hold, (PC) fails; the readings part). Three consequences travel with it and the appendix now carries them: C3's positive gap means no member of $\Pi$ reaches the threshold, for every class, with no hypothesis; the C1-against-C3 diagnostic needs only membership of the current policy and one closure property, not the rungs; control regret is non-negative under C2 only when the replanner is guarded.

Outside the rung, three B.1 record defects in the same files are replaced: the aspiration hypothesis is now a claim about what a bounded planner reaches, so its population is no longer inside its own False-of; `#form-objective-functional` no longer equates incompleteness with (E1) and no longer lists lexicographic ordering as a diagnostic-preserving scalarization on a continuum; `#def-control-regret`'s non-negativity carries its conventions.
