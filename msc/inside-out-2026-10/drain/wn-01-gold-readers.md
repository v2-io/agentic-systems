## What was read and how it was judged

All 192 bullets under the `Readers often ask / wonder` sub-headings of the 120 gold blocks were read whole, from the extract `cat-readers.md` (the wn-01-gold extraction; line numbers are the segment's at `86489b0b`). Two Off-ramp reader-clarity residuals the lead assigned to H.4 were added: `deriv-discrete-sector-condition` WN 230 and `deriv-graph-structure` WN 267. The third, `detail-linear-ode-approximation` WN 236, moved to `item:mismatch-dynamics-model-d-equality-scope` (C.1) while this pass ran, and the matching gold question was raised there.

For each question I grepped the segment body (everything above `## Working Notes`) for the terms it turns on, read the matching lines, and read further where the hits were ambiguous. Called "answered" when a body line (cited) answers the question as asked; "open" when no body line does. Two limits on this: a grep can miss an answer worded differently from the question, so an "open" verdict means I found no answer, not that I proved none exists. And "answered" means stated, not stated well; where the answer is present but buried, the WN item's own pedagogy is for the pedagogy home.

Cross-surface: each open question was checked against `items/` and `questions/` by key phrase (theory of mind, hysteresis, adaptive reserve, rho_Sigma, options framework, IB exactness, the Soviet example, N_h for LLMs, what M_t covers, and others). Sixteen matched existing items and were raised on them with `vsect raise`, not duplicated. I did not search `audits/`, `spikes/` or `msc/` per question: these are reader-experience questions that the de novo audit working dirs already hold as §14 reflections, and that is the surface they were lifted from.

**Tally:** 194 dispositions (192 gold bullets + the 2 off-ramp residuals kept here). Open 92, answered in the body 61, answered downstream 8, existing item 16, pedagogy signal 7, set aside 9, handed to Follow-ups 1.

**Ledger:** one H.4 item per segment with an open question, **62 items**, slug `item:readers-ask-<segment>`. Each lists its open questions with the body lines checked. `vsect note` warned that each new item looks close to the previous ones; that is the shared opening sentence, and every item concerns a different segment. The seven pedagogy signals and the two Findings-block stumbles were raised on `item:pedagogy-home-for-lifted-audit-gold`.

**Two things worth knowing beyond the per-row verdicts.** First, five open questions have their answer written only in a Working Note (`disc-composition-consistency` WN 134, `hyp-directed-separation-under-composition` WN 108, `der-observability-dominance` WN 161, `scope-adaptive-system` WN 142, `def-chronica`'s ordinal-not-metric note behind body line 18).
