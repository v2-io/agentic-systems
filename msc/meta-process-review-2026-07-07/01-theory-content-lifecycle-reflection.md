# Theory-content-lifecycle — reflection (2026-07-07)

*One instance's orientation notes for cluster 01. Honest, not performative; overturn freely.*

## Before

I came in expecting the "Working Notes deluge" to be the usual entropy story: notes accrete because nobody prunes, prune them and move on. My prior was that this was a hygiene problem — untidy, but local, and fixable with a sweep.

## After

Two things changed my read.

First, the deluge is not primarily entropy — it is **a stalled pipeline plus a parked harvest**, and both have identifiable mechanisms. Two-thirds of the AAT Working-Notes volume (101K of 151K words) is one specific thing: the "Incidental audit gold" blocks lifted from the de-novo audits in the 2026-05-30 gold-lift sweep. That sweep was a *good* process — it rescued genuinely valuable cross-substrate ideation (candidate Briefs, figures, pedagogical glosses) from working dirs that would otherwise rot. But it stopped on 2026-05-31, four waves in, with wave 5 and all of TST/logogenic/logozoetic never done, and — more consequentially — the gold it *did* lift has no onward path. The blocks were supposed to graduate into each segment's Brief/Discussion. Zero have. So the WN deluge is, in large part, the visible residue of two mid-motion processes that lost their driver.

Second, the reason the deluge never *drains* is structural and upstream of anything about Working Notes: **the promotion ladder never reaches the stage that empties them.** Not one of 235 segments has reached `candidate` (or even `format-clean`). The whole corpus tops out at `claims-verified` (18 segments). Gate 4 — "Working Notes must be empty at candidate" — is the designed drain, and it has never once fired. So Working Notes are a monotonic accumulator by construction of the current process state. You cannot tidy your way out of that; the pipeline itself is the lever.

The thing I most want to hand forward: Joseph's framing ("a deluge we don't know the stats about and that's underrepresented in PRACTICA") is exactly right, and the stats make it sharper than a vibe. The gold is real and worth keeping — this is not a delete-it problem. It is a *route-it* problem: 122 segments hold harvested gold with no promotion tracker and no owner, while the mechanism that would consume it (promotion to candidate → Brief authoring) is dormant. The leverage is not a WN-pruning sweep; it is deciding whether promotion-to-candidate is a live process at all, and if so, giving the parked gold a home in that flow instead of beside it.

One caution on my own numbers: I counted mechanically (WN sections, word counts, signal-word greps). The *quality* judgments underneath the counts (is this note a real forward-pointer or vanity-changelog?) I spot-checked on a handful of segments, not all 224. Where I say "77 segments carry vanity-changelog language," read that as a grep signal to investigate, not a verified defect count.
