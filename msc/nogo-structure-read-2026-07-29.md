## 0. The short version, stated so you can stop reading here if it is not useful

There **is** genuine cross-cutting structure, and the framework has already found most of it. `#disc-identifiability-floor` (M1), `#disc-constructive-impossibility-posture` (the M1.5 style claim), `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor`, `#disc-implementation-impossibility`, and `#disc-anti-collapse` between them cover the large majority of what a fresh reader can see. I did not find a big unnamed thing sitting in plain sight, and I want to say that plainly before saying what I did find, because "there is one more meta-pattern" is exactly the conclusion this framework would like to be true.

What I did find is four things, in descending order of how confident I am:

1. **M1's mechanism taxonomy is misdescribed at the primary, in a way that a strengthening fixes rather than a softening.** M1's Discussion says the floors are *plural in mechanism* — a "rank-collapse subclass" $\{$Instance 1, 2, 4$\}$ unified by Sylvester's law of inertia, plus a projection/Schur member $\{$Instance 3$\}$. Read against the instance segments, that is a split in *how the escape is blocked*, not in *how the floor is proven*. Every one of the four floors — plus `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation`, plus `#disc-sandbox-evaluation-ceiling` — is proven the same way: **exhibit a witness**, two systems inducing the identical accessible law and disagreeing on the target. The plurality is real but sits one layer down. Separating the two layers makes the family *more* unified, not less, and it hands the framework a strengthening move it has run once (Result 4's genericity theorem) and could run at three more sites. §2, §3.
2. **A family the meta-architecture does not name: escape-set exhaustion.** A distinct class of AAT results whose load-bearing content is not an impossibility but a **count** — *there are exactly $k$ channels, here they are, here is why nothing else qualifies*. `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness` (exactly one under (FW), exactly two without), `#hyp-solicitable-escape` (exactly one solicitable route of three), `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor`'s two-routes-exhausts claim, `#der-architecture-noidentifiability`'s three-not-four escape count. These have a completely different failure mode from a floor (an unlisted channel, not a bad witness), and the framework has independently recognized that failure mode and prescribed the same remedy at three separate sites without noticing it was the same class. §4.
3. **A second family the meta-architecture does not name, and which is cross-volume: self-silencing (absorbing) floors.** Five results across Volumes 1 and 4 share a definable structural property — the failure state is one at which the repair-driving quantity itself vanishes, so the escape is necessarily exogenous and, in the sharp cases, un-targetable from inside. Volume 4 names a three-way version of this internally, in prose, inside `#der-captured-objective-dynamics` and `#der-severed-actuation-dynamics`. Volume 1's instance (`#der-observability-dominance`) is not connected to it, and `#hyp-solicitable-escape` — which lands today and supplies exactly the missing formal axis (targeted vs untargeted) — is not connected to it either. §5.
4. **Several look-alikes that are not kin, one of which is currently miscatalogued in canon.** Instances D and E of the constructive-impossibility posture (`#deriv-sector-condition` Cor A.1S.1; `#deriv-strategic-persistence-hard-ceiling`) are not information-regime impossibilities at all; they are *reachability caps* on an achievable set, and the posture's template visibly stretches to admit them. Also: `#deriv-graph-structure-uniqueness` is not a uniqueness result and says so in its own body, while its slug says otherwise. §6.

And one meta-observation that I think is the most useful thing in here, so I will put it up front rather than bury it: **the framework's discriminating criteria are better developed than its positive taxonomy.** `#disc-additive-coordinate-forcing`'s escape-count criterion, `#disc-identifiability-floor`'s actor-positioning filter, `#der-agent-opacity`'s twice-derived Fano verdict, `#disc-anti-collapse`'s tempting-wrong-merge diagnostic — these are a genuinely sharp set of tests for *what is not an instance of what*. They were each derived locally, in the segment where the exclusion had to be made. Collected, they are close to a decision procedure. §7 collects them.

## 2. The correction to M1: what the floors actually share is a witness, not a rank collapse

### The claim in canon

`#disc-identifiability-floor` §Discussion says the rank-collapse instances are those "whose floor is a rank-deficiency of an information operator — Instance 2 cleanly, Instance 1 structurally, and Instance 4 at one remove", and that "the per-instance 'no reparameterization removes the degeneracy' computations … are special cases of this single law." The Finding restates it: "Three of the four floors (Instances 1, 2, 4) belong to a *rank-collapse subclass* whose escape-irreducibility under coordinate change is Sylvester's law of inertia."

### What the instances actually contain

**Instance 1 (`#der-causal-insufficiency-detection`) contains no Fisher, rank, score, or inertia argument.** I grepped for all six terms; zero hits. Its derivation is: for any L1 world with latent common cause $C$, construct an explicit $\mathcal W_{L0}^\ast$ with edge probabilities matched to the on-policy regime conditionals such that $\mathbb P^{\text{obs}}(\mathcal W^{L1}) = \mathbb P^{\text{obs}}(\mathcal W^{L0\ast})$ under the L0 on-policy regime. That is a **witness-pair construction**: two worlds, one accessible law, disagreeing on a Level-2 target. The word "structurally" in M1's Discussion, and the accompanying assertion that "the causal-parameter block carries no score under a purely observational regime," are doing load-bearing work that the instance segment does not supply. I am not saying the Fisher statement is *false* — it is plausible and might well be derivable — I am saying it is currently an assertion in a meta-segment about a derivation that argues differently, which is the shape M1's own §Discussion warns against elsewhere.

**Instance 4 (`#der-architecture-noidentifiability`)** is scrupulous about this and is where I got the distinction. Its §Mechanism separates the **generating action** (state-space similarity $T(\cdot)T^{-1}$, which produces the indistinguishable pair) from the **escape-side action** (metric congruence $S^\top(\cdot)S$ on the observed Fisher, which is what Sylvester governs). Its floor is proven by exhibiting the pair; Sylvester enters only to close off reparameterization as an escape. The segment says exactly this — "the no-go therefore does **not** inherit Sylvester at the generating-action layer" — and M1 has not propagated the distinction into its own taxonomy language.

**Instance 3** (via `#deriv-critical-mass-composition` / `#deriv-regime-marginal-indistinguishability`) is, per M1's own no-go paragraph, "two symmetric-matched-Tier-1 scalar agents with coupling term $\gamma\mathcal T\cdot\text{sign}(\delta_{\bar i})$" that are consistent with both $\gamma = +\gamma_0$ and $\gamma = -\gamma_0$ — again a witness pair, generated by a $\mathbb Z/2$ action on the sign bit. The Schur-complement / memory-kernel story is a true and different statement, but it is about why a *certificate* does not survive projection, i.e. it lives on the escape/obstruction layer, alongside Sylvester rather than opposite it.

**Instance 2** is the one genuinely infinitesimal case. A rank-1 Fisher with a two-dimensional null space is the *local* form of a witness: instead of two models with equal law, a whole submanifold $\{\hat\phi : \hat\theta_C\hat p_{\mid C} + (1-\hat\theta_C)\hat p_{\mid\neg C} = \mu_j\}$ of models with equal law to second order. That is a witness curve rather than a witness pair.

**Outside M1, the same shape recurs and is stated even more explicitly.** `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation` Result 1 *is* a witness pair ($M_A, M_B$ with $M_A \equiv_3 M_B$ and $q(M_A)=1$, $q(M_B)=0$). `#disc-sandbox-evaluation-ceiling` §3 is a witness pair (Bareinboim–Pearl Fig. 1b, "two deployment models consistent with all sandbox data assign $R$ different values"). `#deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go` Lemma 1 is a witness pair ($\mu_{\text{dist}}$ vs $\mu_{\text{prox}}$, $L_1$-equivalent on protocol-honoring histories).

### The three-layer re-cut I would propose

| Layer | Question it answers | Uniform or plural | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Witness** | *Why is the target not determined?* | **Uniform** across every floor I read | Exhibit two (or a family of) systems inducing the identical accessible law and disagreeing on the target. Global (pair) or infinitesimal (Fisher null along a manifold). |
| **B — Obstruction** | *Why does no cleverness on the accessible side recover it?* | **Plural** | Sylvester/inertia where the agent's freedom is a congruence on an information operator; Schur/memory-kernel where the accessible law is a non-invertible projection; harmonic-triviality (unbounded scale function) for `#deriv-stochastic-non-exit`; **vacuous** where the witness gives exact equality of law, since equality needs no further obstruction. |
| **C — Escape set** | *What is left standing, and how many are there?* | **Plural, and epistemically distinct** | Rank-augmentation / new channel. The *count* is a separate claim with its own failure mode — see §4. |

Two things follow that I think are worth more than the tidying.

**(i) Layer A has a sub-split with a free theorem attached.** Witnesses come in two kinds. **A1, symmetry-generated:** the second system is the image of the first under a group acting on the unobserved coordinates — $\mathbb Z/2$ on the coupling sign (Instance 3), $GL(n)$ similarity (Instance 4), $\text{Aut}(U, P)$ measure-automorphism (`#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation` Result 4), and locally the mixture-indeterminacy manifold (Instance 2). **A2, matching-equation:** the second system is obtained by solving for parameters that reproduce the accessible law — Instance 1's $\mathcal W_{L0}^\ast$, Bareinboim–Pearl's two target models. The difference is not cosmetic: **an A1 witness upgrades from existence to genericity for free.** That is precisely the move `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation` Result 4 makes — "every anchor-mobile SCM disagrees with *its own relabeling*", with anchor-rigidity confined to inert or automorphism-free noise — and it is the strongest single result in the material I read, because it converts "there is a counterexample" into "essentially every model is a counterexample to itself."

That move has not been run for Instances 3 or 4, and it is available in both. For Instance 3 the group is $\mathbb Z/2$ acting on the coupling-sign bit; the genericity statement would read roughly *every composite whose marginals absorb the cross-term into bounded disturbance is indistinguishable from its own sign-flip*, and it would replace "there exist pairs" with a statement about the whole class. For Instance 4 the group is $GL(n)$ and arguably Kalman–Ho already *is* the genericity statement (every minimal realization sits in a nontrivial similarity orbit unless $n=1$), in which case the segment is currently understating what it has: it says "two agents … related by an invertible similarity produce identical summaries", which reads as a construction, when the honest reading is that *no* minimal realization of dimension $\gt 1$ is identifiable, generically. That is a strengthening available at zero mathematical cost — it is a re-statement, not a new proof — and per the strengthen-first discipline it should be tried before anything else in this document is acted on.

**(ii) Layer A gives the exclusion criterion the framework twice discovered empirically.** The framework has ruled out Fano-based floor candidates twice, from two different inferential tasks (`#der-agent-opacity` Working Notes; `#der-architecture-noidentifiability` §"Why Fano Is the Finite-Sample Refinement"), reaching the convergent verdict that Fano is "a continuous quantitative bound on $P_e$ given $H_b$, not a categorical structural no-go in the M1 sense." Layer A says why in one line: **a floor requires a witness with exactly equal accessible law; Fano bounds the case where the laws are close but unequal, and degenerates to vacuity exactly at the equality the floor needs.** Two spikes converged on a verdict that is a corollary of the criterion. That is the kind of convergence `#disc-constructive-impossibility-posture` already treats as evidence a pattern is in the framework.

## 3. Does this reduce M1 to a triviality?

The obvious objection, and the one an auditor already brought adversarially per M1's gold section: *"are the instances really structurally distinct, or four dressings of 'you can't get information you don't have'?"* M1's answer is the plurality-of-mechanism argument. If I move the plurality from Layer A to Layer B, does M1's answer survive?

I think yes, and more cleanly. The genuine content is not that the four floors are proven differently — they are not — it is:

- **Which coordinate the witness lives in.** Coupling sign, similarity orbit, latent parameterization, mixture manifold, reward-channel causation, deployment mechanism. These are genuinely different objects and the framework's contribution at each is the recognition that *this* is where AAT's degree of freedom sits (Instance 4's Novelty Claim says exactly this: the AAT-distinctive content is recognizing that the architectural d.o.f. "*is* the similarity orbit").
- **What blocks the escape (Layer B).** Genuinely plural, genuinely non-obvious, and one member — `#deriv-stochastic-non-exit`'s "the natural route provably cannot work" — is a *demonstrated* dead end rather than a cited theorem, which is the strongest epistemic form in the set.
- **Which channel restores it (Layer C).** Plural, and — as §4 argues — carrying a claim-type the framework has not isolated.

So the re-cut does not deflate M1; it relocates M1's distinctness argument onto firmer ground, because "these four are proven identically but obstructed differently and escaped differently" is a claim I can check against the primaries, whereas "Instance 1's floor is a rank deficiency, structurally" is one I cannot.

There is also a Layer-B observation worth noting because it makes the plurality less arbitrary. Sylvester's law and Khasminskii's unbounded-scale-function fact are the same *kind* of statement at different sites: **the space of available certificates/invariants is trivial.** Sylvester: the inertia of a quadratic form is invariant under the whole congruence orbit, so the number of flat directions cannot be changed by any reparameterization. Khasminskii: the only bounded harmonic functions of an additive-noise generator are the constants, so there is no non-constant certificate to run gambler's-ruin on. In both cases the obstruction is *"the invariant you want to construct does not exist in the available space, and here is the classical theorem that says the space is trivial."* Schur/memory-kernel is not of this kind — it says a certificate exists upstairs and fails to descend. I would call these **rigidity obstructions** (Sylvester, Khasminskii) versus a **projection obstruction** (Schur). That is a two-cell Layer-B taxonomy that includes `#deriv-stochastic-non-exit`, which M1 currently cannot classify at all because it is filed as a "boundary-facet operator-temporal dual" in the posture rather than as a floor.

## 4. Family $\alpha$ — escape-set exhaustion. A claim-type, not a floor

This is the finding I am most confident is genuinely uncovered, and it is the one that most changes what should be written into canon.

### The shape

Some AAT results are not impossibilities. Their load-bearing content is a **count**, arrived at by:

1. Enumerating a **closed set** of candidate channels — closed by an argument about the structure, not by a survey.
2. Eliminating members one at a time, each by an **independent principle**.
3. Reporting the survivors as **exactly $k$**.

### The instances

| Result | Enumeration and its closure argument | Elimination principle(s) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness` | The reconstruction map $f_{\text{init}}$ has **exactly three inputs** — store $\mathcal E_k$, prompt $p_{k+1}$, weights $M_0^{\text{w}}$ — closed by `#obs-context-turnover`'s architecture | DPI kills self-replay through $\mathcal E_k$; (FW)-measurability kills $M_0^{\text{w}}$ ($I(M_0^{\text{w}};Y\mid\mathcal E_k)=0$ for $Y$ singular to the individuated trajectory) | **1** under (FW); **2** when (FW) fails |
| `#hyp-solicitable-escape` | The three escapes `#der-observability-dominance` names, partitioned by whether the route requires prior localization of the deficit | Shock is unsolicitable (not an available action); self-instrumentation is *targeted* and the absorbing condition removes the targeting signal; blanket instrumentation dismissed under a budget bound | **1** |
| `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor` | The off-interface substrates: agent-side adaptive, principal-side protocol | Argued exhaustion of off-substrate axes; no route internal to $V_{O_t}$ | **2** |
| `#der-architecture-noidentifiability` §Boundary | Escapes indexed by which of the three scope conditions is violated — closure *by construction* from the no-go's own scope | Route (d) horizon-extension collapses into (a); route (b) provably void in the linear-Gaussian sub-scope | **3, not 4** |
| `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding` | Constructions (A), (B), (C) "exhaust the objective-side routes a $\Phi$ could come from" | (A) fails (R2); (B) inherits the Lemma 1/2 collision; (C) is the same collision one level up | **0** on the objective substrate |

### Why this is a different claim-type, and why it matters

A floor's failure mode is **a bad witness** — an error in a construction you can inspect. An exhaustion claim's failure mode is **an unlisted channel** — something outside the enumeration, which by construction you cannot inspect, because the enumeration is what you are trusting. These are not the same epistemic object and they do not defease the same way.

The framework knows this locally, at three separate sites, and prescribes the same remedy each time without noticing the repetition:

- `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor` Working Notes: *"Two-routes-exhausts adversarial spike queued (2026-05-28)"* — an audit tried three candidate third routes informally, found each collapses, "did not push hard", and a serious adversarial spike is queued at `spikes/PROPOSED.md` Tier 1. Note the promotion logic recorded there: *"if multiple attempts fail systematically, the claim could be promoted from `discussion-grade` toward `robust-qualitative` or `derived`."*
- `#hyp-solicitable-escape` Epistemic Status, **Falsifier**: *"show that some third mechanism supplies such a signal from inside the region, which would defeat the targeting premise directly."*
- `#der-architecture-noidentifiability`: the honest escape count is stated as three-not-four *because* a proposed fourth was tested and collapsed — the discipline running correctly in the small.

That is the same falsifier, the same promotion criterion, and the same class of adversarial work, arrived at independently three times. **That convergence is the argument that the class is real** — it is exactly the convergence-as-coherence-evidence condition `#disc-constructive-impossibility-posture` and `#disc-anti-collapse` both invoke for their own instance sets.

### Where it sits relative to what is already named

Not in M1: an exhaustion claim's headline is the count, and M1's own instances explicitly *decline* to claim their escape lists are complete ("five routes", "three repair routes"). Not in the posture: the posture's move 2 says "the *unique broadly-available* machinery", which sounds like this but is doing different work — there the escape is the *payload* of a floor that carries the result; in family $\alpha$ the count *is* the result and the floor is the setup. Not in M3: `#disc-additive-coordinate-forcing` already draws the escape-count line, and draws it as a **binary** — floors carve $\geq 2$ escapes, coordinate-forcing has exactly one *because an external uniqueness theorem forbids a second*. Family $\alpha$ is the missing third cell: **exactly $k$ escapes, established by internal enumerate-and-eliminate rather than by an external uniqueness theorem.** That is why `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness` — a Volume 4 result whose whole content is a channel count — appears in none of the Volume 1 meta-segments' catalogs.

### The one thing I would want checked before believing this

Is the closure of each enumeration actually load-bearing, or is it decorative? For `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness` it is clearly load-bearing and clearly sound: "exactly three inputs" is a fact about $f_{\text{init}}$, and the proposition is a two-line DPI argument once you have it. For `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor` the closure is *argued* ("there are no other axes a non-degenerate non-pathological agent can be grounded on") and is the weakest link in the set — which its own Working Notes say. For `#hyp-solicitable-escape` the closure is inherited from `#der-observability-dominance`'s escape list, which was written as an illustrative list, not as a closed enumeration — see §8 on that.

## 5. Family $\beta$ — self-silencing floors, and the axis `#hyp-solicitable-escape` just supplied

### The instances

`absorbing` appears as a load-bearing structural claim in exactly five places across the four `src/` trees (I grepped; the other hits are the word used incidentally, e.g. "absorbing the cross-term"):

| `#der-observability-dominance` | 1 | Strategy investment routed through unobservable nodes | Edge gain $\eta_{\text{edge}} \to 0$ as $U_{\text{obs}} \to \infty$; no mismatch signal is generated | Shock; self-instrumentation; another observer |

| `#hyp-solicitable-escape` | 1 | — | — | *analysis of the first row's escape set* |

### What they share, stated so it is checkable

Write the failure coordinate's dynamics as drift minus repair, $\dot x = d(x) - r(x)$ (or its discrete/reflected analogue). The family is: **results in which $r$ vanishes at the failure state, so the failure state is a fixed point of the repair dynamics itself.** The consequence is not "recovery is hard"; it is "recovery cannot be driven by the mechanism that drives recovery everywhere else," hence every escape is exogenous.

Volume 4 has already recognized a three-way version of this, in prose, twice, in the same words: `#der-severed-actuation-dynamics` — *"in both, the terminal form is a state-dependent silencing of the very channel recovery would arrive through"* — and `#der-captured-objective-dynamics`, extending it to three ("structurally parallel to severed actuation's learned-helplessness fixed point and relational death's absorbing barrier — in all three…"). So the recognition exists. What does not exist:

- **The Volume 1 instance is not connected to it.** I checked cross-citations: nothing in `04-eli-core/src/` cites `#der-observability-dominance`. The only cross-volume citation is from `03-llm-core/src/result-section-ii-survival.md`, on an unrelated axis. `#der-observability-dominance` is the framework's *earliest and most general* instance of the shape and is invisible to the family that would most benefit from it.
- **`#hyp-solicitable-escape`'s axis is not connected to it.** That segment supplies the formal distinction the family needs and does not have: **targeted vs untargeted escape routes**, where a route is targeted if exercising it requires identifying in advance where the deficit is. Applied to the table above this is immediately productive: severed actuation's *communicated restoration* is untargeted (the attestation arrives; the agent need not know where its impotence lies); *vicarious contrast* is untargeted; captured objective's *out-of-band intent channel* is untargeted; identity re-grounding is untargeted. **Every named escape in the family is untargeted and exogenous, and that is not a coincidence — it is forced.** If the repair coefficient vanishes at the failure state, then any escape whose availability is conditioned on the agent's own read of the repair-relevant quantity is unavailable there by construction. `#hyp-solicitable-escape` proves this for one instance from the meaning of instrumenting a named node; the general statement looks like a short argument, and it would convert four independent "the escape is relational" observations into one derived consequence.

### Is it the same in kind or merely analogous? — the honest answer

**Plural in mechanism, uniform in a definable structural property.** The four instances vanish for four unrelated reasons: an observation-noise limit driving a gain to zero; an exploration-collapse under a *named policy class* (and the segment is careful that a Bayes agent with a nonstationarity prior escapes with probability 1 — so this instance's absorbing property is class-conditional, the weakest of the four); an $L_1$-equivalence, i.e. a Layer-A witness doing the work; and a state-dependent coefficient degeneracy from the cohort's side. Nothing is shared at the derivation level.

That sounds like a fatal objection until you notice it is **exactly the epistemic shape M1 has and defends**: plural in mechanism, unified as a presentational family, with the plurality itself named as load-bearing. If M1 earns its keep on those terms, so does this — by the framework's own standard. And this family carries something M1's does not: a **shared design directive**, independently derived at two sites. `#der-identity-continuity-threshold` Working Notes: *"mean-drift certification is necessary but not sufficient once the barrier is state-dependent"* — barrier reachability, not drift sign, is the operative certification. `#der-severed-actuation-dynamics` (6b): exploration floors are the architectural defense, and abdication is worse precisely because no policy-class floor reaches it. Those are the same directive twice.

There is also a sub-split I would want in any landing, because it is checkable and it changes the prescription. In `#der-observability-dominance` and `#der-captured-objective-dynamics` the repair is **regionally zero** — $r \equiv 0$ on a region, fixed by a structural parameter ($\sigma_v$; the (R3) sole-channel condition). In `#der-severed-actuation-dynamics` and `#der-identity-continuity-threshold` the repair **vanishes as a function of state** at a barrier the system can drift into. Regionally-zero traps are entered by *placement* (design or environment puts you there) and the defense is architectural; state-dependent barriers are entered by *drift* and the defense is reachability-bounding. Conflating them would produce the wrong prescription in both directions.

### Where this sits relative to the deaths taxonomy

`TODO.md` line 157 item (c) tracks "per-death terminal forms — reversibility analogues for (D1)/(D4)". So the framework's own tracker treats the terminal-form work as a **per-death completeness project inside Volume 4**. On my read that framing is one level too low: the object is not a property of deaths, it is a property of a class of AAT floors, of which the deaths' terminal forms are the Volume 4 instances and `#der-observability-dominance` is the Volume 1 instance. If the general statement holds — repair-coefficient-vanishing $\Rightarrow$ escapes are exogenous and untargetable — then (D1) and (D4)'s terminal forms are *predicted* rather than separately derived, which is a considerably better position than working them one at a time.

## 6. Look-alikes that are not kin

**`#deriv-strategic-persistence-hard-ceiling` (posture Instance E) and `#deriv-sector-condition` Cor A.1S.1 (Instance D) are not information-regime impossibilities.** E computes $\sup_{\lambda\in[0,1]} \alpha_\Sigma^{\text{ss}}(\lambda) = 1/2$ and concludes the reachable region is the open half-plane $\rho_\Sigma \lt R_\Sigma/2$. There is no information regime, no inference task, no actor frustrated by not knowing something, and no escape-by-augmentation — the "escape" is *change architecture class*. D establishes $P(\tau_R \lt \infty)=1$ and that the achievable value set is the two-point set $\{0,1\}$ selected by the disturbance's support structure. Both are **caps on an achievable set** — a $\sup$ over a design parameter, and the range of an achievable functional. The posture's own move-1 template betrays the stretch in its own wording: *"an external theorem … or — in the strategic-persistence cap case — a sharp algebraic supremum derived from the framework's own update mechanics."* That disjunct is a seam.

I would read the posture's catalog as **two families under one style**: information-floors (A, B, C, F, G) and reachability caps (D, E, plus the NeurIPS $\mathcal A_{\text{decay}}$ class theorem, plus `#result-structural-adaptation-necessity`, which is the same shape — no parametric adaptation can reach below a class floor, so change the class). That reading is not a demotion; the reachability caps are arguably the more *actionable* results, and grouping them would give `#result-structural-adaptation-necessity` a family it currently lacks — the posture's Instance E already calls itself "the canonical strategic-layer analog" of it, and D's Discussion says the same, so the framework has drawn the edge twice without drawing the family.

**`#deriv-graph-structure-uniqueness` is not a uniqueness result.** Its own body is exemplary about this: "Cox's theorem is necessary-and-sufficient…; this result is sufficient only", and "claims that AAT's strategy *must* be a DAG should be read as 'must-if-sufficient-via-this-route,' not as a proved necessity." The slug says `uniqueness`. Given the standalone-citability criterion, a reader who greps the slug and cites it will overclaim, and the body's honesty will not travel with the citation. This is a look-alike for family $\alpha$ specifically — it enumerates P1–P4 and shows they suffice, without eliminating alternatives — and the missing necessity direction is exactly the enumerate-and-eliminate step.

**`#result-specification-bound`** looks like an impossibility ("you cannot implement faster than you can transmit the distinguishing information") and is near-definitional given premises (S1)–(S2), which its Epistemic Status says. It is a transmission bound, not a floor: it has no witness pair, no escape set, and its own body marks the $H_{\text{req}}/R_{\text{spec}}$ line as Formulation. Correctly not in any catalog.

**The heteroscedastic-Gaussian no-go** is already correctly excluded from M1, with the sharpest exclusion argument in the corpus (`#disc-additive-coordinate-forcing`: floors cite theorems that *forbid*, this cites a theorem that *forces*; a uniqueness theorem on the escape side is logically incompatible with the $\geq 2$-escapes structure). I mention it only because that argument is the seed of §7.

**`#hyp-behavioral-self-knowledge-insufficiency`** is a genuine kin of `#disc-law-discovery-ceiling` — its core is the same latent-anchored/PO-specified boundary read at the self-model — and it is correctly wired. Worth noting only that its load-bearing premise ("self-access is Level-3-class, not white-box") is a *witness-availability* claim in Layer-A terms: it asserts that self-instrumentation stays on the outputs side, hence the witness pair survives. Framed that way, the premise's adversarial examination (which its Working Notes hold, deliberately, pending Joseph) has a sharper target: exhibit an architecture for which the witness pair collapses.

## 7. The exclusion criteria, collected

These are the framework's own, each derived locally where the exclusion had to be made. Collected, they are close to a decision procedure for "is this candidate an instance of that family," and I think collecting them would be worth more to a reader than another meta-segment.

1. **Actor position** (`#disc-identifiability-floor` on the mechanism-design cluster; `#disc-value-functional-grounding-floor` on Skalse 2022). Who is frustrated — agent, principal, designer? Determines which of the three clusters, and it is *categorical*, not a matter of degree.
2. **Remedy kind** (same sources). Information augmentation, or design-constraint relaxation? Floors take the first; implementation-impossibilities the second.
3. **Direction of the cited theorem** (`#disc-additive-coordinate-forcing`). Does the external theorem *forbid the task* or *force the escape*? Opposite structural roles.
4. **Escape count** (same). $\geq 2$ structurally distinct escapes $\Rightarrow$ floor. Exactly one *because a uniqueness theorem forbids a second* $\Rightarrow$ coordinate-forcing. Exactly $k$ *because an internal enumeration was exhausted* $\Rightarrow$ family $\alpha$ (my addition).
5. **Categorical vs continuous** (`#der-agent-opacity` + `#der-architecture-noidentifiability`, the twice-derived Fano verdict). Is there a witness with *equal* accessible law, or only a bound on how close two laws can be? Equal $\Rightarrow$ floor; close $\Rightarrow$ finite-sample refinement.
6. **Tempting wrong merge** (`#disc-anti-collapse`). Does a plausible reading conflate two repair-distinct things? If not, it is an ordinary definition, not an anti-collapse instance.
7. **Genericity available?** (my addition, from `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation` Result 4). Is the witness symmetry-generated (A1) or matching-equation (A2)? A1 admits a free upgrade from existence to genericity; A2 does not.

## 8. Epistemic-label observations

Ordered by how much I think they matter, and separated from everything above because these are ordinary findings, not structure.

**(a) `#der-observability-dominance`'s absorbing mechanism conflates two arguments, and `#hyp-solicitable-escape` inherits the weaker one.** The segment says: "frozen beliefs mean no mismatch signal, no mismatch signal means no reason to revise." But the derivation it actually carries is $\eta_{\text{edge}} = U_{\text{edge}}/(U_{\text{edge}}+U_{\text{obs}}) \to 0$ as $U_{\text{obs}} \to \infty$ — a *gain* collapse, not a *signal* absence. These are different claims with different consequences: if the signal exists and the gain is zero, an agent with a variance monitor could in principle detect a persistently-unexplained residual; if no signal is generated at all, it cannot. `#hyp-solicitable-escape`'s targeting premise needs the *stronger* (signal-absence) reading — "the region does not present as a gap" — and `#der-observability-dominance` does not derive it. Its own audit-gold reader-question section asks exactly this ("Seems to require a meta-cognitive monitor on belief *variance*"), and `#hyp-solicitable-escape` answers in the negative by pointing back at the absorbing condition — which is circular unless the signal-absence reading is established. I think this is the load-bearing gap in `#hyp-solicitable-escape` and it is more consequential than either of the two promotion steps that segment names. It is also fixable: at $\sigma_v \approx 0$ the agent cannot observe whether $v$ was achieved, so $\delta$ for edges touching $v$ is not computable — that *is* signal absence, and it is one sentence from the definition of $\sigma_v$. Landing it would make the targeting premise derived rather than asserted, which is one of the two promotion steps.

**(b) `#der-observability-dominance`'s escape list is being used as a closed enumeration, and was not written as one.** "Escape requires external shock, proactive observability investment, or another agent whose observations cover the blind spot" reads as illustrative. `#hyp-solicitable-escape` partitions it as exhaustive and reports a count of one. That is a legitimate move only if the three are closed, and neither segment argues closure. This is family $\alpha$'s characteristic vulnerability appearing in the newest instance. A closure argument is plausible — every escape must break the gain-zero condition, and the ways to do that are enumerable from the gain expression's arguments — which would be the cleaner route and would generalize (it is the same move `#der-compensation-channel-uniqueness` makes off $f_{\text{init}}$'s three inputs, and the same move `#der-architecture-noidentifiability` makes off its three scope conditions). **That parallelism is itself evidence for family $\alpha$: three segments, three different closure-by-construction arguments, same template.**

**(c) `#disc-identifiability-floor`'s Sylvester Finding is overstated in one specific place, and the fix is a strengthening.** "Three of the four floors (Instances 1, 2, 4) belong to a rank-collapse subclass" — Instance 1's membership is unsupported at the primary (§2). The honest current statement is *two* computed rank-collapse instances (2, and 4 which reduces to 2), with Instance 1's rank reading an open conjecture. But per strengthen-first the move is not to soften the Finding: it is to try to *derive* the Fisher statement for Instance 1 (does the on-policy score for the causal-parameter block actually vanish in the $\mathcal W_{L1}$/$\mathcal W_{L0}^\ast$ construction?). If it derives, the Finding is correct and Instance 1 gains a second mechanism-level result. If it does not, the honest landing is the three-layer re-cut of §2, which is *stronger* than what is currently claimed because Layer A is uniform across all four rather than covering three.

**(d) `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation` at `exact` is, if anything, under-labeled relative to its content.** Not the tier — `exact` is right — but the *framing*. Its Epistemic Status buries a genericity theorem, a reducibility boundary, a non-collapse result, and a primary-verified clean priority search inside one dense paragraph, and the Discussion's "Level 4" caution then further dampens it. Result 4 plus its corollary ("latent-anchored content is exactly a commitment to coordinates on the latent space — real content, invisible to all three layers, supplied only by the committed representation") is the sharpest thing I read in this corpus, and the standalone-note decision sitting in the ops queue reads to me as the highest-value item touching any of this.

**(e) `#der-severed-actuation-dynamics` Result 4's absorbing claim is honestly class-conditional and should stay flagged that way in any family landing.** The segment says so plainly (greedy/exploration-free; a Bayes agent with a nonstationarity prior escapes w.p. 1) and it is the weakest of the four family-$\beta$ instances precisely because the absorbing property is a property of a policy class rather than of the dynamics. A family landing that levels the four would be an overclaim; the sub-split in §5 is partly there to keep this visible.

**(f) One naming item.** `#deriv-graph-structure-uniqueness` (§6). Also, and much more minor: `#hyp-solicitable-escape`'s own Working Notes flag `solicitable` as possibly not surviving standalone-citability review. On my read the word earns its place — the distinction it carries (choosable-as-an-action vs merely-existing) is the load-bearing one and I did not find a better single word for it — but I would not weight my aesthetic judgment much here.
