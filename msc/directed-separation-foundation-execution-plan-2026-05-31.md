## 2. The calibrated normativity — three guards (verbatim, load-bearing)

The classes index *positions of one coupling distinguished by what is certifiable about it*, not a ranking of architectural virtue. But the typology *does* carry a real, **earned** directional preference, and the framework should neither smuggle it nor deny it nor over-lean it. Three guards, each against a different failure:

- **Guard 1 — derive, do not declare.** Less coupling → strictly better reality-tracking, all else equal, is *true and derivable* ($M_t$'s job is to track reality; a positive $\kappa$ lets the goal bend the belief-update — motivated reasoning — which, all else equal, is pure degradation: it pulls $M_t$ off the truth, worsens action, and diverts correction-gain from reality-tracking so the persistence margin shrinks). Because it is derivable, the canon may *state* it — but only where it **rides a derivation or an $\varepsilon(\kappa)$ bound**, never as a bare assertion, and never as "Class 1 is the clean architecture."
- **Guard 2 — the word carries it; do not over-lean.** "Causally-disciplined" is itself enough of a directional nudge. Do **not** build a "causal discipline dominates" headline thesis (it would re-create the cheerleading problem from the other side and would need every all-else-equal / cost caveat hung off it). The $\varepsilon(\kappa)$ bounds earn the direction quietly, result by result.
- **Guard 3 — certifiability is not purity.** Class 1 and an idealized Class 2 at $\kappa = 0$ are **equally** causally-disciplined: same zero, same reality-tracking, same performance at that point. Class 1's distinction is the **modal status** of the discipline (structural → provable and perturbation-stable), *not more discipline*. "By construction" must read as *certifiable*, never as *cleaner / purer*. **State this equality at the introduction of the concept** — it is the same statement that makes the noun work (results scope to the shared $\kappa = 0$ object equally; the certificate is the orthogonal axis).

The honest two-handed claim the canon stands on: *more discipline → strictly better tracking, all else equal; and discipline is not free* (the wrapping construction pays a Brooks's-Law tempo overhead to hold the separation), *which is why the spectrum exists — coupled agents are a genuine cost/capability tradeoff, not a failure of virtue.* Both hands are true; state both.

Environment lessons learned this cycle: zsh does **not** word-split a bare `$variable` in a `for` loop (use literal paths, `bash -c`, or `$(… | …)` command-substitution, which *does* split); **backticks inside a double-quoted `git commit -m` are command-substituted** by zsh and silently eat the wrapped word (use `git commit -F <file>` for backtick-heavy messages); `sed -i` / `perl -pi` silently no-op on repo files (use Edit/Write, verify by re-read).
- **Commit granularity** — one commit per logical unit (preserve per-segment blame); the lead commits (agents do not run git); state the batching before executing.

## 6. Exposures to watch (from the proposal's honest §6)

1. **The noun could be a distinction without a downstream difference** if it turns out almost every "Separated" result genuinely needs the certificate. *Resolution:* the §4.3 case-by-case reading + the $\varepsilon(\kappa)$ attempts *are* the test; a successful $\varepsilon(\kappa)$ bound confirms the result-at-the-noun is the right object. And even if few results need only the zero, the noun still corrects a real truth-defect in the scope labels. *Watch:* do not assume the payoff; read each derivation.
2. **"Certifiability is a step, behaviour continuous" rests on one-bit toy sims** with an open amplification edge (a cross-call state that controls gain or accumulates could show threshold/super-linear behaviour). *Resolution:* attach the continuity claim only to the certificate-validity step and the exact behaviour-identity-at-the-limit (those are arithmetic, solid); never imply the leak is always continuous past the boundary.
3. **The form-matters guard and the unification pull in tension** — a future trimmer could let the clean unification swallow the guard, re-committing $\kappa$-as-scalar from the other direction (the error Joseph named). *Resolution:* keep the guard physically in the same paragraph as the unification.
