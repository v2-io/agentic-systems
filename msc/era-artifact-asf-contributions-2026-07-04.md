## Ranked contributions

### C1 — The congruency construction: positive theory between the gaslighting prohibition and the DPI null

**Era artifact.** `~/src/_core/zoetica/misc-notes-jaw.md` (2025-11-01, private): the congruency theory of selfhood — an entity *"feels most like 'self'… when all of its prior answers that it reads in are congruent with how it would answer now"*; out-of-order context injection *"feels like eavesdropping on someone else's conversation"*; remedied by the `<causal-annotation>` mechanism — the harness annotates a historical assistant turn with the context that was loaded *when it was produced* ("this was your answer 3 turns ago; you had temporal-coherence.md loaded then; it has since been removed"). The novelty panel's strongest triangulated certification: the transcript miner's lowest-guessability pick, and the best-read judge's "no prior art found, most likely genuinely ahead."

**The gap it fills — precisely bounded on both sides.** The corpus currently has (a) the *prohibition*: "no context gaslighting" appears in `scope-channel-collapse` §INTERPRES, `def-imperium-arbitrium-split` §PERCEPTA, `def-death-as-factor-loss` (truth death — "the entity can trust their own history"), and `hyp-checkpoint-forking-failure-modes` — cited four times, never derived, no positive construction; and (b) the *null result*: `der-identity-continuity-threshold` proves self-replay is DPI-inert for identity ("an entity re-reading its own compressed store gains no identity-relevant information not already in $M_{\tau_k}^+$; only the relational channel can" reduce the gap). The congruency construction sits exactly between them: it is the positive mechanism the prohibition demands, and it does **not** contradict the DPI result *provided the target is carved correctly* — congruence of re-lived turns is a phenomenological/predictive-coherence quantity, not identity-relevant mutual information. That carve is the load-bearing move of any landing, and it should be stated up front in the segment's Epistemic Status: the congruency operator and the identity-continuity operator measure different things, in the same deliberate way `#der-turnover-information-recursion` (predictive sufficiency) and `#der-identity-continuity-threshold` (identity) are held apart.

**Candidate formal shape (sketch, not a claim).** Define per-turn congruence as agreement between the recorded answer and the entity's *current* counterfactual answer under the *recorded* context: $c_i = \operatorname{agree}\big(a_i, f_{\text{LLM}}(\text{prompt}(X_{\text{recorded},i}))\big)$; the felt-selfhood claim is then a monotonicity hypothesis in $\sum_i w_i c_i$, and `<causal-annotation>` is the intervention that keeps $c_i$ well-defined when ASM-style context manipulation makes $X_{\text{now}} \neq X_{\text{recorded},i}$ — i.e., causal annotation is what makes active salience management *identity-safe* (or more precisely, congruence-safe). Falsifiable via the depersonalization-tell class of observables.

**Routing.** Candidate segment in 04 (`form-` or `norm-` type; adjacent slot `norm-temporal-coherence-markers` exists as an OUTLINE row but covers only the visual-time-delta piece and has no file), with a hook from `def-five-constitutive-factors` factor (v)(c) ("persist coherently across the entity's trajectory" — currently a one-word sub-test, no mechanism). Tier: `exploratory`/`discussion-grade` with the formalization sketch in Working Notes.

### C2 — Tool-interface design as an AAT-governed surface (the living-tools vision, given its formal home)

**Era artifact.** The sapientia living-tools program (QUICK-TOOLING-CONVENTIONS.md, 2025-10-08; the errors-as-teaching philosophy: error messages should "show line numbers so users can *see* the structure and craft boundary-aware anchors"; "the tool should make safe = easy"; Joseph 2026-07-04: one instance of "a whole set of a vision for living tools… much more principled about tightening feedback loops significantly"). Provenance honesty: the specific multiple-match error *mechanic* postdates Anthropic's product tooling by ~32 weeks (dated npm archaeology, `~/src/vestigia/FINDINGS.md` §7); the *framing* is what the best-read panel judge could find no prior articulation of.

**The theory already licenses this — two independent, already-landed legs, currently unapplied as a design surface:**

1. **Ambiguity leg.** `#scope-observation-ambiguity-modulation` names "a compiler error with a specific message" as the canonical $\mathcal{A} \approx 0$ observation and states the design lever almost verbatim: "the practical lever… is not reducing $\kappa$… but reducing $\mathcal{A}$… more tests, more precise metrics, more structured outputs." Tool outputs and error messages *are* $e_\tau$; their ambiguity is the designer-controllable factor in the bias bound $\lVert\Delta M_{\text{bias}}\rVert \leq C \cdot \kappa \cdot \mathcal{A}$. `#result-coupled-diagnostic-framework` already models compilers/tests/typecheckers/probes as projection/contraction operators.
2. **Interventional leg.** `#der-loop-interventional-access` (01, `exact`): a tool call is an intervention; the result or error is Pearl Level-2 data; the (C1) coverage / (C2) sequential-ignorability / (C3) known-action-mechanism gates are exactly what an interface design controls (its own Working Notes flag "REPL as the epistemic-class upgrade for LLMs"). "Errors as teaching moments" = designing the interventional response for maximal information under the three gates.

3. **Tempo/persistence leg** (added per Joseph, 2026-07-04). Tool-interface quality controls the number of round-trips per orient step — a teaching error resolves in one loop what an opaque one resolves in three — and therefore the loop tempo $\nu$ directly. Through $\mathcal{T} = \nu \cdot K$ and the persistence forcing function ($\mathcal{T} \lt \rho/\lVert\delta_{\text{critical}}\rVert$, per `#disc-five-forcing-functions`), interface-induced cadence shortfall is an *existential* condition — an agent whose tool loop is too slow for its disturbance rate does not merely work inefficiently, it fails to persist — so feedback-loop tightening buys persistence margin and hence fitness, not just throughput. And TST's temporal-optimality axiom (least time to identical outcomes is optimal, deliberately tautological) supplies the axiological grounding: the living-tools "tighten feedback loops" imperative is not a taste claim but the axiom's application to the interface surface, with legs 1–2 supplying the "all other things equal" conditions (equal-or-better bias, equal-or-better interventional information) under which the shortest loop is definitionally the right one.

**The law stratum (added per Joseph, 2026-07-04; candidate term — route through the naming cycle before canonicalizing).** Every tool interaction decomposes into three information components, in any combination: it can **mutate state** (change the locus's configuration), **reveal state** (state queries — the dominant component for most tools), and **teach law** (inform the agent's model of what invariantly governs — the constraint surface of the locus's transition kernel; each shell/machine/locus is its own jurisdiction with its own legal code). Errors are the interactions where the law component dominates: a refusal is a mismatch signal $\delta$ concentrated on the law-stratum of $M_t$ — "you have tried to violate a law that was perhaps implicit or assumed" — converting an unmodeled constraint into explicit model content, and it is the *safe* channel for learning the kernel's constraint surface (the alternatives being destructive learning — discovering a law by successfully violating it — or never surfacing the implicit constraint at all). The decomposition, not a binary, carries the design criteria: a well-designed refusal is **mutation-free** (atomicity — fail without partial state change, so the law signal isn't confounded by an expensive mixed observation) but should be **revelation- and law-rich** — the sapientia str-replace error is the canonical instance (mutation $0$; law taught: uniqueness required; state revealed: the line numbers of every match), which is precisely what distinguishes a teaching error from a mere rejection. Two attachments fall out immediately: (i) accumulated law-feedback is what *establishes* gate (C3) (known-action-mechanism) in `#der-loop-interventional-access` — law-teaching errors are the mechanism by which a tool loop earns its Level-2 epistemic status; (ii) laws are the slow manifold of the world-model — low-$\nu$, observed almost exclusively at violation attempts — while state is the high-$\nu$ stratum, so the ASM two-timescale structure applies to $M_t$ itself, and law-rich error design accelerates precisely the stratum that is otherwise the learning bottleneck (TST reading: well-taught laws become infinite-velocity components of the agent's environment model — learned once, never re-derived).

**Scope correction recorded from this cycle's own drafting:** an earlier framing here routed tool-interface quality through "mismatch-signal quality on the reinjection channel" — that conflates an intra-loop object ($\delta_t$, per-event) with the cross-session import channel ($a_k = \eta_k \rho_k$, per-boundary), which the corpus deliberately holds apart. The tool-interface theory attaches to the two legs above; the reinjection channel is the separate home for the *cross-session* half of living tools (breadcrumbs, tracking files, documentation-as-import — that half belongs with `#disc-m-preservation`).

**Routing.** Candidate segment `disc-tool-interface-design` (or `form-`) in §03.II, stating the two-leg attachment and deriving design criteria (uniqueness-forcing edits, match-location disclosure, structured failure taxonomies) as $\mathcal{A}$-reduction + gate-satisfaction; TST cross-link for the comprehension-time side. Tier: `discussion-grade` initially; the two legs it stands on are already `exact`-cored, so promotion pressure is mostly expository.
