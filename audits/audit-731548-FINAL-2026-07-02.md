## §B — Findings under burden of proof

### B-1 — The persistence condition is stated as "iff"; only-if is unproven and false in general. **[High visibility / medium consequence · verified REAL-AND-OPEN, not a regression]**

**Passage.** `deriv-sector-condition` Prop A.1 closes "The agent persists (from within $\mathcal B_R$) iff $R^\ast \lt R$, i.e., iff $\alpha \gt \rho/R$"; echoed as the headline in `result-sector-condition-stability` and `result-persistence-condition` ("when this inequality fails, mismatch grows without effective bound").

**The defect.** The *if* direction is Lyapunov-proven. The *only-if* (necessity) is nowhere proven and is false under the sector generalization, because $\alpha$ is a worst-case floor over $\mathcal B_R$ that can be slack along the actual escape directions. Counterexample (verified independently, re-derived and numerically simulated by the verifier): $F(\delta)=\alpha\delta$ for $\lVert\delta\rVert\le\varepsilon$, $=B\delta$ for $\lVert\delta\rVert\gt\varepsilon$, with $B\ge\rho/\varepsilon$ and $\alpha\lt\rho/R$; canonical sector constant on $\mathcal B_R$ is $\alpha$ (condition fails) yet the trajectory is held in $\mathcal B_\varepsilon$ forever for all admissible $\lVert w\rVert\le\rho$ (with $\alpha\lt\rho/R$). Only-if *does* hold in the linear/tight case $F=\mathcal T\delta$ — which is where it was minted (TFT-era `old-tf-11` states the linear iff and asserts, unproven, that it "becomes necessary" nonlinearly; the sector generalization inherited the word).

**Counterevidence search.** The verifier found: `deriv-sector-condition`'s own Derived-vs-Chosen table has **no necessity row** (it claims only "ultimate bound, Proved (conditional on $R^\ast\lt R$)"); no spike/audit/CHANGELOG/Working-Note ever touched necessity; prior auditors repeated the iff approvingly (the "verified flawlessly" gold is accurate for the *algebra*); nothing downstream consumes the only-if. **Not a regression.**

**Status: still real. Confidence: high** (counterexample independently reproduced). **Type: quantifier-register defect.** **Disposition: strengthen-by-split**, per exact precedent AF-2 (`fb51ff9`, 2026-04-25 — "iff strengthened, not softened"): state sufficiency; add a class-tightness lemma (over the Lur'e uncertainty class the certificate condition *is* necessary-and-sufficient for the *certificate* — extremal member $F=\alpha\delta$ + outward $w$ — a ~5-line non-tautological theorem that answers "when is persistence also necessary?": tightness, not linearity); fix the three register sites (worst: `result-persistence-condition`'s unhedged "grows without effective bound"); land the counterexample as a demonstrated remark per math-lives-in-segments.

### B-2 — Floor-accounting: "reducible" mislabel + a lost canonical distinction. **[Verified REAL-AND-OPEN, and stronger than reported]**

**Passage.** `result-mismatch-decomposition` labels its two-term identity model-error (**"reducible"**) + observation-noise ("irreducible").

**The defect.** The model-error term is *not fully reducible*: even the Bayes-optimal in-class predictor retains $\ge \mathbb E[\mathrm{Var}(\bar o_t\mid\mathcal C_{t-1},a_{t-1})]$ — a state-uncertainty (Bayes) floor, positive whenever residual state uncertainty moves the one-step conditional mean. Three floors, not two: estimation-suboptimality (reducible) / Bayes floor (irreducible-by-modeling, movable-by-*acting* — the CIY hook) / channel noise (irreducible). Notably the derivation's step 1 *cites* the scope condition $H(\Omega_t\mid\mathcal C_t)\gt 0$ and then never uses it — and that unused fact is exactly what breaks "reducible."

**Counterevidence search (decisive, from the verifier).** A 2026-04-24 spike (`spike-rho-additive-variance-strengthening`) drew the boundary *correctly* (Bayes-optimal floor, incl. this floor); a later recheck spike (landed as `#internal-external-decomposition`) **silently redefined** $\rho_\star^2$ down to channel-instant variance while keeping the "even the Bayes-optimal predictor cannot remove" gloss — both false as stated — and `spikes/INDEX.md` marks the family "FULLY RESOLVED 2026-05-21." Confirmed against `#example-kalman` (optimal filter innovation variance $HP^-H^\top+R \gt R$; the excess is the Bayes floor sitting inside "reducible"). **So this finding restores a distinction the working layer had and then lost — a regression in the canon, not in the audit.** One correction to the auditor: "$\gt 0$ exactly when $H(\Omega_t\mid\mathcal C_t)\gt 0$" is too strong — $H\gt 0$ is necessary, not sufficient; the alignment qualifier (already owned by the segment) is needed.

**Status: still real (stronger). Confidence: high. Disposition: strengthen** to an exact three-term Pythagorean decomposition (both cross-terms vanish via tower + GA-1), which makes step 1's unused citation load-bearing; **reopen** the `INDEX.md` "FULLY RESOLVED" row for the $\rho_\star$ definitional reconciliation; propagate the corrected floor gloss to `#internal-external-decomposition`. **Companion (part 2):** `def-model-class-fitness`'s detection signature ("persistent mismatch despite learning ⇒ low class fitness") conflates the class ceiling with the noise floor (an agent at $S=\mathcal F=1$ in a noisy world has high *absolute* mismatch forever). The discriminator — residual autocorrelation (structured⇒class, white⇒noise) — already exists in `result-structural-adaptation-necessity`'s body/gold but is not propagated to the fitness signature. *Known-but-unexecuted* (the wording fix is a staged WN follow-up); genuinely new is the normalized-fraction arithmetic (the WN gold's version is arithmetically wrong; this sharpening is correct) and the propagation demand.
