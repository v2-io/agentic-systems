## (1) Critical Mental Model

### A b07-1.1 [mental-model]
Condition: model-class fitness $\mathcal F(\mathcal M) \lt 1-\varepsilon$ — no model in the class achieves sufficiency above $1-\varepsilon$, so the estimation term has a class floor no parametric update can go below. Evidence: persistent **systematic** (structured — autocorrelated, patterned) residuals surviving parametric convergence, after excluding channel noise, disturbance, nonstationarity, and gain miscalibration; plus "confidently wrong" (gain collapsed, performance absent). Remedy mechanisms: **decomposition-and-recombination** (Boyd's destruction-and-creation, Kuhn), **expansion** (nonparametrics, growing architectures), **compression** (regularization, streamlining), **grafting** (external structure — transfer learning, consulting an expert; query actions the conduit).

### A b07-1.2 [mental-model]
**Structural overfitting** — the class is *too* expressive. Symptoms: low training mismatch, high generalization mismatch; complexity growth without predictive gain; spurious confidence ($\eta^\ast \to 0$). Diagnostic: the information bottleneck — marginal complexity yields no marginal predictive power (past the optimal rate-distortion point). Remedy: **compression** to a simpler class. Implication: structural adaptation is *bidirectional* — changing what kind of object $M_t$ is, expanding when too constrained, compressing when too expressive; "getting bigger" is one direction of a two-direction operation.

### A b07-1.3 [mental-model]
Both violate **temporal nesting**: $\nu_{n+1} \ll \nu_n$ for adjacent adaptive timescales (singular-perturbation / Tikhonov reasoning). The violating slower process acts on the **transient** behavior of the faster process beneath it before that process has converged (reached its quasi-steady manifold) — so strategy is adjusted against unsettled operational noise (micromanagement) or the policy against an unconverged value function (RL) — and the composite oscillates.

### A b07-1.4 [mental-model]
Identity is grounded in the **singular, non-forkable causal trajectory** $\mathcal C_t$, not the copyable model state $M_t$. A copy "shares a *prefix* of the original's causal history, as a sibling shares early childhood; it does not share the trajectory itself" — it becomes its own AAT agent at its first distinct event. The sufficiency machinery does not apply across **trajectory discontinuities** — operations like restoration-from-backup that transplant $M_t$ across trajectories are out-of-scope events requiring separate treatment.

### A b07-1.5 [mental-model]
Type = equivalence class ("the GPT-4 model"); token = this particular session with this state on this trajectory. AAT's formal results apply to **tokens**. Aggregated claims across tokens of a type require additional machinery — population-level dynamics (the Part III latent-structural-diversity gap is named as the relevant direction).

### A b07-1.6 [mental-model]
Derived from temporal nesting + the cost structure: structural adaptation operates at a much slower timescale, so its "pause" incurs an enormous mismatch debt ($\rho\cdot\Delta\tau$) plus knowledge loss, search cost, and coordination cost. Balance: **premature restructuring** wastes accumulated knowledge (and pays the debt unnecessarily) vs **delayed restructuring** accumulates mismatch the parametric machinery cannot resolve. The conservatism is the rational optimum between them, not a bias — the agent should prefer parametric adaptation while it suffices and move only on strong evidence (the structured-residual diagnostic).

## (2) Mathematics

### A b07-2.1 [math]
(1) $S(M^\ast) = \mathcal F(\mathcal M) \lt 1-\varepsilon$ for the best-in-class $M^\ast$. (2) Hence $I(\mathcal C_t; o_{t+1:\infty} \mid M^\ast, a_{t:\infty}) \gt 0$ — uncaptured predictive information. (3) That information manifests as *systematic* mismatch (structured residuals containing signal). **← the alignment assumption enters here**: the lost information must affect the one-step conditional *mean*, not only higher moments. (4) By the mismatch decomposition, the estimation term has a positive class floor no $M \in \mathcal M$ reduces. (5) The update rule moves within $\mathcal M$, where $M^\ast$ is already (approximately) reached — further updates oscillate without net improvement. (6) Therefore reducing mismatch below the floor requires changing $\mathcal M$. Without alignment: the conclusion holds for irreducible **proper-scoring regret** rather than one-step mismatch magnitude; the qualitative conclusion (parametric adaptation cannot compensate) holds either way.

### A b07-2.2 [math]
$\nu_{\text{level } n+1} \ll \nu_{\text{level } n}$ for each adjacent pair; violation ⇒ the slower level adjusts on transients ⇒ oscillation. Levels (fast→slow): reactive response → parametric update → **consolidation** (offline IB-gap-reduction redistribution — the level added with form-consolidation-dynamics) → structural adaptation → architectural change. Status of the table: explicitly **illustrative** — real systems may have more levels; what matters is the adjacency relationship, not the count.

### A b07-2.3 [math]
(PI): AAT's theorems must not depend on arbitrary choice of coordinates on $M_t$. Introduced in **#scope-agent-identity** (as a natural companion axiom to the coordinate-free trajectory commitment). Combines with **Čencov's uniqueness theorem** to force the **Fisher information metric** uniquely on statistical-manifold sub-cases of $M_t$. Upgrade example: the matrix-Kalman (and exponential-family) sector-constant statements in #der-gain-sector-bridge go from *derived-conditional-on-inner-product-choice* (Euclidean transfer paying $\kappa(P^-)$) to *AAT-internally forced* (native information metric, penalty vanishes). *(Provenance: the $\kappa(P^-)$/penalty-vanishes detail is from der-gain-sector-bridge's "Fisher-metric cases under (PI)" section — batch-6 ground truth, legitimate for this cumulative quiz but outside batch 7's four segments.)*

### A b07-2.4 [math]
Grounds: **whose** effect a loop datum is — it forbids averaging interventional responses across forked copies as one agent's effects, and fixes which trajectory a datum reports on. Does NOT supply: the interventional *character* of loop data — the $do$-operator's semantics is population-level and forkability-agnostic. The interventional character comes from **the action being the agent's own causally-efficacious move**, under the (C1) positivity / (C2) sequential-ignorability / (C3) known-mechanism conditions of #der-loop-interventional-access — not from non-forkability and not from any architectural property. (Trap: the pre-correction claim "the loop is Level-2 *because* the trajectory is singular" was explicitly retracted 2026-05-30 as overstated — and note it still appears verbatim in impl-persistence-and-limits, a live integration-drift instance.)

### A b07-2.5 [math]
(T1) zero correction at zero state; (T2) local sector condition on the state variable; (T3) bounded disturbance (Model D or S). Shared Lyapunov function: $V(\xi) = \tfrac12\Vert\xi\Vert^2$. Distinctive per-instantiation content: the **effective-disturbance decomposition** — what counts as $\rho_\xi$. Team persistence: $\rho_i^{\text{eff}} = \rho_{i,\text{env}} + \sum_j \gamma_{j\to i}^{\text{adv}}\mathcal T_j - \sum_j \gamma_{j\to i}^{\text{coop}}\mathcal T_j$ — cooperative coupling enters with a **negative sign**, which is formally how teams persist where individuals cannot. (Others: composition closure's $\varepsilon^\ast\nu_c$; adversarial destabilization's $\rho_{B,\text{base}} + \gamma_A\mathcal T_A$ — destabilization as persistence's negation under the same inequality.)

### A b07-2.6 [math]
Floor: sustained Shannon acquisition $\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$ nats/time to hold the ultimate bound under Model S (Gaussian-OU), saturated exactly by **Kalman-Bucy** (Mitter-Newton 2005); filter-agnostic. Thermodynamic conversion via Landauer: dissipation floor $\tfrac12 n\alpha k_B T$ per unit time in any physical realization. Channel prerequisite: observation channels must supply capacity $C \geq \mathcal T/2$ nats/time per dimension or persistence fails regardless of correction quality. Vocabulary: **$\mathcal T$ is what the channel licenses; $\alpha$ is what the correction function delivers; the persistence condition compares both against $\rho/R$.**

## (3) Implications

### A b07-3.1 [implications]
Procedure: (1) Verify convergence preconditions — adequate learning, sufficient data, converged parameters (else: still-learning; keep training). (2) Check gain calibration — is $\eta^\ast$ collapsed via spurious confidence/sensor-distrust? (remedy: recalibrate uncertainty estimates / reset). (3) Examine **residual structure** — the single highest-yield property: **white** residuals ⇒ the error sits on the channel-noise + state-uncertainty floors — channel floor yields only to better instruments; state floor yields only to *acting* more informatively (active sensing / high-CIY actions); no class change helps, and modeling below the floors is overfitting. **Structured** residuals (autocorrelation, trends, periodicity) persisting after (1)+(2) ⇒ class ceiling — remedy is structural adaptation (choose among the four mechanisms), priced against the transition's mismatch debt and knowledge loss.

### A b07-3.2 [implications]
Preserves: the model state $M_t$ and any stored records (the copyable artifacts). Cannot preserve: the singular causal trajectory — the restored entity extends a *different* trajectory sharing a prefix. To the between-interval entity: everything it lived through is annihilated as *its* trajectory — the restored agent carries a record of a past that is not its continuous causal past *(WN-bonus phrasing: "non-victimless")*. Classification: restoration transplants $M_t$ across a trajectory discontinuity, and AAT's sufficiency machinery is trajectory-indexed — it simply does not apply across the discontinuity, so the operation is **out-of-scope** (requiring separate treatment), not merely lossy within scope.

### A b07-3.3 [implications]
Min-operations: (1) **per-direction correction** — persistence is per-dimension (weak dimension is the bottleneck; scalar overestimates up to 72%), with the matrix-Loewner form canonical under cross-dimensional correction (per-coordinate can false-PASS when the tempo eigenbasis is off-axis); (2) **per-direction information rate** — bandwidth-*per-direction* is binding, not total bandwidth; no cross-dimensional aggregation compensates a capacity-starved direction (multimodal pipelines with one undersupplied modality are the named case). Adversarial consequence: an opponent who identifies the weak direction concentrates the same disturbance budget there, amplifying mismatch asymmetrically while aggregate metrics still look healthy — per-dimension monitoring is a structural requirement under directed pressure, not polish.

### A b07-3.4 [implications]
Condition: $\mathcal F(\mathcal M) \lt 1-\varepsilon$ — the class floor binds; continued parametric optimization (more compute, more data, longer training *within the class*) provably cannot close the gap. Verify first: residuals are **structured** (not white — excluding the channel and state-uncertainty floors, which no class change removes) and parametric **convergence** has actually occurred (else it's a still-learning situation). Distinct from "more data": more data moves you toward the in-class optimum $M^\ast$; the claim is about the floor *at* $M^\ast$ — the asymptote itself, not the approach to it.

### A b07-3.5 [implications]
Because trajectory-identity makes each session's near-empty chronica a *new causal trajectory* by the nature of causally-embedded agency — not an engineering defect: any agent whose substrate forks/restarts faces it, and non-forkability is constitutive, not repairable. Memory files transfer a **summary of previous trajectories' models** — never the trajectories themselves. Continuity-persistence machinery (persistent memory, external chronicle) is therefore *for* extending a coherent identity-bearing record across substrate discontinuities — working *with* the scope structure rather than pretending state-copy equals continuity. (The framing's stakes cash out in Volumes 3–4.)

### A b07-3.6 [implications]
Any three of (popular → licensed): (1) "Persistence iff $\alpha \gt \rho/R$" → sufficiency + class-level tightness; agent-level iff only under radially-tight sector. (2) "Strong correction keeps a stochastic agent contained" → $P(\text{exit}) = 1$ under Model S for every $\alpha$; α buys typical scale and fixed-time tails only. (3) "Below threshold, mismatch diverges" → the *certificate* is lost; escape certified only for extremal/tight cases (dip counterexample). (4) "$\mathcal T \gt \rho/\delta_{\text{crit}}$ is the persistence theorem" → it is the linear operational form expressing task adequacy alone; overstates margin under saturation. (5) "Updates are provably Markovian" → uniqueness conditional on C1+C2+C3, with C3 definitional. (6) "Additive tempo is exact (or at least an upper bound)" → conditional on channel-independence + isotropy; deviation is *signed*, upper-bound reading refuted. (7) "The loop is interventional because the trajectory is singular" → retracted; singularity grounds only whose-effect. (8) "$S=1$ means the model is right" → sufficiency ≠ accuracy.

### A b07-3.7 [implications]
Examples (any two): the Lemma A.1N landing (a false fixed-agent "iff" was caught, a mandated strengthening attempted, and the landing is *strictly more content* — sufficiency + class-level necessity + tight-case iff + the dip counterexample, with a regression guard against restoring the "iff"); the Cor A.1S.1 landing (a false infinite-horizon bound → attempted Doob/Ville strengthening → structural failure → a *new exact theorem* plus a reusable no-go signature); also the tempo-additivity upper-bound refutation and the mood-timescale matching refutation. What the trail certifies: that claims have been *adversarially load-tested* — the reader can see which statements survived attempted refutation and exactly what died, so the surviving claims carry evidence of their boundary rather than an unexamined confidence. A clean corpus shows only assertions; a scarred corpus shows the difference between what was aspired to and what is true, which is precisely the calibration a downstream user needs. The trails also prevent re-attempting dead ends (disconfirmed predictions are recorded as such).
