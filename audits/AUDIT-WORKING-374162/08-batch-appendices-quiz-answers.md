## (2) Mathematics

### A b08-2.1 [math]
$G(t) = e^{2\alpha t}V(\delta(t))$: not a supermartingale because Itô leaves the strictly positive drift $+e^{2\alpha t}\tfrac n2\sigma_w^2 dt$. Compensating: $S(t) = e^{2\alpha(t\wedge\tau_R)}V - \frac{n\sigma_w^2}{4\alpha}(e^{2\alpha(t\wedge\tau_R)}-1)$ has $dS \leq$ martingale term — a genuine supermartingale. Killing blow: $S \lt 0$ whenever $V \lt \frac{n\sigma_w^2}{4\alpha}$ — i.e., inside the typical scatter, since the mean-square persistence condition puts the RMS radius $R_S^\ast = \sigma_w\sqrt{n/2\alpha}$ well inside $\mathcal B_R$, so the system sits there *most of the time*; Ville/Doob require nonnegativity. Generator-level obstruction: additive non-degenerate forcing has an **unbounded scale function** (OU: $s'(u) \propto e^{\alpha u^2/\sigma_w^2}$), so the only bounded harmonic functions are constants — gambler's-ruin machinery cannot certify positive-probability eternal containment.

### A b08-2.2 [math]
OU high-resolution RDF per unit time: $\dot R(D^2) = \frac{n\sigma_w^2}{4D^2}$. Substituting the tight Model-S bound $D^2 = n\sigma_w^2/(2\alpha)$: $\dot R_{\min} = \frac{n\sigma_w^2}{4\cdot n\sigma_w^2/(2\alpha)} = \frac{n\alpha}{2}$. Kalman-Bucy scalar saturation (Mitter-Newton): information supply rate $\dot I = P_{ss}/(2\sigma_o^2) = K_{ss}/2 = \alpha/2$ under the identification $\alpha = K_{ss}$ — the bound is achieved, not merely a lower bound.

### A b08-2.3 [math]
$\Vert\delta_{k+1}\Vert^2 = \Vert\delta_k\Vert^2 - 2\eta^\ast\delta_k^T F_d(\delta_k) + (\eta^\ast)^2\Vert F_d(\delta_k)\Vert^2 \leq (1 - 2\eta^\ast c_{\min} + (\eta^\ast)^2 c_{\max}^2)\Vert\delta_k\Vert^2 = \lambda_{\text{eff}}^2\Vert\delta_k\Vert^2$. Stability ($\lambda_{\text{eff}}^2 \lt 1$) requires $\eta^\ast \lt 2c_{\min}/c_{\max}^2$ (the no-overshoot condition; classical $2/L$ when well-conditioned). Fluid-limit gaps: Model D — **zero** (discrete steady state equals continuous exactly); Model S — additive correction of order $\eta^\ast c_{\max}^2/c_{\min}^2$.

### A b08-2.4 [math]
(R1) smooth log-likelihood with non-degenerate local quadratic expansion, $H_M + H_L \succ 0$; (R2) first-order-in-step-size (cubic terms negligible); (R3) Bayesian-coherent update (posterior coordinate). ### A b08-2.5 [math]
$\Delta = \frac{c(2U_1U_2 - c(U_1+U_2))}{U_1U_2(U_1U_2 - c^2)}$; $\Delta = 0$ iff $c = 0$ or $c = 2U_1U_2/(U_1+U_2)$. ### A b08-2.6 [math]
Model: $n^{(k)} = s + w^{(k)}$, shared $s \sim \mathcal N(0,\sigma_s^2)$, independent $w^{(k)}$; $\Sigma_n = \sigma_s^2\mathbf{11}^T + \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_{w,k}^2)$. Sherman-Morrison: $J_{\text{joint}} = f(q)$ with $q = \sum_k 1/\sigma_{w,k}^2$, $f(x) = x/(1+\sigma_s^2 x)$. Strict concavity with $f(0)=0$ gives $f(q) \lt \sum_k f(q_k)$ (strict subadditivity) whenever $\sigma_s^2 \gt 0$ and ≥2 channels are active. Saturation: $f(q) \to 1/\sigma_s^2$ as $q \to \infty$ — joint information is capped at the **shared-bias floor**; no number of common-source channels buys past it, because the shared component is common to all and cannot be averaged away.

## (3) Implications

### A b08-3.1 [implications]

The two striking ones: **Mehra non-identifiability as an identifiability-floor instance on the meta-gain channel** (the impossibility meta-pattern reaching into the agent's self-calibration), and **mean-field VI's compute-independent sub-optimality**.
