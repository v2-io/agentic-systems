## Predictions vs evidence

- Sufficiency: I predicted $S = I(M_t;Y)/I(\mathcal C_t;Y)$; actual form is the complement-of-lost-fraction: $S = 1 - I(\mathcal C_t; o_{fut} \mid M_t, a_{fut})/I(\mathcal C_t; o_{fut}\mid a_{fut})$. Same in spirit but *not* algebraically identical to my guess in general — the numerator is the conditional MI of history beyond the model (information *lost*), which is the cleaner object (my form would need $M_t=\phi(\mathcal C_t)$ + chain-rule to relate). The well-definedness clause (denominator > 0; undefined in prediction-vacuous regimes) I did not predict, and it's exactly the kind of scope-at-the-definition discipline a summary omits. Also unpredicted: **trajectory-relativity** — $S$ is indexed to *this* agent's singular chronica, not a model-equivalence class; ties to scope-agent-identity.
- Class fitness: predicted exactly ($\mathcal F = \sup_M S(M)$). The teaching content is the **observable signature**: agent can't compute $\mathcal F$; the diagnostic is *structured* residuals persisting after parametric convergence — structure, not magnitude, is the discriminator ($S=\mathcal F=1$ in a noisy world still has a high absolute floor with *white* residuals). This answers the "low $\mathcal F$ vs high $\rho$" disambiguation question directly — and a summary-reader will get this wrong by saying "persistent high mismatch means change your model class." No: persistent *structured* mismatch.
- der-recursive-update: my prediction (completeness + constraints) essentially right; the honest surprise is the *depth of the definitional-character disclosure*. C1 eliminative (physics), C2 eliminative (scope), C3 definitional (can't be violated, absorbs violations by expanding $M$). The result: "Markov is a boundary you draw around the agent, not a fact about the world." Attack 2 (continuous coupling) is admitted as a genuine limitation with the control-theory state-space form as the general case. Attack 3 (C3 circularity) is faced head-on. This segment pair is the framework's template for what "derived" means here — and the single best discriminator of real comprehension so far: a summary-fed agent will say "AAT proves updates must be Markovian"; the actual claim is uniqueness *conditional on the three-constraint set*, with one constraint definitional.
