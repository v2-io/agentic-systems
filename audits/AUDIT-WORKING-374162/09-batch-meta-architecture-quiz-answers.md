## (1) Critical Mental Model

### A b09-1.1 [mental-model]
A symmetric positive-definite form $\mathcal M$ for which the one-point sector condition holds in the $\mathcal M$-inner product on a ball: $\langle F(e), e-e^\ast\rangle_{\mathcal M} \geq \kappa\Vert e-e^\ast\Vert^2_{\mathcal M}$, $\kappa \gt 0$. Four specializations: **Fisher information** (Bayesian agents), **$(P^-)^{-1}$** (Kalman), **loss Hessian** (gradient agents), **plant-selected Lyapunov metric** (linear-Hurwitz/PID). Anchor (exact, #result-certificate-existence): a certificate exists **iff** the agent is exponentially stable about its target — operator-sector in *some* inner product and exponential stability are the same statement, certificate as converse-Lyapunov witness.

### A b09-1.2 [mental-model]
The four meta-patterns are not four peers: M1 (boundary), M2 (scope-of-existence), M3 (forced identity), and M4 (dynamics-on-class, Part III) are **facets of one object** — the certificate — with the spine (disc-stability-certificate) as the recognition naming it, plus a fifth facet-like entry (projection behaviour, composition closure's $\varepsilon^\ast$). Not facets: **constructive-impossibility posture** and **anti-collapse** — both *style claims* (epistemic-architectural recognitions about *how* the framework states results), carrying no theorems of their own, sitting atop the facets.

### A b09-1.3 [mental-model]
(1) **Forced-identity failure** — Helmholtz–Hodge: non-symmetric $J$ ⇒ field is not a gradient ⇒ no potential ⇒ certificate merely *matched*, not Čencov-*forced*; invariant: symmetry of $J$. (2) **Existence failure** — Sylvester's law: the certificate drops rank, and congruence (the agent's entire representational freedom) preserves inertia, so rank-deficiency holds in *every* coordinate; invariant: inertia under congruence; only escape: rank augmentation (new information). (3) **Projection failure** — Mori–Zwanzig/Schur: the metric survives projection (Schur complement of PD is PD) but the dynamic guarantee doesn't; closure defect = memory-commutator norm, zero iff the resolved subspace is $J$-invariant; invariant: $J$-invariance. Mutual invariance: metric change ≠ fix for non-invariance; projection ≠ fix for asymmetry; rank augmentation ≠ fix for a memory kernel. Exhaustiveness: **explicitly open** — three are established exactly; that they exhaust is not proved.

### A b09-1.4 [mental-model]
**Forced**: a uniqueness theorem on an AAT-internal axiom admits exactly one coordinate (e.g., log-odds via Cauchy-FE on evidential additivity). **Matched**: chosen to fit a structure, existence-not-uniqueness (canonical case: **the Lyapunov quadratic $V = \tfrac12\Vert\delta\Vert^2$** — selected via converse-Lyapunov to match the sector form). **Adopted**: imported with citation (Pearl's $do$; the Legendre-Fenchel geometry itself). Why it matters: only forced coordinates carry a "no other coordinate could serve" claim — downstream results leaning on a matched coordinate must not borrow uniqueness authority (a different $V$ could serve; the results are robust to that, but the *coordinate* is not privileged).

### A b09-1.5 [mental-model]

Inverse case: **refusing a spurious split** — recognizing two distinct causes drive the *same* knob and share one remedy; example: #scope-edge-update-causal-validity, where observability failure and identifiability failure both freeze an edge's effective gain.

### A b09-1.6 [mental-model]
**Identifiability floor**: an *agent* inferring from data, blocked by information-theoretic theorems (CHT, Cramér-Rao, Liberzon, Kalman-Ho); remedy: information augmentation (interventional access, latent observability, white-box). **Value-functional grounding floor**: an *agent* anchoring its objective through $V_{O_t}$, blocked by convention-monotonicity/finite-no-oracle (F) or CHT-at-reward-channel (G); remedy: off-interface grounding (adaptive-substrate invariant or principal-side protocol commitment). **Implementation impossibility**: a *designer* constructing a mechanism, blocked by classical social-choice theorems (Gibbard-Satterthwaite, Myerson-Satterthwaite, Arrow); remedy: design-constraint relaxation (domain restriction, transfers, Bayes-Nash IC, randomization). Suffix convention: ***-floor*** = agent-side; ***-impossibility*** = designer-side.

### A b09-3.5 [implications]
