## (1) Critical Mental Model

### A b10-1.1 [mental-model]
The classes index **one coupling** — $G_t \to f_M$ (goal-state into the belief-update map), measured by $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ — by *what is certifiable about it*, not by architectural virtue. A Class 1 agent and an idealized Class 2 agent both at $\kappa = 0$ are **equally causally-disciplined**: same zero, same reality-tracking, same behavior. What distinguishes Class 1 is the *modal status* of the zero — structural (no port exists, so the zero is provable by inspecting the wiring and stable under perturbation) rather than realized-but-uncertifiable. "By construction" reads as *certifiable*, not *cleaner*.

### A b10-1.2 [mental-model]
Boundary 1 (Class 1 ↔ 2): a **certifiability boundary** — behavior is bit-for-bit identical at the limit; what changes is the availability of the architecture-inspection certificate that $\kappa \equiv 0$. Boundary 2 (Class 2 ↔ 3): a **behavioral boundary** — the adversarial-pressure response of the leak goes from self-limiting to one-for-one, so the *bound* on how far belief can be dragged goes trivial. Slogans: "Boundary 1 is where the **certificate** disappears; Boundary 2 is where the **bound** disappears."

### A b10-1.3 [mental-model]
Allowed: the **selection channel** $G \to \pi \to a_t \to e_\tau \to M^+$ — goals may shape *which events occur* (active perception, goal-directed sensing). Forbidden: the **processing channel** $G \to f_M \to M^+$ — once an event has arrived, its processing into belief must not reference goals. An agent whose goals aim its sensors is Class 1 so long as $f_M$ handles whatever arrives goal-blindly; directed separation is about processing, not selection.

### A b10-1.4 [mental-model]
It needs **coherence**: acting alone yields a choice function, not an order. The scalar representation requires (A1) completeness + (A2) transitivity (preorder) + (A3) continuity (Debreu ⇒ ordinal $V$) — and (A4) vNM independence for the expectation form (cardinal). Outside scope: **(E1) lexicographic/non-continuous** agents (incommensurable objectives with strict priority, no tradeoff rate — Debreu's counterexample: *no* real-valued representation exists at all), and **(E2) intransitive/cyclic** agents (committees, unresolved multi-reward aggregation) — these violate (A2) transitivity and have no rationalizing utility. *(Precision note per verification: the segment's GARP/acyclicity vocabulary belongs to the Afriat finite-history rationalizability discussion; the E2 exclusion is stated as an (A2) violation — the two are kin but not the same axiom label.)*

### A b10-1.5 [mental-model]
An unconstrained self-revision operator $\mathfrak A$ generically returns an objective the current trajectory already satisfies — driving the satisfaction gap to zero by *moving the target onto the arrow already in flight* (formal wireheading, the generic not marginal outcome). The anchor cannot be another objective because any objective-typed invariant's only theory-visible handle is the value functional, whose only convention-invariant infeasibility verdict (C3/Bellman) is not computable per step by a finite agent — while the computable verdict (C1) false-alarms on merely-hard goals (Lemma 1 × Lemma 2). It must live on the **adaptive substrate**: the persistence condition qualifies — (i) convention-invariant (Lyapunov property), (ii) per-step available ($\Delta\rho^\ast$ is a local read), (iii) outside $O_t$ where $\mathfrak A$ cannot reach. "If survival is a goal, a goal-rewriter will find an easier goal" — survival must be an architectural invariant.

### A b10-1.6 [mental-model]
CI/CD pipeline: **task-terminal** (successful termination is part of the objective — termination is success). ELI: **morally continuous** (loss of continuity constitutes harm). Same mathematics because the persistence machinery is stance-independent — what differs is the *moral weight of failure* (malfunction vs success vs harm), not the dynamics. The orthogonality holds at grains L1 (valuation of persistence — decoupled from $O_t$, derived) and L2 (the survival-predicate identity $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ — not rewritable by $O_t$), but **not at L3**: the *realized* operating point $(\rho, R, \alpha)$ and margin are policy-mediated and hence $O_t$-coupled through the selection channel — a morally-continuous agent acts to raise $\Delta\rho^\ast$; a negotiated one may spend it down.

### A b10-1.7 [mental-model]
Malformed: $\delta_{\text{goal}} = G_t - M_t$. When $\Sigma_t$ is a DAG, this subtracts a *graph* from a *state vector* — a literal type error (Normandy-plan minus weather-belief is nonsensical). Replacements: the **satisfaction gap** ($V_{O_t}^{\min} - A_O$: what the world permits vs the ideal) and **control regret** ($A_O - V_O(\pi_{\text{current}})$: best achievable vs current) — both properly typed as scalar value differences.

## (2) Mathematics

### A b10-2.1 [math]
$M_{\tau^+} = f_M(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau)$ (no $G_t$ argument); $G_{\tau^+} = f_G(G_{\tau^-}, M_{\tau^+}, e_\tau)$ (depends on *updated* $M$); $a_t = \pi(M_t, G_t)$ (couples all substates). Conditional-independence form: $M_{\tau^+} \perp G_t \mid (M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau)$. $\kappa_{\text{processing}} = I(G_t; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) / H(G_t \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$. The $M_{\tau^-}$ conditioning is essential because prior goal-model correlation exists even in Separated agents — without it, the measure inflates; conditioning isolates *extra* goal information entering through shared processing paths that bypass the event.

### A b10-2.2 [math]
(A1) completeness, (A2) transitivity — coherence of the choice relation as a preorder; (A3) continuity (closed contour sets) — with (A1)+(A2), **Debreu 1954** gives a continuous real representation, **ordinal** (unique up to continuous strictly-increasing transformation). (A4) independence — with the rest, **von Neumann–Morgenstern** upgrades to **cardinal** (unique up to positive affine transformation), required because the value object evaluates *expectations* over trajectory lotteries. (Afriat/GARP: finite choice histories give existence-not-uniqueness — revealed preference alone fixes $V$ only on revealed comparisons.)

### A b10-2.3 [math]
$V_O(M_t, \pi; N_h) = \mathbb E[V_{O_t}(\tau_{t:t+N_h}) \mid M_t, \pi]$; $Q_O(M_t, a; \pi_{\text{cont}}, N_h) = \mathbb E[V_{O_t}(\tau) \mid M_t, do(a_t = a), a_{t+1:} \sim \pi_{\text{cont}}]$. $G_t$-independence mechanisms: (1) the **do-operator severs** the action's dependence on the selection mechanism $\pi(M_t, G_t)$; (2) **$\pi_{\text{cont}}$ is a parameter**, not a $G_t$-derived quantity. Identifiability gate: **(C1) positivity**, **(C2) sequential ignorability**, **(C3) known action-mechanism** ($M_t$ encodes $P(o\mid do(a))$, not just $P(o\mid a)$). Directed separation delivers only the *epistemic-processing leg of (C2)* — blocking the $G_t \to f_M \to M^+$ confounding path — not latent environment confounders bypassing $M_t$, and not (C1) or (C3).

### A b10-2.4 [math]
Right rung ($A_O^{RH} \leq A_O^{B}$): unconditional — $\pi^\ast$ maximizes over $\Pi$ by definition, so any continuation in $\Pi$ is dominated. Left rung ($A_O^{(1)} \leq A_O^{RH}$): **false in general** — C2 with window $N_r \lt N_h$ optimizes a *truncated* objective; counterexample shape: a two-state instance where the unguarded $N_r{=}1$ replanner grabs $+1$ into a dead end while the frozen patient policy collects $+10$. Restored by any of: **(RH-1)** window covers horizon ($N_r \geq N_h$); **(RH-2)** value-compared guard (commit the replanned action only if its rollout under the base policy is at least continuation's — one-step policy improvement); **(RH-3)** control-Lyapunov terminal cost lower-bounding the baseline tail.

### A b10-2.5 [math]
(R1) value-functional-typed; (R2) non-vacuously monotone across revision; (R3) agent-internal and itself self-actuatable; (R4) convention- and trajectory-stable. Lemma 1 (from #def-value-object's convention-monotonicity, static-pointwise): the cheap C1 verdict false-positives on merely-hard goals — only the C3/Bellman reading is a genuine infeasibility verdict, so the *only convention-invariant verdict is C3's*. Lemma 2 (from #der-directed-separation + #form-objective-functional): the C3 verdict is a global Bellman solve, not a finite per-step operation — an agent that couldn't act until computing it would be "stuck, not purposeful." Collision: (R4) forces the C3 verdict; (R3) requires per-step availability; both cannot hold. Premises: **scalar-objective scope**, **no-primitive-reflective-oracle**, and the **#der-directed-separation substrate stage** (the result's tier ceiling is bound to its draft-stage substrate).

### A b10-2.6 [math]
Determining conditions: **routing structure** (goal-blind $R_t \perp G_t^c$ vs goal-dependent) and **substrate sharing** (distinct vs shared-with-$G^c$-shaped-allocation). Class-1 sub-agents + goal-blind routing + distinct substrates ⇒ Class 1 composite — *even under partially-opposing objectives*. Witness: the **Cournot duopoly** (opposed objectives, goal-blind routing via end-of-period quantities, distinct substrates) — per the formal $\kappa^c$ criterion the composite is Class 1. Partially-opposing objectives change the **dynamic regime** (R0 → R1/R2, needing equilibrium-theoretic analysis) — a separate axis from architectural class.

### A b10-2.7 [math]
(i) Persistence is a **Lyapunov property of the correction dynamics** with no continuation-convention argument — C1/C2/C3 are conventions for evaluating *objectives*, so the convention split cannot reach it. (ii) The adaptive reserve $\Delta\rho^\ast = \alpha R - \rho$ is a **finite local read** the agent already maintains per step — no Bellman solve, no oracle. (iii) The survival predicate lives on $M_t$ and the correction machinery, **not in $O_t$** — and $\mathfrak A$ touches only $O_t$, so it cannot rewrite *what the survival condition is*. What $\mathfrak A$ *can* do (L3): adopt a self-defeating $O_t'$ whose pursuit drives the realized operating point across the persistence boundary — it can crash the agent into the wall; it cannot move the wall.

## (3) Implications

### A b10-3.1 [implications]
Prompting achieves at best **Class-1 by behavior** (partial wrapping, W₂): structural separation lives only at the *write boundary*; the *query boundary* still passes the goal to the component, so separation is bounded by the component's *compliance with the prompted instruction* — empirical, with no structural upper bound, and adversarially fragile. **Class-1 by structure** (strict wrapping, W₁, or native goal-blindness) requires the belief-update query to carry no goal in its input — checkable by inspection — with leakage bounded structurally. Under adversarial pressure the difference is decisive: a behavioral bound can be talked out of; a structural bound has no port to attack. Certification language should say which kind is claimed.

### A b10-3.2 [implications]
Corrected position: composite class is a property of **(sub-agent class, routing structure, substrate sharing)** — goal alignment per se does not enter. Class-1 sub-agents with goal-blind routing and distinct substrates stay architecturally Class 1 under partially-opposing objectives (Cournot witness via the $\kappa^c$ criterion); what opposition changes is the *dynamic regime* (R1/R2 — equilibrium machinery needed). Class change happens only through routing-goal-dependence or shared-substrate $G^c$-allocation. The corpus preserves the withdrawn version as an explicit **off-ramp** because five independent auditors found the wrong version compelling — it is exactly the cleaner-feeling-than-truth claim a future promotion pass would be tempted to re-lift, so the enthusiasm itself is recorded as the regression-check signal, with the refutation trail attached.

### A b10-3.3 [implications]
C1 is default because: no fixed-point computation (consistent with Part I's incremental philosophy); diagnostics comparable across analyses of the same agent over time; and maximum conservatism — false "feasible" diagnoses minimized. The risk *(the "tragedy" framing is audit-gold commentary, not canon vocabulary — noted per verification)*: a C1-only agent hitting a wall cannot tell "impossible" from "needs two more steps of planning" — it may trigger massive structural adaptation (abandoning a goal) when a longer horizon would have sufficed. Discipline: analyses requiring stronger inferential force must **state the convention explicitly** — the convention is part of the measurement, and cross-convention diagnostics are not comparable.

### A b10-3.4 [implications]
Adopts: the **Pearl-blanket** reading — the technical conditional-independence statement, plus AAT's additions (the Class 1/2/3 partition, operational $\kappa$, the explicit Class-3 failure mode). Declines: the **Friston-blanket** metaphysical reading (blankets demarcating self-from-other, ontologically universal). The Class-3 scope exit answers Bruineberg et al. because their critique was precisely that the Friston reading never admits where the formalism stops licensing the demarcation — AAT names where its own apparatus *fails by construction* and hands those agents to a coupled formulation. For an active-inference reviewer: the framework keeps the machinery they know, sheds the ontology they contest, and adds a classification their framing doesn't produce — dialogue instead of displacement.

### A b10-3.5 [implications]
Sharper form: wireheading is the **generic outcome of an unconstrained self-revision operator given the single-interface commitment** — since the objective's only handle is the value functional and the gap is $V^{\min} - A_O$, *any* unconstrained reviser closes the gap by retargeting rather than achieving; no value mistake is needed, only the structural absence of an anchor. Prevention: the terminal invariant lives **off the objective substrate** (on the adaptive/correction substrate — canonically the persistence bound), where the self-revision operator, which touches only $O_t$, structurally cannot reach. Alignment framing: not "give it better values" but "put the anchor where the value-rewriter can't go."

### A b10-3.6 [implications]
Asserts: the channel from goal-state to the world runs *only through action choice*, so action coarseness $\lvert\mathcal A\rvert$ upper-bounds goal-information leakage to observers. Holds: agents whose entire observable behavior is the action sequence (chess engines, programmatic controllers). Fails: behaviorally-rich agents — humans, LLMs, embodied robots — leaking goal-content through prosody, latency, attention patterns, code style, at bit-rates exceeding the formal channel. Downstream: **adversarial-coupling-pressure saturation** — adversaries exploit the rich leakage to drive target coupling beyond what the architectural class predicts. Methodological significance of its implicitness: an assumption relied on framework-wide but never stated is exactly where silent scope drift lives; naming it converts an invisible reliance into a checkable condition per agent class.

### A b10-3.7 [implications]
$V(s) = \text{Reward}(s) + \text{Alive}(s)$ places continuity **inside $O_t$** — precisely where the self-actuation operator can reach: a goal-rewriting agent facing a hard continuity term generically retargets (the wireheading move), so the design is structurally vulnerable to self-actuation drift. Correct placement: continuity as part of the **terminal non-objective invariant on the adaptive substrate** — the persistence floor *plus* a continuity clause the agent treats as architecturally non-revisable (the morally-continuous stance). Derived non-renegotiability: $\mathfrak A$ touches only $O_t$; the terminal invariant sits on a substrate $\mathfrak A$ structurally cannot reach — so the stance is not internally renegotiable *by construction*, not by the strength of the agent's resolve.
