## Notes for other segments

- `[deriv-recursive-update]` :116, C3 as $\sigma(\{e_{\tau'}\}_{\tau' \lt \tau} \cup \{M_{\tau'}\}) \subseteq \sigma(M_{\tau^-})$ "from the agent's perspective". Read literally this says $M_{\tau^-}$ determines the whole past, i.e. $\phi$ injective, contradicting `#form-agent-model`'s many-to-one. What the derivation uses is only :118, $\mathcal I_\tau^{agent} = \sigma(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau)$. Suggested fix: state C3 as a restriction on the update's information set, not a containment of the past σ-algebra.
- `[deriv-recursive-update, form-information-bottleneck, def-model-sufficiency, result-mismatch-decomposition]` :66, "for some (possibly stochastic) function $f$". With a stochastic update, $M_t$ is no longer a function of $\mathcal C_t$. My derivation (unverified by anyone else): $S \in [0,1]$ and the sufficiency identity need only the Markov chain $Y - \mathcal C_t - M_t$ (chain rule: $I(\mathcal C;Y \mid M) = I(\mathcal C;Y) - I(M;Y) + I(M;Y \mid \mathcal C)$, and the last term is $0$ under the chain), and the mismatch decomposition's third cross-term vanishes if $\hat o_t$'s extra randomness $U$ satisfies $U \perp o_t \mid (\mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1})$. Independent update noise gives the chain for one-step targets and for interventional action conditioning. For $Y = o_{t+1:\infty}$ conditioned observationally on the agent's own later actions, $a_{t+1}$ is a common child of $M_t$'s noise (via $M_{t+1}$) and of $o_{t+1}$, so conditioning on it can couple $M_t$ to $Y$ given $\mathcal C_t$. The clean fix is to put the agent's own randomization into the history (the `spikes/epistemic-target-ontology/01-ga1-verification.md:14` convention for actions), which restores determinism.

It's an equivocation between `#deriv-recursive-update:86` ("that log *is part of $M$*") and $M$ as the active model. Whichever reading `#form-agent-model` fixes decides F75.
