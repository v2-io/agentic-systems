## What I hadn't anticipated

**CIY ≠ EIG.** Distinguability vs learning value — "beep button: high CIY, zero EIG after first press." Necessary not sufficient for learning. λ-weighting as heuristic EIG-like gating. Query actions (ask expert) as high-density CIY; adversarial mirror is deception through the same channel with trust-mediated γ.

**Tempo status is conditional for real reasons.** Channel independence (echo chamber overcounts; synergistic undercounts — signed deviation, not conservative bound) and isotropy (72% scalar overestimate in anisotropic sim). "You cannot outrun bad $U_o$ by increasing ν" is the Boyd formalization. Agile-with-flaky-tests critique is devastating and concrete.

**Model D vs Model S scaling.** Deterministic: δ_ss = ρ/T; stochastic: ~ σ/√(2T). Correction less effective against noise than drift. Adversarial powers 2 vs 3/2. This was not in my initial mental model.

**Deliberation threshold is clean and self-knowing.** Net: Δη·‖δ_post‖ > ρ_delib·Δτ. High-ρ kills thinking. Circularity of predicting δ_post noted and mostly self-correcting. Action fluency as high-tempo limit of Δτ*→0.

**Bathtub figure framing** of persistence — inflow ρ, drain T, rim R, overflow = certificate loss. Thermodynamic shadow: survival as sustained Shannon burn rate ≥ nα/2 — not a state achieved once.

**Gain-sector bridge demotes α from postulate to property** of gain × directional fidelity. Promissory for next segments; the intro makes it feel like the chapter's moral.
