## What I hadn't anticipated

### The certificate spine as *one object*, not a catalog

I had been carrying M1/M2/M3 as three useful organizers. Reading them as facets of one **stability certificate** changes the grain:

- **Interior** — operator-sector (α > ρ/R and friends)
- **Scope-of-existence (M2)** — where a certificate exists at all
- **Forced-identity (M3)** — *which* stick is forced
- **Boundary (M1 + sisters)** — where the stick goes flat
- **Projection** — composition-closure ε*

The three boundary *dialects* (agent data-inference / agent value-functional / designer construction) are not three parallel catalogs — they are three ways the same boundary speaks. Suffix discipline (*-floor* vs *-impossibility*) encodes actor positioning at slug-grep depth. That felt ornamental until the actor-and-remedy tables made it load-bearing.

### M1 Instance 4 (architecture noidentifiability) is the deep one

Instance 1–3 I had met distributed. Instance 4 is the one that *closes* the rank-collapse subclass:

- Architectural d.o.f. = GL(n) similarity orbit of the closed-loop residual realization
- On-policy summary annihilates the orbit (innovation spectrum, (α,R) summaries)
- **Generating** action = similarity (not congruence); **escape-side** irreducibility = Sylvester at one remove
- Three escapes (interventional / higher-moment-in-β / white-box); horizon-extension collapses
- Elevates loop-interventional access to **third mode**: causal-sufficiency / coupling-sign / similarity-orbit

The "which internal mechanism" of `#der-agent-opacity` *is* that orbit. Opacity is not a soft mystery — it is a Lie-group fiber invisible to Level-1 summaries.

### Separability is the User's Manual; floors are honesty

M2's eight ladders (Correlation, Convention, Architecture, Contraction, Identification regime, Scope, A2'-scope, Dynamic regime) make the whole theory navigable as a grid. Positive half without floors looks unbounded; floors without positive half look conservative in a way that hides achievements. Together = scope honesty at both extremes.

Cross-ladder monotonicity still open ("is General Open infectious?"). That is a real open, not a deferral of reading.

### Additive-coordinate forcing lands on *one geometry*

Four layers (chain log-prob / reverse-KL / log-odds update / Fisher metric) are not four cute uniqueness theorems — they are four surfaces of **exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel**. Forced ≠ matched ≠ adopted:

- Forced: uniqueness theorem on AAT-internal axiom
- Matched: Lyapunov quadratic (chosen to fit sector)
- Adopted: IB Lagrangian (imported Tishby)

The heteroscedastic-Gaussian no-go is a *downstream theorem of coordinate-forcing* (Čencov forces the escape), **not** a fifth floor instance — escape count is 1 by uniqueness, floor instances require ≥2 structurally distinct escapes. Clean taxonomy hygiene.

Composition-layer fails the forcing shape: coupling is the opposite of independent-factor chain-rule structure. Honest: **single-agent family**.

### Anti-collapse is the other half of "how AAT speaks"

If constructive-impossibility forbids an *inference*, anti-collapse refuses a *modeller's merge*. Diagnostic: there must be a **tempting wrong merge** that routes to the wrong repair.

Strongest: β-vs-ρ (IB), emitter-scalar-vs-recipient-regime, κ-vs-𝒜. Catalog also: dual exploration drives, sat-gap ⊥ control-regret, structural bias-floor vs estimation error, target-alignment vs path-alignment (unity). Deeper principle: *individuation at the repair-relevant grain* (absorbs inverse same-knob case).

This is the discipline I had been using un-named when I felt "don't conflate those."

## Surprises / wandering / questions

**Surprise:** Instance 4's three modes of loop-interventional access is the cleanest unification I have met in the volume — one capability, three semantically distinct degeneracies broken.

**Surprise:** The *exclusion* discipline (reward-channel not M1 instance; GS not M1 instance; heteroscedastic no-go not M1) is as load-bearing as the inclusions. Taxonomy that over-absorbs would collapse the actor/remedy distinctions that make the three clusters useful.

**Wandering:** "General Open is infectious" — if one ladder falls to general-open, do others drag? Composition of floors (escape from one blocks another) is the dual question. Both feel like the next real research, not cleanup.

**Question held, not answered:** Is the exp-family / Legendre-Fenchel commitment *the* only coherent geometry given AAT's axioms, or a path-dependent choice excluding Wasserstein-style agents? Segment flags it; I notice it and leave it open.

**Felt texture:** After this batch the theory feels *architecturally complete* in a way the spine alone did not. The spine is what the agent *does*; the meta-architecture is how the theory *knows what it can claim*. Both were always there; only one was fully visible before.
