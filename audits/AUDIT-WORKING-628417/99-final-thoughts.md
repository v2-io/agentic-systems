## The theory in two breaths

### Spine (what the agent is and does)

Information-loss is constitutive of the agent–environment boundary. Double opacity $(h,T)$. Chronica is the singular trajectory; $M=\phi(C)$ is IB-optimal compression. Cycle: mismatch / gain / tempo. Sector Lyapunov $\alpha > \rho/R$ is the persistence bridge. When the class is unfit, structural adaptation — not more gain. Identity is the trajectory, not a snapshot. Lift $X=(M,G)$ with $G=(O,\Sigma)$. Directed separation (GUC 1/2/3) or pay $\kappa$ cost. Satisfaction-gap ⊥ control-regret. Orient cascade revises $O$ last. Strategy DAG; observability dominates unobservable regions (absorbing). Self-actuation cannot ground on the objective tower — persistence on the adaptive substrate is the terminal invariant. Composition needs closure contraction beyond single-agent sector. Wrapping coerces Class 3 → Class 1 at the composite boundary. Adversarial is the signed negation of persistence; tempo advantage $T^2$ / $T^{3/2}$.

### Meta-architecture (how the theory knows what it can claim)

Certificate spine: exists ⇔ exponential stability.  
**M2** where it exists (eight ladders: separable core / structured repair / general open).  
**M3** which form is forced (four layers on exponential-family Legendre–Fenchel).  
**M1** where it fails (four floors; Sylvester rank-collapse trio + projection Instance 3).  
Boundary sisters: **value-functional grounding** (agent; F+G two routes exhaust) and **implementation impossibility** (designer; GS/MS/Arrow).  
Styles atop the boundary: **constructive-impossibility posture** (floor / escape / apparatus) and **anti-collapse** (individuate at the repair-relevant grain).  
**M4** dynamics on architectural class: truthification / strategic self-coupling / adversarial pressure.  
**Composition consistency**: same form at every level; Tier-1M exact transfer.  
**Dynamic-regime axis** R0–R3 orthogonal to architectural class.

The spine is what the agent *does*. The meta-architecture is how the theory *knows what it can claim*. Both were always there; both are now visible.
