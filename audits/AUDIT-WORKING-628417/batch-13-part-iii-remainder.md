## What landed

### Composition consistency = honesty applied vertically

If you can draw the boundary at multiple levels, the theory cannot change its mind because you changed the boundary. Form is level-invariant; parameters ($\alpha$, $\rho$, $R$) level-specific. Three layers: scope → admissibility → transfer.

Tier 1M is exact: parallel $\lambda_c = \min_i \lambda_i$ (team as slow as slowest). Tier 2/3 keep the screening test $\tau_{\rm eq} \ll \tau_{\rm ext}$. Brooks's Law becomes: adding people raises $\varepsilon^*\nu_c$ and stretches $\tau_{\rm eq}$ while the deadline ($\tau_{\rm ext}$) does not move until persistence flips.

Bureaucracy / SOP as Tier-3→Tier-1M *coercion* so the composite can hold a certificate against a fast environment — truthification at organizational scale.

### M4 complete: three operations on modularity

| Operation | Driver | $\kappa$ | Valence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truthification | self | ↓ | restores diagnostics (wrapping + institutional scaffolding) |
| Strategic self-coupling | self | ↑ | enables credibility-dependent actions |
| Adversarial pressure | external | ↑ | expands attack surface |

**Strategic self-coupling** was the missing polarity. Schelling commitment, Ainslie personal rules, identity economics, Frank emotions — same shape: exchange fungibility for credibility; $c_{\rm uncouple} \gg c_{\rm couple}$. (M1) $\mathcal{A}(\kappa)$; (M2) enabling edges in $\Sigma$; (M3) reversibility asymmetry. Inverted-U: over-coupling forecloses reality-dependent actions.

**Adversarial pressure:** identity-binding / affect-urgency / sunk-cost → cascade inversion (O first, then observation flows against the gradient). Diagnostic capture is directional: inflate $\delta_{\rm regret}$ to waste effort; inflate $\delta_{\rm sat}$ to force abandonment. Defensive scaffolding = composite-level dual of wrapping (externalize orient channels). Population for Class-3 analysis expands: *any* agent under sustained pressure, not only native Coupled architectures.

Dual: truthification wraps belief (goal-blind update); self-coupling wraps action (goal-conditioned commitment).

### Composite scope: four routes, not a scalar

(C-i) same goal · (C-ii) hierarchy · (C-iii) mutual benefit · (C-iv) equilibrium convergence. Disjunction is structural honesty — C-iv is strategic composite with macro-state relative to $\mathcal{E}$, not shared $O_c$. Routes map to dynamic-regime entry (C-i–iii → R0; C-iv α' → R1; C-iv β' → R2). No unifying $U_O$ threshold.

### Unity is compressibility, not "goodness"

Content ($U_M, U_O, U_\Sigma, U_{\rm obs}$) × structure ($U_f$). Heterogeneous Kalman: $\varepsilon_x \propto |\Delta K|$ even at perfect content correlation — **culture-fit is $U_f$**. Anti-collapse: $(U_O, U_\Sigma)$ jointly own $\varepsilon_a$ — re-aligning targets cannot fix path divergence. Clausewitz knowledge/alignment/effects ≈ $U_M$ / $U_O$ / ($U_\Sigma$+$U_{\rm obs}$).

Rate-distortion: unity parametrizes the surface; $\varepsilon$ sits on it given compression aggressiveness. Representability ≠ optimality (two independent Kalman filters: $\varepsilon^*=0$ but suboptimal).

### Shared intent + Auftragstaktik

IB of $G_t$ for coordination: purpose before plans before models. $B_O > B_\Sigma > B_M$ under slow-O / fast-M + local adaptive capacity. Ordering **reverses** when local models insufficient (fog of war).

**Standout:** AI agent teams may *invert* the human ordering — $B_M$ cheap (shared weights/DB), $B_O$ hard (alignment). Substrate-dependent cost structure, not universal human doctrine.

### Communication gain = trust formalized

$$\eta_{ji}^* = \frac{U_{M_i}}{U_{M_i}+U_{o,ji}+U_{\rm src,j}+U_{\rm align,ji}}$$

Channel / source / relationship are independent levers. Trust is a meta-model under full AAT apparatus. Risk-asymmetric: high-stakes use conservative quantile. Additive model is defender-side; attacker optimizing over trust dynamics is external game theory.

### Team persistence + tempo composition

Two coops, different slots: communication → $\mathcal{T}$; cooperative action → $-\rho$. Double-count is systematic optimism.

Tempo: $\mathcal{T}_c \leq \mathcal{T}_{\rm joint}$; joint ≶ sum depending on noise structure. Super-additive triangulation exact witness ($\rho_{\rm noise}<0$). Common-source echo chamber saturates. **Diverse allies over many allies** is structural.

### Recipient regimes + opacity dual

Emitter sees scalar $\gamma\mathcal{T}$; recipient sees I / II-a / II-b / III. Opposite repairs: more tempo vs structural adaptation vs infrastructure filter. Regime-I term *negative* in $\rho^{\rm eff}$ (cooperation falls out of classification).

$H_b = H(a_{A,t+\tau}\mid\mathcal{F}_B^t)$: dual of $U_o$. Epistemic + aleatoric split mirrors $U_M+U_o$. Sign-flip from signed $\gamma$ — not a new posit. Tempo × opacity product; 16-cell edge-targeting arg-max closes the GAP. Class-3 agents architecturally higher $H_b$.

### Dynamic-regime axis

| R | Fixed point | Certificate | Macro-state | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | unique $X^*$ | joint Lyapunov | state | C-i–iii |
| R1 | $\mathcal{E}$ | potential/monotone | equilibrium object | C-iv + α' |

Finite-$N$: regime ≡ macro-state type. R3 breaks identity. **Descent is default; ascent costs more** (symbiogenesis R1→R0; mechanism design R2→R1). Implementation-impossibility = floors on regime *ascent*.

## Experiential synthesis of Part III

Part III is where AAT becomes **political / organizational / military theory without leaving the math**.

One signed inequality covers team and adversary. One contested modularity state has three operations. One dual ($U_o \leftrightarrow H_b$) covers sensing and being-sensed. Unity is rate-distortion on composition. Regime axis is orthogonal to architectural class — Cournot can be Class-1 architecturally and R1 dynamically.

The honest GAPs (latent structural diversity, endogenous $\gamma$, Miller transitions, population R3) sit where the classification says open should sit — not as failures of ambition.
