**The walk's first candidate math-error finding in an exact-status result, and it's in the headline sentence.**

**1–3. Verification.**

**(a) The "if and only if" is false in the only-if direction [mine; high confidence; burden-of-proof worked below].** The segment derives, as the headline: *"the agent persists if and only if $\alpha \gt \rho/R$"* (and the appendix's Prop A.1 closes with the same "iff"). The **if** direction is proven (Lyapunov, verified at 25). The **only-if** direction is unproven and is *false in general*, because $\alpha$ is a worst-case floor that may be slack along every actual escape path. **Counterexample:** let $F(\delta) = \alpha\delta$ for $\lVert\delta\rVert \leq \varepsilon$ and $F(\delta) = B\delta$ for $\lVert\delta\rVert \gt \varepsilon$, with $B \geq \rho/\varepsilon$ and $\alpha \lt \rho/R$ (both choosable). The canonical (tightest) sector constant on $\mathcal B_R$ is $\inf_{\mathcal B_R} \delta^T F/\lVert\delta\rVert^2 = \alpha$, so the persistence condition **fails**. Yet for any admissible disturbance ($\lVert w\rVert \leq \rho$): outside $\mathcal B_\varepsilon$ the net radial drift is $\leq \rho - B\lVert\delta\rVert \leq \rho - B\varepsilon \leq 0$, so every trajectory is driven into and held within $\mathcal B_{\max(\varepsilon,\, \rho/B)} = \mathcal B_\varepsilon \subset \mathcal B_R$ — **the agent persists, for all admissible disturbances**, with $\alpha \leq \rho/R$. Hence: $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ is *sufficient for guaranteed persistence*; it is **not necessary** for persistence (nor even for guaranteed persistence — the counterexample's persistence is itself worst-case-guaranteed). The only-if *does* hold in the **linear case** ($F = \mathcal T\delta$: the ratio is constant, an outward-aligned worst-case $w$ forces $\delta_{ss} = \rho/\mathcal T \gt R$), which is presumably where the "iff" intuition was minted before the sector generalization — the generalization kept the quantifier word while invalidating it. **Repairs** (strengthen-first ordering): (1) state the honest scoped iff — "for linear/tight-sector correction, iff; for general sector correction, the certificate is sufficient"; (2) or redefine the claim as *certificate-iff* ("the sector certificate guarantees persistence iff $\alpha \gt \rho/R$" — true but near-tautological and should say so); (3) minimal: "persists if" + one sentence naming why necessity fails (slack floors). Severity: **high-visibility, medium-consequence** — no downstream derivation I've walked *uses* the only-if (they all consume the sufficient direction), but the iff phrasing is the framework's single most-quoted sentence shape ("persistence is not a degree, it's a regime" leans on it) and a referee will find this in an afternoon. It also *retroactively grades* the intro's "below the threshold, mismatch grows without effective bound" (ledger lxvi) from wording-issue to same-error-different-clothes. Counterevidence search: the appendix (walked at 25) proves only the if-direction; nothing in `#form-sector-condition`, the gold harvests, or the prior-audit trail addresses necessity — indeed the harvest note here says the math "verified flawlessly across every substrate," which is accurate *for the computations*: the quantifier word was never attacked.
