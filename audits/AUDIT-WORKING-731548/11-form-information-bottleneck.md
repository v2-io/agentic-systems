**3. Math verification (run, not paraphrased).**
- **$\beta$-vs-$\rho$ double-counting argument: verified.** Under $\min_\phi [I(M;\mathcal C) - \beta I(M; Y)]$, components of $\mathcal C_t$ whose MI with $Y$ is degraded by volatility cost compression budget while buying no predictive term, so the optimum drops them *at any fixed $\beta$*. The claim follows from the objective's structure, not from plausibility. (472913's hard-check concurs; independent verification here.)
- **"Markov chain $Y - X - T$ holds by construction" — true, but only *because* $\phi$ is deterministic, and this entangles with the exactness claim [mine; the segment's real soft spot].** With deterministic $\phi$, $\sigma(M_t) \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal C_t)$, so $Y \perp T \mid X$ holds trivially for *any* $Y$. ✓. But Tishby's theorem — the thing "applied exactly" — is stated over *stochastic encoders* $p(t\mid x)$, and its self-consistent-equations optimum is generically a kernel, not a map. Now the closed loop bites: for a **stochastic** encoder in an *interactive* agent, the chain **fails** — $M_t$ then carries randomness beyond $\mathcal C_t$, future actions $a_{t:\infty} = \pi(M_t,\ldots)$ depend on that extra randomness, and future observations inherit it: $Y \not\perp T \mid X$. So the two halves of the segment's warrant can't both be had: *deterministic $\phi$* ⇒ chain holds but the displayed problem is the deterministic special case, not the cited theorem; *stochastic kernel* ⇒ cited theorem's form, but the chain no longer holds by construction in the closed loop (it requires the open-loop/exogenous-action reading, which the $\pi_{\text{cont}}$ convention partially supplies but the body doesn't connect). The repair is one honest paragraph: "AAT restricts to deterministic encoders, for which the chain is automatic and the IB objective is the deterministic special case; the kernel-level theorem applies under the $\pi_{\text{cont}}$ open-loop convention." Convergence note: 526815's gold item caught the deterministic-vs-kernel half (sharp, and verified against the 1999 paper by them); the closed-loop stochastic-encoder failure of the chain — i.e., why the two issues are *one* issue — is my addition. Status-label consequence: `status: exact` is right for the imported theorem, over-tight for the displayed equation as warranted. Medium severity, small fix.
- **Conditional-MI typing note [mine, minor]:** $I(M_t; o_{t+1:\infty} \mid a_{t:\infty})$ conditions on an *infinite endogenous* action sequence. Under the open-loop convention this is fine; under closed loop it's the territory where plain conditional MI wants replacing by directed-information machinery (Massey). A footnote-grade flag; the policy-relativity paragraph is where it would live.
