**2/3. Consistency + math verification — two real items.**

(a) **The sup is type-broken as displayed [mine].** $\mathcal F(\mathcal M) = \sup_{M \in \mathcal M} S(M)$ quantifies over *elements* of the model space, but $S$'s definition needs its argument to be the *random object* $M_t = \phi(\mathcal C_t)$ (the numerator conditions on it). Read literally with $M$ a fixed element, conditioning on a constant is vacuous: $I(\mathcal C; Y \mid M, a) = I(\mathcal C; Y \mid a)$, so $S(M) = 0$ for every fixed $M$ and the displayed sup equals 0. The intended object is $\sup_{\phi:\, \mathrm{range}(\phi) \subseteq \mathcal M} S(\phi(\mathcal C_t))$ — the class constrains the *codomain* of admissible compressions. The prose (intro and body) says the right thing; the formula doesn't. Small, mechanical, but this is the hinge quantity for "one of the framework's central results," and the fix is one line. (The gold's inheritance item — write $\mathcal F(\mathcal M; \mathcal C_t, \pi_{\text{cont}})$ — is adjacent and also right; neither of its five substrates caught the elementwise-sup issue.)

(b) **The detection-signature conflates the structural floor with the noise floor [mine; the segment's substantive gap].** Discussion: "persistent mismatch despite adequate learning … is the observable signature" of low $\mathcal F$. But $S$ (hence $\mathcal F$) is a *normalized fraction* of attainable predictive information — in an irreducibly noisy world the denominator is small and an agent can sit at $S = \mathcal F = 1$ *while its absolute mismatch floor stays high forever*. Persistent mismatch despite adequate learning is therefore the signature of (low $\mathcal F$) **or** (high irreducible noise with perfectly fine $\mathcal F$), and acting on the signature as stated risks exactly the catastrophic thrashing the gold's reader-questions worry about (rewriting the codebase that just lives in a noisy domain). The framework owns the disentangling tool — `#result-mismatch-decomposition` splits model error from observation noise, and the appendix carries `#internal-external-decomposition` (reducible vs irreducible floor) — but this segment's trigger paragraph cites neither. The honest signature is "persistent mismatch *in excess of the irreducible floor* despite adequate learning." This also *answers* the most-convergent reader question in the gold (low-$\mathcal F$ vs high-$\rho$), which none of the prior audits answered — they all pointed forward to Ch.4 hoping. Medium-high confidence; the claim is checkable against the Ch.4 result when I reach it (if `#result-structural-adaptation-necessity` states its hypothesis in terms of the mismatch floor *net of noise*, the gap is localized to this segment's Discussion; if it doesn't, the gap propagates into an inevitability-core result's trigger condition).
