# Verify 04 — scope-adaptive-system exclusion equivalence (fully-observed-MDP counterexample)

**Claim under test** (AUDIT-WORKING-731548 `06-scope-adaptive-system.md` §2, §14): the exclusion prose in `#scope-adaptive-system` chains three non-equivalent things — $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = 0$ ⇒ "complete knowledge of the environment" ⇒ "known dynamics / solved" — and the predicate as written excludes the fully-observed unknown-kernel MDP (tabular RL) from adaptive scope, unless $\Omega$ is read to include the law of evolution $T$ (a reading no segment states).

**Verifier posture:** independent, adversarial, full historical access (git, spikes, audits, msc, sibling segments). Refutation attempted on every leg.

## VERDICT: REAL-AND-OPEN

The logical gap is confirmed; the counterexample arithmetic is correct; no prior audit, spike, TODO entry, or proposal covers it; the totality-reading rescue is stated nowhere in canon or the working record; and the proposed fix survives quadrant-checking better than the alternatives — but it is not the one-paragraph no-op the claim suggests (two real side-effect sites found). Severity is medium: no downstream derivation consumes the exclusion sentence quantitatively, but the framework's broadest membership test misclassifies a canonical system class, and the same state-knowledge/law-knowledge conflation appears in the root definition's rationale. Disposition: route as a first-class finding + architectural micro-proposal; the fix touches a root definition and is not a unilateral direct-fix (SP-24 precedent).

## 1. The arithmetic holds

`#def-chronica` fixes $\mathcal C_t = (o_1, a_1, \ldots, a_{t-1}, o_t)$ — the conditioning *includes* $o_t$. So for any fully-observed system ($o_t = \Omega_t$), $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = 0$ for all $t$, regardless of whether $T$ is known, unknown, deterministic, or stochastic. The predicate is filtering entropy, and full observation kills it. Meanwhile `#def-mismatch-signal` defines mismatch as prediction error $\delta_t = o_t - \hat o_t$ with $\hat o_t = \mathbb E[o_t \mid M_{t-1}, a_{t-1}]$ — which is alive and non-trivial under full observation with unknown $T$ (it is exactly the TD-error-shaped signal tabular RL runs on), so the machinery the scope exists to warrant is *not* vacuous for the excluded case. The three-link chain in the Discussion ("$H = 0$ ⇒ complete knowledge ⇒ known dynamics, solved") is wrong at both links on the natural (state-only) reading of $\Omega$. Confirmed.

## 2. The conflation is systemic, not segment-local — and the exclusion is *deliberate with a false rationale*

The strongest refutation candidate was: *the fully-observed case is deliberately scoped out at the root, so the exclusion prose is intent, not error.* This is half true. `#def-agent-environment` states the information-loss boundary as constitutive: "a system with direct access to full environment state is outside AAT's scope … because for it the entire adaptive machinery (mismatch signal, model maintenance, correction) becomes vacuous." The exclusion of full-state access is therefore deliberate and repeated. But the stated rationale is false for the unknown-$T$ case by the framework's own definitions (§1 above): model maintenance under full observation with unknown dynamics is precisely non-vacuous — it is what model-based tabular RL *is*. So the deliberateness does not rescue correctness; it shows the same conflation sits in the root definition's justification, not just in the scope segment's Discussion. This *upgrades* the finding rather than refuting it.

## 3. The hyperstate / totality reading is stated nowhere

Searched: all four components' `src/`, `spikes/`, `msc/`, NOTATION.md, LEXICON-generating entries, `_obs/`, prior-art files, and git history (`-S` pickaxe). No segment states $T \subseteq \Omega$ or any equivalent. The nearest moves in the record, none of which is the rescue:

- `#def-action-transition`'s Markov-of-$\Omega$ paragraph licenses extending $\Omega$ to include *history* ("commitments about the breadth of the named object") — the same *family* of move, but history-augmentation, not law-inclusion.
- NOTATION.md glosses $\Omega$ as "Environment state (unobservable totality)" — the word *unobservable* leans slightly toward the totality reading, but the same table lists $T$ as a separate object, and `#def-action-transition` makes "the agent does not know $T$ exactly" a *separate constitutive atom* — which would be redundant if unknown-$T$ were already carried inside $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$. The corpus's explicit structure treats $T$ as outside $\Omega$.
- Working-Notes gold (773921, at `#def-action-transition`) resolves the LLM transition-opacity puzzle by noting "$\Omega$ also includes the human user / API whose dynamics are not known" — $\Omega$-breadth flexibility exercised informally, never ratified as a rule.

**One canon site leans the other way.** `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate`'s bit-accounting distinguishes *environment-bits* (exploration reduces $H(\Omega_t \mid M_t)$) from *inference-bits* (deliberation reduces $H(\theta \mid M_t, \mathcal C_t)$) — latent structural parameters $\theta$ notated as a random object *distinct from* $\Omega_t$. Under the totality reading, $\theta$-uncertainty would live inside $H(\Omega_t \mid M_t)$ and the two channels would overlap on the same random object (still distinguishable as observation-driven vs computation-driven reduction, but the prose would need a clause). This is a real, small side-effect site the claim's "one paragraph, five downstream repairs" tally does not include.

## 4. How RL is actually scoped in: the implicit hyperstate move, exercised case-by-case

The claim's phrase "bread-and-butter case of the RL column" needed checking, and the answer is more interesting than either confirmation or refutation. Canon never actually instantiates a fully-observed MDP:

- `#example-bandit` (the RL worked example) sets $\Omega_t = (\mu_1(t), \ldots, \mu_4(t))$ — **the reward-law parameters themselves are the environment state**, "not directly observable," and its scope row is marked *Mapping: exact*. This is the hyperstate move performed de facto: $\Omega$ is *chosen* to be the unknown law-content, so $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ comes out state-shaped. No license for this choice is stated anywhere; it simply works because the modeler picked $\Omega$ well.
- `#example-kalman` has hidden $x_t$ (noisy channel) — in scope via genuine state uncertainty, no rescue needed.
- `#emp-update-gain`'s domain table row "RL (Q-learning): fixed learning rate $\alpha$" is generic; a reader will map it onto gridworld Q-learning (the paradigmatic Q-learning setting), which is exactly the excluded case.

So: canon's *worked* instantiations are individually consistent with the predicate (each picks a hidden-state $\Omega$), the *gestured* RL coverage is not, and the device that makes the worked cases succeed (put the unknown law-content inside $\Omega$) is exactly the totality reading — exercised silently, per-example, without a stated rule. This is strong evidence the framework's *intent* is the totality reading and the claim's proposed fix is a ratification of existing practice, not an innovation.

## 5. The Kalman constitutive-opacity tension: adjacent, known, and only half-resolved

- `#emp-update-gain` + `#deriv-adaptive-gain-dynamics` resolve the *gain-implementation* face of the tension: the agent estimates $U_o$, $U_M$ endogenously from innovation statistics, so using the optimal gain does not require violating epistemic opacity. Real resolution, but it covers *how the gain is computed*, not *whether known-model agents are in scope*.
- The *definitional* face is known and open: `#def-observation-function` Working Notes (gold lift, four substrates convergent) flag that "the agent knows neither $h$ nor the distribution of $\varepsilon_t$" is stronger than partial observability and that classical known-$R$ Kalman violates it — routed as a "candidate scope-clarification," watch-target `#emp-update-gain` / `#example-kalman`, **not resolved**.
- The verification-added sharpening: the scope predicate and the opacity atoms **dissociate in both directions**. Kalman is *in* scope by the predicate (hidden $x_t$ ⇒ $H \gt 0$) while *violating* the constitutive opacity atoms (handed $T$, $h$, $q$, $r$); the fully-observed unknown-$T$ MDP *satisfies* the opacity atoms' spirit while *failing* the predicate. Two flagship-adjacent cases fall on opposite sides of the two formalizations. That double dissociation is the cleanest demonstration that the scope condition and the opacity atoms are currently measuring different things, and it confirms the claim's §14 diagnosis: one missing definition (what random object is $\Omega_t$, and does it include $T$ and $h$) is being paid for in at least four segments.

## 7. Does anything rely on $T$ being outside $\Omega$? (Fix side-effect audit)

Checked reliance sites for the proposed totality-reading paragraph in `#def-agent-environment`:

- **NOTATION.md** lists $T$ separately — compatible; BAMDP-style treatments keep kernel notation while the unknown parameters sit in the (hyper)state.
- **`#post-causal-structure`** zero-coupling condition $T(\Omega_{t+1} \mid \Omega_t, a_t) = T(\Omega_{t+1} \mid \Omega_t)$ — expressible on the extended state unchanged.
- **GA-1 (fresh noise)** — conditioning on a *larger* $\Omega_t$ only strengthens it.
- **`#result-mismatch-decomposition`** step 1 cites the predicate; under the totality reading the predicate holds more broadly — no breakage (and that segment's own Working Notes already record that the algebra doesn't need the citation).
- **Side effect A (real, the claim understates it).** The claim says the opacity atoms of `#def-action-transition` / `#def-observation-function` "become theorems of the scope condition." Not quite: under the canonical hyperstate construction the *extended* kernel is exactly known ($\tilde T((\omega',\theta') \mid (\omega,\theta), a) = \mathbb 1[\theta' = \theta] \cdot T_\theta(\omega' \mid \omega, a)$ — known as a function), so "the agent does not know $T$ exactly" becomes *frame-relative* — false of $\tilde T$, true of the realized $T_\theta$. The atoms don't become theorems; they become *reinterpretations* (unknown-law ⇔ unobserved law-component of state), and `#def-action-transition`'s sentence "if $T$ were known, action selection would collapse to plain optimization" needs rewording (the extended kernel *is* known and action stays non-trivial, via state uncertainty). Coherent, and arguably cleaner — but it is a rewrite of two constitutive atoms, not a free corollary.
- **Side effect B (real, small).** The $\theta$-vs-$\Omega_t$ notational split in `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate` (§3 above).
- **Side effect C (interpretive, minor).** `03-llm-core` `#scope-observation-ambiguity-modulation` regime (i) has $H(\Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau) \to 0$ for "factually unambiguous" observations; under the totality reading that limit lives in the state-component only. The asymptotic-regime language survives; worth one look in the fix cycle.

**Quadrant check of the fix** (verification-added; the claim did not run this): under the totality reading the predicate classifies all four corners correctly — hidden-state/known-law (Kalman): in scope ✓; observed-state/unknown-law (gridworld RL): in scope ✓ (the repair target); observed-state/known-law, deterministic *or stochastic*: excluded ✓ — this last is the non-obvious win, since purely aleatoric systems (known stochastic MDP, fully observed) genuinely have nothing to *learn* and should be out. The alternative fix routes fail this check: (i) prose-only restatement ("state fully determined *and* dynamics known") repairs the Discussion sentence but leaves the formal set $\mathcal S_\text{adaptive}$ still misclassifying gridworld RL; (ii) shifting the predicate to one-step predictive entropy $H(\Omega_{t+1} \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ would *misinclude* the known-stochastic fully-observed case (irreducible aleatoric uncertainty is not something to adapt to). The totality reading is the only one of the three candidate repairs under which the predicate exactly tracks "some learnable thing remains unknown" — which is evidently the intended scope.

## 9. Corrections to the claim as posed

Three refinements, none verdict-changing:

1. "It's the bread-and-butter case of the RL column" overstates canon's commitment: canon's worked RL instantiations all pick hidden-state $\Omega$ (the bandit puts the law-parameters *in* $\Omega$, silently using the rescue); the fully-observed MDP is gestured at (Q-learning row), never instantiated. The finding is better stated as: *the predicate excludes a class the framework's domain tables invite the reader to include, and canon's own examples avoid the exclusion only by an unratified per-example modeling choice.*
2. "The opacity atoms become theorems of the scope condition" — they become *frame-relative reinterpretations* requiring rewording of two constitutive atoms (§7, side effect A). The fix is one paragraph plus a two-atom reconciliation plus two small notation-site touches, not one paragraph alone.
3. The fix's strongest argument was left implicit in the claim: the quadrant check (§7), under which the totality reading is the *unique* candidate repair that classifies all four knowledge/observability corners correctly, including excluding the purely-aleatoric case.
