## Part 2 — CIY "exactly Level 2 vs Level 1": refuted as substantive; small wording residue

The charge reads the sentence against an $\Omega$-structure notion of Level 2 that is not AAT's. **AAT's Level-2 query is observation-stated by design**: `#def-pearl-causal-hierarchy` line 37 gives Level 2 as $P(o_t \mid do(a_{t-1}), M_{t-1})$, and the framework's agency boundary is explicitly *observation-relative* — a choice several de-novo auditors independently flagged and praised (`#scope-agency` WN §4: *"a brilliant and necessary choice for a purely epistemic theory"*). Under that (the framework's own) reading, proprioceptive contrast is a genuine nonzero interventional contrast on the outcome distribution — Level-2 access that happens to be *about the observation channel*, exactly as `#scope-agency`'s channel-distinction paragraph classifies it. $\text{CIY} \gt 0$ $\iff$ the $do$-distributions differ across ($q$-supported) actions $\iff$ the Level-2 query is non-degenerate. The CIY segment claims *distinguishability*, never $\Omega$-structure learning — indeed its signature move ($\text{CIY} \neq \text{EIG}$; the beeping-button example) is precisely the disclaimer that positive CIY implies no learning. The claimant's own evidence ("high CIY, zero learning") is the segment's *stated* position, not a counterexample to it.

**Residue, honestly stated (two small items, neither finding-grade):**

1. "Exactly what distinguishes Level 2 from Level 1" is a pedagogical gloss with a genuine looseness independent of the proprioception angle: strictly, $\text{CIY} \gt 0$ marks Level-2 access being *non-vacuous/non-degenerate* (the contrast exists), not the L1/L2 *access* distinction itself (a system whose $do$-distributions all coincide still "has" a degenerate Level 2). `#scope-agency`'s own framing is the tighter one ("what makes Level-2 access non-vacuous" / "well-posed"). A one-clause sharpening — e.g. "exactly what makes Level-2 (interventional) access non-degenerate relative to Level-1 (associational)" — plus a cross-pointer to `#scope-agency`'s "Which contrast counts" paragraph would preempt this over-read. Untracked as such; below finding-grade.
2. The adjacent precision caveat — $\text{CIY} \gt 0$ also requires $q$ to put mass on contrasting alternatives — is **already tracked** in the segment's own Working Notes ("Is $\text{CIY} = 0$ for a passive observer a theorem or a convention?", AUDIT-WORKING-742613).

Cross-check: no certified finding targets the "exactly" sentence (searched `audits/audit-findings-*.md`, TODO, PROPOSALS); the CIY segment's audit record is dominated by *positive-confirmation* findings (849201 F2-trail: the CIY-vs-EIG honesty called "exemplary"; 451729: "a more careful epistemic position than I expected"). The naming-residue (CIY connotes learning-gain) is separately tracked to the terminology workflow (471203 ledger S7).
