# Independent verification: the persistence "iff" (finding lxxiv)

**Verifier:** independent adversarial pass, full-history access. **Date:** 2026-07-02. **Claim tested:** the only-if direction of "the agent persists iff $\alpha \gt \rho/R$" is unproven and false in general under the sector generalization ( `#result-sector-condition-stability` headline, `#result-persistence-condition`, `#deriv-sector-condition` Prop A.1 closing).

## Verdict: REAL-AND-OPEN — with a sharper characterization than the finding's own framing

The finding survives adversarial verification, but it is best stated as a **quantifier-register defect with the strong reading recoverable**, not as "the iff is false." Canon carries *two* readings of "persists," and its own definitional layer already uses the one under which the iff is true; the derivation-layer and result-layer prose use the one under which the only-if is false. No prior cycle handled this; nothing downstream consumes the bad direction; the project has an exact precedent (AF-2) for the correct repair shape.

## 1. The mathematics — counterexample verified, with one patch

The counterexample is correct. Independently re-derived and numerically simulated (worst-case outward $w \equiv \rho$, $\alpha = 0.5 \lt \rho/R = 1.0$, $B = 1.2\,\rho/\varepsilon$): the tightest sector constant on $\mathcal B_R$ is $\alpha$, the persistence condition fails, and every trajectory from $\mathcal B_R$ is driven into and held near $\mathcal B_\varepsilon$ — persistence holds for *all* admissible disturbances. One patch needed before canon use: $F$ as stated is discontinuous at $\lVert\delta\rVert = \varepsilon$; a mollified radial gain $F(\delta) = c(\lVert\delta\rVert)\,\delta$ with $c$ ramping continuously from $\alpha$ to $B$ preserves the conclusion and satisfies (A1)/(A3). The mechanism is exactly as claimed: $\alpha$ is a worst-case floor over $\mathcal B_R$ (canon itself defines it so — `#form-sector-condition` §Parameter interpretation: "*the worst-case correction efficiency within the valid region — the minimum ratio*"), and a floor attained only where correction is locally strong-elsewhere cannot certify escape.

Two confirmations of scope:

- **The only-if is nowhere proven.** Prop A.1's proof derives positive invariance + ultimate boundedness (sufficiency) only; the closing sentence "The agent persists (from within $\mathcal B_R$) iff $R^\ast \lt R$" precedes the $\square$ with no supporting argument. Decisively: the segment's own *What Is Derived vs. What Is Chosen* table — its honesty ledger — has **no necessity row**. It claims "ultimate bound $R^\ast = \rho/\alpha$ — Proved (conditional on $R^\ast \lt R$)" and nothing more. The appendix's own accounting does not back its closing sentence.
- **The strong reading is recoverable as a real (small) theorem.** Under the Lur'e absolute-stability semantics canon inherits (Lur'e 1957 is the cited framework), the sector data $(\alpha, R)$ names an *uncertainty class*, and the correct necessity statement is class-level tightness: for $\alpha \leq \rho/R$, the class member $F = \alpha\delta$ with outward-aligned $w = \rho\,\delta/\lVert\delta\rVert$ drives $\delta$ to the region boundary ($\delta_{ss} = \rho/\alpha \geq R$), so no persistence guarantee over the A2'$(\alpha,R)$-class exists. Hence: **$\alpha \gt \rho/R$ is necessary and sufficient for the certificate to guarantee persistence uniformly over the sector class** — true, tight, and non-tautological (it requires exhibiting the extremal member). The per-system iff additionally holds in the linear/tight-sector case, as the finding says.

## 2. Does canon's fuller picture dissolve it? Partially — and that partial context must shape the repair

- **The definitional layer already uses the certificate reading.** `terminology/entries/structural-persistence.md`: structural persistence is "whether the correction machinery has sufficient gain … to keep the mismatch bounded *in principle*. A property of the architecture, not of the agent's current trajectory." `#result-sector-persistence-template` parenthetically defines it as "the ultimate bound fits within the sector-condition region" — i.e. $R^\ast \lt R$ — under which "persists iff $\alpha \gt \rho/R$" is trivially-true algebra. So the framework's *considered* answer to "what does persists mean here?" is the certificate reading, under which there is no false claim.
- **Canon already half-knows the non-necessity.** `#form-sector-condition` §"Why Euclidean A2' specifically" concedes via converse-Lyapunov (Khalil Thm 4.17) that an agent can persist while Euclidean A2'-with-given-$\alpha$ is not the operative certificate — along the *metric* axis. The counterexample is the sibling failure along the *radial-slack* axis. This is corroborating context, not contradiction.
- **Downstream never consumes the per-system only-if.** Grep across all four components: no segment reasons "agent failed $\Rightarrow$ $\alpha$ was below threshold." `#der-adversarial-destabilization` explicitly hedges to the worst-case register ("the conditions under which $A$ *can* push $B$ past its stability boundary … a worst-case bound"). `#result-structural-adaptation-necessity`'s necessity runs through the information-theoretic fitness floor ($\mathcal F(\mathcal M) \lt 1-\varepsilon$ $\Rightarrow$ irreducible residual), not through the persistence iff. The blast radius is prose, not derivation.
- **But the trajectory-register sites are genuinely wrong as written**, and one goes beyond loose shorthand: `#result-persistence-condition` asserts (summary ¶2 and Discussion §"Below structural threshold") "*When this inequality fails, mismatch grows without effective bound*" — an unhedged per-system behavioral claim that the counterexample falsifies (with $\alpha$ = the tightest floor, per canon's own definition of $\alpha$). That is the necessity direction asserted as fact, not merely an ambiguous "iff."
