## F1 — Corollary 4 is false as formalized (HIGH; the segment's only substantive defect)

**The defect.** The goal-intervention family is defined as $\iota_g(m, \gamma) = (m, g)$ for $g \in \mathcal G^\dagger \subseteq \mathcal G$, and $\sim_{\mathrm{cf}}$ as the equivalence *generated by* $\{X \sim \iota_g X : g \in \mathcal G^\dagger\}$ with $X$ ranging over all of $\mathcal X$. Under that definition, for **any nonempty** $\mathcal G^\dagger$, every goal value is identified with every other: for arbitrary $\gamma, \gamma'$ and any single $g \in \mathcal G^\dagger$,

$$(m, \gamma) \sim \iota_g(m, \gamma) = (m, g) = \iota_g(m, \gamma') \sim (m, \gamma').$$

So the classes of $\sim_{\mathrm{cf}}$ are $\{m\} \times \mathcal G$ — *identical to full access* — whenever $\mathcal G^\dagger \neq \emptyset$. Corollary 4's claimed classes $\{m\} \times R(\gamma)$ and canonical epistemic component $\mathcal M \times (\mathcal G / R)$ never arise; the only degenerate case is $\mathcal G^\dagger = \emptyset$, where $\sim_{\mathrm{cf}}$ is equality and the "epistemic component" is all of $\mathcal X$. The boundary story — "purposeful distinctions the intervention family cannot move classify as epistemic," "Proposition 1(b) is exactly this corollary's boundary case," the LLM weights-vs-prompt prediction — is unsupported as formalized. (I also checked the charitable alternative reading, relating only intervention-images to each other: classes $\{m\} \times \mathcal G^\dagger \cup$ singletons — still not $\{m\} \times R(\gamma)$, and still not the frozen-coordinate semantics. Both readings fail.)

**Root cause.** Value-targeted resets indexed by a *subset of target values* cannot express "cannot reach coordinate $c$": any two goal values are joined through one common target. The intended family — prompt-level goal-setting that leaves weight-baked content untouched — is a family of **maps on the goal fiber that preserve the non-intervenable sub-coordinate**, e.g. $\iota : (m, (c, g_{\mathrm{rest}})) \mapsto (m, (c, g'_{\mathrm{rest}}))$, whose *orbits* are the $R$-classes. The corollary's own parenthetical closure condition ("$f_G(g, \cdot, \cdot)$ preserves reachability classes") is evidence the author was reasoning with orbit semantics that the definition does not deliver.

**Strengthen direction (noted, not executed — verifier is not the integrator).** Redefine the intervention family as a set $H$ of goal-fiber maps ($\iota_h(m, \gamma) = (m, h(\gamma))$, $h \in H$), with full access the special case $H = \{$constants$\}$; take $R$ = orbit equivalence of $H$ (symmetrized). The commutation argument of Theorem 2(i) then transfers under the stated closure condition, the classes are genuinely $\{m\} \times R(\gamma)$, and both the "two halves are one phenomenon" unification and the LLM corollary are restored. I did not verify this repaired version end-to-end; it is a direction, and its closure condition deserves its own check.
