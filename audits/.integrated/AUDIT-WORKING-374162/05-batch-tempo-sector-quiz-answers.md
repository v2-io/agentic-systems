## (1) Critical Mental Model

### A b05-1.1 [mental-model]
Tempo = event rate × update gain, summed per channel: $\mathcal T = \sum_k \nu^{(k)}\eta^{(k)\ast}$ — speed times quality, multiplicative. The non-compensable deficiency: **observation noise**. Since $\eta^\ast = U_M/(U_M+U_o)$, high $U_o$ collapses gain and therefore tempo regardless of $\nu$ — "you cannot outrun a bad observation channel by iterating faster" (the derivable form of Boyd's Orient-quality-over-OODA-speed).

### A b05-1.2 [mental-model]
Model D (bounded drift): $\Vert\delta\Vert_{ss} = \rho/\mathcal T$ — linear in correction; doubling tempo halves mismatch. Model S (stochastic): $\Vert\delta\Vert_{rms} = \sigma_w/\sqrt{2\mathcal T}$ — square-root; doubling tempo buys only ~30%. Consequence: against drift, tempo investment pays linearly (read the changelog, track the maneuver); against noise, tempo has sharply diminishing returns — attack $\sigma_w$ at its source because you can't out-tempo a square root. *(WN bonus: the changelog-vs-flaky-architecture software casting is Working-Notes gold; the two scaling laws and their contrast are body text.)*

### A b05-1.3 [mental-model]
Plain: within its operating region, the agent's correction always pushes mismatch inward with at least a baseline efficiency. Shapes accommodated that linearity can't: **saturating** correction ($\alpha$ decreasing at large $\Vert\delta\Vert$), **threshold/dead-zone** correction ($\alpha = 0$ below a small mismatch), and breakdown-outside-region (correction failing beyond $\mathcal B_R$ — the structural-adaptation regime). $R$ = model class capacity: how large mismatch can grow before the correction mechanism (certifiably) fails.

### A b05-1.4 [mental-model]
(1) **Necessity is class-level, not agent-level** (Lemma A.1N): the condition is necessary-and-sufficient for the *uniform guarantee over the whole sector class*; for a fixed agent it is an iff only when the sector bound is radially tight (e.g. linear) — in general, condition-failure does not certify escape. (2) **What is lost at the threshold is the certificate, not boundedness itself**: "persistence is a regime, not a degree" means the containment *guarantee* disappears; the summary's "survives iff" reads a certificate claim as a behavior claim.

### A b05-1.5 [mental-model]
Structural persistence: the correction machinery *works* — bounded mismatch is guaranteed ($\alpha \gt \rho/R$). Task adequacy: the steady-state mismatch is small enough for the agent's actions to remain useful. AAT *derives* the structural condition; the task-adequacy threshold is a **domain parameter** (what counts as "tracking" for the application). Conflating them produces category errors in domain transfer — e.g. declaring a stable-but-useless filter "persistent therefore adequate," or reading task failure as structural failure and rebuilding machinery that works.

### A b05-1.6 [mental-model]
Deliberate while (gain improvement × post-deliberation mismatch) exceeds (pause-window drift rate × duration); stop when the *marginal* improvement rate falls below the drift rate normalized by post-deliberation mismatch. Both slogans are regime-optimal: high $\rho_{\text{delib}}$ (world moves fast during pauses) makes thinking net-harmful quickly — action bias is correct; low $\rho_{\text{delib}}$ with large mismatch makes deliberation a high-return investment — deliberation bias is correct. Neither is universal; each optimizes a different drift regime.

### A b05-1.7 [mental-model]
Sustained information acquisition: under stochastic disturbance, maintaining the persistence bound requires Shannon information at rate $\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$ nats per unit time (Kalman-Bucy saturates it). Survival is a *sustained burn rate*, not an achieved state: cut off from informative observation, the agent drifts immediately at the disturbance rate — independent of how good it was before the channel closed.

## (2) Mathematics

### A b05-2.1 [math]
Setup: mismatch $\delta \in \mathbb R^n$; correction function $F(\mathcal T, \delta)$ mapping into mismatch space so $\delta^T F$ is defined. (A1): $F(\mathcal T, 0) = 0$. (A2'): $\exists\, \mathcal B_R = \{\Vert\delta\Vert \leq R\}$, $\alpha \gt 0$ with $\delta^T F(\mathcal T,\delta) \geq \alpha\Vert\delta\Vert^2\ \forall \delta \in \mathcal B_R$. (A3): $\delta^T F$ monotone increasing in $\mathcal T$ for fixed $\delta$. Linear case: $\alpha = \mathcal T$.

### A b05-2.2 [math]
$V = \tfrac12\Vert\delta\Vert^2$; $\dot V = -\delta^T F + \delta^T w \leq -\alpha\Vert\delta\Vert^2 + \rho\Vert\delta\Vert = -\Vert\delta\Vert(\alpha\Vert\delta\Vert - \rho)$, using (A2') and Cauchy–Schwarz with $\Vert w\Vert \leq \rho$. So $\dot V \lt 0$ for $\rho/\alpha \lt \Vert\delta\Vert \leq R$; ultimate bound $R^\ast = \rho/\alpha$. Invariance: at $\Vert\delta\Vert = R$, $\dot V = -R(\alpha R - \rho) \lt 0$ when $\alpha R \gt \rho$, so trajectories point inward and $\mathcal B_R$ is positively invariant. Scope: the trajectory must *start inside* $\mathcal B_R$ — outside it A2' doesn't hold and nothing is claimed (that is the structural-adaptation regime).

### A b05-2.3 [math]
For a trajectory started in $\mathcal B_R$ under (A1)/(A2')/(A3): $P(\tau_R \lt \infty) = 0$ under Model D (bounded $\Vert w\Vert \leq \rho$, given $\alpha R \gt \rho$ — deterministic positive invariance) and $= 1$ under Model S (additive stochastic $\sigma_w dW_t$, for *every* $\alpha \gt 0$, every $\sigma_w \gt 0$, every A2'-satisfying $F$ — a non-degenerate diffusion exits any bounded region a.s.). The achievable set is exactly $\{0,1\}$, selected by the disturbance model's support structure. **$\alpha$ cannot move the value**; what $\alpha$ buys under Model S is the typical scale ($R_S^\ast = \sigma_w\sqrt{n/2\alpha}$) and the fixed-time tail ($\leq n\sigma_w^2/2\alpha R^2$).

### A b05-2.4 [math]
Class-level: over the hypothesis class $\mathcal F_\alpha$ of all A2'-satisfying correction functions with floor $\alpha$, the condition is necessary and sufficient for the *uniform* containment guarantee (witness for necessity: the extremal member $F = \alpha\delta$ under outward radial disturbance escapes in finite time when $\alpha \lt \rho/R$). Agent-level iff holds exactly under a **radially tight** sector ($\bar\alpha = \alpha$, upper sector bound equal to the floor — the linear case). Killer counterexample: a correction profile with a narrow dip (floor $\alpha = 0.1$, tenfold condition failure) whose high-correction annulus *below* the dip is an impenetrable barrier — no admissible disturbance reaches the weak radii. True 1-D escape threshold: a **mountain-pass** quantity — $\rho$ must exceed $\max_r F(r)$ over the radii the trajectory must traverse — not the global floor $\alpha R$.

### A b05-2.5 [math]
Conditions: (1) cross-channel noise independence (additivity then *derived* exact); (2) shared eigenbasis / isotropy (for the scalar form; else the tensor form is the correct object). Trap answer: **no** — the additive form is *not* a general upper bound. The deviation is **signed**: common-source (echo-chamber) correlation makes it overcount (closed-form redundancy penalty; saturation at the shared-bias floor under persistent bias), but noise-cancelling/synergistic configurations make it **undercount**. The upper-bound reading was explicitly refuted in the corpus (a sign-blind measure like CMI cannot even carry the correction).

### A b05-2.6 [math]
Threshold: $\Delta\eta^\ast(\Delta\tau)\cdot\Vert\delta_{\text{post}}\Vert \gt \rho_{\text{delib}}\cdot\Delta\tau$. FOC (treating $\Vert\delta_{\text{post}}\Vert$ as a pre-estimated parameter): $\frac{\partial\Delta\eta^\ast}{\partial\Delta\tau}\cdot\Vert\delta_{\text{post}}\Vert = \rho_{\text{delib}}$ (with a $(1-\Delta\eta^\ast)$ correction factor when the $\Delta\tau$-dependence of $\delta_{\text{post}}$ is included; negligible for $\Delta\eta^\ast \ll 1$). Conditional on: the **local deliberation-drift assumption** (mismatch grows at approximately constant rate $\rho_{\text{delib}}$ during pause windows) — a short-horizon claim about inaction windows, *weaker than* the global linear mismatch ODE.

### A b05-2.7 [math]
(i) Stopped second moment: $\mathbb E[\Vert\delta(t\wedge\tau_R)\Vert^2] \leq \Vert\delta(0)\Vert^2 e^{-2\alpha t} + n\sigma_w^2/2\alpha$ — steady state $n\sigma_w^2/2\alpha$ (RMS $\sigma_w\sqrt{n/2\alpha}$). (ii) Fixed-time tail: $P(\Vert\delta(t)\Vert \gt R) \leq n\sigma_w^2/(2\alpha R^2)$, stationary-sharp — an instantaneous, not path, guarantee. (iii) Finite-horizon sup bound: $P(\sup_{s\leq T}\Vert\delta(s)\Vert \gt R) \leq (\Vert\delta(0)\Vert^2 + n\sigma_w^2 T)/R^2$ — controls the whole path on $[0,T]$ but grows linearly in $T$ and becomes vacuous for $T \gtrsim R^2/(n\sigma_w^2)$, consistent with a.s. eventual exit.

### A b05-2.8 [math]
Derived (sub-scope α) via **directional fidelity B1** ($\delta^T H g(\delta) \geq c_{\min}\Vert\delta\Vert^2$, the gain-sector bridge Prop B.3), giving $\alpha = \eta^\ast \cdot c_{\min}$: optimal Bayesian updates (Kalman, conjugate), exponential families in natural parameters (on a bounded interior scope, $\alpha = \eta\mu_0$ via Fisher floor), gradient descent on (locally) strongly convex losses ($\alpha = \eta\mu$; B1 ⟺ strong convexity), L2-regularized convex losses, linear corrections with PD gain-observation product. Assumed (sub-scope β): PID with fixed gains, rule-based systems, human judgment/organizational learning, severely misspecified agents, variational/approximate posteriors (partial recovery as sub-scope α′ under controlled KL), non-convex gradient beyond the basin, per-step stochastic gradients.

## (3) Implications

### A b05-3.1 [implications]
The proposal is structurally impossible: under any non-degenerate stochastic disturbance, $P(\text{eventual exit}) = 1$ for *every* correction strength and every A2'-satisfying corrector — no α interpolates. What can be promised: typical scale ($1/\sqrt\alpha$ RMS), fixed-time tail bounds, and finite-horizon path bounds with an honest expiry. Design consequence: for a long-lived agent in a stochastic world, region-exit is a certain eventual event, so **structural adaptation capability** (changing model *kind*, plus detection and recovery machinery for excursions) is generic infrastructure, not an edge-case contingency — though note the corpus itself flags the exit⇒*model-class*-adaptation-necessity increment as an open bridge (#sketch-structural-adaptation-genericity: recurrent exit-and-return is not yet proven to force class change; the narrow licensed claim is generic entry into the regime where the parametric guarantee is unproven) — safety cases must be built on distributional/fixed-time guarantees plus recovery, never on "provably never leaves."

### A b05-3.2 [implications]
(1) **Gain gating**: if the new channels are noisy ($U_o$ high) or the org's model uncertainty attribution is off, $\eta^{(k)\ast} \approx 0$ and the added $\nu$ multiplies into nothing — more reporting, no more tempo. (2) **Channel dependence**: if the dashboards draw from a shared upstream source, the channels' noises are common-source-correlated — the additive sum overcounts, with saturation at the shared-bias floor (no number of correlated channels buys information past it). For an honest tempo increase, the new channels must be *structurally independent* (uncorrelated noise sources) and individually informative (decent gain). *(The org-dashboard casting is WN-flavored; both mechanisms are body text.)*

### A b05-3.3 [implications]
Wrong because for non-locally-Lipschitz $F$ (rule-firing discontinuities), **no scalar sector bound implies contraction of the update map**: the map can jump by $\Omega(1)$ between arbitrarily close states at rule boundaries, so no Lipschitz constant $\lambda \lt 1$ exists — the sector-to-contraction implication (needed for bridge-lemma/composition analysis) fails structurally, not empirically. Counterexample shape: $F(\delta) = \alpha\delta + \operatorname{sign}(\delta)$ for $\vert\delta\vert \geq 1$ — sector bound holds, contraction violated at $\vert X\vert = 1$. Honest apparatus for the class: **hybrid dynamical systems / hybrid-dissipative theory** (dwell-time, Filippov solutions, impulsive-dissipative inequalities) — different machinery on a different regularity class, a structural scope-exit rather than a deficiency.

### A b05-3.4 [implications]
Condition-failure means the containment *certificate* is lost — the framework can no longer guarantee boundedness with the sector information it has; it does not certify escape (the dip counterexample shows a tenfold condition-failure with de-facto containment). The iff is genuine for radially tight agents — including the linear case, which covers many canonical instances (Kalman-type correctors). Lost certificates matter operationally: certificates are what safety cases, composition results (the bridge lemma consumes them), and domain-transfer arguments are built on; an agent operating without one may be fine, but nothing *composable or assurable* can be said about it — and for the extremal members of its class, escape is not just possible but forced. Far from useless: the result is precise about what kind of claim survives where.

### A b05-3.5 [implications]
False claim: an infinite-horizon non-exit probability bound $P(\tau_R \lt \infty) \leq n\sigma_w^2/(2\alpha R^2)$ — conflating the **fixed-time second-moment tail** (an instantaneous Markov-inequality bound) with the **ever-exit probability** (a path property over an unbounded horizon). Attempted strengthening: the Doob/Ville maximal-inequality route — find a nonnegative supermartingale dominating $V$ to certify $P(\tau_R \lt \infty) \lt 1$. Structural failure: the additive-noise generator has no bounded non-constant harmonic function (recurrence — Khasminskii); no such certificate can exist. *(Provenance: the sharper "compensated supermartingale candidate is sign-indefinite inside the basin" gloss is from the OUTLINE's row for #deriv-stochastic-non-exit — read during orientation, not from a batch segment; the full demonstration lives in that appendix, outside this batch.)* Why the no-go beats the soften: the mandated strengthening attempt converted a wording repair into a *new exact theorem* (Corollary A.1S.1 — the categorical, α-invariant dichotomy) plus a reusable no-go signature future proposals can be settled against — strictly more content than "restate as fixed-time," and the epistemology (attempt the strong claim; if it provably fails, the failure is the result) is the framework's strengthen-before-soften discipline working as designed.

### A b05-3.6 [implications]
**State floor (b04)**: part of the agent's standing mismatch may be the state-uncertainty floor — irreducible by any model on this history; priced remedy: *act* to make the history more informative (active sensing, high-CIY actions). **Adaptive reserve (b05)**: $\Delta\rho^\ast = \alpha R - \rho$ is nearly exhausted when mismatch rides near $R$ — the agent is fragile; a modest disturbance shock exceeds the reserve and voids the certificate; priced remedies: raise $\alpha$ (better gain/fidelity), raise $R$ (capacity), or shed $\rho$ (environment shaping). **Structural adaptation (b03)**: if the near-$R$ mismatch is *structured* residual persisting after converged learning, the class ceiling is binding — no parametric spend helps; the priced remedy is changing model class, and (per the dichotomy) in a stochastic world this lever will eventually be required regardless. The three constructs price three different interventions: acting, absorbing, and re-architecting.

### A b05-3.7 [implications]
Without the bridge, A2' — and therefore every persistence result — would rest on an *assumed* structural property ("correction points inward efficiently"), i.e., postulate-grade foundations for the framework's headline theorems; critics could dismiss persistence as true-by-assumption. The bridge derives A2' from the update rule itself for a characterized class (directional fidelity B1 ⇒ $\alpha = \eta^\ast c_{\min}$), making the sector parameter a *property of the gain machinery already established* — the chapter intro's "demotion from postulate to property." The honest concession is the sub-scope partition: the derivation covers sub-scope α (Bayesian/exponential-family/strongly-convex/regularized/linear-PD); for sub-scope β (PID, rule-based, human judgment, misspecified, variational, non-convex-beyond-basin, per-step SGD) A2' remains a per-system empirical claim — scope narrowing stated as such, with the five named failure modes connecting each escape to a concrete mechanism.
