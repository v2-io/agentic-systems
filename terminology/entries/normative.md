A `type: normative` segment states a claim that follows from the framework's axioms or postulates
*given* a precondition that must be verified rather than derived. Unlike a pure `derived` claim
(where all premises are established by the dependency chain), a normative claim has a condition
that must be checked in each application context — it is structurally valid but not automatically
in force.

The label "normative" signals that the claim has normative weight (it constrains what the framework
counts as legitimate agent behavior) while acknowledging that its force depends on whether the
precondition holds. An agent design that violates a normative claim is not necessarily wrong in a
formal sense, but it cannot claim the framework's support for the resulting behavior.
