The framework's cross-sectional organizing principle: an agent persists when its contraction toward the target outpaces the drift injected by the environment. The segment-level form is the stability-certificate equivalence — a certificate exists iff the agent is exponentially stable about its target, with the certificate as the converse-Lyapunov witness and a strict strength ladder (R0-loss ⟸ R0-strict ⟸ R1 ⟸ R2).
