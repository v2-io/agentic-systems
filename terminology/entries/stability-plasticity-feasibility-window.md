The full phrase `stability-plasticity feasibility window` is the citation form; `feasibility window` is sanctioned as the in-segment short form once the full term has been introduced. The four-word compound is dense rather than redundant: `stability-plasticity` anchors the CLS continual-learning prior art (McClelland-McNaughton-O'Reilly 1995; French 1999); `feasibility window` names the parametric-interval object — the range of $\lambda$ satisfying both the plasticity lower bound (from `#schema-strategy-persistence`) and the consolidation-cadence stability upper bound. An empty window is the catastrophic-forgetting regime.
