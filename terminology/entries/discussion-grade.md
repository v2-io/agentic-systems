`status: discussion-grade` indicates that a claim is argued qualitatively or by analogy — it is
not formally derived from the dependency chain, and it has not been validated empirically. The
claim may be insightful and worth stating, but it is at the bottom of the epistemic ladder while
remaining distinct from `sketch` (which indicates *unfinished* work, not *argumentative* work).

Discussion-grade claims carry a specific risk: they can seem more authoritative than they are.
Gate 2 content review must subject Discussion-section claims to the same epistemic scrutiny as
Formal Expression derivations — asking whether each claim follows from the formalism or is merely
plausible-sounding post-hoc rationalization. Claims that survive that scrutiny at `discussion-grade`
are genuinely honest about their epistemic basis; those that don't survive should be cut or labeled
as hypothesis with a falsification criterion.

Equation-level tags use `*[Discussion]*` to mark discussion-grade equations inline.
