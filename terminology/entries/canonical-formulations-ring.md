The second of the three epistemic rings. A segment belongs here when triage question 2 yields
"at least one alternative exists" — the chosen form is the most useful or parsimonious, but it is a representational choice, not a mathematical necessity. Review focus: explaining the choice,
explicitly noting alternatives, and guarding against drift toward inevitability claims that are not warranted.

Current members include most definitions and formulations: `#form-agent-model`,
`#def-adaptive-tempo`, `#form-information-bottleneck`, `#norm-explicit-strategy-condition`.
See [`FORMAT.md`](../../FORMAT.md) §"Three rings of segment content".

Status `weak`: ring assignment is an analytical stance, not a property encoded in segment frontmatter. Do not confuse with `canonical formulation` (a segment `type:` value).
