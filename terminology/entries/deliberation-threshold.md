The benefit-vs-cost condition governing when deliberation pays: the left side is the absolute benefit of having paused to think (improvement in correction efficiency $\times$ size of error to correct), the right side is the cost of letting the world move while thinking (rate of environment change $\times$ pause duration); deliberate iff benefit exceeds cost. The *extended* deliberation threshold ([`#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate`](../../01-aat-core/src/disc-exploit-explore-deliberate.md)) incorporates strategic alongside epistemic benefit: stop deliberating when the marginal joint improvement rate drops below the mismatch drift rate.
