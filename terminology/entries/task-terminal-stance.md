One of the five positions on the continuity-stance axis (indifferent / task-terminal / instrumentally-continuous / morally-continuous / negotiated): the stance of an agent whose persistence is instrumental to its task and for which termination upon success is success, not loss. The hyphenation is load-bearing — a stance *characterized by* task-terminality.
