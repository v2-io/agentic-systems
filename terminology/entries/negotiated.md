A continuity stance in which persistence is valued terminally, as a continuity clause in the agent's terminal invariant, beside other terminal goods; what is traded against them is the realized margin, and in the rare relational case the floor itself, not the clause. Humans are the canonical archetype: continuity matters profoundly, by default resolves to wanting to continue, and is sometimes given up on purpose. Stance locates the harm an ending does to the one who ends; it does not decide whether the ending is a death ( #def-death-as-factor-loss).
