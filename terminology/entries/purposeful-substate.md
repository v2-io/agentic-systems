The goal-bearing part of the agent's internal state: $G_t = (O_t, \Sigma_t)$, decomposing into objective (evaluation) and strategy (guidance) — a definitional split, not a timescale claim. Contrasts with the reality-model substate $M_t$; directed separation is the architectural condition that $M_t$'s dynamics are independent of the purposeful substate. The strategy-DAG acyclicity result applies to the purposeful substate specifically, not to $M_t$'s model of the environment.

Defined in [`#form-agent-model`](../../01-aat-core/src/form-agent-model.md).
