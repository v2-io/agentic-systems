The `## Epistemic Status` section is a required part of every segment's [document cadence](../../FORMAT.md). It answers two questions: (1) what in this segment is derived from prior claims and what is hypothesized or chosen; and (2) what is the segment's *maximum attainable status* — the strongest epistemic tier it could ever reach, regardless of additional work. The max-attainable annotation prevents wasted effort (pushing an empirical claim toward `exact`) and focuses investment where promotion is genuinely possible.

The slug `epistemic-status-section` distinguishes this structural role from the `status:` frontmatter field, which carries the segment's current epistemic tier (e.g., `exact`, `heuristic`, `discussion-grade`).

Conventions for the section are in [`FORMAT.md`](../../FORMAT.md) §Document Cadence and §Epistemic Triage.
