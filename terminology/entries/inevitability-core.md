The innermost of the three rings in FORMAT.md's epistemic triage framework. A segment belongs to
the inevitability core when triage question 2 ("what competing formulation would also fit?") has
only one answer: none. Mathematical inevitability is the ceiling — the result could not coherently
take another form given the prior objects. Review focus for these segments: tightening the
derivation until no alternative formulation escapes.

Current members include `#deriv-recursive-update`, `#result-persistence-condition`,
`#deriv-graph-structure-uniqueness`, and roughly a dozen others. See
[`FORMAT.md`](../../FORMAT.md) §"Three rings of segment content" for the full list and rationale.

Status `weak`: the ring boundaries are an analyst's stance, not a formal property — segment
membership may shift as derivations tighten or relax.
