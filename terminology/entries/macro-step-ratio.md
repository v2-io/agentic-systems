The prose handle for $K_c \geq 1$: the number of micro-timesteps per macro-step in the composition-closure construction. $K_c = 1$ is the lockstep (synchronous-update) composite; $K_c \gg 1$ is the regime where the composite operates strictly slower than its sub-agents and timescale abstraction is recovered. The defining segment currently writes "timescale ratio $K_c$" — retained as an alias; the R2 cohort canonicalized "macro step ratio" as the stable prose handle the bare parameter needed.
