Adopted from Miller (2022, *Ex Machina*), original name retained: the constructive mechanism by which a sequence of *neutral mutations* transforms a system from one behavioral equivalence class to a structurally different one without observable change — until a single additional change opens a transition to the restructured region, producing radical behavioral change. Phases: neutral invasion → neutral drift → niche creation → cascading displacement → consolidation. The strategy-DAG analog: near-zero-credence edges are the latent structure that becomes load-bearing under regime change.
