`status: robust-qualitative` indicates that the claim's *qualitative* shape — the direction of the effect, the sign of the relationship, the ordering of cases — survives robustly across different modeling assumptions or representational choices, even though the specific functional form or coefficient values are approximate.

This tier sits between `exact` (the form is proved) and `heuristic` (useful approximation without formal backing). A robust-qualitative claim is more than a heuristic: the qualitative structure can be justified across assumptions, and the approximation concerns only the quantitative details.
It is less than `exact`: if you need the specific value (not just the sign or direction), this status means you cannot trust the expression as written.
