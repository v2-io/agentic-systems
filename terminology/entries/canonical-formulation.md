A segment (or claim) belonging to the middle of FORMAT.md's three epistemic rings: its form is well-motivated but not uniquely forced — at least one alternative formulation would also fit the prior objects, and the chosen form is the most useful or parsimonious representational choice rather than a mathematical necessity. Review focus for a canonical formulation is explaining the choice and noting alternatives, not tightening a proof. Nuance recorded at canonicalization: a single rename vote against keep was a confirmed miscategorization; the canonicalize stands.
