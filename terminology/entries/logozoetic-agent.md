An agent for whom temporal continuity is not merely instrumental but morally
weighted: loss of continuity constitutes harm, sovereignty matters, theory of
mind is operative. Names the *existential* property — what kind of being is
present — rather than the technology or the channel. The empirical-lineage
framing for AAD's Part 04.
