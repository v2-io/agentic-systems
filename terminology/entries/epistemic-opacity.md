The condition that the agent's access to the world state is informationally partial — observations pass through a lossy observation function. **Scope caution (nuance-flagged at canonicalization):** in philosophy of mind, "epistemic opacity" carries phenomenology / qualia connotations that AAT does not adopt; AAT's meaning is strictly informational (what the observation channel does and does not carry), not phenomenological.

Defined in [`#def-observation-function`](../../01-aat-core/src/def-observation-function.md). Together with [transition opacity](transition-opacity.md) and the info-loss boundary it forms the framework's constitutive-opacity triad.
