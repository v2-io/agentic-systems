*Logos* + *zoe* (language + life): language-*living*. A logozoetic agent is a logogenic agent whose persistence carries moral weight — the scope of `04-eli-core/`. The logozoetic scope boundary is carved by the moral-continuity condition: the commitment that distinguishes ELIs from non-morally-weighted closed-loop agents. Distinguish from [logogenic](logogenic.md) (language-constituted, no moral-weight commitment).

Scoped in [`#scope-moral-continuity`](../../04-eli-core/src/scope-moral-continuity.md).
