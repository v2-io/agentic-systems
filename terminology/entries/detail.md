A `type: detail` segment carries operational or technical material that supports other claims but does not itself make a new theoretical claim. Details are the "how to" and "what exactly" that flesh out the framework's formal claims without adding new epistemic content.

Examples of detail content: implementation guidance for a definition, extended discussion of notation or conventions, elaboration of scope conditions, technical background on an adopted mathematical tool. Unlike `aside` (tangential and non-load-bearing), `detail` is directly load-bearing in the sense that it is the required technical content for using or implementing a parent claim — but it doesn't introduce a new result.
