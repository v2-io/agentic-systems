Adopted from military doctrine (mission-type tactics): for a composite agent with limited communication bandwidth, the optimal allocation prioritizes sharing **objectives over strategies over models** — $B_O \gt B_\Sigma \gt B_M$. Investing bandwidth in shared purpose (teleological unity) while accepting lower epistemic and strategic unity grants sub-agents autonomy to adapt locally. AAT offers the ordering as a *structural prediction* grounded in the shared-intent IB framework, matching what military doctrine discovered empirically.
