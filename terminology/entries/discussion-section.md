The `## Discussion` section appears in every segment and carries the interpretive layer: what the formal result means, how it connects to adjacent claims, and what it implies for the framework's overall picture. Unlike `## Working Notes` (which is a process artifact), the Discussion is part of the published theory — it should be written as the framework speaking, not as a chronicle of how the claim was developed.

Discussion-section claims carry their own epistemic obligation: Gate 2 content review must subject Discussion arguments to the same rigor as Formal Expression derivations. A plausible-sounding explanation that cannot be traced to the formalism is not an interpretation — it is an ungrounded claim that creates false confidence.

The slug `discussion-section` distinguishes this structural role from `type: discussion` (the frontmatter value for segments whose *entire purpose* is conceptual or interpretive).

Conventions are in [`FORMAT.md`](../../FORMAT.md) §Document Cadence and §Voice and provenance.
