Architectural axes parametrizing the quality of a composite agent's
composition. The *content axis* covers what sub-agents share — epistemic
($U_M$, model), teleological ($U_O$, objective), strategic ($U_\Sigma$,
coordinated action), and perceptual ($U_{\text{obs}}$, observations) — while a
separate *structural axis* ($U_f$) covers whether sub-agents implement the
*same* correction rule. Together the axes parametrize the rate-distortion
curves for the composition's closure defects: higher unity along either axis
permits more aggressive coarse-graining at lower defect, and neither axis
alone is sufficient.
