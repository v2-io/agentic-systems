The trace inner-product $\text{Tr}(\Lambda \cdot \mathcal I_o(a))$ appearing in the matrix (LMI) form of the causal-IB survival constraint — the multidimensional analog of $\lambda_{\text{surv}} \cdot \text{CIY}(a)$ from the scalar derivation. In the 1D case the matrix multiplier $\Lambda$ collapses to a scalar shadow price coinciding with $\lambda_{\text{surv}}$ and the bonus reduces to the scalar form.
