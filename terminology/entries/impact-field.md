The Impact field is the second field in a `## Findings` entry. It is a paragraph on what the finding *does* in the framework — what it unlocks, what it closes, what it forces. Cross-references to other segments are encouraged where they carry weight: a finding that resolves a previously-flagged GAP, lifts a prior result's status, or makes a downstream construction newly possible should say so.

The Impact field is catalog-grade external positioning, distinct from the Discussion section's in-segment context. Do not duplicate Discussion content in Impact; the two serve different readers
(Discussion serves someone reading the segment; Impact serves someone scanning the FINDINGS.md catalog).

Impact should be concrete enough that a reader can evaluate the claim without re-reading the Formal Expression — it answers "so what?" at the framework level.
