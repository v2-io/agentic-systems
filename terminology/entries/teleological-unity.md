The objective-alignment dimension of the unity decomposition: pairwise value-correlation aggregated to the group, written $U_O$. The bare prose form "teleological unity" is canonical; $U_O$ is its own NOTATION row (the symbol-tagged variant "Teleological unity $U_O$" was considered and resolved to the bare-prose-plus-symbol-cross-ref shape). Adversarial multi-agent systems have negative teleological unity; Miller's mutual-benefit element (IAM) corresponds to a minimum teleological unity.
