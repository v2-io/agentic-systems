When actuation is severed, active sensing degrades — the agent observes what arrives rather than what it seeks — so agency death casts an *epistemic shadow*: sector-restricted observational starvation that raises pressure against the persistence-cost floor without constituting continuity death. The deaths interact; they do not merge. Also names the credence-collapsed non-attempting state (helplessness) in the severed-actuation dynamics.
