The scope-constitutive condition that the agent does not know the transition dynamics $T$ exactly — it cannot fully predict the consequences of its actions. Pairs with the perceptual info-loss boundary as the framework's *double opacity*: if only perception were unknown the agent could plan against known dynamics; if only $T$ were unknown it could see the world directly but not predict consequences. AAT applies under joint opacity of perception and dynamics.
