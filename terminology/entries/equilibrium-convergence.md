Adopted-standard term (Criterion 9 route d): the game-theoretic name for convergence of strategic interaction to equilibrium — deliberately distinct from generic Lyapunov contraction, marking the *strategic attractor* mechanism where the composite sector constant lives at the joint potential gradient or joint Jacobian's symmetric part. First-encounter cite discipline: Monderer & Shapley 1996 (*Games and Economic Behavior* 14(1): 124-143, potential games); Rosen 1965 (*Econometrica* 33(3): 520-534, concave $n$-person games); Nash 1950 (*PNAS* 36(1): 48-49) for the foundational equilibrium concept.
