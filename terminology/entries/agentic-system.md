The next class above [adaptive system](adaptive-system.md) in the AAT spectrum:
adds an outcome model, goal-directed action, and adaptation of the model itself
(not only its parameters). Lives within Section I — agentic systems do *not*
yet require an explicit goal substate $G_t$ separate from the model.

[`#post-causal-structure`](../../01-aat-core/src/post-causal-structure.md).
