The receiver's estimates of source quality ($U_{\text{src},j}$) and alignment ($U_{\text{align},ji}$) constitute a **trust meta-model** — a model of models. The meta-model is itself subject to AAT's full apparatus: it has its own mismatch (trust prediction errors), should be updated with appropriate gain, and can be structurally inadequate. The framework also names risk-asymmetric trust (decide from a conservative quantile of the trust posterior in high-stakes interactions) and trust transitivity (Bayesian mixture through a trusted intermediary).
