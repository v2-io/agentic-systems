The bare prose form `recursive update` is the canonical handle; the verbose `recursive update derivation` is the segment-title form for [`#deriv-recursive-update`](../../01-aat-core/src/deriv-recursive-update.md). Names the event-driven recursion by which the agent's internal state at event $t$ is constructed from the state at event $t-1$ plus the newly arrived event.
