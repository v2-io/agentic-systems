A `type: proposed-schema` segment identifies the mathematical *shape* of a claim — the structure of the solution space, the relevant variables and their relations — before the formal content is complete. The proposal is specific enough to be pinned down as a schema but doesn't yet have the derivations or validation that would make it a `formulation` or `result`.

`proposed-schema` sits between `sketch` (direction identified, rigor entirely pending) and
`formulation` (representational choice made, formal content present). Use it when the candidate structure is clear enough to be usefully described, but the work of justifying or filling it in remains.
