Adopted-standard term (Criterion 9 route d): the causal-inference-canonical concept, postulated as a property of the environment the agent must track. First-encounter cite discipline: Pearl 2009 (*Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*, 2nd ed., CUP); Spirtes, Glymour & Scheines 2000 (*Causation, Prediction, and Search*, MIT Press). The R2 cohort explicitly considered and rejected a rename, settling on the existing form as shorter and adequate.
