The novelty posture is used in the Novelty Claim field when no direct anticipation has been found
at the search depth conducted. The claim is conditional on that search depth — a result that is
fresh under `cursory` search may not survive `comprehensive` search. The Search Log field carries
the honesty about what depth has been reached.

Prose form: *"Claim novelty on..."* followed by what is being claimed as fresh. The "on" phrase
names the domain or question.

The novelty posture requires the most support from the Search Log and Related Work fields. A
novelty claim under comprehensive search is a strong epistemic statement; under cursory search or
intuition-only, it is an honest preliminary position that signals further search is warranted.
The Search Log is the discipline that prevents the novelty posture from becoming hubris.
