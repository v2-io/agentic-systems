The compound *logos* + *stratum* (language + layer) names the linguistic substrate as an architectural layer: the underlying logogenic substrate (e.g., the LLM backbone) that implements the update function $f_X$. Project-specific term of PROPRIUM lineage; the prose aliases "LLM Substrate" / "LLM model" are sanctioned less-foreign English handles for casual prose (canonicalize-with-add-alias hybrid).
