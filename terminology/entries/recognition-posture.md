The recognition posture is used in the Novelty Claim field when the contribution is *seeing* a structural equivalence, pattern, or isomorphism — between two concepts, between two segments, or between the framework and a prior body of work — rather than producing a new formal derivation.
The claim is not "we proved X" but "we noticed that X and Y have the same structure."

Prose form: *"Claim recognition of structural equivalence (or pattern) between X and Y"* where X and Y name what is being recognized as equivalent or co-structured.

Recognition claims can be genuinely load-bearing: the meta-segments in AAT (`#disc-separability-pattern`,
`#disc-identifiability-floor`, `#disc-additive-coordinate-forcing`) carry recognition findings —
their contribution is naming the cross-segment structure that gives the theory its architectural coherence. The recognition posture is the honest claim for such segments.

The posture also covers cases where a prior external result is recognized as being the same as an AAT result derived independently. Such convergent recognition should be in the Related Work field as *convergent independent*, and the Novelty Claim should note the recognition honestly.
