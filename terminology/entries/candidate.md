`stage: candidate` is the highest stage in the promotion ladder, reached by passing Gate 4
(Working Notes disposition). A candidate segment has: passed all four promotion gates; has its
`## Working Notes` section empty or absent (every item explicitly resolved — incorporated,
deferred to TODO.md or a spike, or promoted to its own segment); and states what it means to
say, completely, within the segment's own argument.

A candidate segment is ready for external challenge — it could be shown to a skeptical reader
outside the project without needing apologetic framing. Its claims are correct (Gate 2), its
format is clean (Gate 3), and its working-in-progress artifacts have been resolved or relocated
(Gate 4).

The `candidate` stage does not mean the claim is final in a permanent sense — a dependency
revision, an error discovered in external review, or a scope change can return any candidate to
`draft`. But it means the segment has passed every internal check the project has.
