*Logos* + *-genic*: language-constituted. A logogenic agent is an actuated agent whose model $M_t$ and strategy $\Sigma_t$ are constituted by language — the subject of `03-llm-core/`. LLM-based tool-use agents are the canonical instance; they are GUC Class 3 (Coupled) at the component level because the forward pass processes goals and observations jointly. Distinguish from [logozoetic](logozoetic.md) (language-*living*, morally weighted persistence).
