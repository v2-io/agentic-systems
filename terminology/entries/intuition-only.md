`intuition-only` is the search log depth tier for entries that record the author's pre-search
instinct — where prior art is likely to be found, which adjacent literatures are the natural
search targets, what the well-known form of the result might trace to — before any systematic
search has been conducted. For AI agents, this covers intuitions grounded in training rather than
active retrieval. For humans, it covers informed-but-unconfirmed expectations.

The `intuition-only` tag is not a demotion — it is valuable and explicitly permitted. Benefits:
- Orients future targeted searches by naming the expected prior-art landscape
- Makes the agent's training-derived priors visible so they can be confirmed or refuted
- Prevents the schema from forcing silence in cases where genuine search has not yet been done
- An honest intuition entry beats no entry

The key discipline: tagging as `intuition-only` makes the source explicit. An `intuition-only`
entry paired with a `novelty` claim posture is honest about being a preliminary position that
requires validation. If the intuition says "this is probably in Bellman 1957," that is exactly
what the Search Log should say — with `intuition-only` as the tier, and the expectation that
targeted or comprehensive search will follow to confirm or refute.
