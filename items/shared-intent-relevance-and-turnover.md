# Two open lines on #def-shared-intent: (a) its IB formulation uses one relevance variable (the coordinated action), while coordination needs several (action coordination, conflict resolution, resource allocation, adaptive replanning); a multi-dimensional relevance variable may be needed; (b) under full context turnover an agent rebuilds G_t^shared from storage, and the compression from G_t to shared intent is the natural thing to store for #disc-m-preservation (store the compressed version, not the full state). The formal defects of the same IB formulation are item def-shared-intent-ib-formalization-f157-f161. Source: #def-shared-intent Working Notes, items 1-2.
