# #deriv-tempo-additivity derives the two endpoints exactly (fresh noise: additive; persistent shared bias: saturation). The intermediate regime, noise correlation time comparable to interarrival times, should interpolate through an effective sigma_s^2(nu); an OU-process shared bias is exactly solvable and would make the crossover explicit.
