# Open extensions of #hyp-communication-gain: (a) the trust-mixture form of corroboration: #deriv-tempo-additivity derives the common-source redundancy penalty and anti-correlated triangulation, but the form for a mixture of trusted intermediaries is open; (b) split source-calibration uncertainty U_src (an estimation problem that converges with data) from alignment uncertainty U_align (strategic: the adversary optimizes over the defender's trust policy) into separate treatments, not only separate denominator terms; (c) state an explicit asymmetric loss, which would turn the conservative-quantile prescription into a derived decision rule; (d) the known-adversary case (U_align low because i is certain j is misaligned, yet gain should be low) is a representational gap in the additive form, distinct from strategic deception. Related: item todo-communication-gain-adversarial-scope-hyp-e79c (trust is game-theoretic).
