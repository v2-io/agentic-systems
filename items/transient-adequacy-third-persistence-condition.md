# Structural persistence and task adequacy are steady-state claims; two agents with equal thresholds can differ a thousandfold in shock-recovery time (1/alpha vs a tolerance window). Formulate transient adequacy as a third condition (and hysteresis with it), so the persistence picture is a triple; if the two-gate figure is built, build it extensible to three. The chapter's gold calls this its strongest single follow-up.
