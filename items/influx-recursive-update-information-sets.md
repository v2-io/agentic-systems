# The recursive-update pair's measure-theoretic restatement and its status table say more than the derivation uses (checked at cbd2f345; the C2 grounding at #deriv-recursive-update:19 is fixed). (a) :121 states C3 as sigma({e_tau' : tau' < tau} u {M_tau' : tau' < tau^-}) subset sigma(M_{tau^-}): read literally M_{tau^-} determines the whole past, i.e. phi is injective, contradicting 'many-to-one' in #form-agent-model; the derivation uses only I_tau^agent = sigma(M_{tau^-}, e_tau). Restate C3 as a restriction on the update's information set. (b) :120 'sigma(Omega_tau) \ sigma(e_tau) is not in I_tau^agent': a set difference of sigma-algebras is not a sigma-algebra; intended is I subset sigma(past, e_tau). (c) :176 'Markov property of the update -- Proved' and the gold Brief candidates (WN :218) equate recursion with RL's Markov assumption; recursion gives a state-like (unifilar) store, not a Markov observation process (a store M_t = o_t under a hidden fixed bias is recursive while P(M_{t+1} | C_t) depends on counts; finder 15 (c), (g)). (d) #der-recursive-update:45 treats 'relevant information outside M_t in the raw history' as a completeness violation, conflating predictive completeness (sufficiency) with behavioral completeness (only M_t is read), the split audit 471203's gold asked for. (e) #der-recursive-update:37 states the between-event dynamics flat ('depend only on the current model state'), which the appendix calls a genuine limitation for continuous coupling (:134-136; WN :229); the event-time side is item influx-event-time-seam. (f) doc/sop/format.sop.md:551 bills the pair as the 'Strongest result in the theory'; 731548/16:28 and audits/audit-findings-742613.md:456 both call it the most secure, because nearest to definitional. (g) #form-consolidation-dynamics:20 calls consolidation 'a regime of the between-event dynamics g_M' while :32 defines it as jumps on replay pseudo-events, not a flow. A candidate strengthening, unverified: for a recursive store with future actions set from outside, one-step sufficiency at every step is equivalent to whole-future sufficiency (finder 15 (d); published analogues Subramanian et al. 2022 Prop. 4, Barnett and Crutchfield App. A Prop. 7); the sandwich lemma in item influx-sufficiency-fitness-horizon-and-class gives it, and it would be this pair's honest payoff line.
