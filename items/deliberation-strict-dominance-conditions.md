# Under what formal conditions does internal deliberation strictly dominate external exploration? The body (:98) gives dominance conditions qualitatively, and the bandit simulation was unfavorable to deliberation by design (no structure, small action space, reversible actions). Candidates: large action spaces needing combinatorial search, irreversible actions, cross-domain knowledge, high model fidelity. A planning-heavy domain (chess, logistics) is the natural test.
