# #post-causal-structure:49 states a normative update rule: 'the model should give more weight to observations that are causally downstream of the agent's actions than to observations that would have occurred regardless'. Nothing in the gain machinery derives it. #emp-update-gain weights an observation by uncertainty (U_M versus U_o), not by its causal position. Action-contingent observations carry Level-2 information, but they can be noisy or biased, so the claim holds at most when the weighting is by CIY and uncertainty together. Either derive it from the gain (strengthen first) or state it as the Level-2 information distinction without the weighting prescription. Checked at HEAD 4175aae7.
