# Three open questions about #scope-composite-agent's routes: (a) asymmetric unity: (C-i) and (C-ii) assume symmetric alignment, while symbiogenesis, acquisitions and hierarchical delegation are host-dominant; (C-ii) covers some of it, and an explicit treatment of role-asymmetric composites is open; (b) composites of Class 3 (Coupled) agents, such as multi-LLM systems, may satisfy the scope through shared training rather than a shared explicit objective: do (C-i)-(C-iii) cover this, or is an implicit-alignment route needed? (c) transitivity of alignment (probably via (C-ii) with a common parent, not via (C-i)); non-transitivity would explain why coalitions need explicit alignment work.
