# #disc-structural-imagination's level-confusion diagnosis of hallucination has a human prior-art literature, and #form-consolidation-dynamics's generated pseudo-events are its W_t content; land both at discussion grade and link the segments (checked at HEAD ac566fcc; the segments cite each other 0 times). (1) Prior art for the relata pass its Working Notes owe ('mental-simulation/prospection literature'): reality monitoring, telling perceived from imagined memories (Johnson & Raye 1981, Psych Rev 88:67-85, doi 10.1037/0033-295X.88.1.67); source monitoring, where source is not read from a stored tag but attributed at retrieval by judging a memory's qualities (Johnson, Hashtroudi & Lindsay 1993, Psych Bull 114:3-28, doi 10.1037/0033-2909.114.1.3); imagination inflation, where imagining an event raises confidence it happened (Garry, Manning, Loftus & Sherman 1996, PBR 3:208-214, doi 10.3758/BF03212420); children confuse did-vs-imagined but not did-vs-watched (Foley & Johnson 1985); 3-year-olds often say imagination reflects reality (Woolley & Wellman 1993); older adults' perceived-vs-imagined confusion is specific to similar items (Henkel, Johnson & De Leonardis 1998). What it adds: the segment hypothesizes that an explicit grounded/explored tag prevents mis-typing; humans have no stored tag, attribute source from features, and show exactly the predicted promotion failure, worst where perceived and imagined contents are similar. That is an existing instance of the falsification handle seen from the untagged side, and a design note (a tag inferred from content fails on similar items). (2) Repetition is the promotion mechanism: re-simulating a counterfactual lowers its rated plausibility but raises ease, detail and valence (De Brigard, Szpunar & Schacter 2013, Psych Sci 24:1329-1334), and fluency and detail are what feature-based attribution reads as having happened. (3) Replay is generative: it constructs never-experienced sequences (Gupta et al. 2010 Neuron 65:695-705; Liu et al. 2019 Cell 178:640-652), and each replay sequence can be 'a hypothesis about a possible configuration' (Schwartenbeck et al. 2023 Cell, doi 10.1016/j.cell.2023.09.004). #form-consolidation-dynamics defines consolidation over 'replayed or internally-generated pseudo-events'; compositional pseudo-events are W_t content by #disc-structural-imagination's typing, so a consolidation pass that writes them into M^slow is the promotion step the level-confusion diagnosis names. One sentence in each segment closes the gap.
