# What strategy topology is optimal is open in two metrics with no shared owner: tempo (#def-strategic-tempo Working Notes) and decision-relevant information under a description-length budget (#form-strategy-complexity-cost Working Notes). One auditor sketched a candidate theorem (Claude 451729). Under a fixed action budget nu and fixed edge count, AND-chain strategic tempo tends to nu/((n+1)(1-theta)) while a flat OR gives nu/(n+1). So for depth above 1/(1-theta) (depth above 10 at theta = 0.9) the shallow OR-tree has the higher tempo, and the Working Notes line 'shallow OR-heavy maximizes tempo' may be promotable to a derived statement. Strengthen first: derive the tempo-maximizing topology under the stated budget, then ask whether it agrees with the description-length optimum.
