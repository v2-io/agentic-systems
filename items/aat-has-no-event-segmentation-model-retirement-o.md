# AAT has no event-segmentation (model-retirement) operation, and #form-event-driven-dynamics calls a mutual information a per-event quantity; one item for the cluster (checked at HEAD ac566fcc). (1) #form-event-driven-dynamics says I(e) 'measures, for any individual event, the mutual information', and its own Working Note (line 102) records that this is an expected quantity, I(E;Omega|M) = E_e[KL(p(Omega|e,M) || p(Omega|M))], i.e. expected Bayesian surprise (Itti & Baldi 2009, Vision Research 49:1295-1306, doi 10.1016/j.visres.2008.09.007); fix the body, and if a per-event quantity is needed name which: the literature separates surprisal, Bayesian surprise over hypotheses, KL between successive predictive distributions (what Kumar et al. 2023 Cognitive Science e13343 call 'Bayesian surprise'), predictive entropy, epistemic variance, change-point posterior P(r_t=0|x), and belief-consistent vs -inconsistent surprise (Antony et al. 2021 Neuron 109:377-390). Human boundary data favour belief-change and uncertainty over raw surprisal, with small effects (cross-validated r ~0.05-0.12 for text; ~20% of variance against a ~36% ceiling for video, Nguyen et al. 2024/2025). Item influx-event-time-seam also lists the ':39 surprise carries much' conflation; fix once. (2) The missing operation: #deriv-update-detection-latency concedes a CUSUM/GLR sidecar beats the log-odds accumulator, and #deriv-adaptive-gain-dynamics carries an IMM case; promoting that case to a boundary decision (continue / restart / switch, judged with lookahead) is the candidate home, with #result-mismatch-decomposition as the reason not to segment on every spike. Formal antecedents with equations: latent-cause inference, where one prediction error is both a learning signal and evidence for a new cause, small errors updating the old cause and large ones opening a new one (Gershman, Monfils, Norman & Niv 2017 eLife 6:e23763); SEM's per-step repeat/restart/switch Bayesian model comparison with log-likelihood -||x - f(x)||^2/2beta (Franklin et al. 2020 Psych Rev 127:327-361); BOCPD run-length recursion and hazard (Adams & MacKay 2007 arXiv 0710.3742). Gershman 2017's two-roles statement is the bridge to #result-structural-adaptation-necessity (when parametric update gives way to structural change); whether that segment's trigger coincides with the new-cause criterion is unchecked. (3) A boundary operation cannot be triggered only by M-side mismatch: endogenous task-set switches suffice for memory boundaries with stimulus, response, cue and difficulty changes ruled out (Wang & Egner 2022 Cognition 221:104992); identical sequences segment only when the working-memory task is tied to the segments (Shim et al. 2024 preprint, doi 10.31234/osf.io/4bdp6); stable context change segments far more than transient prediction error (Gueler et al. 2025 PBR 32:2957-2968). A G-side trigger sits legitimately on #der-directed-separation's selection side (the item aat-s-core-has-no-operation-for-an-agent-reading carries goal-structured archiving).
