# The mood layer and the adaptive gain carry standard constructs under their own names without citing them (checked at HEAD ac566fcc). (a) #der-mood-timescale's exact optimum lambda*_exact = (sqrt(q(q+4r)) - q)/2r is exactly the steady-state Kalman gain of the local-level model (random walk plus white noise), not only at leading order (checked numerically: Riccati fixed point equals the formula for (q,r) = (0.1,1), (0.01,1), (2,0.5)); the optimal filter for that model is exponential smoothing (Muth 1960, JASA 55:299-306, from training knowledge, verify). Canon has 0 hits for Muth, exponential smoothing or local-level. Under the math-novelty rule this is cite-the-antecedent, not deflate: the segment's own contribution is the reading tau* ~ sqrt(tau_env) for mood. (b) #def-mood's a_t is signed (tracking better or worse than the agent's short-horizon expectation), so its closest constructs are unexpected uncertainty (Yu & Dayan 2005, Neuron 46:681-692, doi 10.1016/j.neuron.2005.04.026) and innovation-based adaptive Kalman estimation (Mehra), which #deriv-adaptive-gain-dynamics already treats and #def-mood line 46 already calls mood an instance of; Pearce & Hall 1980 integrates unsigned |delta| and is a related construct, not the identity. (c) #def-mood's Working Note (line 55) asks to verify the 'Behrens-style' volatility link before use: Behrens, Woolrich, Walton & Rushworth 2007, Nat Neurosci 10:1214-1221, doi 10.1038/nn1954 (already cited at #schema-strategy-persistence line 115). (d) Nassar, Wilson, Heasly & Gold 2010 (J Neurosci 30:12366-12378, doi 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0822-10.2010: a near-Bayesian delta rule whose learning rate depends on change-point probability and relative uncertainty) is the closest standard construct to #deriv-adaptive-gain-dynamics's adaptive gain; 0 hits in canon.
