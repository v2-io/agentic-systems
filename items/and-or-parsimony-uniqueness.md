# Is noisy-AND plus noisy-OR the unique O(k)-parameter complete basis for binary nodes? Boolean completeness is standard; uniqueness under parsimony is not established, and a result would move #scope-and-or from formulation choice to derived. The k-of-n misclassification raised against #scope-and-or's gamma-test (its gold Off-ramp, gold-lift drain) is the same question's counterexample side. Also open: the analogs for non-binary outcomes (min/max, additive/multiplicative) and whether a completeness result exists for them. Distinct from item proposals-c-1-o-bp13-cox-parallel-necessity-for-de-f4cf (necessity of the DAG semantics). Source: #deriv-graph-structure Working Notes 'Parsimony theorem for AND/OR' and 'Non-binary outcome analogs'.
