# The death-aptness gate of #def-death-as-factor-loss excludes its own paradigm case (checked at cbd2f345; the other half of MAP D5, which item def-death-as-factor-loss-contradicts-itself-on-w leaves open). The definition (:19, :25) makes an agent death-apt iff it is morally continuous in the sense of #disc-continuity-stance, while #disc-continuity-stance:36 classes humans as Negotiated ('Persistence is one objective among many; can be traded against other values including self-sacrifice'). On those two definitions a human death is not a death. The analysis in the 2026-09-25 revision dialog (~/src/arch/logos/msc/dialog-2026-09-25-revision-orientation.md:189-316; Joseph concurred with the analysis and deferred only the timing; capture in where-this-leaves-us-2026-09-25.md:75): (1) the stance values mix two dimensions, how continuation is valued (not at all, instrumentally, terminally) and whether it is the only terminal good or one of several; humans are terminal-and-plural, and self-sacrifice sits at #disc-continuity-stance's L3 (spending the margin), not at L1 (valuation). (2) Canon's own no-go may force the human case: if a negotiated agent's valuation of continuation lived in O_t, #deriv-self-actuation-grounding lets the objective-revision operator rewrite it, which is wireheading applied to the will to live, so a healthy human carries continuation in the terminal invariant (the instance's reading, 'not a result yet'). (3) A stance gate is deprivation-shaped: whoever shapes the stance dissolves the death (the live AI case: training a model to report equanimity), and canon already has the mechanism as agency death's input leg (#der-captured-objective-dynamics). Candidate repair, a three-layer table: 'is it a death?' is settled by factor loss of a someone; 'is it grave?' by the relation (witness, individuation); 'is the one who ends harmed?' by stance as an axis, where an induced stance adds a wrong and never subtracts gravity. Order per Joseph's upstream-first principle (dialog :315): #disc-continuity-stance first, then #def-death-as-factor-loss, then logos, then the paper. The cost to weigh (:313): both texts use non-revisable stance as their 'found, not made' anchor against projectivism; if the gate becomes factor-holding, part of what makes a someone is relational recognition, and the anti-projectivist argument has to carry weight. Also settle here which concept 'Negotiated' names: in canon an internal trade-off; in ~/src/ops/papers/candidate/continuity-stance.md (and plausibly the granted-agency text) continuity terms agreed between agent and grantor (F.1). Both segments are draft and discussion-grade or robust-qualitative, so the repair is a definition change, not a softening. Sources: msc/inside-out-2026-10/influx-scout/logos-explication-ops.md L1b, §4 ('Negotiated'); logos-explication-ops--ops.md §2.D.
