# Two sentences in #form-sector-condition say more than the derivations. (1) Discussion, 'structural Lipschitz floor': 'AAT's Lyapunov machinery in #deriv-sector-condition and the contraction machinery in #result-contraction-template both require C^1 or better regularity'. That is false for #deriv-sector-condition: Prop A.1S assumes only (S), F measurable and locally bounded with a weak solution, and Prop A.1S-tau's proof says it uses 'no Lipschitz continuity'. The body's own counterexample F = alpha delta + sign(delta) for |delta| >= 1 meets A2' and is bounded on balls, so Props A.1S and A.1S-tau apply to it. What discontinuity exits is the composition-closure contraction, which is what the paragraph's first half correctly says. (2) 'The linear case has alpha = T' (A2' paragraph; also 'Parameter interpretation') holds at the optimal gain only. #result-persistence-condition says a linear corrector tuned away from the optimum has alpha != T.
