# #der-interaction-channel-classification contradicts itself on whether its four regimes are forced, re-read at HEAD 86489b0b. Lines 23, 46 and 76 (heading 'Three Independent Boundaries') and line 149 ('it is forced by the structure of the quantities already in the theory') say the four-regime partition follows from three independent boundaries. The derivation table (line 134) says 'Formulation choice (three-way partition; coarser / finer alternatives possible)'. Three boolean tests give eight combinations, and the four regimes come from a precedence order (fail magnitude -> II-a; else fail class -> II-b; else fail observability -> III; else I). Two of the tests both depend on I(e), so the boundaries are orthogonal criteria rather than statistically independent (526815 F187/F205). The honest statement is the precedence made explicit, the partition as the coarsest useful one under it, and 'independent' replaced by what is true.
