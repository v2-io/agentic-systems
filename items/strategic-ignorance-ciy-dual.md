# Seed: strategic ignorance as CIY's negative space. Under directed separation an agent may stay ignorant of parts of the environment that do not bear on its objective; it reduces mismatch locally within its purpose, not globally. That operational statement ('what am I permitted not to know') is absent from canon: #der-directed-separation treats the Pearl-blanket versus Friston-blanket reading (metaphysical), the CIY cluster treats what to learn, and nothing states what one may decline to learn. It would also give the active-inference contrast its one-sentence operational form (surprise minimized globally versus goal-bounded ignorance), which item:influx-active-inference-four-strengths needs.
