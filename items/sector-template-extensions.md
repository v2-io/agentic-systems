# Candidate extensions of #result-sector-persistence-template, each a real extension of Lyapunov theory: (a) time-varying alpha(t) with a lower bound, for the strategic case; (b) state-dependent noise intensity sigma_w(delta) (multiplicative noise), where the OU analysis and the steady-state formula no longer apply and #deriv-sector-condition covers additive noise only; (c) non-quadratic Lyapunov functions for richer stability regions. Sources: #result-sector-persistence-template Working Notes, second item; #detail-linear-ode-approximation Working Notes, third item.
