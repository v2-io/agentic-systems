# What registered a mismatch, and what settled a node, are not the evidence for them, and canon has no word for the gap. Two observations from building an ascription tool over real sessions, at observation tier: (1) explanation-dissolved aporia: a registered mismatch dissolved by an explanation with no observation behind it, an epistrophe failure detectable as an update with no subsequent epistemic action; it separates the mismatch (at o_t) from its registration, and gives a third reading of zero aporia ('silence because the gap was already closed by a story'), with a reader-side twin (hindsight closes gaps by story). Home: #def-mismatch-signal Discussion, possibly #der-orient-cascade step 1. (2) A node's status has two separable records: the observation bearing on p_v, and anyone's declaration that it is done; the gap is measurable, and the design rule follows that a node is satisfied when a goal-blind check says so, never when the planner says so. Home: near #def-strategic-calibration. Evidence is one session (glyphgif e50 to e57) plus a small masked-vs-hindsight reader comparison (haiku, three each: surprise agreement 0.69 vs 0.45; suggestive only); label as observation and hypothesis, not derived. A middle-fold wire result is a clean instance of (1): a thin fold stub read as 'nothing happened here', while one marked sentence at the fold site turned a confident false history into an honest hole.
