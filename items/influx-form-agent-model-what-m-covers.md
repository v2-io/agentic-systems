# #form-agent-model leaves undecided what M_t covers and what structure it has, and its stock exemplars sit badly with directed separation (checked at cbd2f345). (a) What 'retains' covers: #deriv-recursive-update:89 says a raw log 'is part of M'; #form-consolidation-dynamics:38 treats M as the active model (I(e_replay | M_{tau^-}) = 0), and 526815 F75 (archive replay brings in information from outside the active M) hangs on the choice; it is unfixed. The same gap is the 'agent reading its own record' operation other influx regions converge on (influx-scout/MAP.md §2, 'The missing operation'), and #disc-deferred-observation-processing's buffer 'not yet part of M_t' is canon's precedent. (b) :14, :35 call M_t = phi(C_t) 'a formulation choice' with history-based policies as the alternative, while #def-agent-spectrum:100 says the model side is 'structurally forced for bounded goal-conditioned agents (Richens et al. 2025)'; nothing links the two. By Doob-Dynkin the equation alone says only that M_t is sigma(C_t)-measurable (the named alternative is phi = id); what is chosen is completeness, boundedness and analyzing an internal state at all (the observer-attributed reading of Virgo et al. 2025 is the real alternative), and Richens forces 'a predictive model exists' in a fully observed finite MDP, not completeness. Item spikes-proposed-competence-world-model-the-converse-of-f-7c20 carries the related spike. (c) M is typed as any set (:16, :39), but #def-mismatch-signal:57 uses a tangent space and a score and #emp-update-gain uses M_{t-1} + eta* g(delta); the differentiable or additive structure, and the requirement that M determine a predictive distribution, are introduced nowhere. (d) Two stock exemplars put goal-referenced content in M_t: the PID's error against a setpoint (:43) and the RL value function (731548/10:7, ledger xxii), while #der-directed-separation requires a goal-blind M-update; the aat-refactored phase-2 review found PID/setpoint examples with the same issue through Chapters 3-4 (#emp-update-gain:74, #form-sector-condition:74, #deriv-gain-sector:275, #der-gain-sector-bridge:76). Relabel them as generic error dynamics, or say where the setpoint lives. (e) #form-agent-model:16 reads an LLM's M_t as 'context-window contents plus retrieved memory' while 03-llm-core #scope-logogenic-agent:70 puts the weights' prior into the effective state; the endowment fix (item influx-chronica-endowment-absorb-into-phi) should pick one.
