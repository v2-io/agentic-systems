# Open extensions of #deriv-update-detection-latency: (a) beyond Beta-Bernoulli, a parametric version for exponential-family edges in natural-parameter form (detection time monotone in the accumulated sufficient statistic; the 1/(n+1) is Beta-Bernoulli-specific); (b) a probability bound beyond Markov's (Pr[detect within T] <= T/E[T_detect]), needing the residual distribution's shape; (c) the multi-timescale reading: the slow (structural) timescale slows with accumulated fast-timescale state, so the two are not independent, adjacent to but not in #der-multi-timescale-stability (institutional inertia); (d) a Part III analog, team detection latency bounded below by the slowest member's; (e) a Class 3 analog for LLMs, token-statistic accumulation at pretraining as a myopia analog (Volume 3, #obs-context-turnover).
