# Construct a behavioral estimator that tells O-source bias from Sigma-source attractor on a single agent under controlled probing. The dynamical signature is the discriminator: under sustained counter-evidence, O-source coupling still converges (bias proportional to the gain reduction), while Sigma-source coupling fails to converge (the gain falls toward zero faster than evidence accumulates). The sunk-cost and motivated-reasoning literatures document the clustering; a decisive single-agent test does not exist in canon.
