# Statement-level defects in #def-model-sufficiency and #def-model-class-fitness, separate from the horizon and typing repair (item influx-sufficiency-fitness-horizon-and-class); checked at cbd2f345. (a) #def-model-class-fitness:12 'The parallel to bias vs. variance in statistical learning is exact'. Audit 963715 recorded in July that it is 'not the bias/variance axis; it is bias-floor vs bias+estimation' (audits/audit-findings-963715.md:167), never landed; the standard names are approximation versus estimation error, and nothing is estimated from a sample (the in-class gap is closer to an optimization term). With the log-loss identity the decomposition is exact: (1 - S) I = (1 - F) I + (F - S) I. (b) :14, :38 present structured residuals as the signature of a class ceiling ('structure is the discriminator'). Per run an ideal learner's residuals are structured while it is still learning, and structure does not distinguish retention loss from readout error (aat-refactored Chapter 2 verifier §10); the honest-activation spike's R3 is the other qualification and is already question:does-the-honest-activation-spike-s-r3-r4-undercu. It is a sufficient sign that something is unmodeled, not a complete test. (c) The relativity of F to prediction target, generating policy, trajectory and environment is staged only in WN :57 (526815 F4; three audits converge); TFT stated it in the body and it was dropped at migration (.archive/old-tf-10-structural-adaptation.md:38: 'when the environment changes, F(M) changes even though M does not. This is precisely what triggers structural adaptation'). (d) Downstream reads F three incompatible ways: a per-event capacity fraction in #der-interaction-channel-classification, 'misspecification cost' in #deriv-persistence-cost (F is computed under the true law, so it cannot see misspecification of the agent's prior), and an alpha-shrinker in #result-persistence-condition. (e) #def-model-sufficiency:43 says S = 1 while wrong 'if the full history is also wrong (e.g., systematically biased observations)'; the target is future observations, which carry the same bias, so nothing reaches S that way; what S = 1 leaves open is the agent's own decoder and target coordinates no observation reveals. terminology/entries/model-sufficiency.md says 'S = 1 is exact prediction', contradicting :43 outright. (f) :49 'Trajectory-relativity': the definition is an ensemble quantity (mutual informations average over histories), so two copies running the same phi and policy in loops with the same law have the same S; the per-run version is a KL ratio that can leave [0, 1] (finder 11's example gives -0.37, checked by the verifier). #scope-agent-identity's consequence 1 and Volume 4 cite this paragraph as delivered (WN :75 builds identity drift on it). Restate as a property of loop law, policy and phi, or define a named per-run quantity (phase-2 B8: pointwise excess log-loss, non-negative). (g) Stale gold: #def-model-class-fitness WN :53's Kuhn analogy fails its own isomorphism test (on a fixed task F is a common measure across classes, which Kuhnian incommensurability denies; 731548/13:13).
