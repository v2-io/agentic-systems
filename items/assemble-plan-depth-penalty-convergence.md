# Assemble the depth-penalty convergence on plan depth. Canon states a 'triple depth penalty' (#der-chain-confidence-decay:56; #impl-strategy-dynamics:46-50): confidence decay, evidence starvation, cognitive cost. The 01 audit gold counts four to six independent mechanisms with one direction. The other two are identifiability degradation with depth (#scope-edge-update-causal-validity) and tempo cost and DL budget (#def-strategic-tempo, #form-strategy-complexity-cost), and Miller's horizon bound converges with them. Each penalty is derived in its own segment; what is missing is the statement, with each penalty's mechanism and scope, that they compound and that shallow plans are forced from several independent directions, at the tier the weakest leg supports. Whether that statement is a Meta-Architecture facet or an impl-* section is the assembler's call. Sources: Working Notes gold 'Belongs elsewhere' in #form-strategy-complexity-cost (584721, the six-mechanism version) and #scope-edge-update-causal-validity (584721 and 471203, 'quadruple ceiling'); #norm-explicit-strategy-condition (the triple-penalty pointer, now in canon).
