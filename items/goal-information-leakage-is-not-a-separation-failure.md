# #hyp-directed-separation-under-composition answers a reader question in its gold (line 108: is a sub-agent inferring another's goal from its actions a separation failure?) by pointing at a Working Note, so the answer is not in the body. Put it there: goal-driven actions shape the environment, so observations carry information about goals; that is the action-environment coupling directed separation allows, not a separation failure (an earlier draft conflated the two). Then decide whether goal-information leakage, I(o_c; G^c_t | Omega_t), gets its own home; #der-agent-opacity is the nearest existing one.
