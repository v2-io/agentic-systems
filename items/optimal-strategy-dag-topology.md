# Which strategy-DAG topology is optimal? Two forms, each open in its own metric: (a) for fixed action budget nu, the topology maximizing strategic tempo T_Sigma (#def-strategic-tempo:20 already states the qualitative answer, shallow and OR-heavy, without derivation); (b) for fixed description-length budget and action rate, the topology maximizing decision-relevant information I(Sigma_t; pi* | M_t) (#form-strategy-complexity-cost), likely NP-hard in general and perhaps tractable on trees or bounded treewidth. Say whether the two optima agree.
