# #def-adaptive-tempo, two wording points checked at HEAD 4175aae7. (a) Line 13 glosses T as 'the effective rate at which an agent acquires useful information from its environment'. eta* is a correction fraction, not an information payload, and the precise referent is the Fisher inflow of #deriv-tempo-additivity §1 (526815). (b) Lines 15 and 50 say tempo is reached equally by a fast noisy loop or a slower calibrated one, and that the two are 'substitutable'. That holds only within one information direction and channel-independence regime. A fast redundant channel cannot substitute for a slow non-redundant one, and gain in the wrong dimension cannot substitute for gain in the bottleneck direction (742613, 584721), and persistence need not be invariant to which way the tempo is bought. Line 60 states independence for additivity only. Source: gold Follow-ups (WN lines 82, 84).
