# Open formalizations of the unity dimensions: (a) whether they are independent (epistemic unity likely enables strategic unity; perhaps 'substantially independent inputs to a joint prediction of epsilon*'); content-axis vs structural-axis independence (U_d vs U_f) is cleaner but unproved; (b) test the metrics on concrete cases (a software team, a military unit) for whether they separate well- from poorly-composed groups; (c) a general U_f operator distance across f_M classes (operator norm, Fisher-weighted, IB comparison), the Kalman gain-mismatch form being the only closed one; (d) extension beyond linear-Gaussian, where epsilon_x > 0 is expected even with consistent projections; (e) the exact f_1 and g forms for the joint (U_O, U_Sigma) to epsilon_a dependence, from a joint-LQR vs independent-LQR comparison. Sources: #def-unity-dimensions Working Notes items 1, 2, 4; #result-unity-closure-mapping Working Notes 'Extension to nonlinear cases', 'Structural-unity formalization', 'Joint (U_O, U_Sigma) derivation'.
