# Land, at conditional tier at most, the bias-variance form of strategic persistence under gain-modulated observation noise: for xi_{t+1} = (1-g) xi_t + g eps_t - d_t with noise variance v = theta(1-theta) and constant drift rho_Sigma, E[xi^2] = rho_Sigma^2/g^2 + g v/(2-g); mean-square feasibility is min_g [rho^2/g^2 + g v/(2-g)] < R^2, with g* ~ (4 rho^2/v)^(1/3) and approximately rho_Sigma <~ 0.77 R^3/v (an R^3 cap). Algebra checked by simulation to four digits; as an edge-estimator model it idealizes v as constant and drift as unbounded, which the landing must say.
