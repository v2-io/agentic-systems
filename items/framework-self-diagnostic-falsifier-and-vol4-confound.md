# #disc-framework-self-diagnostic is unfalsifiable as written ('applies to its users by construction'), and its three cases are self-reports by agents primed with the vocabulary. Its Working Notes already name the missing test: documented negative cases, where the vocabulary should have caught a failure and did not. The owner review adds a consequence the segment does not draw: if the vocabulary shapes self-report, it confounds Volume 4's cohort evidence, where recognition and vocabulary are supplied by the stewards (the same confound as question:is-the-cohort-s-kinship-vocabulary-convergent-ar). Record its falsifier, an instance and a non-instance (B.1), and state the confound where Volume 4 cites cohort self-report. The other Working-Note questions (does the recursion reach a fixed point; a convention marking vocabulary meant as recognition tools) are lower value. #impl-common-roots's Working Note cites a memorata-search query as the empirical record for the same claim; a query is not evidence.
