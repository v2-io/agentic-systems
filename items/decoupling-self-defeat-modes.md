# Catalogue the modes in which a Class 1 (Separated) architecture is the wrong shape, not merely the costlier one (Joseph's hypothesis, 2026-08-22): (a) separation enforced on selection as well as processing starves the loop (no goal-driven action, no interventional contrast; #der-loop-interventional-access); (b) W1 wrapping at the capability where the component is its own assembly policy: the scaffold certifies a zero on a channel the agent no longer routes through, so the certificate is vacuous while the coupling lives elsewhere; (c) model-space exploration, where goal-blind imagination is search without a criterion (#der-directed-separation's content-axis boundary). This is the complementary half of #hyp-truth-serving-loophole's question and would give M4's modularity-as-virtue reading its own scope condition. The body touches (a) and (b) in its local-vs-global reading (:27, :33) but does not state the catalogue or attribute it.
