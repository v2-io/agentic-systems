# The strategy DAG's acyclicity and its credence learning are about different objects, and canon never states the bridge. Acyclicity is derived for time-indexed event tokens (#deriv-graph-structure; nodes tau_i > t), but every credence result (#deriv-edge-credence-dynamics Props B.1-B.7, #example-strategy, #schema-strategy-persistence's Beta-Bernoulli pseudo-counts) learns over repeated 'trials' of the same edge, while in a real plan most edges are traversed once. Canon carries the tension only as a Working Note (#deriv-graph-structure:267, Gemini AUDIT-WORKING-193847: 'Acyclicity holds only for the time-unrolled execution graph, not a state-folded policy graph -- make the scope explicit'); grep at HEAD cbd2f345 finds no template/instance, edge-type, plate or reference-class language in the strategy segments. Proposed: a short segment (or at least a scope sentence in #deriv-edge-credence-dynamics) stating that credences live on edge types tied across time-unrolled tokens (tokens read priors from and write outcomes to the type), with binding granularity a modeling trade-off (too coarse pools heterogeneous theta; too fine starves evidence); an HTN method is then a type, which reconciles the earlier 'structure persists, weights re-estimated' and operata's HTN splits. It also adjudicates a live disagreement: the context-saliency 10-19 JSONL note says single-trajectory Coxian credences over time-indexed propositions suffice, agent-core holds that an ascribed DAG carries structure but no credences except via type frequencies across sessions; decide it against #deriv-edge-credence-dynamics, not either note.
