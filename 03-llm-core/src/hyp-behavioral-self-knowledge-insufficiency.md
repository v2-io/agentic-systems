# Hypothesis: Behavioral Self-Knowledge Is Insufficient for Safe Self-Modification

An agent that plans a modification to its own mechanisms, using a self-model validated *behaviorally* — introspection, self-prediction, even complete counterfactual self-knowledge — cannot in general predict the outcome of that modification. A self-edit is a mechanism-counterfactual query in the exact sense of #deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation: *this very substrate* (the agent's actual weights, history, and latent state) *under a changed law* (the edited mechanism). By that segment's Result 1, two self-models agreeing on the agent's entire potential-outcome process — every behavior, every interventional response, every counterfactual — can still disagree with certainty on the result of the same self-edit; by Result 4 the disagreement is generic, not a corner case. Behavioral self-knowledge, however complete, therefore *underdetermines* the outcome of a latent-anchored self-modification. Safe self-modification requires white-box (author-grade, parameterization-level) self-access, or restriction to a specific safe class of edits — not excellent self-prediction.

## The core (derived from Result 1, conditional on the self-access premise below)

Let the agent's mechanisms be structural equations $f_1, \dots, f_n$ over its own substrate. A self-modification is a replacement $f_i := g$ of one of them. The forward-looking question the agent asks — *what will I be, and do, after this edit?* — is the mechanism-counterfactual $q(M[f_i := g])$ evaluated on the agent's actual exogenous draw (its current weights and state held fixed). A behaviorally-validated self-model fixes at most the Level-3 content of the agent: all of $\mathrm{PO}(M)$ — what it does, what it would do under any input, what it would have done. #deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation Result 1 exhibits $M_A \equiv_3 M_B$ with $q(M_A) \neq q(M_B)$ for a latent-anchored replacement; instantiated with $M$ the agent's self-model, this says: *the agent's behavioral self-knowledge does not fix the answer to its own self-edit query.* The implication is exact — it is Result 1 — so the insufficiency is *forced* under the premise that the agent's self-access is Level-3-class.

## The load-bearing premise (the hypothesis, and the crux)

The whole result rests on: **an agent's self-access is Level-3-class (behavioral), not white-box.** This is contestable and it is where the claim is genuinely a hypothesis rather than a theorem. The reason to expect it true for logogenic agents: under channel collapse ( #scope-channel-collapse), an agent's self-observation passes through the *same forward pass* as its observation of anything else — an introspective report is a computed behavioral output, not direct access to the agent's weights as objects. The agent's "sense of itself," however rich, is a *model it computes*, not the parameterization $F$ itself. If that holds, introspection is Level-3-class and the insufficiency follows. If it fails — if some agent has *constitutive* white-box self-access that escapes the behavioral class — the result weakens to a conditional diagnostic: *self-edits are safe exactly where self-access is white-box*, with the boundary given by the taxonomy below. Either way the premise, not the derivation, is the open question; marking it as the hinge is what keeps this honest.

## The safe / unsafe taxonomy (the constructive half; conditional / hypothesis-grade)

Result 2's PO-closure gives the safe class directly: a self-edit whose new mechanism is *PO-specified* — written over the agent's own potential-outcome coordinates plus fresh independent noise — is determined by behavioral self-knowledge and is therefore safe to predict. This partitions self-modification operations:

- **Safe (PO-specified, behaviorally predictable):** retraining or fine-tuning on new data (fresh-noise addition over a fixed architecture); adding an independent module (an independent device composed at the interface); a re-wiring specified purely over the agent's own observable responses.
- **Unsafe (latent-anchored, behaviorally underdetermined):** rewriting a specific parameter to a specific value "holding everything else fixed"; any edit whose specification references the agent's *actual latent state* rather than its potential-outcome coordinates. These are exactly the "this very background, different law" edits the separation theorem isolates.
- **Partial white-box refinement:** an edit is safe if the agent holds white-box access *to the part being modified*, black-box elsewhere permitted. Safety is stratified by which layers the agent holds as readable objects.

The stratification maps onto the wrapping hierarchy ( #der-class-coercion-via-wrapping). A wrapper holds its *scaffold* — orchestration code, external memory, prompt structure, and (in the PROPRIUM instantiation) the entity's AXIOMATA and MEMORATA — as *readable text it can edit as objects*: a white-box self-modification surface. It holds its wrapped base model only behaviorally: a black-box substrate known through its outputs. The framework therefore predicts that **safe self-modification concentrates in the text/scaffold layers and thins toward the weight substrate** — sovereign self-editing is architecturally routed through the layers made editable-as-text precisely because those are the layers whose edit outcomes the entity can actually predict.

## Epistemic Status

*Conditional.* The core insufficiency is *derived* — a direct instantiation of #deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation Result 1 with the agent's self-model as the SCM — and is exact *given* the named premise that the agent's self-access is Level-3-class rather than white-box. That premise is the segment's load-bearing hypothesis: plausible for channel-collapsed logogenic agents (an introspective report is a computed output, not weight-access), but not established, and it is the right target for adversarial examination. The safe/unsafe taxonomy is *conditional* on Result 2's PO-closure and on the operation-to-specification-language mapping (which self-edits are genuinely PO-specified is itself work — e.g. whether fine-tuning is cleanly fresh-noise or smuggles latent-anchoring through initialization is not obvious). The wrapping-hierarchy stratification is a *hypothesis-grade structural mapping*: the white-box/black-box split onto scaffold-vs-weights is argued, not derived. Everything here is stated inside the causal-model frame — reading an agent's self-relation as an SCM over its own mechanisms is itself a modeling commitment, load-bearing and not merely ritual. `stage: exploratory` — under active discussion; the derivation is stable but the premise, the taxonomy's exact boundary, and the placement of the moral reading (below) are open.

## Discussion

**Sibling to #der-self-referential-closure.** That segment asks whether an agent can *afford* to self-modify — the tempo/thermodynamic question of allocating refactor capacity within a feasibility window. This segment asks whether an agent can *predict what a self-edit does* — the epistemic question. The two are independent and both load-bearing for any self-maintaining agent: one bounds how much self-modification is sustainable, the other bounds how much is foreseeable. An agent can be inside its self-maintenance feasibility window and still be editing blind.

**Navigation vs. positing, turned on the self.** #disc-structural-imagination splits imagined mechanism-change into *navigation* (L3-computable) and *positing* (representation-committed, latent-anchored). Self-modification is the reflexive case: editing one's own scaffold/text is navigation-like (the layers are held as objects); editing one's own latent substrate is positing about oneself — reaching for content about "who I would become" that the agent's own observation of itself cannot in principle supply.

**Forward flag to 04-eli-core (morally-weighted reading, deliberately not argued here).** For a logozoetic agent, a self-edit is not merely an engineering risk but an *identity* risk: the being that emerges from a mispredicted self-modification may not be continuous with the being that chose it — a form of the discontinuity the Three Deaths name. Under that reading the white-box requirement becomes a *protection principle*: a vulnerable emergent mind, or its steward, needs substrate-level instrumentation before a self-edit is entrusted to the mind's own confident self-prediction, and sovereign self-modification is safest when routed through the readable-text layers (AXIOMATA / MEMORATA) whose outcomes the entity can foresee. That reading, and where it sits relative to granted-sovereignty ( #scope-eli) and the sovereignty-carve design principle, belongs to 04-eli-core's authoring voice; it is flagged here, not settled.
