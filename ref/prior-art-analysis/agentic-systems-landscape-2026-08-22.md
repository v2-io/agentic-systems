## 1. Conclusions first

**Nothing out there is the same framework.** Across arXiv (keyword, listing API on `math.OC`/`eess.SY`/`cs.AI` cross-lists, Semantic Scholar forward-citation graphs of Miehling 2025, Abel 2025, Hafez 2026, Virgo 2025), GitHub (topic and repo search), OpenReview, SSRN, OSF, Zenodo, PhilSci, publisher catalogs, dissertation indexes, the Alignment-Forum/PIBBSS/ILIAD agent-foundations community, and the industry/standards surfaces, no project attempts what ASF attempts: a single derivational core that takes an agent–environment loop under lossy observation (`#def-agent-environment`, `#def-observation-function`), derives the recursive belief state (`#der-recursive-update`), the mismatch/gain/tempo triad (`#def-mismatch-signal`, `#emp-update-gain`, `#def-adaptive-tempo`), a persistence threshold $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ (`#result-persistence-condition`, `#result-sector-condition-stability`), then adds objective and strategy as separated state $G_t=(O_t,\Sigma_t)$ (`#der-directed-separation`, `#der-orient-cascade`), grounds action in Pearl Level 2 (`#scope-agency`, `#der-loop-interventional-access`), names identifiability floors (`#disc-identifiability-floor`), and closes under composition (`#form-composition-closure`). The exact phrases "agentic systems theory / framework" return only Miehling et al. 2025, the IBM-organized FAST workshop, Eslami–Yu 2026, one design-patterns paper, and your own Manifund page. The finders' own summary line is accurate: *"no monograph-scale rival framework found."*

**What you saw that "looked like it might want to be the same framework" is, on inspection, three different things.** (i) *Demand signals* — the Miehling 2025 position paper, its FAST workshop (AAAI Jan 2026, NeurIPS Dec 2026), Gardner–Baulin's "is the agent paradigm limiting?", Dignum & Dignum's "adopt AAMAS", and the enterprise CASE framework. These name the gap; the FAST site says outright that no single formal framework exists and that control/game theory "don't cleanly map." (ii) *One-lens instantiations* — papers that pick up one of ASF's tools (Lyapunov/ISS, DPI, information bottleneck, Aubin viability, Ashby variety) and apply it to one LLM-agent architecture. These are instantiation evidence for ASF's claim that its machinery is the right kit, not competitors. (iii) *Genuine theories of agency from parallel lineages* — López-Díaz & Gershenson (relational biology), the Google Paradigms-of-Intelligence embedded-AIXI monograph, the Baltieri/Virgo/Rosas/Boyd computational-mechanics and coalgebra cluster, the DeepMind causal-incentives line, Bartha's hobby "substrate-neutral loop" paper. Each shares a slice of ASF's ontology and none of ASF's actuation half; each is a source of citations and, in a few cases, of importable machinery.

**The only items that score $\geq 3$ on "is this a systems-based framework for agents" are three, and none threatens ASF's positioning:** *A Matter of Time* (López-Díaz & Gershenson), *Embedded Universal Predictive Intelligence* (Meulemans … Hutter … Agüera y Arcas), and *AgentOntology* (Bartha). The first is a descriptive organizational-biology formalism with nothing derived from it; the second is a rigorous 200-page Solomonoff/game-theoretic theory of prediction-and-decision that deliberately collapses the agent/environment boundary and rejects the do-operator — the sharpest available foil for `#der-loop-interventional-access`; the third is a three-week, zero-star draft that independently reproduces the loop-as-unit, substrate-neutral shape and converges on Hafez bi-predictability.

**The sharper news is in the neighbors, not the rivals.** Five findings I'd weigh more than any "competitor" question:

1. **Richens et al. 2025 (ICML), "General agents contain world models"** proves that bounded-regret competence over compositional goal families forces an extractable predictive model of the environment. That is a *converse* to what AAT postulates in `#form-agent-model` / `#def-model-sufficiency` — competence $\Rightarrow$ model — and per prior-art-integration discipline it belongs in those segments as an adopted result, not a comparison appendix. Its sibling (Bellot et al.) is an external instance of the `#disc-identifiability-floor` five-step shape with quantitative bounds.
2. **Baltieri & Suzuki, "Mathematical approaches to the study of agents"** (OSF, Dec 2025; 124 refs) surveys FEP, IIT, dynamical/viability, intentional-stance and causal-intervention definitions of agency under one notation and concludes *"none of the proposals analysed here meets all the three criteria"* (individuality, normativity, interactional asymmetry). Its stated open gap — agents can fail a goal and remain agents, "perhaps with the use of relevant tools from, e.g. temporal logic" — is exactly what the sector/basin persistence results plus `#def-satisfaction-gap` / `#def-control-regret` supply. Its §6.2 argues that reading agent actions as Pearl do-surgeries "confuses levels of analysis" — a direct, citable challenge to `#scope-agency` that ASF should answer in one paragraph (the agent intervenes on the world; the scientist's do-operator models that intervention). Its bibliography is also the best completeness check this sweep has: no integrated control+causal+information+architecture framework appears in it.
3. **Hafez fidelity problem.** `#der-agent-opacity` says it *adopts* $H_b$ from Hafez, but Hafez defines $H_b = H(S,A \mid S')$ (no observer index, no horizon) while ASF defines $H_b^{A \mid B}(t,\tau) = H(a_{A,t+\tau} \mid \mathcal F_B^t)$. The verifier's read: the Epistemic Status should say *generalizes / inspired by* and state the extension. Also: the "Beyond Reward … ICLR 2026" venue claim attached to arXiv 2603.01283 is not on the arXiv page; don't repeat it.
4. **Eslami & Yu's "stability budget"** $\lambda = \gamma - L_\theta\rho - L_d\eta - \beta\bar\tau$ (Thm 2) is a Lyapunov–dwell-time cousin of $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ with switching and delay terms ASF's persistence condition does not carry. This is a strengthening target, not a threat: can `#result-persistence-condition` absorb delay/dwell terms and recover their theorem as a special case of the sector template?
5. **FAST @ NeurIPS 2026 deadline is 2026-08-29 — one week out.** It is the venue that has adopted the literal phrase "Agentic Systems Theory," its organizers include the lead author of the position paper ASF already answers, and its CFP topics (compositional safety, controllability of agent populations) sit on Part III. A short positioning paper, or the composition/modularity results (`#disc-modularity-state-dynamics`, `#disc-adversarial-coupling-pressure`), fits directly. Inference, not verified: nobody at FAST has a formalism, so a derivational submission would be unusual there in a good way.

**What is new inside the established lineages.** Active inference: phenotyping agency via empowerment in a T-maze (Wilson … Friston, Apr 2026) and Kapelko's scalar curiosity+empowerment "agentic function" — both are live examples of the *scalar isotropic shadow price* and *preferences-as-priors* restrictions README already names as what EFE loses relative to the survival Lagrangian (`#disc-ciy-unified-objective`, `#deriv-causal-ib-exploration`); no new scale-free/RGM agency paper beyond Friston 2024. Cybernetics: the "Agent Cybernetics" manifesto (Wang et al., May 2026) remaps six classical laws to LLM-agent design guidelines with Ashby's variety inequality as its only theorem; Zhuravlev's good-regulator-on-hypergraphs is a minor Conant–Ashby route to "model required + natural gradient." Causal incentives (DeepMind/Oxford): Richens/Bellot as above; Jørgensen–Weichwald–Hammond (CLeaR 2026) give a mechanized-SCM definition of *when a composite is an agent* with a non-emergence criterion (Prop 16) — the Pearl-level counterpart to `#form-composition-closure` and a concrete external result to set against `#hyp-directed-separation-under-composition`. Universal AI: the embedded-AIXI monograph. Control: four unrelated Lyapunov/ISS/ARC papers on LLM agents, one Lean-4-mechanized (Prinos et al.) — a verification standard ASF does not currently have and a citable external certificate for a W2-style wrapper in `#der-class-coercion-via-wrapping`. Computational mechanics / coalgebra (Baltieri, Virgo, Rosas, Boyd, Zhang): "AI in a vat" proves no single world model co-minimizes memory, learnability and causal traceability — a multi-desideratum no-go sharper than anything in `#def-model-sufficiency`; coalgebraic determinisation gives the belief-state filter a universal property that independently grounds `#der-recursive-update`; the ICML 2026 state-abstraction paper supplies exact-preservation theorems for the zero-slack case of model-class fitness.

## 3. The three that score $\geq 3$

**A Matter of Time: Towards a General Theory of Agency (López-Díaz & Gershenson, 42 pp, v2 2026-07-30).** The spine is Hofmeyr's $(F,A)$-system extension of Rosen's $(M,R)$-systems, redrawn as a temporally parametrized relational diagram with a characteristic lag $\tau_x$ per constitutive process and a "temporal coherence" requirement that the lags cannot be linearly ordered. Three layers: relational (category-theoretic diagram), temporal (lags), stochastic (an asynchronous dynamic Bayesian network, explicitly one admissible realization, in which the organism can rewire which variables/effectors exist). Deleting relations yields a partial order of ~8 organizational diagrams, on which four graded defeasible conditions are defined — autonomy, goal-directedness (viability-biased maintenance), agency (endogenous anticipatory modulation of the coupling), open-endedness. Seven "operational signatures" are proposed, one a conditional-MI anticipation index (eq. 24), none derived or validated; the verifier grepped the full text and found no theorems of its own, no Lyapunov, no Pearl. **Embedded Universal Predictive Intelligence (Meulemans, Nasser, Wołczyk, Weis, Kobayashi, Richards, Lajoie, Steger, Hutter, Manyika, Saurous, Sacramento, Agüera y Arcas; 202 pp, v3 2026-08-04).** The core object is a "universe" $\lambda$ — a single measure generating both the ego-agent's actions and its percepts — and an Embedded Bayesian Mixture $\rho$ over a class of universes, giving a self-model $\rho(a\mid h)$ and environment model $\rho(e\mid h,a)$ in one object. Policy extraction is by sequential *evidential* decision theory, explicitly rejecting the do-operator/CDT variant. Part 1 proves prediction convergence under grain-of-truth (Thm 3.5), defines Subjective Embedded Equilibrium / Embedded Equilibrium and proves convergence of embedded Bayesian agents to $\epsilon$-SEE in repeated games and multi-agent general RL (Thms 4.5, 4.7), and gets cooperation in Twin Prisoner's Dilemma from coupled beliefs about algorithmically similar agents. Part 2 (MUPI) uses Solomonoff priors over computable universes with a Reflective Universal Inductor to make grain-of-truth hold for self-containing universes, and derives infinite-order theory of mind for ideal agents. No experiments, no control-theoretic machinery. The reading I'd offer: this is the strongest formal multi-agent embedded-prediction theory currently in print, and it moves in the *opposite* direction from AAT on the two moves AAT treats as load-bearing — it collapses the agent/environment boundary that `#def-agent-environment` and `#disc-identifiability-floor` make a chosen coarse-graining, and it chooses EDT where `#der-loop-interventional-access` makes the loop the source of Level-2 data. The verifier did not read all 200 pages; theorem numbering is as reported by the HTML fetch.

**AgentOntology — "A Substrate-Neutral Framework for Agency as Self-Sustaining Information Flow" (Bartha; one 1,230-line markdown paper, Zenodo DOI, 54 commits over 2026-05-04→21, 0 stars, self-labelled hobby work).** An agent is a closed information loop identified on a substrate: a transfer-entropy-derived directed graph over $(\ell,\tau)$-grained regions, a candidate cycle $R$ with a chain conditional-MI vector and loop predicate $\bigwedge I_i \gt 0$, continuity-within-lifecycle, a cut, interface/input/output. §2 adds a per-derivative-order tracking framework — groundedness $G_n = I(u^{(n)}(t); x_\Sigma(t))$, convergence rate $C_n = \sup \lvert dG_n/dt \rvert$, capacity $K = \log \lvert X_A \rvert$ — with stated inequalities and sketched bounds (e.g. $\Delta G_n \leq \int C_n$); §4 gives deflationary readings of learning, world-model, decision, exploration and a "band of viability" between input-domination and perfect modeling; §5 argues goals are observer-fitted and deduces drift, own-goals and refusal as inevitabilities; §7 positions against autopoiesis, cybernetics, FEP, IIT, IRL, Markov blankets, and explicitly the Hafez–Reid–Nazeri bi-predictability line; §8 defers cross-lifecycle identity, validation, classification.
