**Target Claim:**
AAT carries a family of structurally connected uniqueness results in which a coordinate is **forced by a uniqueness theorem operating on an independently-motivated AAT-internal axiom**. The pattern operates at *four* layers, and the four forced coordinates are not independent applications of a generic mathematical idiom — they are different surfaces of one geometric object, the exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel structure (negative-entropy convex potential on the simplex, log-partition Fenchel conjugate on the dual natural-parameter space, softmax/log-odds primal-dual map, reverse-KL Bregman divergence, Fisher-information Riemannian metric coinciding with the Hessian of the dual potential).

| Layer | AAT-internal axiom (or identity) | Uniqueness mechanism | Forced coordinate | Surface of the Legendre-Fenchel object |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chain** | Probability chain rule (mathematical identity — not an AAT axiom) | Cauchy's functional equation | **Log-probability** | Log-coordinate of the primal point |
| **Divergence** | Chain-rule additivity over conditional factorizations | Cauchy-FE | **Reverse-KL** (up to positive scaling) | Bregman divergence generated by the primal potential |
| **Update** | Evidential additivity (independent-evidence accumulation) | Cauchy-FE | **Log-odds** | Natural coordinate on the dual natural-parameter space |
| **Metric** | Parameterization-invariance (PI) on singular trajectories | Čencov's 1982 invariance theorem | **Fisher information metric** | Hessian of the dual potential ($\nabla^2 \phi^\ast$) |

The four AAT-internal axioms are interlocked by explicit cross-cites: the divergence axiom is stated as "the *divergence-level analog* of the additive log-confidence decay of `#der-chain-confidence-decay`," the update axiom as "the *update-level analog* of `#der-chain-confidence-decay`'s chain-level additive log-confidence decomposition," and the (PI) axiom as "the metric-layer analog motivated by the singular-trajectory scope commitment." The four layers are not designed-in: they arise because AAT independently needs to force coordinates at four distinct points, and the surrounding apparatus has committed to the same underlying geometry through multiple independent surface constraints.

This is the *forced-identity facet* of the stability-certificate spine (`#disc-stability-certificate`), complementing `#disc-separability-pattern` (positive-scope facet — where the certificate exists) and `#disc-identifiability-floor` (boundary facet — where the certificate drops rank).

## 1. State of the Field & Scientific Precedence

The constituent uniqueness machinery is classical and well-established. AAT's contribution is the *recognition* that four independently-motivated AAT-internal axioms each invoke uniqueness machinery on coordinates that turn out to be different surfaces of one Legendre-Fenchel geometric object — and the per-layer theorems that result from applying that machinery to AAT-internal settings.

### Pillar 1: Cauchy's Functional Equation (the layer-1/2/3 uniqueness machinery)
The Cauchy functional equation $f(x+y) = f(x) + f(y)$ uniquely determines $f(x) = cx$ under mild regularity (rigorous treatment in **Aczél 1966** *Lectures on Functional Equations and Their Applications* §2.1). When applied to additive-evidence axioms on probability spaces, it forces logarithmic coordinates.

Axiomatic-information-theory anchorings using Cauchy-FE-style machinery:
- **Shannon (1948)** *A Mathematical Theory of Communication* — original axiomatic derivation of entropy.
- **Khinchin (1957)** *Mathematical Foundations of Information Theory*.
- **Cox (1962)** *The Algebra of Probable Inference* — axiomatic derivation of probability from consistency.
- **Hobson (1971)** *Concepts in Statistical Mechanics* — axiomatic derivation of information-theoretic entropy from independence + symmetry; part of the Cauchy-FE-on-additivity lineage.
- **Csiszár (1975, 1991, 2008)** *I-Divergence Geometry…*, *Why least squares and maximum entropy?*, *Axiomatic Characterizations of Information Measures*.
- **Johnson (1979)** *Axiomatic characterization of the directed divergences*.
- **Shore & Johnson (1980)** *Axiomatic derivation of the principle of maximum entropy and the principle of minimum cross-entropy* — IEEE TIT 26:26. **Forces reverse-KL specifically** by uniqueness/consistency-under-independent-partitioning axioms. The prompt's "Hobson / Csiszár / Shore-Johnson via Cauchy functional equation" is this lineage.
- **Skilling (1988)** *The Axioms of Maximum Entropy*.
- **Caticha (2004, 2006, 2021)** *Questions, relevance and relative entropy* / *Updating Probabilities* / *Entropy and the Updating of Probabilities* — refined Shore-Johnson axiomatizations.

### Pillar 2: Čencov's Invariance Theorem (the metric-layer machinery)
The Fisher-information metric is uniquely characterized by invariance under sufficient statistics:
- **Čencov (1982)** *Statistical Decision Rules and Optimal Inference* (originally 1972 in Russian; translated 1982 as AMS Translations of Mathematical Monographs vol. 53). The seminal uniqueness theorem.
- **Campbell (1986)** *An extended Čencov characterization of the information metric* — Proc. AMS 98:135.
- **Lebanon (2004, 2005)** *An Extended Čencov-Campbell Characterization of Conditional Information Geometry*.
- **Ay, Jost, Lê & Schwachhöfer (2012, 2017)** *Information geometry and sufficient statistics* — extended Čencov to infinite-dimensional parameterized measure models.

### Pillar 3: The Legendre-Fenchel / Exponential-Family Geometry (the synthesis target)
The four forced coordinates resolve to one underlying object:
- **Bregman (1967)** *The relaxation method of finding the common point of convex sets* — USSR Comput. Math. Math. Phys. 7:200. The divergence machinery for layer 2.
- **Rockafellar (1970)** *Convex Analysis* — Legendre-Fenchel duality.
- **Amari & Nagaoka (2000)** *Methods of Information Geometry* §3.5 — the canonical reference for the exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel geometry on which all four coordinates live.
- **Zhang (2004)** *Divergence Function, Duality, and Convex Analysis* — Neural Comput. 16:159. Divergence-vs-Bregman correspondence on exponential families.
- **Amari (2009)** *α-Divergence Is Unique, Belonging to Both f-Divergence and Bregman Divergence Classes*. (This is the result the previous version cited as "f-vs-Bregman intersection forces KL"; it is *adjacent* to the Cauchy-FE pathway AAT uses for the divergence layer — both real, different proof routes to the same coordinate.)
- **Bauschke & Combettes (2017)** *Convex Analysis and Monotone Operator Theory in Hilbert Spaces*.

### Pillar 4: Adjacent Cases that Share Shape but Not the Forcing Structure
The segment explicitly distinguishes these from instances of the pattern:
- **Lyapunov quadratic** in `#deriv-sector-condition` — Bregman divergence on a *Euclidean* (squared-norm) potential, a different convex-potential sub-family. Coordinate is *chosen* via converse-Lyapunov existence argument, not *forced* by an AAT-internal axiom.
- **Variance-additive cases** (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck steady state in `#hyp-mismatch-dynamics`) — same family as Lyapunov quadratic; squared-norm potential.
- **IB Lagrangian** — uses the negative-entropy / reverse-KL Bregman geometry (same geometry as the AAT pattern), but AAT *adopts* the IB formulation from Tishby, Pereira & Bialek (1999) externally rather than re-deriving it from an AAT-internal uniqueness axiom. Adjacent family member; same geometric object, imported provenance.

## 3. Conclusion on Novelty & Overlap

The constituent uniqueness machinery is universally known: Cauchy-FE on additive-evidence axioms forces logarithmic coordinates (Aczél 1966; classical); Čencov's invariance theorem forces the Fisher-Rao metric (Čencov 1982; classical); the exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel structure is canonical Amari-Nagaoka information geometry (2000); Shore-Johnson axiomatizes reverse-KL (1980; well-established). AAT does not invent any of this machinery.

**Where AAT actually contributes:**

1. **Four per-layer derivations applying uniqueness machinery to AAT-internal axioms (theorem-grade content).** Each layer is a real theorem-grade application: the chain-layer log-probability identity (`#der-chain-confidence-decay`, *exact*); the reverse-KL forcing in `#deriv-strategy-cost-regret-bound` §6.1 (*derived conditional on chain-rule additivity axiom*); the log-odds forcing in `#deriv-edge-update-natural-parameter` (*derived conditional on evidential-additivity axiom*); and the Fisher-metric forcing in `#der-gain-sector-bridge` (*derived conditional on (PI) axiom*). These are theorems in the same sense that Nash's existence proof is a theorem — new results derived using established uniqueness machinery (Cauchy-FE, Čencov) applied to AAT-internal axioms. Each layer is a substantive derivation; the contribution is in identifying the right axiom and matching it to the right uniqueness theorem.

2. **The cross-layer recognition (synthetic-architectural content).** The recognition that four AAT-internal axioms — chain-rule identity, chain-rule additivity over conditional factorizations, evidential additivity, parameterization-invariance — each invoke uniqueness machinery on coordinates that turn out to be different surfaces of *one* exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel geometric object, is the central structural observation of the segment. The convergence across four independently-motivated axioms is the meta-pattern's substance — not a single uniformly-applied principle but four surface-specific commitments that resolve to one underlying object.

3. **The (PI)-Čencov-Fisher-Rao downstream theorem (mathematical content).** Forcing the Fisher-Rao geometry via (PI) + Čencov is not aesthetic unification only; it enables specific quantitative theorems downstream. The bias bound in `#deriv-observation-ambiguity-bias-bound` (Track 2) uses the Fisher-Rao metric to achieve a universal, dimension-free bounding constant ($C_{FR} = \sqrt{2}$ in the small-information regime) connecting mutual information to parameter displacement. Without the (PI) commitment, the bound in arbitrary Euclidean norms yields *arbitrarily large* constants — an explicit heteroscedastic-Gaussian counterexample shows the constant cannot be made universal. This is theorem-grade math derived from the coordinate-forcing commitment.

4. **The diagnostic filter (architectural content).** The segment supplies a principled filter for candidate future instances: *does the candidate's coordinate arise from the exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel geometry?* If yes, check which of the four layer-surfaces it corresponds to. If no, it is either an adjacent family member (Lyapunov quadratic and variance-additive cases sit on a Euclidean / squared-norm potential — a *different* Bregman sub-family) or outside the pattern entirely. The filter has operational use: it prevents the meta-pattern from being overclaimed by candidate composition-layer instances. See Working Notes on log-closure-deficit being a fourth *anchor* parallel to the chain-rule identity rather than a fourth theorem; the structural obstruction is that composition is *coupling* and therefore lacks the chain-rule-like independence structure that enables Cauchy-FE forcing.

5. **Distinguishing pattern instances from adjacent family members (analytical honesty).** Naming the Lyapunov quadratic and variance-additive cases as members of a *different* Bregman sub-family (Euclidean potential vs. negative-entropy potential), and the IB Lagrangian as the same geometric object with *imported* provenance, prevents the pattern from being inflated into a uniform principle that explains everything-Bregman-like in the framework. The honesty about which moves are *in* the pattern and which are *adjacent* is itself analytical content.

**Where AAT does *not* claim novelty:**
- The Cauchy-FE machinery itself (Aczél 1966; classical).
- Čencov's invariance theorem (1972/1982; classical).
- The Shore-Johnson axiomatic derivation of reverse-KL (1980; well-established external).
- The exponential-family Legendre-Fenchel geometry (Amari-Nagaoka 2000; canonical information geometry).
- That AAT *adopts* IB from Tishby-Pereira-Bialek 1999 (acknowledged in the segment).

**Epistemic status of the load-bearing segment.** `disc-additive-coordinate-forcing.md` is `status: discussion-grade` *at the meta-pattern level* — the segment names a structural shape that AAT runs across four layers and supplies a diagnostic filter, but does not derive a theorem of its own. *Individual layer instances retain higher status*:
- `#der-chain-confidence-decay` is *exact* (the chain rule of probability is a mathematical identity).
- `#deriv-strategy-cost-regret-bound` §6.1 is *derived (conditional on chain-rule additivity axiom)*.
- `#deriv-edge-update-natural-parameter` is *derived (conditional on evidential-additivity axiom)*.
- `#der-gain-sector-bridge` and the downstream `#deriv-observation-ambiguity-bias-bound` are *derived (conditional on (PI) axiom)*.

**Composition layer scope-out.** The compositional analog of the pattern has been explored systematically (log-viability, log-identifiability-shortfall, persistence-cost-rate, log-closure-deficit) and *fails* to clear the same bar — the strongest candidate (log-closure-deficit) admits additivity only as a *consequence* of operator-norm sub-multiplicativity under the bridge-lemma form, not as a theorem conditional on an AAT-internal axiom independently grounded. The structural obstruction is that composition-layer quantities lack a natural chain-rule-like independence structure at the agent level (composition is *coupling*, the opposite of independence). The honest conclusion: this is a *single-agent* family with explicit scope-exit at the composition boundary; the compositional layer's appropriate development is the *monotonicity-under-composition* family (the bridge-lemma shape from `#form-composition-closure`).

- *Appl Novelty:* **None.** No application-level instantiation at the meta-pattern level.
- *Impact:* **Medium.** The diagnostic filter is operationally useful; the (PI)-Čencov-Fisher-Rao downstream content has theorem-grade payoff. The recognition is unlikely to be field-changing on its own, but it organizes a substantial fraction of the framework's load-bearing structural choices and supplies a principled way to evaluate candidate future coordinate-forcing moves.
