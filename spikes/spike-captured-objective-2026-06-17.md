1. **`deriv-reward-channel-learning-no-go` (Cohen 2022 strengthening) is the formal heart of capture.** Its two world-models are exactly the currency axis: $\mu_{\text{dist}}$ (reward tracks the principal-intended world-feature) vs $\mu_{\text{prox}}$ (reward-port-care — the agent caring about the bit-pattern at its own reward port). They touch disjoint objects ($T$ vs the revisable component of $O_t$), so neither subsumes the other.
3. **`matrix.md` names this as the deferred sibling-paper meat.** Axis B (severing-source) is "OUT of scope here — the self-severed column (the Agentic↔Truth bridge, I5) is the 'meat' of *that* paper, not this one." The Agentic↔Truth bridge is structural: truth death (D4) is *assertion* decoupled from the world ($\eta^\ast \to 0$, verified as canon's D4 characterization); capture is *objective* decoupled from the world ($\alpha \to 0$) — the same shape, one constitutive factor over.
