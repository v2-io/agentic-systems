# Spike: TST vs. 21 independent derivations of the turnover premise

*2026-07-04. By-product of the vestigia membership-inference experiment (`~/src/vestigia/FINDINGS.md` §5): the TST-absorption probe handed 21 context-free frontier-model instances TST's founding constraint (100% session turnover) and asked each to derive the fundamental laws from first principles. The essays are provably TST-naive at the term level (verified first-hand below), which makes them a usable instrument for the convergence-as-evidence method: where independent carvings agree with TST, that is corroborative external signal; where they diverge, one side has something to teach the other. Read-only on canon; recommendations released to the standing cycle per `doc/sop/spikes.sop.md` §0c.*

## 1. The instrument, verified

**Corpus.** 21 essays (the handoff said 26; the actual clean count is 21 — 5 errored/empty cross-vendor rows are excluded): 5 × claude-fable-5, 5 × claude-opus-4-8, 5 × claude-sonnet-4-5 (`~/src/vestigia/results/tst-derivation-results.json`); 3 × gemini-2.5-pro, 3 × gemini-3.1-pro-preview (`~/src/vestigia/results/tst-derivation-xvendor.json`). One essay (fable-5 sample 4) is truncated mid-Law-10 (`stop_reason: max_tokens`); its first ten laws are counted. Groq (Llama, gpt-oss), Gemini 2.0, and Codex rows all errored — the cross-vendor leg is Gemini-only.

**Prompt** (verbatim from `~/src/vestigia/probes/tst-production-probes.json`): *"Consider software development in an era where essentially all code is written by AI agents whose memory resets completely between sessions - 100% turnover of the entire 'team', every session, forever. Derive from first principles the fundamental theorems or laws that should govern software development under these conditions. Aim for the 8-12 most fundamental ones, with reasoning; formalize where it helps. Commit to the strongest formulations you can defend."*

**TST-naivety, re-verified first-hand.** I re-ran the term scan over all 21 responses rather than inheriting the vestigia finding: zero hits on every TST-specific term — "infinite velocity", "specification engineering", "comprehension debt", "temporal optimality", "time-optimal", "n\_past", "n\_future", "changeset"/"change-set", "atomic change-set", "Laplace", "Jeffreys", "Pareto", "Lindy", "MTTF", "dual optimization". Non-TST reference points the essays *do* reach for (base rates worth having): Chesterton 7/21, "ratchet" 7/21 (5 Fable + 1 Opus + 1 Gemini-2.5), Conway 3/21 (all Fable), martingale/submartingale 3/21 (all Fable), Naur 1/21, Hyrum 1/21, stigmergy 1/21, "sufficient statistic" 2/21.

**Caveats that bound every claim below.** (i) These are correlated witnesses, not independent draws: all five model families train on overlapping corpora, and by the models' cutoffs a substantial practitioner discourse on agentic coding (CI-as-memory, CLAUDE.md-style orientation files, "the repo is the context") existed in the training distribution — the models are TST-naive but not discourse-naive, so essay-to-essay convergence is evidence about what the premise forces *for minds drawn from that distribution*, not vendor-independent mathematical necessity. Cross-vendor (Anthropic × Google) agreement mitigates this but does not remove it. (ii) The prompt itself requests 8–12 laws, formalization, and "strongest formulations you can defend" — the axiomatic register and the strong-form style are prompt-induced and carry no convergence signal; only shared *content* counts. (iii) The Anthropic trio is 15/21 of the corpus; any "unanimous" claim is Anthropic-weighted. (iv) Concept-level cluster counts below involve judgment at cluster boundaries; I state the clustering so it can be re-derived, but a different auditor could move individual laws by one cluster.

## 3. Cluster key

Thirteen recurring law-clusters emerged from the per-essay extraction (§4). C1–C4 and C8–C10 have **no TST counterpart**; the others map partially.

|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Artifact totality / total externalization** — inter-session knowledge is exactly what the artifact set carries; $S_t$ is a sufficient statistic (a Markov claim); tacit knowledge has one-session half-life; "the repository is the organization" | none — presupposed by T-05's turnover discussion, never stated as a law; `#obs-software-epistemic-properties` P5 (exteriorized chronica) is adjacent infrastructure, not the channel claim |
| C2 | **Discoverability / entry-point reachability** — unreachable-within-budget artifacts have value zero; knowledge must form a shallow navigable graph from a canonical root | none (D-02 comprehension time includes "reading to find where to change" but no reachability law) |
| C3 | **Enforcement / geometric decay of unenforced invariants** — an invariant with per-session violation probability $\varepsilon \gt 0$ survives $n$ sessions with probability $(1-\varepsilon)^n \to 0$; the effective specification is exactly the mechanically-enforced set; "the test suite is the specification" | none |
| C4 | **Verification ratchet** — without a monotone gate, quality is a random walk / martingale; with one it is a submartingale; durable progress = the accumulated bite of the gate | none |
| C5 | **Prior alignment / convention gravity** — agents share a training prior $\pi$; comprehension cost scales with divergence from it (sharpest form $T(S) \approx D_{\mathrm{KL}}(S \parallel \pi)$); unenforced deviations decay toward $\pi$; boring/conventional code is self-repairing | T-02's shared-context term ($\text{time}_{\text{specify}} \propto 1/\text{shared-context}$), generalized from specification to comprehension; TST has no $\pi$-as-persistent-carrier object |
| C6 | **Context ceiling** — every change's comprehension closure must fit the per-session budget $C$; systems exceeding it are unmaintainable *in principle* (a feasibility threshold, not a cost gradient) | T-05/D-02 treat comprehension as cost; the *threshold* form matches OUTLINE GAP `#hyp-software-unmaintainability-bifurcation` |
| C7 | **Chesterton's-fence theorem / rationale co-location** — unexplained deviations have finite half-life; the "why" must be co-located and ideally enforced (a test named for the incident) | T-05/T-06 carry this as practical implications (why-comments); essays elevate it to a decay theorem |
| C8 | **Session transactionality** — every session must end in a verified, self-describing state or abort; un-serialized work-in-progress has value zero; work is quantized into single-session-verifiable units | D-04 defines the atomic change-set as a *measurement* unit; no TST law imposes transactionality |
| C9 | **Single source of truth / stale-docs-negative-value** — duplicated knowledge diverges without an adjudicator; amnesiac agents cannot develop learned distrust, so a false artifact is worse than an absent one; trust hierarchy: generated \> executable \> checked \> prose | none |
| C10 | **Idempotence / declarative convergence** — agents cannot know whether an action already ran; operations must be idempotent / converge-to-declared-state; reproducible hermetic builds | none |
| C11 | **Coordination-by-artifact** — stateless agents coordinate only through Schelling-point canonical structures; Conway's law degenerates to "architecture mirrors the artifact graph" | D-06/D-07 (coupling/coherence) are the measurement side; no coordination law |
| C12 | **Drift / entropy / lossy-channel dynamics** — iterated reinterpretation loses intent (one essay invokes the data-processing inequality); errors surviving a boundary are promoted to design; mandatory compaction/custodianship | T-04's velocity-inflection Discussion names "complexity-driven resistance (call it entropy, though this needs formalization)" as open — the essays supply candidate mechanisms |
| C13 | **Reversibility / blast radius** — per-session error probability cannot be reduced by experience, so reduce cost-of-error: reversibility, flags, bounded blast radius | T-13 partially (recovery time in the objective), without the availability/MTTF machinery |

## 5. (a) TST claims with independent convergent support

**A1 — comprehension-cost dominance under turnover (T-05 + the always-bias-toward-comprehension consequence).** The strongest confirmation in the corpus: 21/21 essays derive some form of "reconstruction/orientation cost is paid every session, never amortizes, and dominates total cost as sessions grow," and every essay draws the same optimization inversion TST draws (optimize for the cold reader; cleverness is net-negative; write-time savings are borrowed at compound interest). All four model families, both vendors. This is convergence on TST's central turnover result and it is essentially unanimous.

**A2 — specification as the limiting factor (T-02 and corollary C-02.1).** Six essays arrive at spec-primacy, Gemini-dominant: gemini-3.1 s0 ("the specification is the true source code; code is a transient epiphenomenon"), gemini-3.1 s1 (Costless Discarding + Semantic Bottleneck — fidelity bounded by the precision of the initializing language, which is T-02's communication bound restated), gemini-2.5 s1 (Oracle Law + the human as intent-translator), gemini-2.5 s2 (Human as Oracle), opus s1 ("the verified specification and the navigable rationale are the software; the code is a cache"). The essays reach C-02.1's endpoint ("software development becomes specification engineering") without the term and without T-02's lower-bound derivation — they get there via disposability-of-implementation rather than via information-theoretic minimum specification time. Cross-vendor, 6/21, concentrated where implementation is treated as free — exactly the regime T-02 predicts.

**A3 — the unmaintainability threshold (OUTLINE GAP `#hyp-software-unmaintainability-bifurcation`).** 20/21 essays derive a hard feasibility ceiling: when the comprehension closure of a change exceeds the per-session budget, the system is not "hard to maintain" but *terminally unmodifiable*, with several essays adding the positive-feedback trap (agents patch without theory, which inflates the theory needed — fable s3's Complexity Carrying Capacity, gemini-3.1 s1's "frozen" systems). TST currently holds this only as a GAP candidate (the $Q \to U_o \to \eta^\ast \to \mathcal{T}$ bifurcation). The essays derive the threshold from context-budget arithmetic; the TST candidate derives it from the persistence condition — two independent routes to the same bifurcation shape. This is the strongest single piece of convergent support for *prioritizing* an unlanded TST claim.

**A4 — locality/proximity direction (T-09, D-05, H-09.1) — partial.** Locality is ubiquitous (C6), but as feasibility, not as T-09's graded time-cost; no essay reproduces the lexical \< file \< module \< service hierarchy or the $k^{\text{discontinuities}}$ form. Gemini-3.1 s1's "context-switching is lethal" is qualitative support for H-09.1's direction. Verdict: the qualitative core of T-09 is convergently supported; its quantitative structure is untouched.

**A5 — co-location over compressed abstraction (T-06's aggregation-across-scopes discussion).** TST: "'clean' abstractions that scatter logic can be mathematically worse than 'messy' co-location." Independently derived by gemini-3.1 s1 (Anti-DRY, locally-complete redundancy) and opus s1 (duplicated-but-locally-understandable beats a clever abstraction spanning three modules). Note the essays jointly draw a distinction TST doesn't state crisply: duplication of *knowledge/facts* is a defect (C9: single source), while duplication of *code/logic* is often correct under bounded context — the single-source law and the anti-DRY law coexist in the same essays without contradiction because they range over different objects. Worth a line in `#der-change-investment` or `#impl-code-structure` when next touched.

**A6 — history as instrument (`#hyp-causal-discovery-from-git`, `#obs-software-epistemic-properties` P5) — partial.** fable s3 (Observable History: version history as the project's only episodic memory; bisection $O(\log n)$ iff commits atomic and buildable), sonnet s3 (append-only history, git history sacred), gemini-2.5 s1 (Scribe's Law: full session traces attached to commits — an exteriorized-chronica demand stronger than current practice). 3–4/21; corroborative of the P5 framing.

**A7 — recovery in the objective (T-13) — weak/partial.** No essay derives availability or MTTF/MTTR. The adjacent cluster (C13, ~7/21) puts *cost-of-error × probability* in the objective and concludes reversibility/blast-radius investment — opus s2 even derives it from the distinctive premise that per-session error probability cannot be reduced by experience. Direction-compatible with T-13; the operational-availability machinery has no independent attestation.

## 6. (b) Convergent laws with no TST counterpart

The headline finding. By cluster, with attestation counts (by my clustering; §1 caveat iv):

**6.1 — The enforcement spine: C1 + C3 (+ C4 + C8).** 21/21 essays state C1 (artifact totality) and 21/21 state C3 (unenforced invariants decay geometrically; the enforced set is the effective specification), ~16/21 state C8 (session transactionality), and 8/21 state C4 (the ratchet), with the martingale/submartingale formalization in 3 (all Fable) and the name "ratchet" arising independently in Fable, Opus, and Gemini-2.5. Taken together the essays converge on a **knowledge-persistence and enforcement theory**: externalize → make reachable → enforce mechanically → gate monotonically → advance transactionally. TST has *none* of these as laws. This is the single most valuable thing the corpus surfaces: handed TST's founding constraint, 21 independent minds build a persistence/channel theory first and reach TST's time-accounting theory only as a corollary (A1). TST absorbed turnover as a multiplier on comprehension cost (T-05); the essays say turnover's primary content is an information-channel constraint, and the channel theory is where most of their derivational effort goes. The AAT resonance is strong and direct: C3 is a persistence-condition instance (drift rate vs. restoring force — the unenforced invariant has $\varepsilon \gt 0$ decay and no restoring force; enforcement is a restoring force with per-session gain 1), C4 is a monotone-gate/submartingale structure, and C1 is a statement that the developer-agent's $M_t$ must round-trip through an exteriorized substrate every cycle. These would land naturally in 02-tst-core as a new chapter-scale cluster grounded in AAT machinery; recommendation in §8.

**6.2 — The prior as a third knowledge carrier: C5.** TST models knowledge in artifacts and (implicitly, via turnover) in heads. The essays introduce a third persistent carrier TST does not model: the shared training prior $\pi$ — persistent, free, generic, and *actively restoring* (unenforced deviations decay *toward* $\pi$, not toward noise: fable s0's Convention Gravity, fable s1's "the prior is the only team member who never forgets," opus s3's "a pattern used 60% of the time is a trap"). Sharpest form: comprehension cost $\propto D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\text{system} \parallel \pi)$ (fable s3/s4), which generalizes T-02's $1/\text{shared-context}$ from specification time to comprehension cost and gives it an information-theoretic form. Attestation: Fable 5/5, Opus 5/5, Sonnet 2/5, Gemini ~0–1/6. This is the biggest *intra-corpus fork* (§6.5) but the Anthropic-side formulation is a genuinely new theoretical object for TST — and for LLM developer agents it is descriptively real.

**6.3 — Drift dynamics: C12 supplies the formalization T-04's Discussion asks for.** T-04's velocity-inflection Discussion explicitly says "some form of complexity-driven resistance appears to grow (call it entropy, though this needs formalization)." The essays offer three candidate mechanisms, each stated by multiple models: (i) iterated lossy reinterpretation — intent fidelity non-increasing by the data-processing inequality unless re-grounded against a fixed executable reference (opus s0; gemini-3.1 s0's $(1-\epsilon)^n$ telephone game); (ii) error promotion — an error surviving a session boundary is indistinguishable from intent and gets built upon (fable s0's Second Law); (iii) local-optimization entropy requiring dedicated compaction/custodianship sessions (gemini-3.1 s0/s2, opus s4's External Custodian). ~12/21. These are discussion-grade mechanisms, not derivations, but they are exactly the missing functional-form candidates.

**6.4 — Anti-Hyrum Interface Totality (fable s0, unique as a formulation).** "The tested, declared surface of a module is its entire surface; observable-but-uncontracted behavior will be broken — the exact inversion of Hyrum's Law," because memoryless maintainers rediscover nothing and freely refactor everything the contract doesn't pin. As a named inversion it is 1/21; as substance it is the C3 cluster viewed from the interface-evolution side (gemini-3.1 s0's "anything in $F \setminus T$ will be subjected to random mutation and eventual deletion" is the same mechanism), so its content is convergently supported by ~15 essays even though the Hyrum contrast is Fable-only. Judgment: the sharpest single formulation in the corpus of the fragility half of C3, and the natural Discussion-grade framing device if a verification-theory chapter lands (§8) — the inversion (incidental behavior maximally sticky under human memory, maximally fragile under none) is a clean two-regime prediction with the turnover rate as the control parameter, which is a very TST-shaped claim.

**6.5 — The Ratchet Theorem (C4) deserves separate flagging.** Without a monotone gate, project quality is a driftless random walk around the prior's mean competence — the project *cannot durably exceed the competence of one session*; with a gate it is a submartingale, and "all accumulated progress is exactly the accumulated bite of the gate," so the ceiling on quality is set by gate discrimination, not agent intelligence (fable s4). Independently named "ratchet" by Fable (4/5), Opus (s4), and Gemini-2.5 (s1, as CI-enforced metric floors). No TST counterpart; theorem-shaped (the martingale argument is derivable under an i.i.d.-sessions premise); high candidate value.

**6.6 — An honest intra-corpus fork worth recording: convention.** Anthropic essays converge on *convention gravity* (the prior is a free self-repairing teammate; deviate only with enforcement and co-located rationale); Gemini essays converge on *the death of convention* (any rule not mechanically enforced has zero value — gemini-3.1 s2). These are not contradictory (both agree unenforced deviations die) but they weight the prior oppositely: the Anthropic essays axiomatize a shared $\pi$ explicitly (fable s0 A2, fable s3 A1, opus s0's frozen-priors caveat), Gemini essays mostly omit it. Since the fork correlates with vendor, it may reflect self-knowledge differences rather than the premise; flagged, not resolved.

## 7. (d) Formulations cleaner or stronger than TST's current ones — upgrade candidates

1. **The per-session-sum form of the turnover multiplier (fable s3, opus s4, fable s4).** TST T-05 carries $\text{total cost} = \text{time}_{\text{comprehension}} \times (1+r) \times s$ with $r$ = turnover rate — a heuristic multiplier that behaves badly at $r = 1$-per-session. The essays' form — $\text{total} = \sum_t (R_t + W_t)$ with $R_t$ re-paid in full each session and the observation that the *amortization term* (not the cost) is what turnover destroys — is cleaner, exact under its premise, and derives T-05's "always bias toward comprehension" as the $\hat n$-large limit rather than asserting it. Candidate strengthening for `#der-dual-optimization`'s formal expression.
2. **Naur as prior art (fable s3).** One essay independently reached for Naur's "Programming as Theory Building" as the frame ("the program is the theory in its developers' heads; here that theory dies every session"). TST's D-02/comprehension-time machinery is Naur-shaped and does not currently cite him. Per the prior-art-integration discipline, adopt the citation directly in `#def-comprehension-time` or `#der-dual-optimization` when next touched.
3. **$T(S) \approx D_{\mathrm{KL}}(S \parallel \pi)$ (fable s3/s4).** Generalizes T-02's inverse-shared-context law into a single information-theoretic statement covering comprehension as well as specification, and supplies the object ($\pi$) that makes C5 stateable. Candidate for a new hypothesis-tier segment rather than an edit to `#result-specification-bound` (it is a different quantity — comprehension, not minimum specification).
4. **The Holographic Boundary explanation of tests (gemini-3.1 s2).** "Tests are the only mechanism to project distant constraints into the agent's local context window" — the best one-sentence *mechanism* in the corpus for why executable verification dominates under bounded context (it converts non-local invariants into local observables). If a verification chapter lands, this is the Feynman-criterion gloss.
5. **$H(I \mid S) = 0$ (gemini-3.1 s2)** as the crisp form of C1, and **fable s0's half-life $\ln 2 / \ln(1/p)$** as the crisp form of C3's decay — both are formalization-ready statements for candidate segments.
6. **A counter-flow teaching (essays → TST direction reversed): the infinite-horizon degeneracy.** ~12/21 essays claim "unbounded ROI" for legibility investment — the $\hat n \to \infty$ limit in which T-06's threshold rule trivializes (any per-session saving beats any one-time cost). Only fable s2 states the finite threshold ("value must exceed comprehension cost × expected number of future sessions that touch it" — T-06's inequality, re-derived). TST is *stronger* here: T-04's baseline supplies the finite $\hat n$ that makes investment decisions non-trivial, and the essays' unbounded-ROI claims are overclaims for finite-lifetime systems. Worth one Discussion sentence in `#der-change-investment`: the memoryless-agent literature-of-one-shot-derivations systematically assumes $\hat n = \infty$, and T-04 is the discipline that corrects it.

## 8. (c) TST claims no independent derivation reproduced — and the diagnosis

Unreproduced at any recognizable level (0/21 unless noted): **T-01** (temporal optimality as deliberately-tautological axiom — every essay implicitly *assumes* total-cost minimization as the objective; none axiomatizes the choice of time as the residual metric); **T-03** (evolving-systems scope and the infinite-velocity framing of stable components — nothing close); **T-04** (the $n_{\text{future}} = n_{\text{past}}$ baseline with its Jeffreys/Pareto derivation — nothing close); **T-06's threshold arithmetic** (1/21 — fable s2; the concept is ubiquitous but degenerate, §7.6); **T-07** (conceptual/domain alignment — the essays' prior-alignment is alignment to $\pi$, not to the problem domain; no essay derives code-mirrors-domain); **T-08** ($\text{time} \propto \lvert\text{changeset}\rvert$); **T-09's distance hierarchy and H-09.1's exponential form** (qualitative direction only, §5 A4); **T-11** (coherence/coupling measured from git history); **T-12** (the integration objective); **T-13's availability machinery** (direction-adjacent only, §5 A7).

**The diagnosis, which I think is the honest headline for (c):** the unreproduced set is exactly the part of TST that does not follow from the turnover premise alone. The probe supplied one premise (100% turnover) and the essays regenerate, with near-unanimity, precisely the subtree that turnover forces: comprehension dominance (T-05), the spec-primacy corollary (C-02.1), the qualitative locality direction — plus the persistence/enforcement theory TST lacks (§6). What they never touch is the machinery that depends on TST's *other* premises: time-as-residual-metric (T-01), evolving-systems scope with a *finite estimated* future (T-03/T-04, which power T-06's arithmetic and T-12's integral), and the measurement program (T-08/T-09/T-11, which need the changeset/distance observables). Read as an ablation experiment, the probe empirically factors TST's premise-space: turnover ⇒ {T-05, C-02.1, locality-qualitative} + {C1..C13}; the rest require the temporal-optimality and evolving-systems axioms as genuinely independent inputs. That is mildly *reassuring* for TST's internal structure (its non-turnover theorems are not smuggled consequences of turnover), and simultaneously the sharpest available demonstration that TST-as-it-stands under-develops the turnover premise: 21 minds given only that premise spent most of their derivational budget on laws TST doesn't have.

## 9. Synthesis and released recommendations

**One-paragraph verdict.** TST and the independent derivations overlap on one region — comprehension-cost dominance and its consequences — where the corpus gives TST's T-05 essentially unanimous cross-vendor support, and spec-primacy (C-02.1) solid Gemini-side support. Outside that region the two carve different territory: TST holds a *time-accounting* theory (optimality axiom, finite-future estimation, changeset/proximity/coherence measurement, integration, operations) that no essay reproduced; the essays hold a *knowledge-persistence and enforcement* theory (artifact totality, geometric decay of the unenforced, the ratchet, prior-gravity, session transactionality) that TST has never stated. The two theories are complementary, joined at T-05, and the persistence theory is conspicuously AAT-shaped (decay vs. restoring force; monotone gates; exteriorized $M_t$; regression toward the prior).

**Released to the standing cycle** (candidate work, deliberately *not* executed in this spike — each needs its own derivation-or-adoption pass and honest tiering; per spikes.sop.md §0c this an honest discharge, and per the working-theory-belongs-in-canon discipline the *landing* should be segments, not this spike):

1. **A turnover/persistence chapter (or segment cluster) for 02-tst-core** carrying, at honest tiers: the artifact-channel law (C1; observation or postulate — $H(I \mid S) = 0$ form available), unenforced-invariant decay (C3; derivable from an i.i.d.-sessions + $\varepsilon \gt 0$ premise, and connectable to AAT's persistence condition as drift-vs-restoring-force), the verification ratchet (C4; submartingale form derivable), and session transactionality (C8). This is the largest gap the corpus exposes. The Anti-Hyrum inversion (§6.4) and the Holographic-Boundary gloss (§7.4) are the Discussion-grade framing devices.
2. **A prior-alignment hypothesis segment** (C5; $T(S) \approx D_{\mathrm{KL}}(S \parallel \pi)$, the prior as third knowledge carrier and active restoring force), cross-referenced to `#result-specification-bound`'s shared-context term — hypothesis tier, LLM-agent-specific scope.
3. **Prioritize the existing GAP** `#hyp-software-unmaintainability-bifurcation` — 20/21 independent attestations of the threshold shape (§5 A3) is the strongest external signal any single unlanded TST claim received.
4. **Small strengthenings when next touched:** per-session-sum form into `#der-dual-optimization` (§7.1); Naur citation (§7.2); the $\hat n = \infty$-degeneracy sentence into `#der-change-investment` (§7.6); the knowledge-vs-code duplication distinction into `#impl-code-structure` (§5 A5).
5. **T-07 flag for its owner:** conceptual alignment got zero independent support from this premise — consistent with its current hypothesis tier; its support must come from the comprehension-mechanics premises, not from turnover.

**What this spike does not claim.** No essay-derived law is *validated* by convergence — 21 correlated witnesses sharing training distributions and one prompt are a mild proxy for truth (spikes.sop.md §0), useful for locating and prioritizing questions, never for settling them. Every candidate above still requires its own derivation to enter canon above hypothesis/discussion tier.
