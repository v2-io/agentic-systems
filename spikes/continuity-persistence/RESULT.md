# Result: dynamic across-turnover continuity-persistence — the contraction–reinjection no-go

**Status:** *Resolved against `FRAMING.md`.* Completion state **B (no-go) on the critical path**, with a conditional-positive corollary (A, with inverted content) and a model-refinement that replaces `#disc-m-preservation`'s accumulation inequality (C). The framing's §9 central open question is answered **definitively in the negative**. Author: Agentic Systems instance, 2026-05-19. Reviewed: see `## Review` below.

**One-breath statement.** The inter-session regime is an **affine information recursion** — multiplicative SDPI contraction plus additive reinjection — *not* the sector-persistence rate regime. The self-compressing (no-reinjection) destroy-and-reconstruct walk decays **geometrically** to zero: there is no inter-session $\alpha\gt\rho/R$, and `#result-sector-persistence-template` provably does **not** transfer. Persistence is therefore **not a property the composite can possess intrinsically** — it is *wholly imported* through a non-vanishing reinjection channel, at level (reinjection)/(contraction-gap). The dichotomy is robust across the §3 commitments, which is itself the load-bearing finding.

## 1. The three (+1) modeling commitments — made and justified, not assumed

Per `FRAMING.md` §3 and §6.2 these are choices that must be *argued from structure*, not extracted from AAT.

**(C1) Accumulation operator = affine: multiplicative contraction + additive reinjection.** Justification, from `#obs-context-turnover`'s own exact content: the boundary map is information passing through a *lossy stochastic reconstruction kernel* $f_{\text{init}}(\mathcal E_k, p_{k+1}, M_0^{\text{w}})$. The strong data-processing inequality (SDPI; Ahlswede–Gács 1976; Polyanskiy–Wu, contraction-coefficient theory) states that mutual information through any non-degenerate noisy/rate-limited channel contracts *multiplicatively*: for the Markov chain $Y \to X_k \to M_{k+1}^+$,

$$I(M_{k+1}^+;\,Y)\;\le\;\eta_k\, I(X_k;\,Y),\qquad \eta_k\in[0,1],$$

with $\eta_k\lt1$ strictly whenever the boundary is genuinely lossy (budget below the $Y$-content, or noisy reconstruction — the *characteristic* `#obs-context-turnover` 100%-reset condition) and $\eta_k=1$ only in the non-characteristic lossless case. So the faithful operator is *multiplicative-contraction on the loss side*; the purely-additive $\epsilon^{(n)}=\sum_k\Delta\epsilon_k$ of `#disc-m-preservation` is the **small-loss linearization** ($\eta_k\approx 1-\delta_k$, additive in $\delta_k$), valid only near the lossless limit. This is `FRAMING.md` §3-(1)'s "multiplicative $\to$ different recurrence class" branch, argued from the channel structure, not chosen for convenience.

**(C2) State variable = $I_k := I(M_k^+;\,Y)$**, the un-normalized relevant mutual information about the arc-relevant target $Y$ (the continuant kernel / sufficiency target; finite at fidelity $\Delta$, $I_k\in[0,H_\Delta]$). Justification: SDPI acts natively on mutual information; the dichotomy is assumption-free in this coordinate. Maps to the other candidates (the §3-(2) non-monotone family, stated explicitly per the framing's instruction): $S_{\text{id}} = I_k / I(\mathcal C;Y)$ (the `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound` ratio — one *normalization* of $I_k$, not a distinct dynamical object); $S(M)$ is the predictive-sufficiency specialization ($Y\leftarrow$ future-observations instead of identity); $\epsilon_{\text{recon}}$ is *not* a global monotone transform of $I_k$ — which is precisely why $I_k$ is fixed as primary and the others read off it. The $S_{\min}$ floor enters as a **task-adequacy readout** ($I_k \ge S_{\min}\,I(\mathcal C;Y)$), *not* as a reflecting/absorbing barrier in the dynamics; the dynamics live on $[0,H_\Delta]$ and $S_{\min}$ is merely the line below which the composite is operationally dead.

**(C3) Compensation $\rho_k := I(X_k;\,Y\mid M_k^+)\ge0$** — the fresh $Y$-information in the end-of-session-$k$ content $X_k$ *conditional on* the reconstructed start-of-session state $M_k^+$ (exactly the part not derivable from the compressed state) — the un-bottlenecked reinjection channel. The conditional definition is what makes the §2 bound an exact chain-rule identity rather than a (false) sub-additivity claim. For practica this is concretely the durable-artifact + curated-narrative + relational-re-attestation layer; structurally it is the §03.II *scaffold*. Deliberately left as a *general non-negative sequence*: the result is proven **robust across its sub-models** (exogenous-stationary / channel-bounded / state-correlated), so C3 need not be over-resolved — that robustness is finding **R4**.

**(C4, the framing's footnote promoted) Driving / target evolution.** **Uniform** contraction $\sup_k\eta_k\le\bar\eta\lt1$. Honest scope: `#obs-context-turnover`'s 100%-reset gives *each-boundary* lossiness ($\eta_k\lt1$ pointwise); *uniform* boundedness-away-from-1 is the **additional stated commitment** — a fidelity-ever-improving externalization schedule with $\eta_k\to1$ satisfies pointwise lossiness yet evades the uniform bar (the no-go-evasion regime, named §4-(4)). $a_k:=\eta_k\rho_k$ a general non-negative sequence; the moving target $I(\mathcal C;Y)$ of `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound` enters only through the $\rho_k$ / normalization and does not affect the dichotomy (R4). This confirms the framing's §9 "inferred, not verified" point and resolves my own proposed §3.4: the driving-process commitment is **separable but subordinate** — it sets level and regularity, never the qualitative outcome.

## 2. Formal Expression

With (C1)–(C4), write $I_k:=I(M_k^+;Y)$ and $\rho_k:=I(X_k;Y\mid M_k^+)$ (C3). The mutual-information **chain rule** gives $I\big((X_k,M_k^+);Y\big)=I(M_k^+;Y)+I(X_k;Y\mid M_k^+)=I_k+\rho_k$, and adjoining a variable cannot decrease MI, so $I(X_k;Y)\le I\big((X_k,M_k^+);Y\big)=I_k+\rho_k$ — **exact, via the chain rule; not sub-additivity** (MI is *not* sub-additive — synergy/XOR is the standard counterexample). Hence the boundary recursion is the **affine information recursion**

$$\boxed{\,I_{k+1}\;\le\;\eta_k\,(I_k+\rho_k)\;=\;\eta_k I_k + a_k\,,\qquad a_k:=\eta_k\rho_k\,,\qquad\text{and}\qquad I_{k+1}\le B_k\ \text{(budget cap)}.}$$

**R1 — the no-go (exact under (C1)+(C4); critical path).** Isolated walk, $a_k\equiv0$ (no reinjection):

$$I_n\;\le\;\Big(\textstyle\prod_{j=1}^n\eta_j\Big)\,I_0\;\le\;\bar\eta^{\,n}\,I_0\;\xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{}\;0\quad\text{geometrically.}$$

There is **no inter-session persistence condition**: persistence is impossible by construction for the self-compressing destroy-and-reconstruct walk; the relevant-information half-life is $\approx 1/\log_2(1/\bar\eta)$ boundaries. **`#result-sector-persistence-template` does not transfer.** The template requires a *bounded perturbation* $\rho$ rejected by *correction* $\alpha$ within *capacity* $R$, persistence $=\alpha\gt\rho/R$. Here there is no perturbation being rejected and no internal restoring term — only multiplicative information decay. No $(\alpha,\rho,R)$ instantiation exists because the template's structural ingredients have **no counterpart** in an unforced contraction. This is the definite, negative answer to `FRAMING.md` §9's central open question.

**R2 — conditional positive (corollary; the content is the inversion).** With reinjection,

$$I_n\;\le\;\bar\eta^{\,n} I_0\;+\;\sum_{j=1}^{n}\bar\eta^{\,n-j}\,a_j\,,\qquad\text{hence}\qquad \limsup_n I_n\;\le\;\frac{\bar a}{1-\bar\eta}\quad(\bar a:=\limsup_k a_k),$$

and $\liminf_n I_n\gt0$ **iff** $\liminf_k a_k\gt0$. Persistence holds *iff the reinjection channel is non-vanishing*, at level $\bar a/(1-\bar\eta)$. The content, stated plainly because it is the point: persistence is **not intrinsically possessable** — it is wholly *imported* through the reinjection channel; the achievable level is *forcing / contraction-gap*, not any internal correction outpacing any internal drift. The "persistence condition" is **structural/architectural** (a non-vanishing reinjection channel exists), categorically not a rate inequality. This is `FRAMING.md` §6-hazard-1 honored: a positive statement appears, but only by exhibiting *why* the destroy-reconstruct structure admits it (an exogenous forcing term), not by assuming the rate regime transfers.

**R3 — model refinement (replaces `#disc-m-preservation`'s inequality; completion C, executed).** The discussion-grade $\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon_k]\le\mathbb E[\Delta I_k]$ is not merely unproven — it is the **wrong form**: it presumes additive loss with a break-even threshold. Corrected exact statement: persistence $\iff \liminf_k a_k\gt0$, level $\bar a/(1-\bar\eta)$; *break-even is not the threshold*, because under multiplicative contraction any $\bar\eta\lt1$ with vanishing reinjection decays to zero however the additive bookkeeping balances. Per *integration-is-replacement*: the inequality is **deleted and replaced** by R1+R2; the history ("was discussion-grade; presumed the wrong operator") lives only in the changelog / Working-Notes layer, never in the segment body.

**R4 — the meta-finding (robustness; robust-qualitative).** The R1/R2 dichotomy is **invariant** across (C3)'s $\rho_k$ sub-model (exogenous-stationary / channel-bounded / state-correlated) and (C4)'s stationary-vs-non-stationary driving. Those change the *level* $\bar a/(1-\bar\eta)$ and the *regularity* (a.s. vs in-expectation; $\limsup$ vs convergence) — never the qualitative outcome. Therefore the **architectural question** (does a non-vanishing reinjection channel exist) *dominates every modeling-detail question*. This is the result most directly load-bearing for practica.

## 4. Honest open edges (not papered over)

1. **The SDPI coefficient $\eta_k$ for the concrete $f_{\text{init}}$ kernel is not computed** — only $\bar\eta\lt1$ (genuine lossiness) is argued. Its *value* (hence the half-life and the level $\bar a/(1-\bar\eta)$) is architecture/matched-channel-dependent; same status and same open-ness as `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound`'s tightness caveat. Computing $\eta_k$ for a specific reconstruction architecture (e.g. practica's durable layer) is a clean follow-on.
2. **R4's full generality is argued, not proven, over adversarially-correlated reinjection.** If $\rho_k$ is *state-correlated so as to vanish exactly when $I_k$ is low* (re-grounding fails precisely when there is little left to re-ground — the dynamically-reached unconditional-witness regime), then $\bar a\gt0$ marginally is *not* sufficient and R1 can reassert. This is a candidate **second no-go** (an absorbing-barrier refinement of R2) and is the single most valuable follow-on. Flagged first-class, not hidden.
3. **The budget-cap branch** ($I_{k+1}\le B_k$) yields the bottleneck-monotonicity statement of the superseded single-branch exploration as a *degenerate special case* of R1/R2; it is subsumed, not separately load-bearing.
4. **Pointwise vs uniform contraction (the evasion regime).** R1's geometric decay needs *uniform* $\sup_k\eta_k\le\bar\eta\lt1$; per-boundary lossiness alone ($\eta_k\lt1$ each $k$ but $\eta_k\to1$) does **not** give it. A reconstruction schedule whose fidelity improves without bound across turnovers — contraction weakening toward losslessness — evades R1. Whether any realizable externalization architecture can sustain $\eta_k\to1$ against the 100%-reset (which plausibly forces a uniform bar) is the honest scope question; the result is about *uniformly* lossy turnover, and the landed segment's Epistemic Status must say so.
