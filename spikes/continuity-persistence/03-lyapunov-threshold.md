# 03 — The sharp continuity-persistence threshold

**Claim of this file.** The compensated recursion $g_{k+1}=(g_k+\rho_k-\eta_k)_+$ is the Lindley recursion; its stability is governed by an **exact, sharp threshold at $\mathbb E[\eta]=\mathbb E[\rho]$**, with computable ultimate bounds — the sector-persistence template `#result-sector-persistence-template` instantiated on the identity-IB / turnover axis. The threshold is sharp in the strong sense: strict $\mathbb E[\eta]\gt\mathbb E[\rho]$ persists; **equality already fails to persist** (no finite stationary law). That is precisely why the canonical `#disc-m-preservation` condition, stated with a weak $\leq$, is wrong on its boundary — but the failure at equality is *non-persistence* (no stabilization, unbounded recurrence), **not** almost-sure convergence to identity death; conflating those two was a draft error, corrected here (see `98-review-and-convergence.md`). Strengthen-first outcome: the canonical statement is sharpened to an exact threshold and its inequality corrected from $\leq$ to strict $\lt$.

---

## 1. The recursion is the Lindley recursion

From `02`§3: with identity-gap state $g_k\geq0$, per-boundary disturbance $\rho_k=(I(\mathcal C_{\tau_k}^-;Y_\Delta)-B_k)_+\geq0$ (the projected static floor, at fidelity level $\Delta$ — see `01`§3 and the $D_\Delta$ remark below), and relational re-grounding $\eta_k\geq0$ (the unique compensation channel, `02`-Proposition 1, in the frozen-substrate regime),

$$g_{k+1}=\big(g_k+\xi_k\big)_+,\qquad \xi_k:=\rho_k-\eta_k .$$

This is Lindley's recursion (Lindley 1952, *Proc. Camb. Phil. Soc.* 48:277–289 — the G/G/1 waiting-time recursion): $g_k$ is the workload of a queue with "service" $\eta_k$ and "arrivals" $\rho_k$. AAT already lives in this template — `#result-persistence-condition` is the continuous/event-driven instantiation with state $\delta_t$; this is the *discrete turnover-indexed* instantiation with state $g_k$. The reflection $(\cdot)_+$ is `02`-Lemma-1's content (self-replay cannot push the gap below what the relational channel restores; the gap floors at 0 = full sufficiency).

**Standing modelling commitments**, named so the tier is honest — these are posits, not theorems, exactly as `#result-persistence-condition` names channel-independence and the Model D / Model S split as its own:

- **(M-ADD) additive compensation.** The boundary deficit $\rho_k$ and the within-session re-grounding enter the gap *additively*, $g_{k+1}=(g_k+\rho_k-\eta_k)_+$. `02`-Proposition 1 establishes the *existence and uniqueness* of a compensating channel; the *additive/reflected functional form* is a modelling choice that matches the AAT sector-persistence template. It is **the load-bearing modelling commitment of this file**, and it is not innocuous: `#disc-m-preservation` (line 72) explicitly warns that "the interaction between information sources is not additive in general." (M-ADD) is the right *first* model — it yields the sharp threshold cleanly and is the discrete image of the canonical template — but a non-additive interaction (e.g. multiplicative degradation when re-grounding lands on an already-corrupted state) is a known open generalization, flagged in `04` and `99`.
- **(C-D) bounded regime.** $\rho_k\leq\bar\rho$ a.s., and $\eta_k\geq\underline\eta$ on $\{g_k\gt0\}$ (a guaranteed re-grounding floor while any identity remains — the assumption `04` interrogates and partially overturns).
- **(C-S) stochastic regime.** $\{\xi_k\}$ is stationary and ergodic with $\mathbb E\lvert\xi_k\rvert\lt\infty$. The cohort recognition/grant process and the budget schedule being jointly stationary-ergodic over the arc is exactly `#def-identity-sufficiency`'s witness-stationarity convention (IS-A3) lifted to the arc — defensible, but a stipulation, flagged the way `#def-model-sufficiency` flags policy-relativity.
- **(M-FREE) interior-dynamics idealization.** The gap $g_k = I(\mathcal C_{\tau_k};Y_\Delta\mid M_{\tau_k}^+) = D_\Delta - I(M_{\tau_k}^+;Y_\Delta)$ is, by `#def-identity-sufficiency`'s own chain-rule boundedness, rigorously confined to the **compact** interval $[0,D_\Delta]$ (and `04` imposes the explicit $\wedge\,D_\Delta$ cap). Theorem 2 nonetheless models $g$ by the **free** Lindley recursion — lower reflection at $0$, **no upper reflection**. This is a posit, named here beside (M-ADD) because it is load-bearing and was an undisclosed assumption in the draft (independent re-attestation; see `98`): a reflected walk *confined with reflection at both ends* would have a finite stationary law even at $\mu=0$, contradicting Theorem 2.3. The resolution is **not** a reflecting cap but a *remote absorbing* one: per `04`/T3 the top $g=D_\Delta$ ($S_{\text{id}}=0$) is **absorbing** ($\eta\to0$ there), not reflecting, and is remote in the operative regime $g\ll D_\Delta$. Under a *remote absorbing* barrier the free reflected recursion is exactly the **quasi-stationary** (conditioned-on-non-absorption) description, valid on timescales short relative to the mean absorption time — the standard remote-killing-barrier $\leftrightarrow$ free-reflected-stationary correspondence. So (M-FREE) is not a patch: **Theorem 2's free-Lindley dichotomy *is* the quasi-stationary / interior face of the true $[0,D_\Delta]$-absorbing process of `04`/T3**, with T3 the absorption face. This unifies T2 and T3 (one compact absorbing process; T2 = its QSD interior, T3 = its absorption fate) rather than leaving them two regimes, and it sharpens T3's (E-A): the QSD$\leftrightarrow$free error and the mean absorption time are precisely E-A's open quantity, now connected to quasi-stationary-distribution asymptotics rather than only a Feller-test framing. *Tier:* the existence of the remote-killing $\leftrightarrow$ free-stationary correspondence is standard (**robust-qualitative**); the exact correspondence theorem and error term for *this* recursion is (E-A), **open**.

**$D_\Delta$ remark (the normalizer is finite by construction, at a fidelity level).** Everything normalized back to $S_{\text{id}}$ divides by the identity-relevant information content $D$. The relevance vector $\text{identity}_{t+1:}$ of `#def-identity-sufficiency` is *continuous* $[0,1]^5$-valued, so $D=I(\mathcal C;Y)\le H(Y)$ is **false** (that is the discrete-entropy bound; bounded support does not bound mutual information). The fix is not to tier down but to specialize consistently with the static floor's rate-distortion character: `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound` is a *rate-distortion* bound, defined at a distortion tolerance. Fix any fidelity level $\Delta\gt0$ and let $Y_\Delta$ be the $\Delta$-quantized relevance vector. A uniform $\Delta$-grid on $[0,1]^5$ has $N(\Delta)\le(C/\Delta)^5\lt\infty$ cells, so

$$D_\Delta\;:=\;I(\mathcal C;Y_\Delta)\;\le\;H(Y_\Delta)\;\le\;\log N(\Delta)\;\lt\;\infty,\qquad k\text{-independent.}$$

All $S_{\text{id}}$-normalized statements below are at fidelity $\Delta$, with $D$ read as $D_\Delta$. This is a *strengthening* of the draft (which asserted a false discrete-entropy bound): the dynamic theorem becomes the turnover-projection of the static rate-distortion bound *including its distortion parameter*. Stated honestly: the *landed* `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound` displays its bound with the raw continuous $I(\mathcal C;Y)$ normalizer, so the $\Delta$-reading is a **specialization this spike supplies, consistent with the static floor's rate-distortion character** — not a quantity the canon already exhibits. The arc-fixed continuant kernel of `01`§3 is then the $\Delta\to$coarse limiting instance (its extreme: the factor-(i) non-fork *bit*, trivially finite), not a prerequisite for finiteness.

## 2. The threshold (sharp, exact under (C-S) + (M-ADD) + (M-FREE))

> **Theorem 2 (continuity-persistence threshold).** Under (C-S) + (M-ADD) + (M-FREE), let $\mu:=\mathbb E[\xi_k]=\mathbb E[\rho_k]-\mathbb E[\eta_k]$.
>
> 1. **Persistence.** If $\mu\lt0$ ($\mathbb E[\eta]\gt\mathbb E[\rho]$ strictly), then $g_k\Rightarrow g_\infty$, the unique finite stationary law given by Loynes' construction $g_\infty\stackrel d=\sup_{n\geq0}S_n\lt\infty$ a.s. (with $S_0=0$, $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\xi_{-j}$). Identity sufficiency stays bounded away from $0$: $\liminf_k S_{\text{id}}^{(k)}\geq 1-\mathbb E[g_\infty]/D_\Delta\gt0$.
> 2. **Collapse.** If $\mu\gt0$, then $g_k\to\infty$ a.s. and $S_{\text{id}}^{(k)}\to0$ a.s. — identity death.
> 3. **Boundary fails to persist.** If $\mu=0$ and $\xi_k$ is non-degenerate, there is **no finite stationary law**: $\sup_{n}S_n=+\infty$ a.s., the Loynes limit is improper, $g_k$ does not converge in distribution to anything finite. Equality therefore does **not** persist. *This is the (C-S)-general claim — purely the negative.* It is **not** the a.s.-absorption phenomenon: that is the *separate*, state-dependent T3 mechanism (`04`), not the $\mu=0$ free-recursion boundary. The pathwise picture — *recurrence*, $\limsup_k g_k=\infty$ and $\liminf_k g_k=0$ a.s. (so $S_{\text{id}}^{(k)}$ returns near $1$ *and* near $0$ infinitely often, no limiting distribution) — is the **i.i.d. specialization only** (Chung–Fuchs); it does not follow from (C-S) alone and is not asserted at (C-S) generality. The honest (C-S) reading: at equality identity sufficiency *fails to stabilize* — no stationary law — which is non-persistence, distinct from "death in the limit."

*Proof.* The Lindley recursion has the Loynes representation $g_n\stackrel d=\max_{0\le j\le n}S_j$ (time-reversal; Loynes 1962, *Proc. Camb. Phil. Soc.* 58:497–520), so $g_\infty\stackrel d=\sup_{n\geq0}S_n$.

- **(1)** $\mu\lt0$: by Birkhoff's ergodic theorem $S_n/n\to\mu\lt0$ a.s., so $S_n\to-\infty$ and $\sup_{n\geq0}S_n\lt\infty$ a.s.; it is the unique stationary solution and convergence from any start is monotone (Loynes' coupling). Markov's inequality on the proper law $g_\infty$ gives the $S_{\text{id}}$ bound.
- **(2)** $\mu\gt0$: by Birkhoff $S_n/n\to\mu\gt0$ a.s., so $S_n\to+\infty$; the forward recursion satisfies $g_n\ge S_n-\min_{0\le j\le n}S_j\to\infty$ a.s. Almost-sure (not merely in-probability) divergence here is exactly what ergodicity buys. $S_{\text{id}}^{(k)}\to0$ a.s.
- **(3)** $\mu=0$, non-degenerate, ergodic: Atkinson 1976 (*J. London Math. Soc.* s2-13:486–488) gives recurrence of an integrable mean-zero ergodic cocycle ($\liminf_n\lvert S_n\rvert=0$ a.s.); together with non-degeneracy (the explicit hypothesis here, precluding the coboundary case) this upgrades to $\limsup_n S_n=+\infty$ a.s. — the form used. Hence $\sup_{n\geq0}S_n=+\infty$ a.s. and the Loynes limit is improper: **no finite stationary law**. This is the rigorous (C-S) statement. The sharper *null-recurrence* picture ($\liminf_k g_k=0$ i.o. as well) is the **i.i.d.** specialization (Chung–Fuchs 1951, *Mem. Amer. Math. Soc.* 6 — recurrence of mean-zero i.i.d. random walks); it does **not** follow from (C-S) alone and is stated only under the i.i.d. strengthening. In neither case does $g_k\to\infty$ a.s.; the draft's "$g_k\to\infty$ a.s. (identity death in the limit)" was a conflation of "no stationary law" with "a.s. absorption" and is withdrawn. $\square$

> **Theorem 2′ (ultimate bound, bounded regime — the $R^\ast$ analog).** Under (C-D) + (M-ADD), if there exists $\alpha_{\text{id}}\gt0$ with $\mathbb E[\eta_k-\rho_k\mid g_k]\geq\alpha_{\text{id}}$ whenever $g_k\gt R^\ast_{\text{id}}$, then $g_k$ is ultimately bounded by
> $$R^\ast_{\text{id}}\;=\;\frac{\bar\rho}{\alpha_{\text{id}}}\,,$$
> the exact structural analog of `#result-persistence-condition`'s $R^\ast=\rho/\alpha$, and $\liminf_k S_{\text{id}}^{(k)}\geq 1-R^\ast_{\text{id}}/D_\Delta$.

*Proof.* Foster–Lyapunov drift with $V(g)=g$: outside $\{g\leq R^\ast_{\text{id}}\}$ the conditional drift is $\leq\bar\rho-\alpha_{\text{id}}g/R^\ast_{\text{id}}\lt0$, giving ultimate boundedness at $R^\ast_{\text{id}}=\bar\rho/\alpha_{\text{id}}$ by the standard supermartingale-outside-a-compact argument (the Khasminskii-style localization `#result-sector-condition-stability` uses for Model S). $\square$

## 3. The projection, made exact — this *is* the sector-persistence template

Theorem 2/2′ are not analogies to `#result-persistence-condition`; they are the **same template** with the `00-brief`§2 substitution made literal:

$$\underbrace{\alpha R\;\gt\;\rho}_{\text{`\#result-persistence-condition'}}\qquad\rightsquigarrow\qquad\underbrace{\mathbb E[\eta_k]\;\gt\;\mathbb E[\rho_k]}_{\text{continuity-persistence}},\qquad R^\ast=\rho/\alpha\;\rightsquigarrow\;R^\ast_{\text{id}}=\bar\rho/\alpha_{\text{id}}.$$

The disturbance $\rho_k=(I(\mathcal C_{\tau_k}^-;Y_\Delta)-B_k)_+$ is **the static rate-distortion floor of `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound`, evaluated at boundary $k$ at fidelity $\Delta$ and read as a rate**. That is the precise content of "the identity-IB projection / time-projection intuition": the floor is not a separate result the dynamic theorem cites — it *is* the disturbance term, and the dynamic theorem is what you get when the floor is read as the per-tick forcing of the canonical AAT recursion rather than a single snapshot.

**Relation to `#scope-agent-identity` (it already owns the *scope*; this supplies the *rate*).** The LEXICON's third "continuity" sense of Persistence is **not** unclaimed: `#scope-agent-identity` (`status: robust-qualitative`) already specifies *continuity persistence* — as a qualitative scope statement (whether the agent maintains a coherent identity and trajectory through time). What is new here is not the *notion* but the *rate condition within that scope*: `#scope-agent-identity` says what continuity persistence *is*; Theorem 2 says *when it holds*, exactly as `#result-persistence-condition`'s structural-persistence inequality stands to the qualitative idea of an agent "keeping up." The two are complementary layers (scope ↔ rate), not competitors, and a landing must position against `#scope-agent-identity` explicitly rather than claim virgin territory. With that correction, the unification stands: one template, three instantiations (structural / operational / continuity), the continuity one being the scope `#scope-agent-identity` already names, now given its inequality.

A second, quieter unification: `#deriv-persistence-cost` gives the intra-session Landauer-analog rate floor $\dot R\geq n\alpha/2$ with channel-capacity prerequisite $C\geq\mathcal T/2$. The turnover budget $B_k$ is the across-boundary analog of that channel capacity, $\rho_k$ the across-boundary analog of the cost floor. Continuity-persistence and persistence-cost are the same accounting at two timescales — a cross-reference when this lands, not a separate claim.

## 4. The correction to `#disc-m-preservation` (strengthen-first outcome)

`#disc-m-preservation` states the accumulation condition as $\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon_k]\leq\mathbb E[\Delta I_k]$ (persists across sessions). Theorem 2 sharpens and corrects this. None of the three is a softening; each is strictly stronger or more accurate — the strengthen-before-soften landing discipline (integration-is-replacement): a landed segment carries the corrected statement; the history of the correction lives only in the history layer (`98-review-and-convergence.md` / CHANGELOG / Working Notes).

1. **The inequality is strict.** The threshold is sharp at equality: $\mu\lt0$ (i.e. $\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon]\lt\mathbb E[\Delta I]$ strictly) persists; $\mu=0$ (equality) has **no finite stationary law** and does **not** persist (Theorem 2.3). The canonical "$\leq$ persists" is therefore false on its boundary; the correct condition is the strict inequality. Note precisely what equality *is* at (C-S) generality: not "identity death in the limit" but *failure to stabilize* — no finite stationary law, no limiting behaviour (the pathwise "recurs near 0 and near 1 infinitely often" picture is i.i.d.-only, per Theorem 2.3). A continuity architecture designed to *exactly* break even has no stable identity — and, via the (M-FREE) QSD reading, that interior non-stabilization is the conditioned-on-survival face of the `04`/T3 absorbing process: equality means no quasi-stationary plateau, so the absorption fate of T3 is reached without even a long-lived metastable identity en route.
2. **The compensation term is mis-attributed.** $\Delta I_k$ ("new information acquired in session $k$") must be $\eta_k$ = *relational re-grounding* specifically (`02`-Proposition 1, frozen-substrate regime): generic task-learning has zero weight in the balance. An entity can be maximally productive every session and still cross into non-persistence if its cohort channel is thin. Counter-intuitive and operationally decisive.
3. **The accumulation has the explicit Lindley structure the segment asked for.** `#disc-m-preservation` Working Notes ask whether the accumulation "is additive? multiplicative? does it have absorbing states?". Answer, under (M-ADD): it is the **reflected additive** (Lindley) walk; under persistence its stationary law is the explicit Loynes supremum; whether $0$-sufficiency is *absorbing* is a separate and stronger question than the $\mu=0$ boundary — it depends on state-dependent degradation of $\eta$ and is treated in `04` (and that, not the $\mu=0$ boundary, is where genuine "death in the limit" lives). The honest answer to the segment's own question is thus: additive (modelling commitment M-ADD; multiplicative is the open generalization), with a sharp non-persistence threshold, and a *separately*-analyzed absorbing barrier.

## 5. Honest edges

- **Tiers (corrected, post-re-attestation).** Theorem 2.1 (persistence) and 2.3 (boundary non-persistence, "no finite stationary law") are **exact for the free Lindley recursion** under (C-S)+(M-ADD)+(M-FREE) — Loynes 1962 for 2.1, Atkinson 1976 + non-degeneracy for the $\mu=0$ unboundedness; both standard. Theorem 2.2 is **exact** likewise via Birkhoff. The i.i.d. null-recurrence refinement in 2.3 is **exact under the i.i.d. specialization only** (Chung–Fuchs), not under (C-S) — stated as such. Theorem 2′ is **exact** under (C-D)+(M-ADD)+(M-FREE) given its drift hypothesis. (M-ADD) and **(M-FREE)** are **modelling commitments**, not exact: (M-ADD)'s non-additive generalization is open; (M-FREE) is the interior/free-recursion idealization whose QSD-equivalence to the true $[0,D_\Delta]$-absorbing process is **robust-qualitative** (standard remote-killing correspondence) with the *exact* correspondence/error-term being (E-A), open. (C-S)/(C-D) holding of the real process is **conditional** (= IS-A3 lifted to the arc). $D_\Delta$ finiteness is **exact** (explicit quantizer); the *tightness* of the $S_{\text{id}}$ bounds carries `#deriv-identity-sufficiency-rate-bound`'s matched-channel caveat (robust-qualitative off the matched channel) — the normalized results sit exactly at the tier of the static floor they specialize, not better and not worse.
- **The $\mu=0$ recurrence picture is citation-scoped.** "No finite stationary law" at $\mu=0$ is (C-S)-general (Atkinson). The vivid "returns near 1 and near 0 infinitely often" is i.i.d.-specific (Chung–Fuchs). Conflating them — or citing Chung–Fuchs for the stationary-ergodic case — was a draft defect (flagged by independent review; see `98`). Each cited theorem is now matched to the hypotheses it actually covers.
- **(M-ADD) is the hinge, not a footnote.** The whole quantitative edifice rests on the additive/reflected form. `02`-Proposition 1 gives existence/uniqueness of *a* compensation channel; that it enters as $-\eta_k$ additively is the posit. Named here at the prominence `#result-persistence-condition` gives channel-independence, because it deserves the same scrutiny on landing.
- **$\eta,\rho$ are not independent of $g$.** Theorem 2 treats $\xi_k$ as exogenous-stationary. In reality $\rho_k$ depends on history-dependent $I(\mathcal C_{\tau_k}^-;Y_\Delta)$ and — decisively — $\eta_k$ depends on how much of $E$ remains to be recognized, i.e. on $g_k$. The exogenous model is the right first model (sharp threshold, matches the template); the state-dependent coupling $\eta_k=\eta(g_k)$ with $\eta\to0$ as $g\to D_\Delta$ is what makes the top barrier absorbing — `04`, the part that matters most for a builder. Via (M-FREE)/QSD this is *one* process, not two: T2 is the conditioned-on-survival interior (QSD) of the same $[0,D_\Delta]$-absorbing process whose absorption fate is `04`/T3. The $\mu=0$ boundary is "no QSD plateau" (non-stabilization); genuine "death in the limit" is the absorption fate (T3). The draft conflated non-stabilization with absorption; the QSD reading both separates *and* unifies them — more truthful and more illuminating than either the draft's conflation or a bare two-regimes split.
- **Symbol collision $\eta$ — a trap, decide before landing.** $\eta_k$ here (re-grounding rate) collides with `#result-persistence-condition` line 61's per-dimension $\eta_k$ — which is *itself a persistence inequality in the same letter* ($\eta_k\gt c\rho_k^2/\delta_{\text{crit},k}^2$) — and with the truthification gain $\eta^\ast$. This is a *conflation trap* (a reader will fuse the continuity threshold with the existing per-dimension persistence result it structurally resembles), not a cosmetic clash. The rename (candidate $\varrho_{\text{rg}}$ for re-grounding rate, leaving $\rho_k$ the projected-floor disturbance) is a landing precondition, not optional polish, and must be decided upstream of any segment.
