## 1. The question, restated exactly (so success vs. no-go is unambiguous)

The two prior spikes localized the open problem to a single object. Stating it
with full precision is half the work, because the no-go turns on a distinction
the loose statement hides.

**What §2.2/§2.3 of `spike-self-actuation-grounding.md` need.** The tower
recursion is: a non-degenerate self-actuation operator $\mathfrak{A}$
presupposes an invariant $\Phi$ (WF, §2.1); $\Phi$ is claimed to *be* a value
functional on objective-sequences, one level up, so the self-actuator at level
0 *is* an actuated agent whose objective is $\Phi$ at level 1; WF re-applies at
level 1; the tower must be well-founded or $\mathfrak{A}$ is degenerate
(Result G). For the recursion to *bite* — for "$\Phi$ is an objective one level
up" to be a fact and not a type pun — $\Phi$ must satisfy **all** of:

- **(R1) Value-functional type.** $\Phi:\ \{\text{objective-sequences along }\mathcal{C}_t\}\to\mathbb{R}$, with the `#form-objective-functional` type signature (scalar codomain, totally ordered — `#form-objective-functional` Epistemic Status §1).
- **(R2) Preserved/monotone across revision.** Along the realized $\mathcal{C}_t$, $\Phi$ (or its effective domain) is held fixed or monotone under each admissible $O_t\mapsto O_t'$ — *not* vacuously (not a constant indicator; the WF-strengthening witness's correct objection to $\chi$).
- **(R3) Agent-internal and self-actuatable.** $\Phi$ must be the *kind of thing the level-1 agent revises*, i.e. an object $\mathfrak{A}^{(1)}$ operates on, so that WF re-applies *to $\Phi$ itself* with the *same argument*. This is the load-bearing requirement the loose statement hides: the recursion needs $\Phi$ to be a member of the same category as $O_t$ (an AAT objective the agent can self-actuate on), or "WF re-applies at level 1" is false by type mismatch.
- **(R4) Convention-and-trajectory stable.** "Preserved/monotone across revision" must be a fact about *the agent on its singular trajectory* (`#scope-agent-identity`), not an artifact of the analyst's measurement convention. If whether $\Phi$ is monotone depends on whether the analyst computes under C1, C2, or C3, then $\Phi$ is not an invariant of the agent — it is an invariant of a measurement choice, and the recursion (which is about the agent's dynamics, not the analyst's bookkeeping) does not run.

**Success** = exhibit, or prove the existence of, a $\Phi$ satisfying
(R1)–(R4) for every non-degenerate self-actuator in AAT's covered scope
(`#def-agent-spectrum` actuated-agent quadrant, restricted to the architecture
classes Section II covers exactly — Class 1 Separated, with Class 2 Partial as
approximation; `#der-directed-separation`).

**No-go** = prove no such $\Phi$ exists, *constructed from AAT's covered
objective-side machinery*, and state exactly the class of agents for which the
absence is provable and exactly what kind of object *can* play the grounding
role instead.

The two prior attempts each silently dropped a requirement: §2.1 dropped (R2)
(constant indicator passes (R1)(R3)(R4) but fails (R2)); the WF-strengthening
attempt dropped (R4) (its (M) is monotone *only under a convention-stable exact
`Lic`* it could not get) and also (R3) (its $\Phi$ — the
admissible-continuation correspondence — is a Lyapunov-sublevel certificate on
the *dynamics*, not an AAT objective the agent self-actuates on; its own OQ4
concedes the level-1 re-application is argued, not derived). The no-go below is
exactly: **(R2)+(R3)+(R4) cannot be jointly satisfied by any objective-side
construction**, and the proof is AAT's own convention-monotonicity theorem.

## 2. The success direction, attempted at full strength

Per strengthen-before-soften this is attempted before any no-go is asserted.
There are exactly three places an agent-internal $\Phi$ could come from. Each
is pushed until it provably breaks; the *pattern* of the three breaks is the
no-go.

### 2.1 Candidate A — $\Phi$ = the admissible set's structure (the §2.1 line, re-pushed)

Take $\Phi$ to encode the proper-subset structure of
$\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adm}}=\mathrm{range}(\mathfrak{A})$. **Strengthening
attempt:** can the bare set be enriched to a monotone object without importing
the revision dynamics? Try a *measure-theoretic* enrichment: let
$\Phi(\sigma)=$ "the $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adm}}$-volume not yet excluded along
$\sigma$." For this to be monotone we need each revision to *exclude* objectives
and never re-admit. But set membership alone carries no exclusion dynamics —
$\mathfrak{A}$ is a map *into* $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adm}}$, not a map that
shrinks it. Re-admission ($O\to O'\to O$) is consistent with a fixed
$\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{adm}}$. **Break:** the static set has no monotone
enrichment that is not *imported from the revision dynamics*. This is the
WF-strengthening spike's own correct §2 diagnosis (static set-algebra cannot
yield a dynamical potential). Candidate A fails (R2) irreparably from inside
set algebra. *The strengthening must come from the dynamics — Candidate B.*

### 2.2 Candidate B — $\Phi$ = the cascade-licensing potential (the WF-strengthening line, re-pushed at full strength)

This is the strongest candidate and gets the hardest push, because it is where
strengthen-before-soften most demands the attempt actually be made rather than
the no-go declared.

The WF-strengthening construction: define the admissible-continuation
correspondence $\mathcal{K}_t$ and let $\Phi$ be the selection functional whose
effective domain is $\mathcal{K}_t$; show $\mathcal{K}_t$ is monotone
non-increasing under trajectory extension (M), because every non-degenerate
revision is cascade-licensed (a commitment that deletes continuations and adds
none — branch (i)), the only alternative being the gap-minimizing selector
(branch (ii), excluded by non-degeneracy). The monotone contraction of
$\mathcal{K}_t$ is then the (R2) structure.

**The whole construction rests on one object: $\mathrm{Lic}(O,M_\tau)\in\{0,1\}$
must be an exact, total, convention-stable feasibility verdict** ("$O$ is
*confirmed infeasible* at $M_\tau$"), because branch (i) is *defined* by
$\mathrm{Lic}=1$ and the dichotomy's exhaustiveness is "$\mathrm{Lic}$ is
two-valued." Push this as hard as possible — can AAT's machinery be made to
deliver such a $\mathrm{Lic}$?

Walk the cascade with full strength. Step 5d fires when $\delta_{\text{sat}}\gt 0$
persists across 5a ($M_t$-correction), 5b ($\Pi$-expansion incl. L1), 5c
(convention escalation). The candidate $\mathrm{Lic}(O,M_\tau)=1$ wants to
mean: *no model correction, no policy enrichment, no convention escalation
closes the gap — $O$ is genuinely infeasible.* For $\mathrm{Lic}$ to be the
needed exact total predicate, "$\delta_{\text{sat}}(O)\gt 0$ persists across all of
these" must be an **exact two-valued fact about $(O,M_\tau)$**.

It is not, and AAT says exactly why, as a theorem. From `#def-value-object`
(Monotonicity, `status: exact`) and `#def-satisfaction-gap` (Epistemic Status,
`status: exact`):

$$\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{B}}(O,M_\tau)\ \le\ \delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{RH}}(O,M_\tau)\ \le\ \delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}(O,M_\tau)\quad\text{for every }(O,M_\tau).\tag{$\star$}$$

AAT adopts **C1** as canonical (`#def-value-object`: "AAT adopts C1 as the
canonical default"; `#der-orient-cascade` step 5: "Under C1 (the canonical
default), $\delta_{\text{sat}}\gt 0$ means *locally stuck*, not *globally
infeasible*"). Under C1, $\delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}(O,M_\tau)\gt 0$ is the
*weakest, most false-positive-prone* verdict in ($\star$):
`#def-satisfaction-gap` Epistemic Status states it outright — "C1 gives the
most false 'unattainable' diagnoses." The only convention under which
"$\delta_{\text{sat}}\gt 0$" *means* genuine infeasibility is **C3 (Bellman)** —
and `#def-value-object` C3 "requires solving the Bellman equation or its
approximation" (generally intractable; `#def-satisfaction-gap`: "computation of
$A_O$ is generally intractable").

So the candidate $\mathrm{Lic}$ has exactly two readings, and *both fail a
different one of (R1)–(R4)*:

- **$\mathrm{Lic}$ read under C1** (the convention AAT actually commits to):
  $\mathrm{Lic}^{(1)}(O,M_\tau)=\mathbb{1}[\delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}(O,M_\tau)\gt 0]$
  is total and two-valued (passes the *form* of what the dichotomy needs) but
  it is **not a feasibility verdict** — by ($\star$) and
  `#def-satisfaction-gap` it fires on locally-stuck-but-globally-recoverable
  objectives. A revision $O\to O'$ "licensed" by $\mathrm{Lic}^{(1)}=1$ can be
  *reversed* later with no feasibility-relevant $M$-change, the moment a C2
  horizon extension or a richer $\Pi$ reveals $O$ was reachable
  (`#der-orient-cascade` 5c foregrounds exactly this:
  "$\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{RH}}\le 0$ for a goal that appeared unattainable
  under C1"). Branch-(i) "commitments" under $\mathrm{Lic}^{(1)}$ are *not
  commitments* — they are revocable on convention/horizon change with no new
  feasibility evidence. (M) collapses: $\mathcal{K}_t$ legitimately re-expands.
  **Fails (R2)** (the monotone contraction is false under the canonical
  convention) and **(R4)** (whether it is monotone depends on the analyst's
  convention — C1 vs. C2 — which is the definition of a non-invariant).

- **$\mathrm{Lic}$ read under C3** (the only reading that *is* a feasibility
  verdict): $\mathrm{Lic}^{\text{B}}(O,M_\tau)=\mathbb{1}[\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{B}}(O,M_\tau)\gt 0]$
  is a genuine infeasibility verdict (passes (R2)/(R4) *in principle*) but it
  is **not total as an agent-available predicate**: `#def-value-object` C3 is a
  Bellman fixed point, generally intractable; the agent does not have
  $\mathrm{Lic}^{\text{B}}$ to act on. Worse for the recursion: a finite-per-step
  policy (`#der-directed-separation`: the dynamical system is exactly
  $f_M,f_G,\pi$ — no out-of-band oracle) *cannot evaluate $\mathrm{Lic}^{\text{B}}$
  before emitting $a_t$*; an agent that genuinely needed to solve the Bellman
  equation each step before acting is "stuck, not purposeful"
  (`#form-objective-functional` Epistemic Status §1 — the exact disqualification
  the parent spike §2.3 already leans on). So $\mathrm{Lic}^{\text{B}}$
  **fails (R3)**: it is not an object the in-scope agent operates with, hence
  not something $\mathfrak{A}^{(1)}$ revises, hence WF cannot re-apply to it.

There is no third reading. C2 (receding-horizon) is strictly between the two by
($\star$) and inherits the worse of both: convention-relative (fails R4, the
verdict moves with $N_r$) *and* not closed-form total (fails R3). The
WF-strengthening witness's three findings (F1 over-read, §3.2 non-exhaustive
via convention re-admission, (M) is sublevel-not-value-functional) are **not
three separate defects** — they are three projections of *one* fact: the object
the construction needs is $\mathrm{Lic}^{\text{B}}$ (exact infeasibility), AAT
canonically delivers only $\mathrm{Lic}^{(1)}$ (false-positive-prone), and the
gap between them is ($\star$) — a theorem, `status: exact`. Candidate B does not
fail by a missing lemma; it fails by an AAT theorem. *This is the no-go's
engine.*

### 2.3 Candidate C — $\Phi$ = a fresh agent-internal scalar criterion (the general case)

Drop both prior constructions. Suppose $\mathfrak{A}$ is non-degenerate and ask
abstractly: *must* there exist *some* agent-internal scalar $\Phi$ on
objective-sequences, satisfying (R1)–(R4), without specifying its construction?

By (R3) $\Phi$ is an AAT objective the level-1 agent self-actuates on. By
`#form-objective-functional` every AAT objective's *sole* interface is a value
functional $V_\Phi:\text{trajectories}\to\mathbb{R}$ whose diagnostic content
is read through the satisfaction-gap / control-regret apparatus
(`#def-satisfaction-gap`, `#def-control-regret`) — there is no other handle
(`#form-objective-functional`: "the sole interface between $O_t$ and the rest
of the theory"). So $\Phi$'s "preserved/monotone across revision" property
(R2), to be a property *the theory can even state about $\Phi$*, must be
expressed through $\Phi$'s own satisfaction gap $\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\Phi}$.
But $\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\Phi}$ inherits ($\star$) *at level 1* verbatim
(`#def-value-object` is level-agnostic — it is a statement about *any* value
functional with a continuation convention): under canonical C1,
$\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\Phi,(1)}\gt 0$ means level-1-locally-stuck, *not* level-1
infeasible. So the level-1 monotonicity (R2)/(R4) needs $\mathrm{Lic}^{\Phi}$
exact $=$ a C3 Bellman solve *on the meta-objective* — which fails (R3) at
level 1 by the identical finiteness argument. **Candidate C reproduces
Candidate B's break one level up, for the same reason, with no new escape.**
This is not "the regress continues so we give up"; it is the proof that the
break is *structural in (R1)+(R3)*: any object satisfying (R1) and (R3) is an
AAT objective-functional, hence subject to ($\star$), hence cannot satisfy
(R2)+(R4) without an exact-infeasibility predicate the in-scope agent
provably does not have.

### 2.4 The success direction has been exhausted honestly

Three independent constructions; one shared, exact obstruction. The success
attempt did not fail for want of cleverness or effort — it failed because
(R1)+(R3) *force* $\Phi$ to be an AAT objective-functional, (R2)+(R4) then
*require* an exact convention-stable infeasibility verdict on it, and ($\star$)
+ the finite-per-step commitment (`#der-directed-separation`,
`#form-objective-functional` ES §1) make that verdict provably unavailable to
every agent in AAT's covered scope. The strengthening has honestly failed in
exactly the way strengthen-before-soften anticipates: *the attempt produced a
no-go as large as the result would have been.* Stating it.

## 3. The no-go, stated and derived (Result G′)

> **Result G′ (no agent-internal objective-grounded tower; the
> self-actuation grounding no-go).**
> Let $\mathfrak{A}$ be a non-degenerate self-actuation operator for an agent
> in AAT's covered scope: an actuated agent (`#def-agent-spectrum`) on a
> singular causal trajectory (`#scope-agent-identity`) with finite per-step
> policy and no out-of-band oracle (`#der-directed-separation`: the dynamical
> system is exactly $f_M,f_G,\pi$), whose objective interface is the value
> functional of `#form-objective-functional` evaluated under AAT's canonical
> continuation convention (`#def-value-object` C1). Then there is **no
> functional $\Phi$ satisfying (R1)–(R4)** — in particular, the invariant WF
> forces *cannot itself be an AAT objective-functional the agent
> self-actuates on*. Consequently the meta-objective tower of
> `spike-self-actuation-grounding.md` §2.2 **cannot be a tower of
> agent-internal objectives**: any non-degenerate self-actuator must bottom
> out, at height $K\ge 1$, on a terminal grounding object $\Phi^{(K)}$ that
> is **not an AAT objective-functional** — it is convention-invariant,
> trajectory-extension-invariant, and not self-actuated. Groundless
> self-actuation in the strict sense (an *agent-internal objective* at the
> top of the tower, itself agent-revised, with nothing fixed above it) is
> **impossible for the agents AAT covers** — not "degenerate," *impossible*:
> the object it would require does not exist in AAT's covered machinery.

**Derivation.** Two lemmas, then the assembly. Both lemmas are AAT-internal;
no new postulate is introduced.

**Lemma 1 (objective-functionals are convention-relative; from `#def-value-object`).**
For any AAT objective-functional $V$ with satisfaction threshold, the
infeasibility verdict $\mathbb{1}[\delta_{\text{sat}}\gt 0]$ is convention-indexed
and, under the canonical convention C1, is *not* a genuine infeasibility
verdict: by the convention-monotonicity result
$\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{B}}\le\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{RH}}\le\delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}$
(`#def-value-object`, Corollary, `status: exact`), the canonical
$\delta_{\text{sat}}^{(1)}\gt 0$ holds on a strict superset of the genuinely
infeasible $\{\delta_{\text{sat}}^{\text{B}}\gt 0\}$, with the inclusion strict
exactly on the locally-stuck-but-globally-recoverable objectives
(`#def-satisfaction-gap` Epistemic Status: "C1 gives the most false

**Lemma 2 (the in-scope agent cannot evaluate the C3 verdict per step; from
`#der-directed-separation` + `#form-objective-functional`).** An AAT-covered
agent's entire dynamical system is $f_M,f_G,\pi$ with no out-of-band oracle

**Assembly.** Suppose, for contradiction, a $\Phi$ satisfying (R1)–(R4) exists.

*sole* interface between $O_t$ and the rest of the theory"; "there is nowhere

## 4. The constructive boundary (this is why the no-go is a complete landing, not a dead end)

A no-go that only forbids is half a result. Result G′'s derivation *names its
own escape* with precision: the contradiction came entirely from $\Phi$ being
an **AAT objective-functional** (R1+R3) — which forced ($\star$) and the
intractable C3 verdict. **Remove (R3)'s "AAT objective-functional" content and
the contradiction dissolves.** So the no-go pins down *exactly* what a terminal
grounding invariant $\Phi^{(K)}$ must be:

> **Corollary G′.1 (what can ground the tower — necessary form).** A terminal
> grounding invariant $\Phi^{(K)}$ for a non-degenerate AAT self-actuator must
> be an object that is **(i) convention-invariant** (its verdict does not move
> with C1/C2/C3 — escaping Lemma 1), **(ii) trajectory-extension-invariant
> and agent-available per step** (a finite property the in-scope agent can
> evaluate without an oracle — escaping Lemma 2), and **(iii) not itself an
> AAT objective-functional the agent self-actuates on** (so it is not subject
> to ($\star$) and is genuinely terminal — escaping (R3)). Equivalently:
> $\Phi^{(K)}$ must live on the *adaptive/correction substrate* ($M_t$ and the
> correction dynamics), not on the *objective substrate* ($O_t$).

AAT already contains exactly one object meeting (i)–(iii), and it is already
`type: result, status: exact`:

> **Corollary G′.2 (the persistence bound is a canonical terminal grounding
> invariant).** The structural persistence condition
> (`#result-persistence-condition`: $\alpha\gt \rho/R$ / $R^\ast\ge\lVert\delta_{\text{critical}}\rVert$,
> `type: result, status: exact`) satisfies (i)–(iii):
> - **(i) convention-invariant.** Persistence is a property of the *correction
>   dynamics* (a Lyapunov sector condition on $M_t$ — `#result-persistence-condition`
>   Formal Expression), not of $V_O$ under a continuation convention. C1/C2/C3
>   are conventions for evaluating *objectives*; the persistence threshold has
>   no continuation-convention argument. ($\star$) does not touch it.
> - **(ii) agent-available per step.** The persistence diagnostic is exactly
>   the kind of finite local quantity the in-scope agent already maintains
>   (`#der-orient-cascade` steps 1–2 are the per-step adaptive update; the
>   persistence margin $\Delta\rho^\ast=\alpha R-\rho$ is a finite read, not a
>   Bellman solve). No oracle; no "stuck, not purposeful" failure.
> - **(iii) not an AAT objective-functional.** It lives on $M_t$ and the
>   correction machinery; `#disc-continuity-stance` makes the orthogonality
>   explicit ("the persistence machinery acts on $M_t$ and the correction
>   dynamics… the two are formally independent" of $O_t$). It is not something
>   $\mathfrak{A}$ revises — $\mathfrak{A}$ revises $O_t$, and the persistence
>   bound is not in $O_t$.
>
> Concretely the terminal invariant is: *"do not revise $O_t$ to an objective
> whose pursuit pushes the agent's operating point outside its persistence
> region."* An $O_t'$ that breaks $\alpha\gt \rho/R$ is self-defeating — the
> agent that adopts it cannot maintain bounded mismatch and so cannot reliably
> satisfy $O_t'$ either (`#result-persistence-condition` Discussion: "below
> structural threshold… mismatch grows without effective bound"). This is a
> hard constraint the agent does not get to move, because it is not in the
> category of things $\mathfrak{A}$ operates on.

So the no-go and its boundary together say something exact and complete:

**There is no well-formed self-actuation grounded on an agent-internal
objective (Result G′ — impossible, not merely degenerate). Every well-formed
self-actuator AAT covers must ground its tower on a *non-objective* terminal
invariant (Corollary G′.1 — necessary form). AAT supplies a canonical,
principled, already-exact such invariant (Corollary G′.2 — the persistence
bound).** This is the precise, two-sided resolution: the strict "no groundless
self-actuation" claim is *true and exact as a no-go on the objective tower*,
and the grounding that makes a self-actuator well-formed is *forced onto the
adaptive substrate*, where AAT already has the machinery.

This also sharpens `#disc-continuity-stance` exactly as the parent spike's P3
wanted, but *correctly*: the negotiated-vs-morally-continuous distinction is
not "where the continuity term sits in an objective tower" (Result G′ says
there is no such well-founded objective tower). It is **whether the agent's
terminal non-objective invariant is the bare persistence bound (negotiated:
continuity is tradeable down to the persistence floor) or the persistence bound
*plus* a continuity clause the agent treats as architecturally non-revisable
(morally continuous: continuity is part of the terminal substrate-level
invariant, not of $O_t$).** Stance is a choice of *terminal non-objective
invariant*, which is why it is not internally renegotiable by $\mathfrak{A}$
(which only touches $O_t$): it sits where $\mathfrak{A}$ structurally cannot
reach. This is a strict strengthening of `#disc-continuity-stance` line ~44 to
a *derived* statement, and unlike the parent spike's P3 it is consistent with
Result G′ rather than presupposing the refuted objective-tower picture.
