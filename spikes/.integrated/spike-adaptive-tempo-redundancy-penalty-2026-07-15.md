# Spike: The adaptive-tempo redundancy penalty — derivation attempt, and what is actually true

**Date:** 2026-07-15. **Commissioned by:** Joseph (directly), following the 2026-07-14 adjudication that moved `#def-adaptive-tempo` from `exact` to `conditional`. **Task:** derive the asserted inequality $\mathcal{T} \leq \sum_k \nu^{(k)} \eta^{(k)\ast}$ (equality iff channels informationally independent), or find the no-go / deeper intrinsic reason.

**Verdict up front: DERIVED — with a correction that replaces the asserted claim.** The strengthening attempt succeeded, but what it proved is *not* the asserted inequality. The asserted general claim ("correlated channels $\Rightarrow$ additive form overcounts; $\mathcal{T} \leq \mathcal T_{\text{add}}$ with equality iff informationally independent") is **false in both directions**: correlated channels can make the additive form an *under*count (synergy — noise cancellation), and equality holds on a strictly larger set than independence. What is true, each part exact in the Gaussian/Fisher-local regime:

1. **(Additivity)** Cross-channel noise independence (conditional independence of the observation noises given the environment state) $\Rightarrow$ additive tempo exact. Sufficient, **not** necessary.
2. **(Signed deviation)** The exact deviation from additivity is $\Delta = \sum_k J^{(k)} - J_{\text{joint}}$ with $J_{\text{joint}} = \mathbf{1}^{\top}\Sigma_n^{-1}\mathbf{1}$, and $\Delta$ **has no definite sign** — explicit closed form for $n=2$; three counterexample families for super-additivity.
3. **(Redundancy penalty — the true home of the asserted bound)** In the **common-source noise regime** ($n^{(k)} = s + w^{(k)}$, shared persistent bias $s$) the additive form *is* an exact upper bound, with explicit penalty and equality iff $\sigma_s^2 = 0$; and with persistent $s$ the joint information about the environment **saturates** at $1/\sigma_s^2$ while the additive accounting grows without bound — the echo-chamber theorem.
4. **(Mini no-go)** No penalty functional of any sign-blind dependence measure — conditional mutual information $I(e^{(1)};e^{(2)} \mid M_{\tau^-})$, Watanabe total correlation — can be the exact correction, because the true deviation is signed while those measures are not. The segment's current "the penalty involves the mutual information" is therefore unrescuable as an exact form; CMI survives only as a *witness* of potential non-additivity.
5. **(Deeper intrinsic reason — PID-shaped, as suspected)** The *total* deviation is canonical, but a **channel-local** attribution of it ("channel $k$'s redundancy-corrected tempo contribution") is convention-dependent for $n \geq 3$: this is exactly the partial-information-decomposition impossibility (Williams-Beer 2010 lattice; Rauh-Bertschinger-Olbrich-Jost 2014 no-go for nonnegative decompositions satisfying the identity axiom with $\geq 3$ sources). Only $\mathcal T_{\text{joint}}$ and the total $\Delta$ are convention-free.

Landed as `#deriv-tempo-additivity` (new appendix derivation segment, `conditional`, draft) + replacement of `#def-adaptive-tempo`'s §Channel-independence Discussion (integration-is-replacement: the false general inequality is deleted, not softened). Ripple sites recorded in §7 below, **not** executed.

## 1. Fixing the operationalization — what $\mathcal{T}$ measures, precisely

The 2026-07-14 adjudication's sticking point was that "$\mathcal{T}$ = effective rate of useful information acquisition" was advertised but never given an exact referent under correlated channels. The referent that makes everything derivable (and matches the Fisher-local machinery already in canon, `#deriv-fisher-local-update-gain`):

*[Definition]* Work in the Fisher-local / Gaussian location regime: environment parameter $\theta$, current model uncertainty $U_M$ (posterior variance), channel-$k$ events at Poisson rate $\nu^{(k)}$, per-event observation $o^{(k)} = \theta + n^{(k)}$ with noise variance $U_o^{(k)}$, per-channel-in-isolation optimal gain $\eta^{(k)\ast} = U_M/(U_M + U_o^{(k)})$. Define the **effective tempo** $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}}$ as the fractional uncertainty-contraction rate of the *optimal joint filter*: $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}} := -\tfrac{d}{dt}\log U_M(t)$ evaluated at the operating point, where $U_M(t)$ is the joint-filter posterior variance. Equivalently, with $\mathfrak{J} :=$ posterior-precision inflow rate (Fisher information per unit time delivered by the optimal filter), $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}} = U_M \cdot \mathfrak{J}$ exactly, since $\tfrac{d}{dt}(1/U_M) = \mathfrak{J}$.

*[Derived]* **Single channel, consistency check.** Per event, precision gain $1/U_o$, so $\mathfrak{J} = \nu/U_o$ and $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}} = \nu \, U_M/U_o$. The gain form: $\nu\eta^\ast = \nu\, U_M/(U_M + U_o) = \nu U_M/U_o \cdot (1 + U_M/U_o)^{-1}$. So $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}}$ and $\nu\eta^\ast$ agree exactly to first order in the per-event information ratio $U_M/U_o$, which is the rate-form / small-per-event-gain regime in which tempo is a rate at all (the same first-order-in-step-size condition (R2) of `#deriv-fisher-local-update-gain`). All in/equalities below are exact at the Fisher-information level and transfer to gain-form tempo at first order in $U_M J$; nothing below depends on higher-order terms. (The alternative "mean-mismatch contraction" reading of $\nu\eta$ is discussed in §6 — it *hides* the correlation failure in a bias floor instead of a rate, which is itself instructive.)

**Multichannel additive claim, restated exactly:** is $\mathfrak J_{\text{joint}} = \sum_k \nu^{(k)}/U_o^{(k)} = \mathfrak J_{\text{add}}$? This is a question about **Fisher-information additivity across channels**, and everything reduces to it.

## 2. Result A — additivity is exact under cross-channel noise independence

*[Derived]* If the channels' event streams are conditionally independent given $\theta$ (each event carries fresh noise, independent across channels and events), then the log-likelihood of the pooled event stream is a sum of per-event terms, the scores add, cross-covariances of scores vanish, and the joint Fisher information is the sum of per-event Fisher informations: $J_{\text{pooled}}(t) = \sum_k \nu^{(k)} t \cdot J^{(k)}$ with $J^{(k)} = 1/U_o^{(k)}$. Hence $\mathfrak J_{\text{joint}} = \mathfrak J_{\text{add}}$ and additive tempo is **exact**. This is the standard additivity of Fisher information over independent observations — general, not merely Gaussian.

*[Derived]* **Asynchrony refinement (new, and operationally important).** Independent Poisson streams have almost surely no simultaneous events. Therefore if the noise is *fresh per event* (i.i.d. across events), instantaneous cross-channel noise correlation is *never sampled* and additivity remains exact even for "correlated channels" in the simultaneous-draw sense. What breaks additivity is **noise dependence across events** — a shared *persistent* noise component (the broken dashboard everyone reads). The redundancy question is governed by the ratio of the noise-correlation timescale to the event interarrival times: fresh noise $\to$ additive exact; persistent shared bias $\to$ §4's saturation. *[Discussion]* Intermediate (finite-correlation-time) regimes interpolate; not derived here.

## 3. Result B — the exact deviation, and its indefinite sign

Consider the synchronized-batch form (one event on each of $n$ channels, jointly Gaussian noise $n \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_n)$, $(\Sigma_n)_{kk} = U_o^{(k)}$): the batch delivers joint Fisher information

*[Derived]* $$J_{\text{joint}} = \mathbf{1}^{\top} \Sigma_n^{-1} \mathbf{1}, \qquad \Delta := \sum_k \frac{1}{U_o^{(k)}} - J_{\text{joint}}.$$

(Standard Gaussian location-model Fisher information; for multivariate $\theta$ with channel maps $A_k$ this is $A^{\top}\Sigma_n^{-1}A$, see §5.)

*[Derived]* **Two channels, closed form.** With $c := \mathrm{Cov}(n^{(1)}, n^{(2)})$, $U_1 U_2 - c^2 \gt 0$:

$$J_{\text{joint}} = \frac{U_1 + U_2 - 2c}{U_1 U_2 - c^2}, \qquad \Delta = \frac{c\,\bigl(2U_1U_2 - c\,(U_1 + U_2)\bigr)}{U_1 U_2 \,(U_1U_2 - c^2)}.$$

Verification of the numerator: $(U_1+U_2)(U_1U_2 - c^2) - U_1U_2(U_1+U_2-2c) = 2cU_1U_2 - c^2(U_1+U_2)$. So $\Delta = 0$ iff $c = 0$ **or** $c = 2U_1U_2/(U_1+U_2)$ — the *harmonic mean* of the variances, admissible whenever $U_1 \neq U_2$ (HM $\lt$ GM $= \sqrt{U_1U_2}$, the PD bound). **Consequence: "equality iff informationally independent" is false** — at $c = \mathrm{HM}$ the channels are strictly dependent (CMI $\gt 0$) yet additivity is exact; redundancy and synergy cancel exactly.

*[Derived]* **Super-additivity counterexamples (the asserted bound fails):**

- **(C1) Anti-correlated noise.** $U_1 = U_2 = \sigma^2$, correlation $\rho \lt 0$: $J_{\text{joint}} = \tfrac{2}{\sigma^2(1+\rho)} \gt \tfrac{2}{\sigma^2}$. Averaging cancels anti-correlated noise: **synergy**, $\Delta \lt 0$.
- **(C2) Positively correlated, heterogeneous variances.** $U_1 = 1$, $U_2 = 100$, $c = 9.9$ ($\rho = 0.99$): $J_{\text{joint}} = (101 - 19.8)/(100 - 98.01) = 81.2/1.99 \approx 40.8$, versus $\sum J^{(k)} = 1.01$. The noisy channel acts as a *noise reference* — the combination $o^{(1)} - (c/U_2)\,o^{(2)}$ nearly cancels channel 1's noise. Positive correlation, forty-fold **under**count by the additive form.
- **(C3) Equal variances, all correlations nonnegative, $n = 3$.** $R$ with $\rho_{12} = \rho_{23} = 0.7$, $\rho_{13} = 0$ (PD: eigenvalues $1 \pm 0.7\sqrt{2}$, min $\approx 0.010$). Solving $Rx = \mathbf{1}$: $x = (15, -20, 15)$, so $\mathbf{1}^{\top}R^{-1}\mathbf{1} = 10 \gt 3 = \sum J^{(k)}$. Chain structure lets the middle channel be used as a reference to cancel the outer channels' noise. So even *nonnegative equal-variance correlation* does not give the bound.

The intuition behind the original assertion — "correlation = overlap = overcount" — is the *redundant* half of a two-sided phenomenon; the *synergistic* half (noise cancellation via correlated references) is equally real and gives the opposite sign. This is the redundancy/synergy dichotomy of the information-decomposition literature, showing up at the Fisher level.

## 4. Result C — the true theorem: the common-source (echo-chamber) regime

The regime the segment's own worked intuition lives in (three VPs reading one broken dashboard) is the **common-source noise model**, and there the asserted bound is exactly right, with an explicit penalty:

*[Derived]* **Setup.** $n^{(k)} = s + w^{(k)}$ with $s \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_s^2)$ shared across channels, $w^{(k)} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{w,k}^2)$ independent. Then $\Sigma_n = \sigma_s^2 \mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\top} + W$, $W = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_{w,k}^2)$. Sherman-Morrison: $\mathbf{1}^{\top}\Sigma_n^{-1}\mathbf{1} = q - \tfrac{\sigma_s^2 q^2}{1 + \sigma_s^2 q} = \tfrac{q}{1 + \sigma_s^2 q}$ where $q := \sum_k 1/\sigma_{w,k}^2$. Per-channel isolated information: $J^{(k)} = \tfrac{1}{\sigma_s^2 + \sigma_{w,k}^2} = \tfrac{q_k}{1 + \sigma_s^2 q_k}$, $q_k := 1/\sigma_{w,k}^2$.

*[Derived]* **Redundancy penalty theorem (batch form).** $f(x) := x/(1 + \sigma_s^2 x)$ is strictly concave with $f(0) = 0$, hence strictly subadditive for $\sigma_s^2 \gt 0$:

$$J_{\text{joint}} = f\Bigl(\sum_k q_k\Bigr) \;\leq\; \sum_k f(q_k) = \sum_k J^{(k)}, \qquad \text{penalty } R_s = \sum_k f(q_k) - f(q) \;\gt\; 0,$$

with equality iff $\sigma_s^2 = 0$ or at most one channel is active. Fully heterogeneous channels allowed; the penalty is explicit and closed-form. This is the exact statement the segment's Discussion was reaching for, on its true scope.

*[Derived]* **Saturation theorem (persistent bias — the sharpest form).** If $s$ is *persistent across events* (the dashboard stays broken), then every observation is a measurement of $\theta + s$ plus fresh noise: the pooled data identify $\theta + s$ at additive rate, but the posterior precision about $\theta$ itself is bounded for all time by $1/U_M^{(0)} + 1/\sigma_s^2$ (since $\mathrm{Var}(\theta \mid \theta + s) = (1/U_M^{(0)} + 1/\sigma_s^2)^{-1}$ and conditioning on the data is coarser than conditioning on $\theta+s$). Meanwhile additive accounting claims precision $\approx \mathfrak J_{\text{add}}\, t \to \infty$. **The additive tempo overcounts without bound; the effective tempo decays to zero as the $1/\sigma_s^2$ ceiling is approached.** No number of correlated channels, at any event rate, buys information past the shared bias floor.

*[Derived]* **Overconfidence corollary (the failure phenomenology).** A naive filter that *believes* the channels are independent tracks an internal $U_M^{\text{naive}}(t) \to 0$ like $1/(\mathfrak J_{\text{add}} t)$ while its true mean-square error stalls at the bias floor $\approx \sigma_s^2$-scale: the overcount is manufactured false confidence, and since $\eta^\ast$ is computed from $U_M^{\text{naive}}$, the gain collapses ($\eta^\ast \to 0$) while genuinely wrong — the quantitative form of Boyd's incestuous amplification, and the bridge to `#emp-update-gain`'s gain-collapse pathology. (First-order argument; stated at *[Discussion]* precision in the segment.)

*[Discussion]* In this regime the deviation *is* monotonically witnessed by the conditional mutual information ($I(e^{(1)};e^{(2)} \mid \theta)$ increases with $\sigma_s^2$, as does $R_s$) — which is why the CMI intuition felt right. It is right *here*; it is not right in general (§5).

## 5. Result D — two no-gos: sign-blind penalties, and channel-local decomposition (PID)

*[Derived]* **No-go 1 (sign-blindness).** No exact correction of the form $\mathcal T_{\text{eff}} = \mathcal T_{\text{add}} - g(D)$ where $D$ is any dependence measure invariant under noise-correlation sign flip (conditional mutual information, Watanabe 1960 total correlation, any copula-symmetric functional) can exist. Proof: in C1's family, $\rho$ and $-\rho$ give identical Gaussian CMI $= -\tfrac12\log(1-\rho^2)$ and identical total correlation, but joint informations $\tfrac{2}{\sigma^2(1+\rho)} \neq \tfrac{2}{\sigma^2(1-\rho)}$. Hence the segment's asserted "the penalty involves $I(e^{(1)}; e^{(2)} \mid M_{\tau^-})$" cannot be completed to an exact formula: **the deviation is not a function of the dependence strength; it depends on the dependence *geometry***. The exact correction is the Fisher deficit $\Delta$ of §3 — computable, but requiring $\Sigma_n$, not a scalar dependence summary. CMI survives as a *witness*: in the Gaussian location model, $\Delta \neq 0 \Rightarrow$ CMI $\gt 0$ (converse false at the harmonic-mean hypersurface).

*[Derived (imported)]* **No-go 2 (no canonical channel-local decomposition — the deeper intrinsic reason).** The natural wish behind an additive redundancy-corrected formula is a decomposition of the joint information into per-channel contributions — unique + redundant + synergistic parts — that is channel-local and convention-free. That is precisely the **partial information decomposition** program: Williams & Beer 2010 (arXiv:1004.2515) define the redundancy lattice; Harder-Salge-Polani 2013 and Bertschinger-Rauh-Olbrich-Jost-Ay 2014 ("Quantifying unique information," *Entropy* 16(4)) give competing two-source measures; and Rauh-Bertschinger-Olbrich-Jost 2014 (ISIT, "Reconsidering unique information") prove that **no nonnegative decomposition on the Williams-Beer lattice satisfying the identity axiom exists for three or more sources**. Barrett 2015 (*Phys. Rev. E* 91, 052802) shows that for Gaussian systems the main two-source candidates collapse to the minimal-mutual-information redundancy — so even Gaussian-exact AAT inherits the convention-dependence the moment a *per-channel attribution* for $n \geq 3$ is demanded. Consequence for AAT: $\mathcal T_{\text{joint}}$ (equivalently $\mathfrak J_{\text{joint}}$) and the **total** deviation $\Delta$ are canonical, exact, and derivable; "channel $k$'s corrected tempo contribution" is **not a well-defined quantity** in general, and any future segment that needs one must declare its convention (e.g. sequential/Shapley attribution) explicitly. This is the PID-shaped obstruction the commission anticipated, stated with its actual citations and its actual scope: it blocks the *channel-local* form, not the *joint* form.

## 6. Anisotropy interaction — orthogonal, not subsuming

*[Derived]* Multivariate $\theta$, channels $o^{(k)} = A_k \theta + n^{(k)}$: with cross-channel-independent noise, joint Fisher *matrices* add exactly, $\mathcal{J}_{\text{joint}} = \sum_k \nu^{(k)} A_k^{\top} (\Sigma_n^{(k)})^{-1} A_k$ — so "channels measuring different aspects" is *not* a redundancy phenomenon at all; it is carried by the tensor form of `#def-adaptive-tempo` already (per-direction rates; Loewner comparisons per `#deriv-matrix-persistence-condition`). Redundancy/synergy is an *off-block-diagonal noise* phenomenon; anisotropy is an *eigenbasis/observation-map* phenomenon. They compose but neither subsumes the other: the Loewner-order analogs of §3–§4 hold verbatim ($\mathbf{1}^{\top}\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf{1}$ becomes $A^{\top}\Sigma_n^{-1}A$ vs $\sum_k A_k^{\top}(\Sigma_n^{(k)})^{-1}A_k$, with the common-source subadditivity holding in the Loewner order by the same Sherman-Morrison-Woodbury argument). Not expanded further here; recorded as a Working-Note extension in the segment.

*[Discussion]* **The mean-contraction reading.** If one reads $\nu\eta$ as the contraction rate of *expected* mismatch under the naive per-channel filter, correlation does not change the rate — it changes the *fixed point*: with persistent shared bias the filter converges at the additive rate to the consensus-biased state $\theta + \bar{s}$, i.e. the failure appears as a bias floor rather than a slower rate. Both readings agree that the additive form misstates effective corrective capacity in the echo-chamber regime; the information reading localizes it cleanly in the rate, which is why §1 fixes that reading.
