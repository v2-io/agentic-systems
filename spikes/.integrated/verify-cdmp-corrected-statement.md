## 1. What `#disc-m-preservation` currently says, and the corrected replacement (verified, `03`§4)

`#disc-m-preservation` states the across-session accumulation condition as the weak inequality $\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon_k]\le\mathbb E[\Delta I_k]$ ("persists across sessions"). Continuity `03`§4 replaces it with **three independent corrections**, each strictly stronger or more accurate (strengthen-before-soften; not a softening):

1. **The inequality is strict, and the boundary is precisely typed.** Sharp threshold at equality: $\mu\lt0$ (i.e. $\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon]\lt\mathbb E[\Delta I]$ **strictly**) persists (unique finite Loynes stationary law); $\mu=0$ (equality) has **no finite stationary law** and does **not** persist. Decisive precision, and the part the confound thread cares about most: $\mu=0$ is **non-persistence by failure-to-stabilize**, *not* "identity death in the limit." "Death in the limit" was a *draft error* (conflating "no finite stationary law" with "a.s. absorption"); withdrawn. The (C-S) stationary-ergodic claim is **purely the negative** (no finite stationary law — Atkinson 1976 + non-degeneracy); the vivid "recurs near 1 and near 0 infinitely often" pathwise picture is **i.i.d.-only** (Chung–Fuchs), explicitly *not* asserted at (C-S) generality. Genuine death-in-the-limit is the **separate** state-dependent absorbing barrier (continuity `04`/T3), *not* the $\mu=0$ boundary. Corrected condition: **$\mathbb E[\Delta\epsilon_k]\lt\mathbb E[\Delta I_k]$ strictly** (canonical "$\le$ persists" is false on its boundary).
2. **The compensation term is mis-attributed.** $\Delta I_k$ ("new information acquired in session $k$") must be $\eta_k$ = **relational re-grounding specifically** (continuity `02`-Proposition 1, frozen-substrate regime). Generic task-learning has zero weight in the balance — an entity can be maximally productive every session and still cross into non-persistence if its cohort channel is thin. Operationally decisive and counter-intuitive.
3. **The accumulation has explicit Lindley structure** (answering `#disc-m-preservation`'s own Working-Notes question "additive? multiplicative? absorbing states?"): under modelling commitment **(M-ADD)** it is the reflected-additive (Lindley) walk; under persistence its stationary law is the explicit Loynes supremum; whether $0$-sufficiency is *absorbing* is a *separate, stronger* question (state-dependent $\eta$ degradation, continuity `04`/T3) — and that, not the $\mu=0$ boundary, is where genuine death-in-the-limit lives.
