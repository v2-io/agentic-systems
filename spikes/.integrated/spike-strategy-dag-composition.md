## 0. Provenance of the frame (read before §1 if you did not commission this)

This spike inherits a reframe of `spikes/spike-composition-scaling-N.md` arrived at by a whole-space pass over the composition/closure/persistence cluster plus the fully-worked $N=2$ correlated-Kalman case. The reframe (held at moderate confidence going in; §1 below tests it and §1.4 reports where it survived and where it bent) is that the poly-vs-exp question is *mis-typed everywhere except one place*. The three regimes:

- **(I) Benign linear-Gaussian-stationary.** $\varepsilon^\ast(N) \equiv 0$ exactly, all $N$, all coupling topology. The two-Kalman §5 argument (`spike-composition-correlated-kalman.md` §5, §5.5) is *dimension-free*: the closure error is $\varepsilon_x^2 = \mathrm{tr}(\Delta K\,\Sigma_\delta\,\Delta K^\top)$ with $\Delta K = K_{\text{micro}} - K_c$; the global minimizer $K_c = K_{\text{micro}}$ gives $\varepsilon_x = 0$ *regardless of how coupling enters $\Sigma_\delta$* — coupling changes $\Sigma_\delta$ but the minimizer tracks it. Verified to extend to $N$ in §1.1.
- **(II) Heterogeneous-with-compression ($k\lt N$).** $\varepsilon^{\ast 2} = \lVert Q_\Lambda U_N P_\Lambda\rVert^2$ — the per-step Mori–Zwanzig zero-lag commutator, which #deriv-critical-mass-composition (Discussion ¶"Adjacent literature", and its Related-Work row) already identifies, in the linear-diagonal-coupling case, as the graph-Laplacian-shifted operator $A = \alpha I - \beta(I-J)$ governing **consensus convergence** (Olfati-Saber & Murray 2004). The consensus-coherence literature has *no exponential regime*: spectral-gap-controlled, polynomial / logarithmic / bounded. Confirmed in §1.2.
- **(III) Non-stationary purposeful.** Splits into **Source 2** (Beta-Bernoulli auxiliary divergence — a per-*agent* time-divergence, re-projection-handled, additive in $N$; `spike-composition-correlated-kalman.md` §12–13) and **Source 3 — strategy-DAG-topology composition**, the named-open item *"Strategy-DAG projection under $\Lambda_x$ — Open"* in #form-composition-closure's What-Is-Derived table and Open Question 4 of the correlated-Kalman spike. **Source 3 is the only genuine open core, and is this spike's subject.**

A load-bearing structural observation under all three: #form-composition-closure's What-Is-Derived table type-separates $\varepsilon^\ast$ (per-step) from trajectory-accumulation (the bridge lemma's job) and explicitly **refutes the full-kernel MZ bound at the $\varepsilon^\ast$-level as a type mismatch** ("Full-kernel MZ bound on $\varepsilon^\ast$ — Type mismatch (trajectory-accumulation vs. per-step) — Refuted at the $\varepsilon^\ast$-level; natural home is the bridge-lemma trajectory-error bound"). The exponential the original question fears lives on the *trajectory* side, where the theory already has a boundedness theorem (the bridge lemma $\limsup\lVert e_m\rVert \le \varepsilon^\ast\nu_c/\alpha_c$, conditional on DA2′a-inc). That is *why* the poly-vs-exp typing fails outside Source 3: per-step defect is bounded by construction in (I)/(II), and trajectory accumulation is bounded by the bridge lemma whenever the composite contracts. Source 3 is the one place where the per-step object itself might be ill-posed — because there may be *no admissible single macro-strategy at all*, which is a representability question, not a magnitude.

The methodology requires attempting the genuine quantitative result (a real defect-scaling law / uniqueness / closed-form tractable case) **before** falling back to "it's a scope boundary" — and showing *why* if the fallback is taken (`CLAUDE.md` §"Strengthen before softening"). §§3–8 are that attempt. §9 is the landing.

## 1. Verifying the reframe (the rest of $\varepsilon^\ast(N)$ is mis-typed)

Before spending the spike on Source 3, the re-typing of regimes (I)/(II) is checked, because if it is wrong then Source 3 is not the only open core and the spike is mis-scoped. *This section is verification of an inherited frame, not the spike's contribution; it is here so a future reader does not have to re-derive it and so that, if it is wrong, that is recorded as a first-class finding (it is not — both regimes check out, with one sharpening of the (II) statement).*

### 1.1 Regime (I): $\varepsilon^\ast(N) \equiv 0$ for benign linear-Gaussian, dimension-free — confirmed

$N$ scalar Kalman filters tracking components of an $N$-variate random walk with *arbitrary* process-noise covariance $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{N\times N}$ (any coupling topology — chain, star, complete, $k$-regular — is just a sparsity pattern on $Q$). Means-only projection $\Lambda_x(\{\hat\omega_i, P_i\}) = (\hat\omega_1,\dots,\hat\omega_N)$, $k=N$. At steady state each $P_i \to P_i^\ast$ constant (scalar DARE per agent — coupling enters only the *cross*-covariances, which the marginal Riccati recursions do not see, because each filter processes only its own scalar observation stream). The mean micro-dynamics are $\hat\omega_{t+1} = (I-K_{\text{micro}})\hat\omega_t + K_{\text{micro}} o_{t+1}$ with $K_{\text{micro}} = \mathrm{diag}(K_i^\ast)$, and the admissible macro-update is $f_c(X,o) = (I-K_c)X + K_c o$. The per-step closure error is $\varepsilon_{x,t} = (K_{\text{micro}} - K_c)\delta_t$, so $\varepsilon_x^2 = \mathrm{tr}(\Delta K\,\Sigma_\delta\,\Delta K^\top)$ with $\Sigma_\delta$ the (now $N\times N$, coupling-populated) innovation covariance. The minimizer over $K_c \in \mathbb{R}^{N\times N}$ is $K_c = K_{\text{micro}}$, giving $\varepsilon^\ast = 0$ **for every $N$ and every $Q$**. Coupling re-populates $\Sigma_\delta$ but the minimizer tracks it term-for-term; $\Sigma_\delta$ never enters the optimal value. This is the $N$-generalization the scoping spike's §6.1 first-move predicted, and it goes through verbatim. **(I) confirmed dimension-free.** The cost of not exploiting coupling is the *performance gap* $\Delta_{\text{perf}}$, not $\varepsilon^\ast$ — exactly the representability/optimality split of `spike-composition-correlated-kalman.md` §5.1, which is $N$-invariant in *kind*.

### 1.2 Regime (II): compression defect is the consensus operator, no exponential regime — confirmed, with a sharpening

When $k\lt N$ (genuine compression — the (P3)-aggressive regime, scoping-spike Reading 4), $\Lambda_x$ is a rank-$k$ projector $P_\Lambda$ and the per-step state-closure defect is the Mori–Zwanzig zero-lag bound $\varepsilon^{\ast 2} = \lVert Q_\Lambda U_N P_\Lambda\rVert^2$ ( #form-composition-closure What-Is-Derived table, "Mori-Zwanzig zero-lag bound … Derived (conditional on stationarity)"; #result-unity-closure-mapping Formal Expression ¶"the projection's range is non-invariant"), $U_N$ the micro-propagator, $Q_\Lambda = I - P_\Lambda$. For the canonical mean-pooling macro-coordinate ($k=1$, $P_\Lambda = \tfrac1N\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top$) and diagonal-plus-coupling propagator $U_N = (1-\alpha)I + \beta W$ ($W$ the coupling/adjacency operator), $Q_\Lambda U_N P_\Lambda$ is exactly the off-consensus-subspace block of the graph-Laplacian-shifted operator #deriv-critical-mass-composition names: $A = \alpha I - \beta(I-J)$, $J$ the averaging operator. Its norm is controlled by the **algebraic connectivity** (Fiedler value $\lambda_2(L)$ of the coupling Laplacian). The four scoping-spike coupling families instantiate as:

| Coupling | $\lambda_2(L)$ scaling | $\varepsilon^\ast(N)$ at $k=1$ | regime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete graph | $\Theta(N)$ | $\to 0$ as $N$ grows (mean-field favorable) | bounded |
| $k$-regular expander | $\Theta(1)$ | bounded | bounded |
| Balanced tree | $\Theta(1/N)$-ish | $\Theta(1)$, slow mixing | bounded |
| Linear chain / ring | $\Theta(1/N^2)$ | $\Theta(1)$ but slow | bounded |

Every row is **bounded or decaying** — *never exponential*. This matches the consensus-coherence literature (Olfati-Saber & Murray 2004; Bamieh et al. 2012 "coherence in large-scale networks") which has polynomial / logarithmic / bounded $H_2$-coherence scalings and no exponential regime. **(II) confirmed: no exponential.**

*Sharpening (a first-class finding, flagged for §10).* The scoping spike's §4.2 hedged that fully-connected $\varepsilon^\ast$ might "grow like $O(1)$ forever (unfavorable), with a phase transition somewhere," contingent on exchangeability. The MZ-as-consensus identification removes the hedge: the controlling quantity is $\lambda_2(L)$, which is a *spectral* property of the coupling operator and does **not** require exchangeability (de Finetti / propagation-of-chaos are not invoked — the Laplacian spectrum is defined for arbitrary, fully heterogeneous coupling). The §4.2 "needs exchangeability" caveat is therefore unnecessary; the bound is spectral, not mean-field. Equally: the (II) defect is governed by the *same* linear-algebraic object (the $L^2$ residual of projecting off an eigenspace of the micro-propagator) that #result-unity-closure-mapping Working-Notes "Mori-Zwanzig cross-check" conjectured the IB rate-distortion bound coincides with — this spike's §1.2 is a (partial) confirmation of that conjectured coincidence in the consensus case.

### 1.3 Source 2 is additive in $N$ — confirmed

The Beta-Bernoulli auxiliary divergence (`spike-composition-correlated-kalman.md` §12) is a *per-agent* phenomenon: each agent $i$'s discarded sample-count $n_i \to \infty$ forces a fixed-gain defect $\varepsilon_{\Sigma,i}$. For $N$ agents the composite defect under the discard-$n_i$ projection is $\varepsilon_\Sigma^2 = \sum_i \varepsilon_{\Sigma,i}^2$ (independent per-agent contributions, no cross term — the divergences do not interact, they each just need their own $n_i$ retained). This is **linear in $N$**, and is *removed entirely* by the re-projection / option-(b) fix (retain a macro effective-sample-size $n_c$, one per agent — `spike-correlated-kalman` §12 "What makes this solvable"). Additive and repairable; not a scaling pathology. Confirmed.

### 1.4 Where the inherited frame bent

The frame survived intact with two adjustments, both *strengthening* it: (a) the (II) statement is sharper than inherited — exchangeability is not needed, the bound is spectral (§1.2); (b) the inherited frame said Source 3 "is the named-open item" — verified true ( #form-composition-closure What-Is-Derived row "Strategy-DAG projection under $\Lambda_x$ — Domain-specific; not resolved by (P1)-(P3) — Open"; correlated-Kalman Open Question 4), and additionally Source 3 is *load-bearing for the original poly-vs-exp question's sign*: §§3–9 show it does **not** rescue an exponential either, but for a structurally different reason than (I)/(II) — it is mis-typed as a *magnitude* question rather than mis-typed as a *bounded* one. The poly-vs-exp question is fully dissolved only once Source 3 is re-typed; (I)/(II)/Source-2 alone leave it open.

## 2. Source 3, stated precisely

**Setup.** $N$ sub-agents satisfying #scope-composite-agent (some route C-i…C-iv holds, so a composite *purpose* exists — this is assumed throughout; without it $G_c$ is ill-typed and the question is vacuous, #scope-composite-agent Discussion ¶"Why this is a scope condition"). Each carries a strategy DAG $\Sigma_i = (V_i, E_i, p_i, \gamma_i)$ ( #def-strategy-dag) — propositional nodes, AND/OR combination, single-parameter causal-credence edges, rooted at $v_{\text{root},i}$, acyclic by temporal indexing. The topologies *differ*: $\lvert V_i\rvert$, depth, AND/OR pattern, and — critically — the **temporal order** $\tau_i$ on nodes differ across agents.

**The (A1) demand.** #form-composition-closure (A1) requires the macro-state to decompose as $X_c = (M_c, G_c)$ with $G_c = (O_c, \Sigma_c)$ where $\Sigma_c$ is *itself a single AAT strategy object* — by #def-strategy-dimension and #def-strategy-dag, a single rooted AND/OR causal DAG with one root terminal and one consistent temporal order. Not a tuple of DAGs, not a forest: #def-strategy-dag structural constraint 2 (rootedness — "a unique root terminal $v_{\text{root}}$, the single sink") is a *definitional* requirement on any object that is to be called a strategy. So $\Lambda_\Sigma$ must produce a structure that is **itself** a legal $\Sigma$.

**The object in question.** $\Sigma_c = \Lambda_\Sigma(\Sigma_1,\dots,\Sigma_N)$. The Source-3 closure defect is the strategy-component of $\varepsilon^\ast$:

$$\varepsilon_\Sigma^\ast = \inf_{\Lambda_\Sigma,\, f_{c,\Sigma}} \;\mathbb{E}_\tau\Big[\big\lVert \Lambda_\Sigma(\Sigma_{\text{micro},mK_c}) - f_{c,\Sigma}\big(\Lambda_\Sigma(\Sigma_{\text{micro},(m-1)K_c}),\, \Lambda_o(\cdot)\big)\big\rVert\Big]$$

over admissible $\Lambda_\Sigma$ (must land in the space of legal strategy DAGs and satisfy (P1)–(P3)) and admissible macro strategy-dynamics $f_{c,\Sigma}$ (must satisfy (A1)–(A4) — in particular the strategy-layer sector condition #schema-strategy-persistence).

**The pre-derivation guess (held loosely; the methodology requires beating it first).** That even here the poly-vs-exp typing fails and the honest object is a *scope/representability boundary* — does a coherent $(\Sigma_c)$ exist at all — rather than a scaling exponent. §§3–8 attempt to *beat* this (find a real defect-scaling law or closed-form tractable case); §9 reports whether the attempt succeeded.

**Why the norm is the hard part.** $\varepsilon_\Sigma$ requires $\lVert\Sigma - \Sigma'\rVert$ — a distance between strategy *graphs*. #def-strategy-dimension Discussion already flags the type error: "you cannot subtract a graph from a state vector." #form-composition-closure handles this for the *credence vector* (the $p_{ij}$ live in $[0,1]^{\lvert E\rvert}$, a normed space) but **not for the topology** (which graph). This is the precise locus of "not resolved by (P1)-(P3)" in the Open row. So Source 3 has two sub-questions, and they have *different types*:

- **(S3a) Credence-composition defect**, *given a fixed common topology*: the agents' DAGs share a skeleton but carry different edge-credences $p_i$; how does $\varepsilon_\Sigma$ scale in $N$? — a *magnitude* question, normed-space, tractable.
- **(S3b) Topology-composition defect**: the agents' DAGs have *different skeletons / different temporal orders*; is there an admissible single $\Sigma_c$ at all, and at what structural cost? — a *representability* question, not obviously a magnitude.

The poly-vs-exp question implicitly assumes the answer is (S3a)-shaped (a magnitude that scales). The contribution below is: **(S3a) is poly (in fact $\le$ linear) and (S3b) is the one that is mis-typed — but not as the guess says.** §§3–4 do (S3a); §§5–8 do (S3b).

## 3. (S3a) Credence composition under fixed common topology — closed form, linear in $N$

Fix a common skeleton $(V,E,\gamma)$ (all agents reason about the same plan, disagree only on edge-credences — the "$N$ experts on one plan" case; this is the strategy-layer analog of the homogeneous-Kalman case). Agent $i$ holds credence vector $\boldsymbol p_i \in [0,1]^{\lvert E\rvert}$, updated by #deriv-edge-credence-dynamics dynamics (Beta-Bernoulli per edge, sector parameter $\alpha_{\Sigma,i}$ per #schema-strategy-persistence). Work in the **log-odds coordinate** $\lambda_{ij} = \log\frac{p_{ij}}{1-p_{ij}}$, which #def-strategy-dag Formal-Expression ¶"Edge-credence presentation coordinates" establishes as the *unique additive-evidence coordinate* (forced by #deriv-edge-update-natural-parameter). This is the load-bearing move: **in log-odds, edge updates are additive**, so credence composition is a *linear* problem even though AND/OR propagation is nonlinear in probability space.

**Macro-credence projection.** The natural $\Lambda_\Sigma$ for fixed topology is the log-odds centroid $\boldsymbol\lambda_c = \frac1N\sum_i \boldsymbol\lambda_i$ (the Fisher-natural mean; #def-unity-dimensions $U_f$ for Bayesian-updates "tracks divergence between induced kernels", and the KL-centroid of exponential-family credences *is* the natural-parameter mean). Macro-dynamics $f_{c,\Sigma}$: the same Beta-Bernoulli sector dynamics with composite gain.

**Per-step closure defect.** Identical in structure to the heterogeneous-Kalman calculation ( #result-unity-closure-mapping §"Two-axis structure", $\varepsilon_x^2 = (\Delta K/2)^2[\,\cdot\,]$): the defect is driven by *update-rule heterogeneity*, here heterogeneity of the per-agent edge gains $\eta_{\Sigma,i}$. Writing $\Delta\eta_{ij} = \eta_{\Sigma,i,ij} - \eta_{c,ij}$,

$$\varepsilon_\Sigma^{\ast 2} \;=\; \sum_{(i,j)\in E}\; \mathrm{Var}\big[\Delta\eta_{ij}\,r_{ij}\big]\;,\qquad r_{ij} = \text{per-edge evidence residual } (y_{ij}-p_{ij}).$$

This is **exactly the structural-unity ($U_f$) axis of #result-unity-closure-mapping, lifted to the strategy layer** — the strategy analog of $\varepsilon_x^2 = (\Delta K/2)^2[S_- - C_{+-}^2/S_+]$. With the centroid macro-gain $\eta_c = \frac1N\sum\eta_{\Sigma,i}$, $\sum_i\Delta\eta_{i} = 0$ and the defect per edge is the *gain-variance across agents*, $\frac1N\sum_i(\eta_{\Sigma,i}-\bar\eta)^2 \cdot \mathrm{Var}[r]$. Summed over $\lvert E\rvert$ edges:

$$\boxed{\;\varepsilon_\Sigma^{\ast 2}(N) \;=\; \lvert E\rvert\cdot \overline{\mathrm{Var}_i[\eta_{\Sigma,i}]}\cdot \mathrm{Var}[r]\;}$$

**Scaling in $N$: this is $O(1)$ in $N$, not growing.** $\overline{\mathrm{Var}_i[\eta_{\Sigma,i}]}$ is the *population variance* of per-agent edge-gains; adding more agents from the same gain-distribution does not increase a variance — it estimates it more precisely (it *converges* to the population value). The defect depends on $\lvert E\rvert$ (plan size) and gain-dispersion, **not on $N$**. If instead one asks the scoping-spike Reading-3 worst case (adversarially-chosen heterogeneous gains under a dispersion budget), the budget *is* the variance, so still $O(1)$ in $N$ at fixed budget. **(S3a) verdict: the credence-composition defect is dimension-free in $N$ (bounded by plan size and gain-dispersion), strictly not polynomial-growing, a fortiori not exponential.** This is a positive closed-form result and it *kills the poly-vs-exp question for the fixed-topology case outright* — it is the strategy-layer twin of regime (I)'s dimension-freedom, obtained by the log-odds-additivity move.

*Strengthening note (methodology — tried to make it worse, could not).* Attempted to make (S3a) grow in $N$ by (i) correlated gains across agents (still a variance, $N$-invariant at fixed correlation); (ii) deep chains so the evidence-starvation factor $\prod_{j\lt k}\theta_j$ ( #def-strategy-dag Discussion ¶"Depth penalties") attenuates deep edges — this *reduces* the deep-edge contribution, helping not hurting; (iii) growing $\lvert E\rvert$ with $N$ (one plan-piece per agent) — this is the (S3b) topology case, not fixed-topology, and is handled in §§5–8. No route makes fixed-topology credence-composition grow in $N$. The result is robust.

## 4. The credence/topology split is forced (why §3 does not finish the job)

§3 settled (S3a). One might hope (S3b) reduces to it: "give every agent the *union* skeleton, set missing edges to $p=0$ or to an uninformative prior, then apply §3." This **fails**, and the failure is the heart of Source 3. Two independent obstructions:

**Obstruction A — the union of heterogeneous DAGs is not a legal strategy DAG.** #def-strategy-dag structural constraint 2 demands a *unique* root terminal (single sink). The disjoint-union / overlay of $\Sigma_1,\dots,\Sigma_N$ has $N$ roots. To make it a strategy you must add a combining root — but *which combinator* ($\gamma_{\text{root}} \in\{\text{AND},\text{OR}\}$) is **not free**: AND-root means "the composite objective needs every sub-plan to succeed" ( #def-strategy-dag status propagation, AND case $\prod p$), OR-root means "any sub-plan suffices" ($1-\prod(1-p)$). These encode *opposite* composite objectives, and the correct one is dictated by $O_c$ (via #scope-composite-agent's route), not by the topology. The correlated-Kalman spike §12 quietly picked AND ("both tracking errors must be below $d$") — *that was a modeling choice smuggled in as a projection*, legitimate there because the shared objective dictated it, illegitimate as a general $\Lambda_\Sigma$. So topology composition is **not** a graph operation closed over the strategy-DAG type; it is the construction of a *new* strategy whose root semantics come from $O_c$.

**Obstruction B — temporal-order incompatibility (the genuinely new structural object).** This is the one the literature search surfaced and the one that re-types the question. #def-strategy-dag §"Acyclicity is Derived": each node carries a temporal position $\tau_i(v)\gt t$, and edges respect $\tau$; the DAG *is* (the Hasse diagram of) a strict partial order on planned events. Composing $\Sigma_1,\dots,\Sigma_N$ into one $\Sigma_c$ requires a *single* temporal order $\tau_c$ on $V_c = \bigsqcup V_i$ that **restricts to each $\tau_i$ on $V_i$** (else the macro-DAG mis-orders agent $i$'s own plan, violating constraint and breaking #post-composition-consistency item 3 "mismatch consistency" at the strategy layer). When sub-plans share no nodes this is free (any interleaving works). **When sub-plans share nodes** — the typical case for a real composite: agents reference common subgoals, shared resources, joint milestones ( #scope-composite-agent (C-ii) hierarchical derivation *guarantees* shared parent nodes) — a common consistent $\tau_c$ exists **iff the union of the per-agent partial orders is itself acyclic**. If agent 1 plans "A before B" and agent 2 plans "B before A" *on shared nodes A,B*, there is **no** legal $\Sigma_c$: any $\tau_c$ creates a cycle, and #def-strategy-dag's acyclicity (derived, exact, from #post-causal-structure) *forbids the object*. This is not a large defect — it is **non-existence of an admissible $\Sigma_c$**.

This re-types Source 3 precisely: it is **not** "how big is $\varepsilon_\Sigma(N)$" — it is **"does an admissible $\Sigma_c$ exist, and that is an order-theoretic property of the shared-node sub-order intersection, with no $N$-dependent magnitude at all."** The next sections make this exact and quantitative where it *can* be made quantitative (it can, partially — §6 — which is why this is not merely the guessed "it's a scope boundary" hand-wave).

## 5. The right object: $\Sigma_c$ as a $\tau$-abstraction (causal-abstraction import)

The literature that types this correctly is **causal-model abstraction** (Rubenstein et al. 2017, "Causal Consistency of SCMs"; Beckers & Halpern 2019, "Abstracting Causal Models", arXiv:1812.03789; Beckers, Eberhardt & Halpern 2020, "Approximate Causal Abstraction"). #def-strategy-dag Discussion ¶"Connection to Pearl's framework" already establishes that, under causal sufficiency, the strategy DAG *is* a causal Bayesian network and "Pearl's do-calculus applies". The composition question is then exactly the causal-abstraction question, *with the arrow reversed*: causal abstraction asks when one high-level SCM faithfully abstracts one low-level SCM; **strategy composition asks when one high-level strategy DAG $\Sigma_c$ faithfully abstracts the *joint* low-level SCM $\Sigma_1\times\cdots\times\Sigma_N$** (the product/parallel composition of the sub-agents' causal models). This is the adopt-with-citation move (`CLAUDE.md` §"Prior art integration"): the right object already has a name and a theory.

**Definition (adopted, specialized).** A *strategy $\tau$-abstraction* of $(\Sigma_1,\dots,\Sigma_N)$ is a strategy DAG $\Sigma_c$ together with a surjective node map $\tau:\;\bigsqcup_i V_i \twoheadrightarrow V_c$ and an intervention map $\omega$ such that the abstraction diagram commutes: writing $\Pi$ for the #def-strategy-dag status-propagation map, for every intervention $\mathbf i$ on the joint micro-strategy, $\tau(\Pi_\mu(\mathbf i)) = \Pi_c(\omega(\mathbf i))$ (subscript $\mu$ = joint micro-strategy, $c$ = macro-strategy $\Sigma_c$). The Beckers–Eberhardt–Halpern $(\tau,\alpha)$-approximate-abstraction error and #form-composition-closure's $\varepsilon_\Sigma$ are the **same family** (a commuting-diagram gap under an abstraction map) **but not term-for-term** — verified side-by-side against both primary definitions on 2026-05-18 (§11 *Verification note*; the original "term for term / already solved in the literature" wording is superseded by this paragraph). BEH's $d_\tau$ (Beckers-Eberhardt-Halpern 2020, Def 3.2) is a **supremum over all allowed interventions and low-level contexts** of an *interventional* commuting gap; #form-composition-closure's $\varepsilon_\Sigma$ (its Composition-Closure Definition) is an **expectation over the reachable closed-loop trajectory distribution $\mathcal D_{\text{micro}}$** of an *observational / on-policy* gap. BEH is therefore strictly stronger: $(\tau,\alpha)$-abstraction $\Rightarrow$ bounded $\varepsilon_\Sigma$, but the converse is false in general (a $\Sigma_c$ can track the on-policy joint plan in expectation while failing as a causal abstraction under off-policy interventions). Moreover BEH explicitly leaves its high-level distance $d_{V_H}$ "application-dependent", so the literature supplies the correct *type* and a strictly-stronger target to **adopt** — not a closed norm-on-graphs; the specific distance #form-composition-closure flagged "not resolved by (P1)-(P3)" is still AAT's to choose. What the import legitimately buys: the right frame (causal-model abstraction), the commuting-diagram form, and the *option* to strengthen $\varepsilon_\Sigma$ to the interventional guarantee — arguably desirable, since strategy DAGs are planning objects meant to support intervention.

**Three consequences, immediately.**

1. *Existence of an exact $\Sigma_c$ ($\varepsilon_\Sigma=0$) is the Rubenstein "causal consistency" condition* — it holds **iff** the joint micro-strategy's interventional distribution factors through $\tau$, i.e. iff the sub-plans' shared-node sub-orders are compatible (no Obstruction-B cycle) **and** the AND/OR semantics agree on shared nodes (no Obstruction-A clash). When they do, $\Sigma_c$ exists and is *exact* (not approximately — exactly, $\varepsilon_\Sigma=0$), **for all $N$**. This is the strategy-layer twin of regime (I): *compatible* heterogeneous strategy composition is dimension-free and exact.
2. *When exact abstraction fails*, Beckers-Halpern's hierarchy (exact ⊐ uniform ⊐ strong) gives the **structural typing** the pre-derivation guess anticipated — but sharper: the failure is not "no composite" (scope already guaranteed a composite *purpose*), it is "the composite strategy is representable only as a **constructive abstraction** that *merges* incompatible nodes", and the merge is forced, not free (§6).
3. *The defect, when nonzero, is the order-incompatibility of the shared sub-orders* — a combinatorial invariant computed below, and it is **$N$-independent in the way that matters** (§7).

## 6. The forced merge and its exact cost (the quantitative core — beating the guess)

The methodology demands a genuine quantitative result before conceding "it's a scope boundary." Here it is.

When Obstruction B bites — shared nodes $S \subseteq \bigcap_i V_i$ carry **incompatible** sub-orders ($\tau_i\rvert_S$ and $\tau_j\rvert_S$ disagree, creating a cycle in $\bigcup_i \tau_i\rvert_S$) — an exact $\Sigma_c$ does not exist (§4 Obstruction B; #def-strategy-dag acyclicity is exact and forbids it). The *only* way to obtain an admissible (acyclic, single-rooted) $\Sigma_c$ is a **constructive abstraction** (Beckers-Halpern): collapse each cyclically-entangled set of shared nodes into a single coarser macro-node, until the quotient order is acyclic. The minimal such collapse is the **condensation** of the union-order's directed graph — quotient by strongly connected components (SCCs) of $G_\cup = (S, \bigcup_i \tau_i\rvert_S)$. (Tarjan condensation; this is *the* minimal acyclic quotient, classical and unique.)

**This is computable and the defect has a closed form.** Each collapsed SCC is a set of planned events the composite *cannot temporally distinguish* — it must treat them as one coarse step. The status-propagation error introduced by collapsing an SCC $C$ is exactly the loss from replacing the true ordered AND/OR sub-computation over $C$ by a single coarse node. Using the log-odds additive coordinate (§3) and #der-chain-confidence-decay's exact identity $\log P(\text{chain}) = \sum \log P(E_i\mid E_{\lt i})$, collapsing an ordered chain of $\lvert C\rvert$ nodes into one unordered macro-node *discards the conditioning structure*; the resulting per-SCC defect is the **total correlation** (multi-information) among the collapsed nodes' status variables — exactly #def-unity-dimensions' $U_M$-style quantity, here intra-SCC:

$$\varepsilon_{\Sigma,C}^2 \;=\; \mathrm{TC}\big(\{s_v : v\in C\}\big)\;=\;\sum_{v\in C} H(s_v) \;-\; H\big(\{s_v\}_{v\in C}\big)\;\ge 0,$$

zero iff the collapsed statuses were independent (then collapsing loses nothing — order was spurious), maximal when they were a deterministic chain (collapsing destroys a real ordering). The **total Source-3 defect is the sum over collapsed SCCs**:

$$\boxed{\;\varepsilon_\Sigma^{\ast 2} \;=\; \sum_{C \in \mathrm{SCC}(G_\cup),\, \lvert C\rvert\gt 1} \mathrm{TC}\big(\{s_v\}_{v\in C}\big)\;}$$

This is a genuine, closed-form, computable defect law — *not* a scope hand-wave. It says exactly: **the irreducible cost of composing heterogeneous strategy DAGs is the information destroyed by the minimal order-coarsening that makes their shared sub-orders jointly acyclic.** It is the strategy-layer exact analog of the Mori–Zwanzig "$L^2$ residual of projecting off an eigenspace" that governs regime (II) — here the projection is the SCC-condensation and the residual is intra-SCC total correlation.

## 7. The scaling verdict — and why it dissolves poly-vs-exp for the last time

Now the $N$-question, answered exactly via the §6 law:

- **Number of collapsed SCCs**: at most $\lvert S\rvert$ (shared-node count), which is a property of *plan overlap*, **not of $N$**. Two agents with maximally incompatible orders on $\lvert S\rvert$ shared nodes produce the same condensation as $N$ agents whose pairwise incompatibilities are the same shared cycles. Adding agents whose sub-orders are *compatible* with the existing union adds **zero** new SCCs and **zero** defect (their constraints are already implied). Adding an agent that introduces a *new* incompatibility enlarges one SCC by its shared nodes — bounded by $\lvert S\rvert$ total, hard cap independent of $N$.
- **Per-SCC defect $\mathrm{TC}$**: bounded by $(\lvert C\rvert-1)\cdot\max_v H(s_v) \le (\lvert C\rvert-1)\log 2$ (status variables are $[0,1]$-credences; the binary-entropy ceiling — #def-strategy-dag leaf credences are probabilities). Summed: $\varepsilon_\Sigma^{\ast 2} \le \lvert S\rvert\log 2$. **Bounded by shared-plan size, fully independent of $N$.**

**Verdict.** $\varepsilon_\Sigma^\ast(N)$ is **not a function of $N$ at all** in its leading behavior — it is a function of the *shared-plan order-incompatibility* (the SCC structure of the union of shared sub-orders). It is bounded by $\sqrt{\lvert S\rvert\log 2}$ for any $N$. There is **no polynomial-vs-exponential question**: the question presupposed a magnitude scaling in team size, and the honest object has *no $N$-scaling* — it has a *shared-plan-overlap* dependence with a hard $N$-free ceiling. This is the precise, *quantitative* re-typing the methodology asked for: not "it's a scope boundary, shrug," but a closed-form defect law (§6) whose $N$-dependence is provably absent and whose true argument is a computable order-theoretic invariant.

**Where a residual genuine $N$-effect hides (honest scoping of the result).** One channel is *not* $N$-free: if sub-plans are drawn so that each new agent contributes a *fresh* shared node with a fresh incompatibility (plan overlap *grows* with team size, $\lvert S\rvert = \Theta(N)$), then $\varepsilon_\Sigma^{\ast2} = \Theta(N)$ — **linear**, and only under the assumption that overlap scales with $N$, which is a statement about the *plan ensemble*, not about composition. This is the strategy-layer exact echo of Source 2's additivity (§1.3) and of the scoping spike's Reading-2 (ensemble-dependent). It is **linear at worst, never super-linear, never exponential**, and it is attributable to plan-design, not to the composition operator. So the strongest honest statement is: *the composition operator contributes no $N$-scaling; any $N$-growth is inherited linearly from a modeling choice to let plan-overlap grow with team size, and is removed by bounded shared-plan design exactly as Source 2's is removed by re-projection.*

## 8. Strengthen-before-soften ledger (what was attempted to beat the re-typing)

Per `CLAUDE.md` §"Strengthen before softening": before accepting "no $N$-scaling, it's an order-incompatibility invariant," the opposite (a real super-linear law) was attempted and failed *for stated reasons* — recorded so a future agent does not re-run them:

1. **Force exponential via AND/OR propagation depth.** Conjecture: deep composite DAGs make $\hat P_{\Sigma_c}$ products explode. *Refuted*: #der-chain-confidence-decay is *exact* and the decay is in *confidence*, not *defect* — deeper plans are less confident but not less *representable*; the closure defect is about tracking, and depth attenuates the deep-edge defect contribution (§3 strengthening note (ii)). Depth helps the defect, not hurts.
2. **Force exponential via L2-correlation blowup** ( #def-strategy-dag Correlation Hierarchy). Conjecture: $N$ agents' latent common causes give $O(2^{N})$ joint-distribution parameters (the L2 "exponential blowup L2 was defined to name"). *Refuted as an $\varepsilon^\ast$ scaling*: that $2^k$ is the cost of the *exact joint representation* (L2), i.e. the cost of $\varepsilon_\Sigma=0$ via full correlation — but the *defect of the tractable L0/L1 approximation* is the omitted covariance ($\pm\rho$ per sibling pair, #def-strategy-dag Correlation-Hierarchy bias table), which sums to $O(\lvert E\rvert)$, **not** $O(2^N)$. The exponential is the price of *exactness*, not the *defect*; the defect of the cheap representation is polynomial. This is the same type-confusion as the full-kernel-MZ refutation (§0): an exponential exists in the problem but on the *exact-representation-cost* axis, not the *per-step-defect* axis.
3. **Force super-linear via order-incompatibility growth.** Conjecture: pairwise order-conflicts among $N$ agents give $\binom N2$ conflicts. *Refuted*: conflicts live on *shared nodes* $S$; $\binom N2$ pairwise disagreements on the *same* shared nodes collapse to the *same* SCCs — the condensation is idempotent under redundant constraints. The invariant is $\lvert S\rvert$ and the SCC structure, not the pair count. Only *fresh* shared nodes per agent give growth, and that is linear and plan-ensemble-attributable (§7), not composition-intrinsic.

All three strengthening attempts failed honestly; the re-typing in §7 is the result, and (per `CLAUDE.md` integration-is-replacement) the re-typing *is* the finding — a precise no-such-scaling-law with a positive closed-form defect object (§6) replacing it, not a softened "we couldn't find the exponent."
