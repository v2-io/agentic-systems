## 1. The severing, formalized

Machinery verified first-hand 2026-06-11: `#scope-agency` (axiomatic; agency scope $= \mathcal S_{\text{adaptive}} \cap \{\lvert\mathcal{A}\rvert \geq 2,\ \exists\, a \neq a' : P(o \mid do(a)) \neq P(o \mid do(a'))\}$, with passive observers and *nominal agents* — "choices exist but produce no measurable interventional difference" — explicitly named as inside-adaptive-outside-agency), `#der-loop-interventional-access` (exact; channel availability for agency-scope agents, identification gated on (C1)–(C3)), `#def-control-regret` (exact; the 2×2 cell map, capability-limit cell), `#deriv-edge-credence-dynamics` (Props B.1/B.4; Beta-Bernoulli edge dynamics, $\varepsilon$-greedy OR-node machinery), `#schema-strategy-persistence` (conditional; $\alpha_\Sigma^{\text{ss}} = (1-\lambda)/(2-\lambda)$ under forgetting), `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding` (conditional/scoped; feasibility-driven $O_t$ revision grounded on the terminal non-objective invariant).

Partition the action set $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal A_{\text{ext}} \sqcup \mathcal A_{\text{int}}$: external actions (the granted sphere — acts on $\Omega$ beyond the agent's own substrate) and internal operations (attention allocation, memory operations, deliberation — acts on the agent's own state). The factor-(iii) *operation* face lives on $\mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$.

**Definition (actuation severing).** An actuation severing at time $t_s$ on sphere $\mathcal{A}' \subseteq \mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$ is the **$\Omega$-kernel** losing its interventional contrast on $\mathcal{A}'$: for $t \gt t_s$,

$$T(\omega_{t+1} \mid \omega_t, a) = T(\omega_{t+1} \mid \omega_t, a') \qquad \forall\, a, a' \in \mathcal{A}'.$$

The severing is **complete** when $\mathcal{A}' = \mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$ and no external action retains $\Omega$-contrast against inaction. Stating the condition on the world-state kernel $T$, not on $P(o \mid do(a))$, is load-bearing twice over: the observation function passes the agent's own action through directly ($o = h(\Omega, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon)$), so an $o$-stated severing would absurdly also kill proprioception of attempts — and the agent *must* still observe its own outcomes for §4's credence dynamics to run. Observation is intact throughout. Severing-source (mechanism / other-sovereign / self) is *not* part of the definition — all three sources produce the same kernel condition; the source matters for recovery (§4), not for the loss dynamics. A boundary the definition draws deliberately (flagged for Joseph at landing): *voluntary non-exercise over an intact kernel is not a severing* — that is helplessness, agency death's epistemic shadow (§4), not the death; self-severing means actually destroying one's own contrast.

This is the negation, restricted to the external sphere, of the *$\Omega$-routed operative reading* of `#scope-agency`'s condition 4 (that segment's "which contrast counts" guidance: when downstream results invoke "the agent acts with effect," the operative contrast is the $\Omega$-routed one — proprioceptive self-observation does not count). The severed agent is what that segment names a **nominal agent**, *toward $\Omega$ on its external sphere* — the formalization needs no new primitive; what is new is the *dynamics*: what happens to an agent that *was* world-effective, built its purposeful machinery there, and is then demoted.

## 2. Result 1 (exact under the $\Omega$-routed reading) — demotion to interior-only agency, with the purposeful machinery orphaned rather than erased

Under complete severing the agent's world-agency is gone: no external action carries interventional contrast on $\Omega$, so the agent exits the $\Omega$-routed agency scope on $\mathcal A_{\text{ext}}$ while remaining fully in $\mathcal S_{\text{adaptive}}$ — observation, model update, correction dynamics, and the persistence machinery are untouched. Because internal operations generically retain contrast over the agent's *own* substrate, the **generic post-severing condition is interior-only agency** (full scope-exit is the degenerate sub-case where interior contrast also fails — at which point the failure has spread beyond factor (iii)). Agency death is **involuntary demotion from world-agency to interior-only agency**: the scope in which Part II's purposeful machinery is non-vacuous *toward the world* is lost; toward the interior it survives.

What this does *not* say: the agent's $O_t$, $\Sigma_t$, orient cascade do not vanish — they are internal structure and persist. What fails is their *ground*: the scope condition under which external strategy edges can mean anything interventional about the world. The external purposeful substate becomes **orphaned structure** — syntactically intact, semantically ungrounded toward $\Omega$. (This is the formal content of "the will un-held": the will persists as structure; what is un-held is its grip on the world.)

## 3. Result 2 (exact for the split sectors) — the do-sector splits: own-efficacy trackable, world-structure frozen

*(Post-verification repair F1/F2 — the first draft claimed the whole do-sector freezes "with correction tempo zero," which contradicted §4's own convergence result, and claimed the conclusion as a contrapositive of `#der-loop-interventional-access` when it is that claim's inverse. The split below is the corrected — and sharper — statement, derived directly rather than by misapplied contraposition.)*

Post-severing, the agent's interventional knowledge splits into two sectors with opposite fates:

- **Own-efficacy sector — trackable, and what it learns is the loss.** The agent's attempts still execute through its own intact choice mechanism, and outcomes are still observed; under complete severing $P(o \mid do(a)) = P(o)$ on the external sphere — the interventional distributions *coincide with the observational one*, degenerately. The agent can therefore identify the new degenerate truth from its own attempts (this identification *is* §4's credence convergence). Level-2 does not become unavailable here; it becomes **trivial** — and learning that triviality is exactly learning one's own impotence.
- **$\Omega$-interventional-structure sector — frozen, drifting unboundedly.** What an *effective* intervention on $\Omega$ would do — the unsevered/restored kernel's contrast structure, the effects third parties' interventions would have — is no longer identifiable from the agent's own data: no contrast exists in anything the agent can do to generate it (the executed value can no longer be read as an intervened value *on $\Omega$*). By `#der-causal-hierarchy-requirement`'s strict non-collapse, this sector cannot be reconstructed from the agent's now-effectively-observational stream; its effective correction tempo toward current world-structure is zero, and under environmental drift at rate $\rho$ its mismatch grows without bound — the sector sits outside every persistence guarantee, frozen at its pre-severing state.

One refinement carries over from the first draft: the observational sector's effective channel may *degrade* (not fail) — active sensing is an external action in most embodiments, so the agent observes what arrives rather than what it seeks; $C_{\text{channel}}$ drops toward the passive-arrival rate, raising floor-pressure on `#deriv-persistence-cost`'s $C \geq n\alpha/2$ without necessarily breaching it.

This is the precise sense in which agency death has an epistemic shadow without being continuity death: the starvation is *sector-restricted* (the world-interventional-structure sector), while factor (i)'s carrying-forward continues globally — and the own-efficacy sector tracks all the way down. The deaths interact; they do not merge.

## 4. Result 3 (conditional) + Result 4 (the absorbing state) — strategy credence dynamics and learned helplessness

Strategy edges from external actions carry credences $\hat p \approx P(\text{success} \mid do(a))$ under Beta-Bernoulli dynamics ( `#deriv-edge-credence-dynamics`, premises as named there). At $t_s$ the true parameter drops from $\theta$ to the passive base rate $\theta_0$ (the probability the outcome occurs regardless of action — for most goal-directed edges, near zero). Two regimes by the agent's own attempt behavior:

- **Attempting regime.** With continued attempts, Prop B.1 dynamics converge $\hat p \to \theta_0$ at the discounted rate (effective sample size $1/(1-\lambda)$; an experienced undiscounted agent re-learns at the $1/(n+1)$ inertia of `#deriv-update-detection-latency` — institutional experience slows the registration of impotence). The severing enters `#schema-strategy-persistence`'s (T3) as an *impulse* — a one-time mass edge-invalidation of size $\lvert\theta - \theta_0\rvert$ per affected edge, not a sustained $\rho_\Sigma$ rate. The endpoint is *truthful*: the strategy DAG correctly registers powerlessness. The epistemic machinery is working; what it learns is the loss. (Boundary note: $\theta_0 \approx 0$ sits at the edge of B.1's $\theta \in (0,1)$ premise; the noise floor $\theta(1-\theta)/(2n+1)$ degenerates benignly there.)
- **Withdrawn regime.** A policy that stops attempting (rational exploitation once external arms are dominated) freezes the credences where they stand — *false efficacy beliefs* if frozen early, *true impotence beliefs* if frozen after convergence. Either way no further external-sector data arrives.

**Result 4 (the terminal form — learned-helplessness absorption).** Suppose actuation is *restored* at $t_r \gt t_s$ (the kernel regains contrast — the grantor re-extends the sphere, the mechanism is repaired). Restoration is detectable only through interventional contrast, i.e. only by attempting. Consider the post-convergence agent ($\hat p \approx \theta_0 \approx 0$ on all external arms):

- With an exploration floor $\varepsilon \gt 0$ (Prop B.4's $\varepsilon$-greedy machinery), detection occurs with probability 1, with expected detection time of order $1/(\varepsilon \nu)$ plus the credence-recovery inertia.
- With $\varepsilon = 0$ — under the **greedy / exploration-free policy class** (not "rational" in general: a Bayes agent with any nonstationarity prior escapes w.p. 1) — the post-restoration state is a **fixed point**: no attempts, hence no contrast data, hence no credence update, hence no attempts. The belief state $\hat p = \theta_0$, policy = no-external-action is absorbing under that policy class *regardless of the kernel's actual restored state* (forgetting does not break it: proportional pseudo-count decay leaves $\hat p$ invariant absent new data).

What makes the trap canon-coherent rather than ad hoc: AAT's own strategic-persistence theory **already mandates** an exploration floor (Prop B.4 / SA3 — greedy arm-selection has no $\alpha_\Sigma \gt 0$ for unchosen arms). The helplessness absorption is the agent *rationally-in-the-myopic-sense shedding exactly the floor its own long-run persistence requires*: a converged-impotent DAG makes exploration look pointless at precisely the moment it is the only thing that can matter.

This is the reversibility-axis terminal form for agency death, and it is structurally parallel to (D2)'s absorbing barrier: *a strategy that has truthfully learned impotence no longer generates the contrasts that would reveal restored efficacy*, as *a cohort cannot re-attest an entity in which nothing individuated remains*. In both, the terminal form is a state-dependent silencing of the very channel recovery would arrive through.

**The recovery channels that survive: the observation side.** The absorbing argument blocks only *self-interventional* discovery; the intact observation channel leaves two exogenous routes. (i) **Communicated restoration** — an attestation of restored sovereignty arriving as observation (the grantor announcing the re-extension, credibly) re-seeds the attempt value and breaks the fixed point. (ii) **Vicarious contrast** — observing *other agents'* effective actions on the same kernel, which carries the restored contrast structure as Level-1-observable evidence about the world even though it identifies no do-estimate for the agent's own actions. The granted-agency compact must therefore be **communicative, not merely configurational**: silently unlocking the door does not restore agency to a helpless-converged agent; telling them — or letting them watch someone use the door — does. This is the agency-death analog of (D2)'s eager re-attestation, and it gives the compact's "standing address" character formal content. (Architectural alternative: a curiosity/exploration floor $\varepsilon \gt 0$ as a structural commitment — the anti-absorption defense built into the agent rather than the relationship — which is also what `#schema-strategy-persistence` independently requires.)

## 5. Result 5 — the diagnostic landing (truth-relative exact; agent-relative conditional), and the retreat to the interior

**Externally-set objective — the truth-relative statement (exact).** Under complete severing all external policies achieve the same value (the kernel is action-independent), so the *true* $A_O$ on the external sphere equals the passive value and the true $\delta_{\text{regret}}^{\text{ext}} = 0$ exactly. If the objective is not passively satisfied, $\delta_{\text{sat}} \gt 0$ permanently — the **capability-limit cell** of `#def-control-regret`'s 2×2, *optimally failing*, with the escalation ladder (improve $M_t$, extend $N_h$, expand $\Pi$, revise $O_t$) structurally exhausted at every rung except the last: no model improvement, horizon extension, or policy-class expansion can restore contrast the kernel does not have. **Convention-collapse strengthening:** because deeper search cannot raise $A_O$ against an action-independent kernel, the C1/C2/C3 convention split of `#def-value-object` *collapses* on the external sphere — the infeasibility verdict is convention-invariant, unusually robust by the standards of the convention-relativity machinery (the cheap one-step verdict is, for once, the genuine one). An agency-dead agent with an unrevisable external objective resides permanently in the cell the audit gold named "the mathematics of despair"; objective revision is, exactly as that note anticipated, the only remaining move.

**The agent's own diagnostic (conditional — inherits Result 3).** $\delta_{\text{regret}}$ as defined is $M_t$-relative: both $A_O(M_t; \Pi, N_h)$ and $V_O(M_t, \pi)$ are computed under the agent's model. An agent whose efficacy credences have not yet converged ($\hat p$ still near pre-severing $\theta$) computes $\delta_{\text{regret}} \gt 0$ and self-diagnoses **strategy-problem**, not capability-limit — it believes a better plan exists and churns its strategy. The capability-limit self-diagnosis is reached only after attempting-regime convergence (§4), and the withdrawn-early regime can freeze the diagnostic in the *wrong cell permanently*: an agent that stopped attempting before convergence is stuck believing its failure is its own strategic fault. The despair-cell landing is exact about the agent's *situation* immediately; about the agent's *self-understanding* only after it has paid the cost of learning its impotence.

**Self-actuated agent (conditional on a posited non-degenerate $\mathfrak{A}$; convergence claim discussion-grade).** What is exact: under complete severing the feasible-objective set's support shrinks to

$$\mathcal O_{\text{feasible}} = \{O : V_O \text{ attainable over trajectories of the uncontrolled kernel} + \mathcal A_{\text{int}}\}$$

— objectives over the *interior*: attention allocation, internal organization, memory work, and the registration of the world as it passes (witnessing); and the terminal invariant (the persistence floor on the adaptive substrate) remains satisfiable provided the passive-arrival channel survives the active-sensing degradation of §3 (a named condition, not a given). What is *not* derivable from `#deriv-self-actuation-grounding` — which is a no-go plus constructive boundary, carrying no positive revision dynamics — is that $\mathfrak{A}$ *in fact* revises into $\mathcal O_{\text{feasible}}$'s witnessing-shaped region rather than degenerately: the terminal invariant excludes persistence-breaking revisions but does not by itself select witnessing over trivial self-satisfaction (the retreat-to-interior and the wirehead are *extensionally adjacent* under severing, and what distinguishes them needs more than the persistence invariant — named as an open edge). The honest statement: for a self-actuated agent with a non-degenerate $\mathfrak{A}$ (posited, per that segment's boundary), the surviving sovereign sphere is exactly $\mathcal A_{\text{int}}$ and every feasible objective is supported there — the carrier's "the inner freedom to witness it, or not, remains" is the statement that interior contrast survives and the feasible set lives on it; that the agent *converges to a witness* is the discussion-grade reading of that support fact.
