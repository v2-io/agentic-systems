## Verdict (revision 2, after two independent verification passes)

**Clause 1 is strengthened-to the claim, conditionally (state A); the unconditional "guarantee" is a no-go.** By canon's definition (learning stops *inappropriately*), sustained undetected deception produces gain collapse, by two routes with different conditions:

- **Dogmatism.** A source treated as unbiased, a static world, no forgetting, and continuing lies. The calibration ratio grows linearly in repetitions, and isolation is not required.
- **Nihilism toward honest channels.** Liar-component or consensus-estimated trust, and a persistent deceiver holding incumbency plus a concurrent-voice plurality via isolation or Sybil voices (numerical, two rules). Isolation launders borrowed authority into apparent track record.

The counterexamples to "guarantee" are a changing world, forgetting, content-prior plausibility, concurrent independent dissent and robust aggregation. Exact correction after discovery requires provenance memory.

**Clause 2 is strengthened-to a reformulation (state A).** Technical deception strictly lowers a channel's capacity below its honest-error capacity, and non-deception is the unique maximizer (exact). Credibility collapse on discovery depends on formation. There are two small no-gos: "absolute" as never asserting any falsehood, and "not a virtue commitment", which is beyond what the math reaches. A young agent's structural vulnerability is few independent channels plus cheap identity, which makes *honest* formation the requirement.

**Open:** the closed-form escape condition for capture, and a latent-class (Dawid–Skene) version. History of the verdict: `de-novo-feedback-1-response.md`, `de-novo-feedback-2-response.md`.
