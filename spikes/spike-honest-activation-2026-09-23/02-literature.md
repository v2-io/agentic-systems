**What the literature does not already contain (as far as this scan reaches).** The individual mechanisms are known in economics and filtering: persuasion bias, correlation neglect, filter divergence, credibility, misspecified learning. What this scan did not find: (i) the unification of authority, repetition and isolation as one mechanism acting on a single second-order object, the source-bias prior, with a derived confidence floor; (ii) the attribution floor, and the log-odds reading of isolation versus corroboration; (iii) provenance as a necessary-and-sufficient condition for exact correction *after discovery*; (iv) capability-independence as a DPI statement about token reliability, which is closer to known than first listed (Douceur; Friedman & Resnick); (v) formation as supplying authenticated identity and discovered deceptions, which is close to Friedman & Resnick's newcomer result and to the developmental selective-trust literature. The original (v), a linear-pool "formation dilemma", was false and has been replaced (`03` R7). This is a search log, not a novelty claim. A targeted search (Undermind-style) would be owed before any novelty statement lands in canon.
