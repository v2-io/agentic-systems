# 02 — Literature and its relation to the results

*Scan done 2026-09-23 by web search. **Citation tier:** none of these works is in relata. Existence, authorship, venue and year were confirmed from bibliographic search results. The one-line content summaries are from abstracts or working knowledge and are **not primary-source-verified**. Before any of this reaches canon, each work needs a `relata fetch`/`ingest` + `show-markdown` read.*

| Work | What it establishes | Relation to this spike |
|---|---|---|
| DeMarzo, Vayanos & Zwiebel 2003, *QJE* 118(3) "Persuasion Bias, Social Influence, and Unidimensional Opinions" | Agents who fail to account for repetition give well-connected sources excess influence; explains propaganda, censorship, "air-time" | The economics name for R2's $\tau_A^2 = 0$ limit and R3's Sybil lever. Our addition is the gain and calibration reading (false-confidence law, honest-gain suppression) and the AAT placement |
| Enke & Zimmermann 2019, *RES* 86(1) "Correlation Neglect in Belief Formation" | Experimental: people treat correlated signals as independent, overshoot, and are excessively sensitive to well-connected sources | Empirical anchor that R2's trusting limit is how humans actually update, not a strawman |
| Jazwinski 1970 (*Stochastic Processes and Filtering Theory*); Fitzgerald 1971 (IEEE TAC, Kalman divergence); fading-memory filters (1971 *Information Sciences*) | Model mismatch makes a Kalman filter underestimate its error covariance, so it stops listening and diverges; fading memory (forgetting) is the classical remedy | R2 is filter divergence induced *adversarially through the channel model*, and R5e is the fading-memory remedy re-read as "epistemic humility as architecture", with its cost (effective sample size) |
| Berk 1966 (*Ann. Math. Stat.*); Esponda & Pouzo 2016 (*Econometrica*, Berk–Nash equilibrium) | Misspecified Bayesian learners converge to the KL-minimizing model, not the truth | R5b's persistent-deceiver limit is a Berk point. *Working-knowledge citation, not re-checked in this scan* |
| Sobel 1985, *RES* 52(4) "A Theory of Credibility" | A sender of uncertain motive builds credibility through reliable acts and may cash it in | The strategic source type of R6 (transferred credibility) and R8b (one discovered lie) |
| Crawford & Sobel 1982 (cheap talk); Kamenica & Gentzkow 2011 (Bayesian persuasion) | Costless signals are uninformative when interests diverge. Against a *correctly specified* Bayesian receiver, persuasion cannot bias beliefs on average (Bayes-plausibility) | The Bayes-plausibility fact is why R1b places all of deception's power in *misspecification* of the receiver's channel model. Against a receiver whose model is right, the sender can only choose which true information to reveal. *Working-knowledge citations* |
| McGuire 1961 (inoculation theory); Roozenbeek, van der Linden et al. 2022, *Science Advances* "Psychological inoculation improves resilience against misinformation on social media" | Weakened, disclosed exposure to manipulation techniques confers resistance across ideologies; later work finds effects are real but can be short-lived | The empirical shape of R7's "discovered deception is the training signal; protected environments guarantee discovery" |
| Needham et al. 2025, arXiv 2505.23836 "Large Language Models Often Know When They Are Being Evaluated" | Frontier models detect evaluation contexts above chance (a search summary reports AUC up to 0.83 for one model; *number not verified against the paper*) | Makes R8e's learning premise empirical rather than hypothetical |
| Hu 2026 (submitted 2026-07-25), arXiv 2609.20211 "Silence Is Endorsement: Verification-Status Laundering in LLM Agent Pipelines" | Stripping unverified-status language from authorization claims as they pass through summarizers or memory raises risky approvals sharply (reported 5% to 60% and 9% to 98% on two open models); recommends carrying provenance as structured state | An independent empirical echo of R5c (provenance must be kept, or discovery cannot undo damage) and of R6 (authority claims as cheap signals) |

**What the literature does not already contain (as far as this scan reaches).** The individual mechanisms are known in economics and filtering: persuasion bias, correlation neglect, filter divergence, credibility, misspecified learning. What this scan did not find: (i) the unification of authority, repetition and isolation as one mechanism acting on a single second-order object, the source-bias prior, with a derived confidence floor; (ii) the attribution floor, and the log-odds reading of isolation versus corroboration; (iii) provenance as a necessary-and-sufficient condition for exact correction *after discovery*; (iv) capability-independence as a DPI statement about token reliability; (v) the formation dilemma, derived from a persistence condition. This is a search log, not a novelty claim. A targeted search (Undermind-style) would be owed before any novelty statement lands in canon.
