**Consequence for the task.** There is no Φ research output to mine. The literal request ("download their papers, see what their research teaches AAT") rests on a premise that is not yet true. The honest value-direction is therefore *reversed*: AAT is a theoretical lens for the problem-class Φ has correctly identified as neglected; Φ is not (yet) an input of results to AAT. The Φ proposal's own one-line thesis is the load-bearing prompt: *a world model whose predictions must remain useful when an opponent is actively shaping the dynamics.* That is the object the two threads below interrogate.
