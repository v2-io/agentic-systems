### 2026-05-30 Objective-functional revealed-preference defense — strengthen-first verdict (B): scoped representation result

The same deeply-mathematical de-novo auditor (AUDIT-WORKING-526815) challenged the **revealed-preference defense** in `#form-objective-functional`: the segment grounds the real-valued codomain $V_{O_t}: \text{trajectories} \to \mathbb{R}$ as "follows from the comparability requirement," via "an agent that acts has implicitly scalarized." The challenge: a *local* choice relation is not a *total order over all trajectories*, and recovering a representable real-valued functional needs named axioms.
