### 1. The developer's $\mathcal T_{\text{probe}}$ channel — prototypes and tracer bullets as Level-2 interventional probes with characterized $(\nu_{\text{probe}}, U_{o,\text{probe}})$ profiles

**Class:** A
**AAT-relevance:** Directly fills the developer tempo channel decomposition gap flagged in `02-tst-core/OUTLINE.md` Ch.2 ($\mathcal T_{\text{dev}} = \mathcal T_{\text{obs}} + \mathcal T_{\text{explore}} + \mathcal T_{\text{probe}}$); extends `#hyp-causal-discovery-from-git`'s "tests as reusable Level-2 probes" framing; uses AAT's `#def-causal-information-yield` per-probe-class.

**The content (briefly).** Pragmatic Programmer's most rigorous decomposition isn't tracer-vs-prototype as practitioner advice; it's a *typology of distinct probe classes with characterizable cost/yield profiles*. Tracer bullets are persistent-skeleton probes ($S_i \subseteq S_{i+1}$ monotone-growth, production-quality, defer scope not quality); prototypes are disposable structural probes ($t_{\text{convert}} \gt t_{\text{reimplement}}$ as the disposability invariant, defer correctness/completeness/robustness/style to maximize information yield per implementation cost); end-to-end skeletons are integration-uncertainty probes; performance prototypes are stress-response probes; UI prototypes are user-mental-model probes; throwaway architecture spikes are constraint-discovery probes. Each class has its own (effort, information-yield, target-uncertainty) signature. The 005 analysis explicitly models tracer bullets as a control-theoretic feedback tracker on a moving target $T(t)$ with $X(t+1) = X(t) + \alpha \nabla \text{feedback}(t)$ and convergence under $\alpha \lt 2/\lambda_{\max}(H)$; the 013 analysis gives the prototype-vs-tracer decision boundary as $n_{\text{expected}} \lt t_{\text{tracer}}/t_p$ where $n_{\text{expected}}$ is expected iterations on the component.

**Translation into AAT/TST.** This grounds the $\mathcal T_{\text{probe}}$ channel concretely. In AAT vocabulary, the developer's probe channel decomposes by probe-class $k \in \{\text{tracer}, \text{prototype}, \text{e2e}, \text{perf}, \text{ui}, \text{spike}\}$, each with its own $(\nu^{(k)}, \eta^{(k)\ast})$ — sampling rate and Bayesian gain on the targeted facet of $M_t$ (resp. the strategy DAG $\Sigma_t$, since architecture prototypes are interventions on $\Sigma_t$ not $M_t$). The matrix-Loewner weakest-channel bottleneck then applies: a developer with strong tracer-channel tempo but absent prototype-channel coverage has a rank-deficient $\mathcal T$ relative to environments requiring constraint-discovery. Disposability ($t_{\text{convert}} \gt t_{\text{reimplement}}$) is recognizable as a *purity guarantee* on the probe: a disposable probe is one whose state cannot leak back into the production substate. This is structurally identical to AAT's W₁/W₂ leakage discipline applied to developer-internal probing — disposable prototypes are W₁ (no structural leakage by construction: nothing built atop them); tracer bullets are W₂ (behavioral leakage bound: the skeleton is reused, leakage rate from "prototype habits" into production code bounded by review discipline). Likely lands as a new segment `#def-probe-channel-decomposition` under Ch.2, with the per-probe-class table as the operational content and the W₁/W₂ analogy in Discussion. The five-probe-class table is also the most concrete operationalization yet of where the various $\eta^{(k)\ast}$ live in software development practice.

**Honesty.** The probe-class typology is genuinely AAT-novel content — Pragmatic Programmer names the practices distinctly but does not analyze them as channels with quantified $(\nu, U_o)$ signatures, and TST does not yet have this. The decision boundary $n_{\text{expected}} \lt t_{\text{tracer}}/t_p$ in 013 is a clean partial-rationality argument but the constants are gestural; landing this needs the AAT-internal version where the decision is between "invest in skeleton with low leakage rate" vs. "invest in disposable probe with maximum information yield," and the boundary follows from comparing posterior-variance-reduction-per-unit-time across the two regimes. The W₁/W₂ analogy is the load-bearing translation move and needs to be checked carefully — disposable purity is structurally simpler than goal-blindness, and conflating them would be a category error.

### 2. The composite developer-agent under AI augmentation — supervision/wrapping as a Class 1 (Separated) coercion of Class 3 (Coupled) AI components

**Source analyses:** [437-supervision-tree-design.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/437-supervision-tree-design---fault-tolerant-process-hierarchies-in-actor-model-systems.md), [069-actors-processes.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/069-actors-processes.md), [068-shared-state-incorrect.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/068-shared-state-incorrect.md), [159-dead-programs.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/159-dead-programs.md), [433-error-recovery-vs-failure.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/433-error-recovery-vs-failure---when-to-recover-vs-when-to-fail-and-crash-early-philosophy.md), [435-chain-of-command.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/435-chain-of-command---organizing-responsibility-chains-and-dependency-injection-patterns.md), [065-design-by-contract.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/065-design-by-contract.md)
**Class:** A
**AAT-relevance:** Class-coercion via wrapping (AAT `#der-class-coercion-via-wrapping`, `#der-logogenic-as-wrapping`); supervision/actor-isolation as textbook W₁/W₂ instantiations; Joseph's named gap on the composite developer-agent.

**The content (briefly).** The actors-and-supervision-tree literature in this corpus encodes a *constructive recipe for Class-1 composite agents from Class-3 components*, and it is unusually clean because the implementation actually exists (Erlang/OTP). The 437 analysis explicitly models supervision trees as converting *exponential* cascade-failure risk ($P_{\text{cascade}}^{\text{traditional}} = 1 - (1-p_e)^{n_{\text{deps}}}$) to *linear* recovery cost ($P_{\text{cascade}}^{\text{sup}} = p_e \cdot (1 - p_{\text{restart}})^{\max\_\text{restarts}}$). The 069 analysis derives a similar conversion for actor isolation: shared-state debugging time scales $2^{s/3}$ in shared-state-points; actor debugging scales as $1 + 0.1n$ in actor count. The 159 analysis ("Dead Programs Tell No Lies") gives the operational principle: crash-at-detected-invariant-violation is the *structural commitment* that keeps the wrapper Class 1 — recovering from invariant violations *inside* a worker would let goal-state leak back into the worker's belief-update query (the Class 3 failure mode). The 065 design-by-contract analysis gives the *boundary condition*: contracts at module boundaries are structural-commitment artifacts (preconditions = goal-blind-query specification, postconditions = goal-blind-output specification).

**Translation into AAT/TST.** The composite developer-agent (developer + AI assistant + supervision wrapper + contract-enforced boundaries + crash-early discipline + test/CI infrastructure as auxiliary checking) is *textbook* class-coercion-via-wrapping. The AI assistant is a Class 3 (Coupled) component — its belief-update query is irreducibly goal-conditioned (the prompt is the goal). The composite is Class 1 (Separated) when (a) the AI's outputs flow into typed/parsed structures (structural commitment), (b) those structures are then validated against goal-blind invariants (the developer's tests, type-checks, contracts), (c) invariant violations crash early rather than being silently absorbed, (d) the wrapping supervisor (the developer + CI pipeline) restarts the AI on failure with bounded retries (the supervision strategy). This is W₂ in practice for most current AI-augmented workflows (output-structuring + behavioral leakage bound through review), with W₁ accessible when the AI is constrained to typed completion with rejection-on-parse-failure. Likely lands as a new chapter or chapter-extension in `02-tst-core/` titled something like "The Composite Developer Agent," with the OTP analogy front-loaded and the W₀/W₁/W₂ regime hierarchy applied directly to AI-augmented development workflows. The 437/069/068/159 cluster is the natural worked example. The chapter probably belongs after the current Ch.2 (Developer Agent), as Ch.2 extended.

**Honesty.** This is among the highest-leverage findings in the slice — Joseph specifically named this gap, AAT has the machinery (`#der-class-coercion-via-wrapping`), and the OTP literature gives a fully-worked engineered example of the construction. The work to land it: (1) the W₀/W₁/W₂ analogy needs careful application — the wrapping construction in AAT was developed for LLM-as-Class-3-component contexts, and the OTP supervision case is structurally cleaner (the worker truly *cannot* leak because it cannot access its supervisor's state). Whether OTP supervision is W₁-exact or merely W₁-in-the-limit needs verification against the AAT segment. (2) The crash-early / let-it-crash discipline as *structural commitment to goal-blindness* is a substantive claim that needs the AAT-internal derivation: why does recovering-from-invariant-violations leak goal-state? Likely because the recovery code itself is goal-conditioned (it knows what the higher-level operation was trying to do), so absorbing failures inside the worker re-introduces the coupling that the wrapping was meant to break. (3) The contract/precondition story (065) is the most direct W₁ instantiation — preconditions specify the *query* and postconditions specify the *output structure*, both goal-blind. This is genuine new TST content, not a restatement of what's in `#scope-developer-agent`.

### 3. Software persistence and the unmaintainability threshold — folk anchors for the $Q \to U_o \to \eta^\ast \to \mathcal T$ bifurcation hypothesis

**Source analyses:** [004-broken-windows.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/004-broken-windows.md), [416-software-entropy-and-decay.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/416-software-entropy-and-decay---technical-debt-and-rot-progression-beyond-broken-windows.md), [417-contagious-quality-standards.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/417-contagious-quality-standards---how-quality-spreads-both-good-and-bad-within-teams-and-codebases.md), [419-gradual-change-awareness.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/419-gradual-change-awareness---detecting-slowly-changing-conditions-like-the-boiled-frog.md), [007-stone-soup-boiled-frogs.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/007-stone-soup-boiled-frogs.md), [461-preventive-vs-reactive-maintenance.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/461-preventive-vs-reactive-maintenance---boarding-up-broken-windows-approach.md), [460-project-psychology-and-culture.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/460-project-psychology-and-culture---team-mindset-impact-on-code-quality.md)
**Class:** A (anchors for) / D (empirical anchors)
**AAT-relevance:** Directly anchors the OUTLINE Ch.4 GAP "Software persistence: the unmaintainability threshold formalized"; strengthens `#der-code-quality-as-observation-infrastructure` ($Q \to U_o \to \eta^\ast \to \mathcal T$).

**The content (briefly).** The broken-windows / software-entropy cluster gives a *bifurcation-shaped* dynamics for the rate of defect accumulation: $dW/dt = b_0 + \alpha \lvert W \rvert - (r_0 - \beta \lvert W \rvert)$ with critical point $W^\ast = (r_0 - b_0)/(\alpha + \beta)$, where below $W^\ast$ the system self-repairs and above it the system enters self-accelerating decay. The 004 analysis cites a 2024 empirical study (29 developers) finding $\alpha \approx 0.31$ (31% increase in new TD per existing window) — *empirical anchor for the bifurcation hypothesis*. The 416 analysis composes this with a psychological-health decay $\psi(t) = \psi_0 e^{-\lambda d(t)}$ and a feature-implementation-time blowup $t_f(d) = t_{f0}(1 + d/d_{\text{critical}})^2$ below threshold transitioning to $t_f(d) = t_{f0} e^{\kappa(d - d_{\text{critical}})}$ above. The 419 ("boiled frog") analysis points at the *detectability* problem: gradual drift accumulates below the human-perception threshold, so the bifurcation is approached from below without observable warning.

**Translation into AAT/TST.** This is the empirical/folk anchor cluster for the segment named in OUTLINE Ch.4 as the unmaintainability threshold. In AAT, the chain is: defect accumulation $\lvert W \rvert$ degrades code quality $Q$, which degrades observation quality (raises $U_o$, which the existing TST segment names), which lowers Bayesian gain $\eta^\ast = U_M/(U_M + U_o)$, which lowers adaptive tempo $\mathcal T$. The persistence condition $\mathcal T \gt \rho / \lVert \delta_{\text{critical}} \rVert$ then *fails* once $\mathcal T$ drops below the disturbance-rate floor — the codebase is *unmaintainable* in the precise sense that the developer's adaptive tempo cannot keep up with the environmental disturbance rate (requirements change, dependency churn, user feedback). The bifurcation in $dW/dt$ then translates into a bifurcation in persistence: the codebase is in the basin of attraction of "maintainable" if and only if $W \lt W^\ast$ at all times. This naturally lands as a new segment under Ch.4: `#hyp-unmaintainability-bifurcation` or similar, with the chain $Q \to U_o \to \eta^\ast \to \mathcal T \to \text{persistence-violation}$ composed forward, and the broken-windows literature as the empirical anchor. The 2024 $\alpha \approx 0.31$ empirical is a Class D anchor — useful but should be marked clearly as analysis-claim with primary-source citation needed.

**Honesty.** The bifurcation shape is principled and the chain to AAT machinery is clean. What needs real work to land: (1) The $\alpha \approx 0.31$ empirical citation in 004 says "2024 research" without a paper; this needs to be tracked down to the primary source or reframed as "anchor-grade evidence pending citation" — generative agents invent citation details is the standing risk here, and the 004 analysis is an LLM-authored secondary source. (2) The bifurcation is currently named in TST as a hypothesis (`#der-code-quality-as-observation-infrastructure` Discussion); making it the unmaintainability-threshold segment requires the bifurcation to be derived under stated conditions (where does $\alpha + \beta \gt 0$ guarantee an unstable equilibrium?) — strengthen-before-soften applies. The strengthening is plausible because the bifurcation is generic to logistic-with-contagion dynamics. (3) The "boiled frog" detectability problem (419) is actually a separate AAT phenomenon: it is *agent opacity $H_b$ of the codebase's own decay process from the developer's observational point of view* — the developer's observation channel has higher noise floor than the rate of drift. This connects to AAT's signed-coupling four-regime classification (gradual decay below detection threshold is "ambient-noise" regime relative to the developer's update channel). Worth its own subsegment.

### 5. Conway's Law as a directed-separation diagnostic — the team communication graph imposes its structure on the system architecture

**Source analyses:** [117-working-together.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/117-working-together.md), [448-mob-programming.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/448-mob-programming.md), [450-team-communication.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/450-team-communication.md), [486-team-orthogonality.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/486-team-orthogonality---reducing-interpersonal-dependencies.md), [075-pragmatic-teams.md](file:///Users/josephwecker-v2/src/_core/tst/planning/analysis/075-pragmatic-teams.md)
**Class:** A (composite-agent / multi-agent gap) / C (candidate new segment)
**AAT-relevance:** Composition machinery, shared intent, Auftragstaktik bandwidth allocation, GUC-class composition; directly applies to multi-developer-agent composites.

**The content (briefly).** Conway's Law: the team communication graph $T = (V_{\text{people}}, E_{\text{communication}})$ becomes isomorphic to the system architecture graph $S = (V_{\text{modules}}, E_{\text{dependencies}})$ over time — $\text{Distance}(S, T) \to 0$ as project age increases. The 117 analysis derives this from $\text{team-coupling} = \lvert E_T \rvert / \lvert V_T \rvert^2 \approx \lvert E_S \rvert / \lvert V_S \rvert^2 = \text{system-coupling}$. The 486 analysis ("team orthogonality") gives the implication: minimizing inter-team coupling reduces system coupling structurally, with team-org-coordination cost as the dominant determinant of system-architecture coupling. Mob and pair programming (117, 448) operationalize the collective-intelligence formalization with diversity bonuses and shared comprehension effects.

**Translation into AAT/TST.** Conway's Law is, in AAT vocabulary, the empirical claim that *the system's GUC class is upper-bounded by the team's GUC class*: a team with high inter-member coupling (everyone needs to coordinate with everyone) produces a system with high inter-module coupling — the team operates as a Class 3-like (Coupled) composite agent, and the system inherits the structural coupling. A team of agents with directed-separation-by-construction in their communication graph (clear interfaces, decoupled responsibility regions) is operationally a Class 1 (Separated) composite, and produces a Class-1-shaped system. This is the multi-agent generalization of TST's `#scope-developer-agent` (which currently treats the developer monolithically), and connects directly to AAT's composition machinery (shared intent as IB-compressed purpose, Auftragstaktik bandwidth allocation, communication gain) applied to the dev-team composite. Likely lands as a new segment in the "Composite Developer Agent" chapter (paired with finding #2): `#hyp-conway-law-as-guc-bound` or `#der-team-coupling-bounds-system-coupling`, with the AAT composition machinery (shared intent + Auftragstaktik + signed coupling) as the formal substrate.

**Honesty.** Conway's Law is a 1968 empirical/normative observation, widely cited but not formally derived from anything; it has the status of a robust qualitative regularity. Landing it as derived in AAT requires showing why team-graph structure imposes itself on system-graph structure — likely via the developer-agent's $\Sigma_t$ (strategy DAG): each developer's individual $\Sigma_t$ has rich decomposition along communication paths the developer actually has access to, and is impoverished along communication paths the developer does not have; the system architecture emerges as the union of these per-developer-decomposable strategies, hence reflects the team communication graph. This is plausible-but-not-yet-derived. The strengthening attempt should be made first (per Joseph's working convention).
