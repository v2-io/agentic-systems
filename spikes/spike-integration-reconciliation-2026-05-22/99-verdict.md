## §1. R-letter naming reconciliation (the executor's first concern)

**The collision is apparent, not real.** Verified by reading both canonical segments in current state.

`#result-certificate-existence` lines 49–55 — the certificate-strength ladder:

| Rung | Condition | Equivalent to | Certificate is |
|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | one-point (C), some $\mathcal M$, local | $A = -J$ Hurwitz + remainder dominated | converse-Lyapunov $\mathcal M$ |
| R1 | incremental (two-point) $\mathcal M$-strong-monotonicity on $\mathcal B_R$ | global $\mathcal M$-strong-monotone (cocoercive class) | curvature-like $\mathcal M$ (potential sub-case) |
| R2 | R1 with $\mathcal M$ forced by uniqueness theorem | natural-gradient in the Čencov-unique Fisher metric | Fisher metric (Čencov-forced) |

This is a **single-agent linearized certificate-strength** ladder. The object classified is *the agent's stability certificate* $\mathcal M$ on $\mathbb R^n$. The progression R0 → R1 → R2 is about how *strong* a property the certificate has (just exists / cocoercive / Čencov-forced). Each rung is a strict refinement of the previous; R0 is the *widest* rung.

`#disc-dynamic-regime-axis` lines 33–39 — the dynamic-regime axis:

| Tier | Fixed-point structure | Lyapunov certificate | Macro-state type | Convergence rate | Scope route entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R0 Contraction** | Unique attracting $X^\ast \in \mathcal X^c$ | Joint Lyapunov $V$, global or basin-restricted | State-variable $X^\ast$ | Exponential at $\alpha_{\text{joint}}$ | C-i / C-ii / C-iii |
| **R1 Equilibrium ($\alpha'$)** | Fixed-point set $\mathcal E \subset \mathcal X^c$, locally attracting | Potential $\Phi$ or weighted-norm | Fixed-point object $\mathcal E$ | Exponential near $\mathcal E$ | C-iv + potential/monotone |
| **R2 Cyclic-distributional ($\beta'$)** | No pure-strategy fixed point, or saddle-only | None on $\mathcal X^c$; doubling-trick CCE | Distributional object $\mu \in \Delta(\mathcal X^c)$ | Polynomial $O(1/\sqrt T)$ | C-iv without potential/monotone |
| **R3 Mean-field equilibrium** | Distributional fixed point in measure space | MFG-specific | Distribution-on-population $\rho \in \mathcal P(\mathcal A)$ | MFG-specific | Population-scope gap |

This is a **composite-joint-dynamics regime** classifier. The object classified is *the type of fixed-point set of the joint best-response field $F$ for a composite* with sub-agents $i \in \{1, \ldots, N\}$. The progression R0 → R1 → R2 → R3 is about how the joint-dynamics object weakens (unique attractor → equilibrium set → distributional / cyclic → measure-space mean-field). The Lyapunov certificates *available* at each tier weaken correspondingly, but the tier-classification is the structural property of the game, not the certificate.

**Two different objects, layered.** The single-agent ladder lives on $\mathbb R^n$ (one agent's error space); the composite axis lives on $\mathcal X^c = \prod_i \mathcal X^{(i)}$ (joint strategy/state space). The letter overlap is real but unfortunate; it is *not* a content collision. Worked example: a composite at dynamic-regime R0 with two contracting sub-agents has each sub-agent at single-agent R0 (or R1, or R2) *individually*; the composite-level R0 means "the joint dynamics contracts to a shared target," which is structurally distinct from "each sub-agent's individual certificate is at the R0 rung."

**The §1 cluster's "R0-loss" is a refinement of `#result-certificate-existence`'s R0 row.** It splits Case (i) Hurwitz (R0-strict, the current R0 contents) from Case (iii) imaginary-axis-semisimple ($\mathcal M \succ 0$ exists with $\kappa = 0$, the new R0-loss content) from Cases (ii)+(iv) no-certificate (outside the ladder). The split happens *within the R0 row of the single-agent ladder*; it does not propagate up to R1 or R2 (which are stronger conditions implying Hurwitz).

**Compositional bridge between the two layers — the substantive relationship.** A composite at dynamic-regime R2 (saddle-only Nash; e.g., matching pennies under best-response) has its linearization-at-Nash with imaginary-axis spectrum. The single-agent §1 R0-loss is the *linearization-spectrum fingerprint* of dynamic-regime R2's saddle-Nash equilibria. This is exactly what the §1 verdict's "FTRL × graphical-constant-sum-with-fully-mixed-Nash is a worked R0-loss instance" claim says (verdict line 33), with the relationship being: the joint Jacobian $J_{\text{eff}}$ at the fully-mixed Nash has imaginary-axis-semisimple spectrum, which is the §1 R0-loss linearization, which is consistent with the dynamic-regime axis's R2 reading at the linearization level.

**This bridge is genuinely informative and worth surfacing in canon** — it tells the reader of `#result-certificate-existence` that R0-loss at the single-agent level is not exotic; it is the linearization-fingerprint of strategic composites in dynamic-regime R2. And it tells the reader of `#disc-dynamic-regime-axis` that R2's saddle-Nash equilibria have a *single-agent linearized* characterization via the antisymmetric-Jacobian / Helmholtz-$S/A$ split.

**Naming recommendation.** Do not rename either layer's R-letters. Add explicit cross-references at the moment of definition in each segment that name the bridge. The §1 spike's "R0-loss" already reads as "R0-loss" in the single-agent ladder by §1 cluster vocabulary; making it `R0-loss` in `#result-certificate-existence` keeps the §1 spike trail interpretable. The `#disc-dynamic-regime-axis` segment's R0/R1/R2/R3 is on a different layer; a one-sentence Discussion note in each segment naming the cross-layer bridge resolves any reader confusion. This is the §6 of the executable plan.
