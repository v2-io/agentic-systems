**Findings Brief at Feynman-criterion.** Draft attempt:

> *Composites come in four tiers by what kind of attractor their joint dynamics admit. Aligned teams have a single shared target they all want to reach — like rowers in a boat synchronizing. Strategic interactions where everyone gets something out of cooperation have an equilibrium structure — like firms in a market each pricing optimally given others' prices, settling at a stable joint configuration. Strategic interactions without that structure cycle — rock-paper-scissors players never settle on one move. The tier the composite is in determines what kind of guarantees you can get — exponential convergence in the first two tiers, only polynomial in the third, and special machinery in the fourth. The tier is a structural property of the composite — you can read it off the game definition without running the dynamics — and what strategic composition changes (against aligned composition) is exactly this tier, not the composite's processing architecture.*

## §M.2 Standard 2 — Meta-segment status

### Criteria

Per `#disc-separability-pattern` and `#disc-identifiability-floor` precedent, a meta-segment lands when:

- The pattern recurs across multiple instances within AAT (the seven-ladder shape of `#disc-separability-pattern`; the four-instance shape of `#disc-identifiability-floor`).
- The pattern has explicit complementarity with at least one other meta-segment.
- The pattern is load-bearing for downstream segments — surfacing it consolidates content otherwise scattered.
- The pattern has standalone-paper potential (per Joseph's framing of meta-segments as candidate cross-domain contributions).

### Present state

**Pattern recurrence.** The dynamic-regime axis is currently a single pattern (the four-tier classification) operating on one axis (composite joint-dynamics). It does not have the seven-instance recurrence of `#disc-separability-pattern` or the four-instance recurrence of `#disc-identifiability-floor`. *However*, §G's cross-axis observation surfaces a different kind of recurrence: the dynamic-regime axis has the same three-operation modularity-dynamics shape as the queued `#disc-modularity-state-dynamics` (alignment-strengthening / strategic-divergence-acquisition / adversarial-divergence-pressure parallel to truthification / strategic-self-coupling / adversarial-coupling-pressure). The parallel-pattern shape is itself a finding — the framework has *two* axes (architectural class via Axis A, dynamic regime via Axis B) that both admit three-operation modularity-like dynamics. This is closer to the kind of cross-pattern observation that warrants meta-segment status.

**Complementarity.** §H.5 sketches a candidate identifiability-floor Instance 6 (regime-identification from component marginals). If derived, this provides the negative-half complementarity that `#disc-separability-pattern` has with `#disc-identifiability-floor`. **Load-bearing for downstream.** The axis is load-bearing for: `#impl-strategic-composition` (the modular-safety-fails implication is mechanism-axis-clean only via the dynamic-regime reading); `03-llm-core/` (the wrapping construction's regime-independence is operationally critical); `04-eli-core/` (population-scope work depends on the R3 frontier formalization); the mechanism-design impossibility candidate (`#deriv-strategic-composition` Discussion) is regime-axis-shaped. **Standalone-paper potential.** Speculatively positive. The four-tier classification of multi-agent dynamics (contraction / equilibrium / cyclic-distributional / mean-field) is recognizable as a unifying construct across control theory (contraction via Lohmiller-Slotine), game theory (equilibrium via Monderer-Shapley + Rosen + Hart-Mas-Colell), and population dynamics (MFG via Lasry-Lions + Huang-Malhamé-Caines). Whether this is recognizable in the prior-art landscape requires the Pillar-style search — neighbors include the dynamical-systems-regime literature, the population-games literature, and the multi-agent-learning convergence literature.

## §M.3 Standard 3 — Standalone-paper candidacy

### Criteria

Per `#disc-separability-pattern`'s Working Notes (B-N-Sep entry), a meta-pattern qualifies for standalone paper when:

- The cross-domain recurrence is genuine — the pattern appears in multiple academic literatures with different vocabulary, and AAT's contribution is the unifying construct.
- The novelty has been verified by independent prior-art search (Pillar standard).
- The pattern is publishable as a contribution to the cross-domain literatures rather than just to AAT.

### Present state

**Cross-domain recurrence — plausible but not investigated.** The four-tier dynamic-regime classification appears (under different vocabulary) in:

- *Control theory:* contraction analysis (Lohmiller-Slotine 1998), passivity theorems (Khalil 2002), Lyapunov classification of stability regimes (LaSalle, Krasovskii).
- *Game theory:* potential games (Monderer-Shapley 1996), variational inequalities (Facchinei-Pang 2003), coarse correlated equilibria (Hart-Mas-Colell 2000), saddle-Nash + cyclic-Nash classification (Rosen 1965 + later).
- *Multi-agent learning:* convergent vs cyclic learning dynamics (Bowling-Veloso 2002 *AI*), no-regret learning convergence taxonomy (Cesa-Bianchi-Lugosi 2006 *Prediction, Learning, and Games*), multi-agent RL convergence regimes (Yang-Wang 2020 survey).
- *Evolutionary game theory:* replicator-dynamics regime classification (Hofbauer-Sigmund 1998, Sandholm 2010), evolutionarily stable strategies vs cyclic dynamics.
- *Mean-field games:* Lasry-Lions 2007, Huang-Malhamé-Caines 2006, Carmona-Delarue 2018 textbook (the R3 tier substrate).

The pattern is recognizable across all five fields under different names. Whether the *four-tier unification* (or some isomorphic structure) has been done is the prior-art question.

**Novelty verification.** Not done. Pillar-style search is required before publishability can be assessed. The Pillar protocol would target: (a) the four-tier classification itself (is the contraction / equilibrium / cyclic-distributional / mean-field partition recognized as one classification anywhere?); (b) the macro-state-type identity (§C.1 — is the regime / macro-state-type structural identity a known result?); (c) the transition asymmetry no-go (§C.3 — is the ascent-cost asymmetry derived anywhere?); (d) the regime-identification-from-marginals floor (§H.5 — adjacent to Liberzon 2003's coupling-sign-bit result).

**Publishability assessment.** Speculatively positive *if* prior-art search returns "wholly novel" or "substantial novelty with adjacent literature" on the four-tier consolidation. Speculatively negative if the four-tier structure is recognized in any of the five adjacent literatures — in which case AAT's contribution would be the cross-domain integration vocabulary rather than the classification itself, which is a weaker pitch.

### Verdict for Standard 3

**Standalone-paper candidacy is premature without the prior-art search.** The Pillar-style search is non-discretionary for this standard; until it returns, the candidacy is unassessed.

**Recommendation:** **defer Standard 3 assessment** pending Pillar search. The intermediate landing at Standard 1 (segment) does not commit to or against the standalone-paper trajectory.
