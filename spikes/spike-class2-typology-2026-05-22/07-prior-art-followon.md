## 7.2 The specific sub-spike scope (if launched)

A Pillar-style search would ask:

1. **Does any prior literature decompose the goal→belief-update coupling into a *stage* axis?** (Pre-input attention vs likelihood-weighting vs aggregation-gain vs storage-decision.)

2. **Does any prior literature distinguish *additive bias* from *non-separable entanglement* in goal-coupled inference?** (The content vs process distinction.)

3. **Does any prior literature derive the *source asymmetry* — that strategy-coupling produces self-stabilizing attractors but objective-coupling does not?** (Result 4.)

4. **Does any prior literature provide a *wrapping*-style coercion construction with a *form*-dependent regime selection?** (Result 6.)

The likely answers, based on intuition: the stage axis is new in this specific form; the content/process distinction has analogs scattered across literatures (most explicitly in Bénabou-Tirole and dual-process); the source asymmetry result is probably partially in the sunk-cost literature implicitly but not stated as a general structural theorem; the wrapping correspondence is novel.

If the typology is promoted, the prior-art-search should at minimum confirm:

- The stage decomposition is not directly anticipated by active-inference or motivated-reasoning frameworks.
- The form distinction is not directly anticipated by Kruglanski or Bénabou-Tirole as a wrapping-relevant property.
- The source asymmetry result is not a known theorem in the dynamics-of-belief literature.

Honest verdict on a Pillar verdict-likely: probably *Wholly Novel* with Medium confidence on the integrated typology; *Substantial Overlap* on individual axes (each axis has analogs); *Wholly Novel* on the source-asymmetry result as a derived structural theorem.

## 7.3 Why not run the sub-spike now

Two reasons:

1. **The spike's value is the structural carve, not the prior-art coverage.** Promotion is a separate decision (per `INTEGRATION.md`) that Joseph should make based on whether the typology is worth the cost. If yes, then a Pillar search is warranted. If no, the search is wasted work.

2. **The candidate literatures span psychology, economics, philosophy, neuroscience, and AI — a comprehensive Pillar search is non-trivial work** (cf. Bruineberg 2022 took the Markov-blanket field months to land). Running it speculatively without Joseph's promotion decision is inefficient.

The honest path: scope the sub-spike here, mark the typology's novelty claims as *intuition-only* until searched, and leave the search trigger to the promotion decision.
