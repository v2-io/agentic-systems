# GA-1 Asymmetry — Independent Verification (spike §4 push item 1)

*Deposited 2026-07-04 by a delegated verification agent (Fable substrate), briefed refute-posture: all mathematics below re-derived from the canon definitions (`#def-observation-function`, `#def-mismatch-signal`, `#result-mismatch-decomposition`, NOTATION.md GA-1 row) without assuming §4's conclusions. Closed forms checked by Monte Carlo ($N = 2{\times}10^6$ trials; script summary in §6). No canon files touched.*

## Verdict summary

- **Half 1 (transition-law ignorance is safely housed): TRUE**, under two hypotheses that were implicit and should be named — the observation channel $(h, \mathrm{law}(\varepsilon_t))$ carries no unknown persistent parameter, and $\varepsilon_t$ is *exogenous-fresh* (jointly independent of $\theta_T$, all past and current states, actions, agent randomization, and past noises). Under these, GA-1 is a *theorem* of the $\theta_T$-augmented model, not an added assumption, the three-term decomposition stays exact at every $t$, and $\theta_T$-uncertainty enters exclusively through term (ii) as an additive law-uncertainty component (law-of-total-variance split, §2). The Bayes floor does include optimal law-learning, exactly as claimed.
- **Half 2 (observation-law ignorance breaks GA-1): TRUE as scoped**, i.e., in the Bayesian description in which $\theta_h$ is a random object on the probability space and the conditioning object is state-only — which is the description §4 itself sets up ("marginalizing $\theta_h$ over its state-conditional prior") and the only description in which $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B}$ means "attainable by optimal learning." Verified constructively: a minimal conjugate model (§3) gives the GA-1 violation, the surviving cross-term, and the exactness failure in closed form; the labeled decomposition overstates $\mathbb E[\delta_t^2]$ by exactly $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1}) = \tau^2 - \sigma_{t-1}^2$ — *the amount of law-information the chronica has accumulated* — growing to the entire prior law-uncertainty $\tau^2$ as $t \to \infty$. The Bayes predictor does genuinely beat the "true conditional mean" $\bar o_t$ in mean-square past an explicit crossing time (§3.4). Placing $\theta_h$ inside the conditioning object restores exactness, verified by direct computation (§3.5).
- **Two sharpenings the spike should absorb** (§5): (a) GA-1-as-independence fails *more often* than the decomposition's exactness does — the decomposition uses only the mean-orthogonality corollary, so half 2 needs a nondegeneracy condition (chronica-correlated $\theta_h$-posterior-mean movement of the residual), and there is a genuine knife-edge (symmetric prior, zero-mean state, unknown *gain*) where exactness survives $\theta_h$-ignorance by symmetry; (b) under the frequentist fixed-$\theta_h^\ast$ reading the identity survives but the term labels lose their operational meaning — so the honest general statement is a two-horn no-go, which is *stronger* than §4 as written.
- **Strongest form of the finding** (§4 of this file): an observationally-equivalent model pair — identical joint law of the observation process, $\theta$ housed in the transition in one and in the observation law in the other — in which the decomposition is exact in the first and fails in the second. GA-1 relative to a state-only $\Omega$ is therefore **not determined by the system's observable behavior; it is a property of the chosen state description**. Without a rule for where persistent structure lives (the $\theta$-slot), GA-1 has no determinate content in the presence of law-uncertainty. This confirms and sharpens §4's closing claim.

## 1. Setup, and what exactness requires

Per canon: $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$; chronica $\mathcal C_{t-1}$ generated by past observations and actions (actions chronica-measurable up to agent randomization $u_{0:t-1}$); $\bar o_t = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}]$; $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}]$; GA-1 states $\varepsilon_t \perp \mathcal C_{t-1} \mid (\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$. The derivation in `#result-mismatch-decomposition` needs precisely two consequences: **(O1)** $\mathbb E[o_t - \bar o_t \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \mathcal C_{t-1}] = 0$ a.s. (kills the two noise cross-terms), and **(O2)** $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[\bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}]$ (step 2; kills the third cross-term and equates the middle leg's second moment with the labeled term (ii)). (O2) follows from (O1) by the tower property, so everything reduces to (O1): *the residual $o_t - \bar o_t$ must be conditionally mean-zero given the past, not merely given the present.* Note (O1) is strictly weaker than GA-1's conditional independence — "fresh in mean" suffices; this matters in §5a.

## 2. Half 1 — unknown $\theta_T$, known observation channel: exactness survives, and the flow-through is exhibited

**Hypotheses (H).** $\theta_T$ is a random parameter of the transition kernel (fixed over the trajectory, arbitrary prior). The observation channel is *parameter-free*: $h$ and $\mathrm{law}(\varepsilon_t)$ are fixed (no $\theta_h$), and the noise is exogenous-fresh, $\varepsilon_t \perp (\theta_T, \Omega_{0:t}, a_{0:t-1}, u_{0:t-1}, \varepsilon_{1:t-1})$ jointly.

**Claim 1a (GA-1 is a theorem under (H)).** $\Omega_t$ is measurable w.r.t. $(\theta_T, \Omega_0, w_{1:t}, a_{0:t-1})$ (transition noises $w$), $a_{t-1}$ and $\mathcal C_{t-1}$ w.r.t. $(\Omega_{0:t-1}, \varepsilon_{1:t-1}, u_{0:t-1})$; by (H), $\varepsilon_t$ is independent of the *joint* vector $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \mathcal C_{t-1})$, and joint independence implies the conditional independence $\varepsilon_t \perp \mathcal C_{t-1} \mid (\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$. Hence (O1), (O2), and the full derivation go through verbatim — the segment's steps 1–4 nowhere require $\theta_T$ known, only that the conditional law of $o_t$ given $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$ is fixed, which (H) supplies. Note also $\bar o_t = \int h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, e)\,\mathrm dP_\varepsilon(e)$ is $\theta_T$-free, so term (iii) $= \mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(o_t \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1})]$ is untouched by law-ignorance: **no $\theta_T$-uncertainty can leak into the channel-noise label.** The decomposition is exact at every finite $t$.

**Claim 1b (the flow-through, made explicit).** The only place $\theta_T$ enters is the posterior of $\Omega_t$ given the chronica, i.e., term (ii). Law of total variance over $\theta_T$:

$$\operatorname{Var}(\bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}) = \underbrace{\mathbb E\!\left[\operatorname{Var}(\bar o_t \mid \theta_T, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}) \,\middle|\, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}\right]}_{\text{state uncertainty at known law}} + \underbrace{\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\mathbb E[\bar o_t \mid \theta_T, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}] \,\middle|\, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}\right)}_{\text{law-uncertainty component}}$$

so $\theta_T$-ignorance sits *inside* the state-uncertainty floor as an additive, chronica-decaying component, and the Bayes predictor $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[\bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}]$ automatically averages over the $\theta_T$-posterior — "the Bayes floor already includes optimal law-learning" is exactly right. Closed-form instance: $\Omega_t = \theta_T + w_t$, $w_t \sim \mathcal N(0, q^2)$ i.i.d., $o_t = \Omega_t + \varepsilon_t$, $\varepsilon_t \sim \mathcal N(0, r^2)$, $\theta_T \sim \mathcal N(0, \tau^2)$: term (ii) $= \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2$ with $\sigma_{t-1}^2 = \tau^2 v / (v + (t{-}1)\tau^2)$, $v = q^2 + r^2$, and LHS $= \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2$ for the Bayes agent — identity exact at every $t$, Monte Carlo confirms to 4 decimals (§6, model A rows).

**One label nuance (not a defect in the identity).** With $\theta_T$ unknown, term (ii) contains a component that shrinks by *passive accumulation of chronica* (law-learning), not only by informative action choice; the segment's gloss "movable only by acting" is written for the known-law regime. The typed split above is what makes the gloss honest again: the state-given-law part is action-movable, the law part is learning-movable. This is a small independent argument for the $\theta$-slot even on the side that "works."

**Hypothesis sharpness.** Both parts of (H) are load-bearing. If $\mathrm{law}(\varepsilon_t)$ depends on $\theta_T$, that is observation-law ignorance in disguise ($\theta_h := \theta_T$) and half 2 applies. If $\varepsilon_t$ correlates with transition noise $w_t$ (as in general state-space models with cross-correlated noises) or with its own past (sensor drift — the correlated-noise failure the segment's Working Notes already flag), GA-1 fails for reasons unrelated to $\theta$; exogeneity excludes this. So the honest statement of half 1 is: *transition-law ignorance never breaks GA-1; only channel-side persistent structure (or channel-side noise correlation) can.*

## 3. Half 2 — unknown $\theta_h$, state-only $\Omega$: the break, in closed form

**General mechanism.** Let $o_t = h_{\theta_h}(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$ with $\theta_h$ random, fixed over the trajectory, and let the conditioning object be state-only. Then $\bar o_t = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}] = \mathbb E_{\theta_h \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}}\big[\mu(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h)\big]$ where $\mu(\omega, a, \vartheta) = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \Omega_t = \omega, a_{t-1} = a, \theta_h = \vartheta]$, so the residual decomposes as $o_t - \bar o_t = \underbrace{\big(\mu(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h) - \bar o_t\big)}_{\text{persistent component}} + \underbrace{\big(o_t - \mu(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h)\big)}_{\text{fresh at known law}}$. The second piece is conditionally mean-zero given anything $\theta_h$-measurable-plus-past; the first is a fixed function of $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h)$, and the chronica carries information about $\theta_h$, so $\mathbb E[o_t - \bar o_t \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \mathbb E\big[\mu(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h) \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \mathcal C_{t-1}\big] - \bar o_t$, which is generically nonzero: (O1) fails, and with it (O2) — so *both* the noise cross-terms and the step-2 identification $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[\bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}]$ break, and the middle leg's second moment no longer equals the labeled term (ii). The failure is not one cross-term but a coupled two-point failure. (Genericity is qualified in §5a.)

**3.1 Minimal conjugate model.** Unknown observation *bias* (the additive case of unknown-$h$-parameter; the unknown-gain case behaves identically off the §5a knife-edge but is not conjugate): $\theta_h \sim \mathcal N(0, \tau^2)$; $\Omega_t \sim \mathcal N(0, q^2)$ i.i.d. (transition law fully known — resampling); $\varepsilon_t \sim \mathcal N(0, r^2)$ i.i.d.; all independent; no actions; $o_t = \Omega_t + \theta_h + \varepsilon_t$. Given $\theta_h$, $o_s \sim \mathcal N(\theta_h, v)$ i.i.d. with $v = q^2 + r^2$, so the posterior is conjugate: $\theta_h \mid \mathcal C_{t-1} \sim \mathcal N(\hat\theta_{t-1}, \sigma_{t-1}^2)$, $\hat\theta_{t-1} = \tau^2 \sum_{s \le t-1} o_s / (v + (t{-}1)\tau^2)$, $\sigma_{t-1}^2 = \tau^2 v / (v + (t{-}1)\tau^2)$, and $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1}) = \tau^2 - \sigma_{t-1}^2$.

**3.2 GA-1 fails.** $\bar o_t = \Omega_t + \mathbb E[\theta_h] = \Omega_t$, so the residual is $o_t - \bar o_t = \theta_h + \varepsilon_t$ — visibly persistent — and since $\Omega_t$ is a fresh draw independent of $(\theta_h, \mathcal C_{t-1})$, $\mathbb E[o_t - \bar o_t \mid \Omega_t, \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \hat\theta_{t-1} \ne 0$ a.s. for $t \ge 2$. The violation *grows with learning*: $\mathbb E[\hat\theta_{t-1}^2] = \tau^2 - \sigma_{t-1}^2 \uparrow \tau^2$. The better the agent's epistemic position, the worse the fresh-noise fiction.

**3.3 The exactness failure, exactly quantified.** $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[\Omega_t + \theta_h + \varepsilon_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \hat\theta_{t-1}$, while $\mathbb E[\bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \mathbb E[\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] = 0 \ne \hat\theta_{t-1}$ — step 2's identity fails. The noise-middle cross-term is $C_1 = \mathbb E\big[(o_t - \bar o_t)(\bar o_t - \hat o_t^{\mathrm B})\big] = \mathbb E[(\theta_h + \varepsilon_t)(\Omega_t - \hat\theta_{t-1})] = -\mathbb E[\theta_h \hat\theta_{t-1}] = -\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1}) \lt 0$ for $t \ge 2$ (tower: $\mathbb E[\theta_h \hat\theta_{t-1}] = \mathbb E[\hat\theta_{t-1}^2]$). Taking the agent to be the Bayes predictor itself (term (i) $= 0$, the cleanest case — general agents also break the other two cross-terms, e.g. the always-predict-zero agent gives $C_3 = -\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1})$ as well): true LHS $\mathbb E[\delta_t^2] = \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2$; labeled term (ii) $= \mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1})] = q^2$; labeled term (iii) $= \mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(o_t \mid \Omega_t)] = \operatorname{Var}(\theta_h + \varepsilon_t) = \tau^2 + r^2$. Hence

$$\underbrace{0 + q^2 + (\tau^2 + r^2)}_{\text{labeled RHS}} - \underbrace{(\sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2)}_{\text{true } \mathbb E[\delta_t^2]} = \tau^2 - \sigma_{t-1}^2 = \operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1}) \gt 0 \quad (t \ge 2),$$

i.e., **the labeled decomposition overstates the true expected squared mismatch by exactly the law-information the chronica has extracted**, tending to the whole prior law-uncertainty $\tau^2$. The misattribution has a definite address: term (iii), "channel noise (irreducible)," silently absorbs $\tau^2$ of *learnable* structure — the deepest sense in which the labels lie when $\theta_h$ is unhoused. Consistency check of the raw Pythagorean expansion: $\mathbb E[(o_t - \bar o_t)^2] + \mathbb E[(\bar o_t - \hat o_t^{\mathrm B})^2] + 2C_1 = (\tau^2 + r^2) + (q^2 + \operatorname{Var}\hat\theta_{t-1}) - 2\operatorname{Var}\hat\theta_{t-1} = \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2$ ✓ — note the middle leg's second moment ($q^2 + \operatorname{Var}\hat\theta_{t-1}$) also differs from the labeled term (ii) ($q^2$), the (O2)-side failure.

**3.4 Bayes beats the "true conditional mean."** $\mathbb E[(o_t - \bar o_t)^2] = \tau^2 + r^2$ (constant in $t$) versus $\mathbb E[(o_t - \hat o_t^{\mathrm B})^2] = \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2$ (decreasing), so the chronica-only predictor strictly beats the state-informed-but-law-marginal predictor as soon as $\sigma_{t-1}^2 \lt \tau^2 - q^2$, i.e., whenever $q^2 \lt \tau^2$ and $t - 1 \gt q^2 v / \big(\tau^2(\tau^2 - q^2)\big)$ — an explicit crossing time (with the §6 parameters, from the second observation on). The two conditioning $\sigma$-algebras are genuinely non-nested and either can win: $q^2 \gt \tau^2$ keeps $\bar o_t$ ahead forever. Consequence for the segment's rhetoric: in this regime "the best any chronica-measurable model could do" is *not* bounded below by the state-floor legs, because $\bar o_t$ has lost its status as the projection onto a finer information set — the "floor" ordering itself presupposes housed $\theta$.

**3.5 The totality repair, verified.** Condition on $S_t = (\Omega_t, \theta_h)$: $\bar o_t' = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \Omega_t, \theta_h] = \Omega_t + \theta_h$; residual $= \varepsilon_t$, genuinely fresh ((O1) holds); $\mathbb E[\bar o_t' \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \hat\theta_{t-1} = \hat o_t^{\mathrm B}$ ((O2) restored); term (ii)$' = \mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(\Omega_t + \theta_h \mid \mathcal C_{t-1})] = q^2 + \sigma_{t-1}^2$; term (iii)$' = r^2$; sum $= \sigma_{t-1}^2 + q^2 + r^2 = \mathbb E[\delta_t^2]$ — exact again at every $t$, Monte Carlo confirmed. The repaired term (ii)$'$ wears the typed split on its face: $q^2$ movable by acting, $\sigma_{t-1}^2$ movable by learning, and term (iii)$'$ is now honestly irreducible.

## 4. The observationally-equivalent twin — the strongest form of the finding

Compare the §2 closed-form instance and the §3 model: **model A** ($\theta$ in the transition) has $\Omega_t^A = \theta + w_t$, $o_t = \Omega_t^A + \varepsilon_t$; **model B** ($\theta$ in the observation law) has $\Omega_t^B = w_t$, $o_t = \Omega_t^B + \theta + \varepsilon_t$. The joint law of the observation process $(o_1, o_2, \ldots)$ is *identical* — same data, same chronica, same Bayes predictor $\hat\theta_{t-1}$, same posterior. Yet the three-term decomposition is exact at every $t$ in model A and fails by $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1})$ in model B, because the conditioning object $\Omega_t$ differs. (The simulation runs both housings on the *same* sampled trajectories; §6.) Therefore: **whether GA-1 holds is not a property of the agent-environment system's observable behavior; it is a property of the chosen state description** — of where persistent structure is housed. In the presence of law-uncertainty, GA-1 with an unspecified housing rule is not merely sometimes-false, it is *indeterminate*: its truth-value can be toggled by a bookkeeping choice that moves no observable fact. This is §4's closing claim ("the fresh-noise boundary is only well-posed relative to a named $\theta$-slot") in its sharpest available form, and it also *ratifies* the bandit segment's silent move: putting the reward-law parameters into $\Omega$ is precisely choosing the housing under which GA-1 is true — the move is forced wherever exactness is wanted, and the missing rule is what licenses it.

## 5. Sharpenings — where §4 needs more precise hypotheses

**(a) Independence vs mean-orthogonality: half 2 needs a nondegeneracy condition, and there is a knife-edge.** GA-1 as stated is conditional *independence*; the decomposition consumes only the mean-zero corollary (O1). Unknown $\theta_h$ voids the independence statement as soon as the chronica carries *any* $\theta_h$-information (even variance-only), but exactness fails only when the *persistent mean component* moves: precisely, when $\mathbb E[\mu(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \theta_h) \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \mathcal C_{t-1}] \ne \bar o_t$ on a positive-probability set *and* the induced cross-moments are nonzero. Genuine knife-edge where exactness survives $\theta_h$-ignorance: unknown gain $o_t = \theta_h \Omega_t + \varepsilon_t$ with symmetric prior on $\theta_h$ and $\Omega_t$ zero-mean i.i.d. — the chronica learns $\vert\theta_h\vert$ through the variance, the posterior stays sign-symmetric, $\mathbb E[\theta_h \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] \equiv 0$, and all the broken quantities vanish by symmetry; break the symmetry ($\mathbb E[\Omega_t] \ne 0$ or an asymmetric prior) and the general-position failure reappears (for the nonzero-mean gain model, $C_1 = -m^2 \operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta_{t-1})$ by the same tower argument). So the honest half-2 statement carries the same *alignment* flavor as the segment's own term-(ii) qualifier: *unknown $\theta_h$ breaks GA-1-as-independence whenever the chronica is $\theta_h$-informative, and breaks the decomposition's exactness whenever the $\theta_h$-posterior-mean of the observation is chronica-movable* — mean-relevance, not mere existence, of law-uncertainty. This parallelism (both floors carry mean-alignment qualifiers) is itself tidy evidence the decomposition's natural habitat is second moments.
**(b) The frequentist horn: what breaks if $\theta_h$ is fixed-but-unknown rather than random.** If one refuses the Bayesian embedding and treats $\theta_h^\ast$ as a fixed constant of the true law, then $\bar o_t$ is computed under the true channel, (O1) holds, and the identity is exact — half 2's algebra never engages. But then $\hat o_t^{\mathrm B}$ is the conditional mean *under the true unknown law*: still the MMSE chronica-measurable predictor in hindsight, but no longer "what optimal learning attains," because the formalism now contains no learning-about-$\theta_h$ story at all, and term (i) silently contains a component no realizable agent can remove at finite $t$ (the regret of not knowing $\theta_h^\ast$), while the "reducible by modeling" label claims otherwise. So the two readings trade failure surfaces: **the mixture reading keeps the labels meaningful and loses the identity; the fixed reading keeps the identity and hollows the labels.** No assignment of unhoused $\theta_h$ preserves both — a small no-go that is *stronger* than §4 as written, and the cleanest argument that the $\theta$-slot is not optional bookkeeping. (Housing $\theta_h$ in the conditioning object restores both simultaneously, §3.5.)
**(c) De Finetti view, connecting to the correlated-noise flag already in the segment's Working Notes.** Marginalizing a persistent latent out of the channel turns fresh noise into *exchangeably correlated* effective noise — the mixture-of-i.i.d. structure is exactly de Finetti's. So "unknown observation law" is not a new failure mode alongside sensor drift; it is the canonical *persistent-latent* species of the correlated-noise failure the Working Notes already name, and the $\theta$-slot is the repair that re-freshens it: conditionally on the named latent, the noise is i.i.d. again.

## 7. Proposed honest statement of GA-1's well-posedness condition

> **GA-1 is a statement about a description, not about a system.** "Fresh" means *independent of the past given the named present*, and is well-posed only when the named present includes every persistent quantity bearing on the observation channel. The invariant form is: *there exists a named persistent object $\theta$ (possibly trivial) such that $\varepsilon_t \perp \mathcal C_{t-1}$ given $(\Omega_t, \theta, a_{t-1})$, with the decomposition's conditioning object taken to be $S_t = (\Omega_t, \theta)$.* When the observation law is fully known, $\theta$ is trivial and this reduces to GA-1 as currently stated. When it is not, GA-1-over-state-only-$\Omega$ has no observation-determined truth-value (§4), and the labeled decomposition is exact precisely when the housing choice puts channel-side persistent structure inside the conditioning object. Transition-side persistent structure never needs housing *for this result*: it cannot violate freshness, and its uncertainty is automatically carried — additively, by total variance — inside the state-uncertainty floor (§2).

Two label refinements travel with this: term (ii) under a nontrivial $\theta$ splits into an action-movable state part and a learning-movable law part (both floors for *modeling*, neither for *behavior*); and term (iii) is honestly "irreducible" only after the housing — before it, it can silently contain the entire learnable law-uncertainty (§3.3).
