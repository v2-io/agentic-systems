## 0. Verdict in brief

The typed predicate formalizes cleanly, on standard-Borel footing, with one genuinely canonical construction underneath it (the invariance cut, §3) and one forced sharpening the spike did not anticipate ($\theta_h$ must be an observation *kernel*, not a function-plus-noise-law pair — the pair is never identifiable, §2.2). The four quadrants come out identical to the totality reading — necessarily, since the typed target *is* the hyperstate with its product structure remembered (§5.1) — but the typed version adds two rows the bare $2\times2$ cannot express (drifting law; compute-bound prediction) and converts the quadrant table's verdicts into statements about *which component* carries the warrant and *whether that warrant decays* (§5). The side-effect sites all improve under the typed repair, and one improves dramatically: the constitutive opacity atoms become frame-stable graded statements, dissolving the frame-relativity cost (verifier side effect A) that the bare totality reading has to pay (§7.2). The single strongest new result of the pass: the four-way ignorance typology of spike §6 is not just a plain-language table — it is, term for term, the structure of a four-term refinement of `#result-mismatch-decomposition`, derived in §6 (unverified here; segment-bound if it survives review). On the boundary question: **the two-tier cut is honest at the root** — the root cut is the *binary* invariant-vs-varying cut, which is canonical (tail-$\sigma$-field grade), and the timescale tower is a derived stratification of the state component connected to the root by a horizon-indexed "effectively-law" overlay (§4). The typed version is worse than bare totality on textual footprint, front-door machinery weight, and by making the prior over $\theta$ visible (§8 — though I argue the last is an honesty gain wearing a cost's clothing); and there are boundary sites that stay genuinely ambiguous under either reading (§9).

## 1. Setting and what has to be delivered

The bare predicate $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ already presupposes more than canon states: a conditional entropy needs a joint law over $(\Omega_t, \mathcal C_t)$, and when the kernels $T, h$ are unknown, no such joint law exists until the modeler has *mixed over the unknowns* — i.e., placed a distribution on law-content. So the untyped predicate was already, silently, an expectation over a latent law-index. The typed ontology does not add this object; it **names an object the predicate could never have been well-posed without**. That observation does most of the work in the honest comparison (§8): the machinery cost of the typed version is largely the cost of writing down what the bare version was already spending.

Deliverables: (i) $\theta$ as a measure-theoretic object; (ii) the predicate, stated so it survives continuous spaces; (iii) the naming construction ("everything persistent into $\theta$") with its canonicity status; (iv) the drift/tower interaction; (v) the stress tests.

## 2. The formal object

### 2.1 Spaces

Let the state space $\mathcal X$ (values of $\Omega_t$), observation space $\mathcal O$, and action space $\mathcal A$ be standard Borel. For standard Borel $\mathcal Y, \mathcal Z$, write $\mathcal K(\mathcal Y, \mathcal Z)$ for the space of Markov kernels $\mathcal Y \to \mathcal P(\mathcal Z)$, equipped with the $\sigma$-field generated by the evaluation maps $\kappa \mapsto \kappa(B \mid y)$ ($B$ Borel, $y \in \mathcal Y$); since $\mathcal P(\mathcal Z)$ under the weak topology is standard Borel, so is $\mathcal K(\mathcal Y, \mathcal Z)$ (kernels are measurable maps $\mathcal Y \to \mathcal P(\mathcal Z)$). Nothing exotic is needed: this is the same footing BAMDP / Bayesian-nonparametric treatments use.

### 2.2 $\theta$ is a random kernel pair — and $\theta_h$ is a kernel, not a $(h, \mu_\varepsilon)$ pair

Define the **law space**

$$\Theta \;=\; \mathcal K(\mathcal X \times \mathcal A,\; \mathcal X) \;\times\; \mathcal K(\mathcal X \times \mathcal A,\; \mathcal O),$$

whose elements are pairs $\vartheta = (T_\vartheta, H_\vartheta)$: a transition kernel and an **observation kernel** $H_\vartheta(\cdot \mid \omega, a)$ — the conditional law of $o_t$ given $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$. Then $\theta = (\theta_T, \theta_h)$ is a **random element of $\Theta$**, defined on the same underlying probability space as the run, with distribution $\pi_0 \in \mathcal P(\Theta)$ (the modeler's reference prior; §8.3 on whose prior), and the process law is the mixture $\mathbb P = \int_\Theta \mathbb P_\vartheta \, \pi_0(d\vartheta)$ with $\mathbb P_\vartheta$ the closed-loop law under the realized kernels.

The forced sharpening: canon's slot for observation-law content is the pair "$h$ and the distribution of $\varepsilon_t$" (`#def-observation-function`'s epistemic-opacity atom names both). But the pair $(h, \mu_\varepsilon)$ is **never identifiable** — uncountably many pairs induce the same conditional law of $o_t$ given $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$, and the chronica only ever carries samples from that conditional law. The learnable object, hence the object that belongs in the epistemic target, is the induced kernel $H_\vartheta(B \mid \omega, a) = \mathbb P(h(\omega, a, \varepsilon_t) \in B)$. So the typed ontology forces: **law-content = the kernel equivalence class**. The familiar functional form $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$ is recovered, not assumed, by noise extraction: for standard Borel $\mathcal O$, any kernel $H_\vartheta$ admits a measurable realization $o_t = f_{\vartheta}(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, U_t)$ with $U_t \sim \mathrm{Unif}[0,1]$ i.i.d. and independent of everything prior (the standard randomization/transfer construction). This matters for GA-1 below: **freshness of $\varepsilon$ is a property of a *realization*, and a fresh realization exists exactly when the conditioning object is rich enough** (§3.3).

### 2.3 The typed target and the predicate

$$S_t \;=\; (\Omega_t,\, \theta) \;\in\; \mathcal X \times \Theta.$$

**Scope predicate (typed).** The pair $(\text{Agent}, \Omega)$ is in adaptive scope at $t$ iff $\mathcal O \neq \emptyset$ and the conditional law $\mathbb P(S_t \in \cdot \mid \mathcal C_t)$ is non-degenerate (non-Dirac) with positive probability — rendered $H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ in the discrete case.

Two remarks the formalization forces, both applying equally to the *bare* predicate (so they are repairs owed anyway, surfaced by this pass rather than caused by it):

- **Non-degeneracy, not differential entropy.** For continuous $\Omega_t$ or nonparametric $\Theta$, Shannon entropy becomes differential entropy, which can be negative while genuine uncertainty remains, and is not even defined on $\mathcal K$-valued variables without a reference measure. The honest primitive is "the conditional law is not a point mass"; $H \gt 0$ is its discrete-case rendering. Canon currently states the predicate in the $H \gt 0$ form with no caveat.
- **The chain-rule decomposition is order-dependent; the predicate is not.** $H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) + H(\theta \mid \Omega_t, \mathcal C_t) = H(\theta \mid \mathcal C_t) + H(\Omega_t \mid \theta, \mathcal C_t)$. The two orders apportion the total differently (state-uncertainty given history vs. given history-and-law, etc.), but positivity of the total — the scope question — is order-independent, and each quadrant verdict in §5 is too. Segments that *use* a typed component (the four-term decomposition in §6, the bit-accounting in §7.1) must name which conditioning they mean; the scope predicate itself need not.

## 3. The construction: what "everything persistent is named into $\theta$" means

The spike defines $\varepsilon$ as "whatever remains fresh once everything persistent has been named into $\theta$." As stated that is a slogan with a circularity risk (fresh relative to what conditioning?). Here is the construction that discharges it. It has three closure conditions, one per slot, and the punchline is that **freshness is a theorem given the other two closures, not a third assumption**.

### 3.1 The $\theta$-closure: invariance

Call a random variable $Z$ on the run-space **persistent** if it is a.s. invariant along the run — measurable with respect to the invariant $\sigma$-field $\mathcal I$ of the interaction process (equivalently: $Z$ is a.s. equal to a tail-measurable variable whose value is the same from every $t$ onward). The **canonical $\theta$** is the $\mathcal I$-conditional structure: condition the process law on $\mathcal I$ and the mixture $\mathbb P = \int \mathbb P_\vartheta\, \pi_0(d\vartheta)$ is the ergodic-decomposition-style disintegration; $\theta$ is the (a.s. unique) random component index. "Everything persistent is named into $\theta$" = **$\theta$ generates $\mathcal I$** (modulo $\mathbb P$-null sets). This is the same canonicity de Finetti's directing measure has for exchangeable sequences: the decomposition into "mixture over laws" is unique once you demand the index carry *exactly* the invariant content — no more (minimality: $\theta$ is $\mathcal I$-measurable) and no less (completeness: $\mathcal I \subseteq \sigma(\theta)$).

Two consequences worth recording:

- **Conserved quantities are law.** A conserved function of the initial state ($\Omega_1$'s energy-like invariants in a non-mixing system) is $\mathcal I$-measurable and hence lands in $\theta$. This is not an embarrassment; it matches physics usage exactly — conservation laws are laws — and it is the formal reason the intuition "law = how things work" and the criterion "law = what never varies" agree on more than kernel parameters.
- **Controlled-process caveat.** $\mathcal I$ is a property of the *closed-loop* process and so, in principle, of the policy. The policy-free statement is: $\theta$ = the content invariant under *every* admissible closed loop, which for the standard situation (unknowns index the kernels; kernels do not depend on the policy) is exactly the kernel content of §2.2, and the declared parametric $\Theta$ is a chart on it. Two modelers with different parametrizations name the same intrinsic $\theta$ up to a.s. bijection — the same parameterization-invariance posture (PI) that `#scope-agent-identity` already takes as axiomatic, available here for free. The fully general controlled case (policy-dependent invariant structure, e.g. lock-in effects) is real residue for an appendix-grade writeup, flagged not solved.

### 3.2 The $\Omega$-closure: Markov sufficiency

The state slot has its own closure, and canon already owns it: `#def-action-transition`'s Markov-of-$\Omega$ breadth commitment — $\Omega$ is *defined* wide enough that, **given $\theta$**, $\Omega_{t+1} \perp \mathcal C_{t-1}, \Omega_{1:t-1} \mid (\Omega_t, a_t)$. Note the typed form adds "given $\theta$" to the existing commitment: sufficiency of the state for its own evolution is only a well-posed demand once the kernel doing the evolving is fixed. This is the second slot's naming rule: **memory into $\Omega$** (any environment memory is absorbed by widening the state), as **persistence into $\theta$** (any cross-run invariant is absorbed by widening the law index).

### 3.3 The $\varepsilon$-slot: freshness as a theorem

With both closures in place, define the **conditional-GA-1 property**:

$$o_t \;\perp\; \mathcal C_{t-1} \;\big\vert\; (\Omega_t,\, a_{t-1},\, \theta), \qquad \text{i.e. GA-1 with } S_t \text{ in the conditioning slot.}$$

**Claim (freshness realization).** If $\theta$ generates $\mathcal I$ (§3.1) and $\Omega$ satisfies the $\theta$-conditional Markov-sufficiency commitment with the observation kernel $H_\theta$ depending only on $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$ (§3.2), then conditional-GA-1 holds, and by noise extraction (§2.2) there is a realization $o_t = f_\theta(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$ with $(\varepsilon_t)$ i.i.d. and independent of $(\theta, \Omega_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}, \varepsilon_{1:t-1})$. Conversely (this is the direction that belongs to the 01-agent's assignment; stated here at sketch grade because the predicate work needs its shape): if a persistent $Z$ with $I(o_t; Z \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}) \gt 0$ recurrently is *omitted* from the conditioning object, then $I(o_t; \mathcal C_{t-1} \mid \Omega_t, a_{t-1}) \gt 0$ (the past carries evidence about $Z$, $Z$ shapes the present observation), so no realization with fresh $\varepsilon_t$ relative to $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$ alone exists. A two-line witness: $o_t = \theta_h + \varepsilon_t'$ with $\theta_h \sim \mathcal N(0,1)$ unknown, $\varepsilon_t'$ i.i.d. $\mathcal N(0,1)$, $\Omega_t$ trivial — then $\mathbb E[o_t - \bar o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] = \mathbb E[\theta_h \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}] \neq 0$ for $t \geq 2$.

So the typed ontology re-types GA-1 itself: **from an empirical assumption about the world to a well-posedness condition on the modeling cut.** GA-1 (typed) says *the naming is complete*; a GA-1 violation is a *detection of unnamed persistent structure*. This gives GA-1 an operational diagnostic it never had in the untyped form: serial dependence in the innovations is exactly the classical whiteness test of filtering practice (innovations-whiteness as model-adequacy check, Kalman-filter tradition — Anderson–Moore-style), now derivable as "the $\theta$-slot is incomplete" rather than adopted as folklore. The empirical content does not vanish — it relocates to the *tractability* of the naming (whether a finite-dimensional or otherwise learnable $\Theta$ suffices), which is where `#def-model-class-fitness` picks it up (spike §10 item 5, not developed here).

### 3.4 The three-slot naming discipline (summary)

| Slot | Closure rule | What gets named into it | Status of the rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\theta$ | shift-invariance ($\mathcal I$-completeness) | everything persistent (kernels, conserved quantities) | new; canonical via ergodic/de Finetti disintegration |
| $\Omega_t$ | $\theta$-conditional Markov sufficiency | everything remembered (environment memory, history augmentation) | existing commitment ( `#def-action-transition`), now correctly $\theta$-conditional |
| $\varepsilon_t$ | none — residual | whatever remains; fresh **by theorem** given the two closures | GA-1 re-typed as well-posedness (§3.3) |

## 4. $\theta$-drift: two-tier root, derived tower

The question given particular weight in the assignment: where does the state/law cut sit when the "law" drifts ( `#der-mood-timescale`'s AR(1) regime latent $\theta_t$ with autocorrelation time $\tau_{\mathrm{env}}$; the nonstationary bandit's drifting $\mu_i(t)$)? Is the two-tier cut honest at the root, or does the root need the full timescale tower?

### 4.1 The root cut is binary, hence two tiers suffice — but only because the cut is *invariance*, not *slowness*

Under §3.1 the cut is qualitative: invariant ($\rho_{\text{component}} = 0$ exactly) vs. varying ($\rho \gt 0$, however small). A drifting regime latent $\theta_t$ **is not $\theta$** — it varies, so the invariance criterion re-types it as *slow state*: a coordinate of (a suitably widened) $\Omega_t$. What is genuinely $\theta$ in the mood model is the drift process's own structure: $(\phi, \sigma_\xi^2, r)$ — equivalently $(\tau_{\mathrm{env}}, \sigma_\theta^2, r)$ — the AR(1) form itself. The tower then *derives* by self-similarity: apply the same cut inside the state component — coordinates stratify by autocorrelation time $\tau^{(0)} \lt \tau^{(1)} \lt \cdots$, each tier's "effective law" is the next tier's state, and the recursion terminates at the genuinely invariant residue, which is the root $\theta$ ($\tau = \infty$ as the fixed point of "law of the law of the law…"). Formally the tower is an increasing family of $\sigma$-fields indexed by timescale, with $\mathcal I$ as its limit; but none of that needs to sit in the root definition — the root needs only the binary cut, because "varies at all vs. never varies" is binary.

The operational cash-out of the cut, and the sharpest one-line characterization found in this pass: **$\theta$ is the zero-depreciation stratum of the epistemic target.** Information about $\theta$, once earned, never goes stale — the chronica's evidence about invariants compounds forever. Information about any varying component depreciates at that component's drift rate ($1/\tau^{(k)}$; this is exactly what `#der-mood-timescale`'s lag-vs-noise tradeoff prices, and what GA-2's $\rho$ measures at the fast tier). The state/law cut is the $\{\text{depreciation} = 0\}$ vs. $\{\text{depreciation} \gt 0\}$ boundary; the tower is the spectrum of positive depreciation rates. This gives the persistence machinery a clean typed reading: $\rho$ applies to the varying strata; the invariant stratum contributes no drift term, only a (decaying) learning term.

### 4.2 The honest cost, and the overlay that pays it

Strictness has a price: real environments rarely carry *exactly* invariant law-content — regime change is generic — so under the strict cut, $\theta$ is thin for many realistic systems and almost everything is (slow) state. Does that re-break anything? **Scope: no.** A slowly drifting component keeps $H(\Omega_t^{\text{slow}} \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ standing, so membership is unaffected — the predicate never cared which component carries the warrant. **The plain-language typology: yes, slightly.** Spike §6's repair-route table ("law → learn by accumulating experience") *wants* slow-drifting regimes in the "learn" row — their repair route is learning-shaped, not sensing-shaped — but the strict cut books them as state. The bridge is a graded overlay, defined at the agent, not at the root:

**Effectively law at horizon $h$**: a component $Z_t$ of the epistemic target is $(h, \epsilon)$-quasi-invariant if its expected variation over any window of length $h$ is at most $\epsilon$ (in the component's natural metric). For an agent with planning/learning horizon $h$ and estimation error floor $\epsilon_{\text{est}}$, the "law" column of the typology is $\theta \cup \{(h, \epsilon_{\text{est}})\text{-quasi-invariant strata}\}$ — invariant-for-all-practical-purposes content the agent should treat with law-type repair routes, while the root ontology keeps the exact cut. The overlay is where the tempo machinery hooks in: whether a stratum is effectively law is a *ratio* question ($\tau^{(k)}$ against the agent's horizon and tracking tempo), which is the same agent-relative type as spike §6's compute-shortfall row — reinforcing that the root ontology should carry only coupling-properties and the agent-relative gradings should live in the dynamics layers.

**Answer to the boundary question: the two-tier cut is honest at the root, *provided* the root cut is stated as invariance (binary, canonical) rather than as slowness (graded, judgment-laden), the tower is presented as a derived stratification of the state component (self-similar application of the same cut), and the typology's "law" column is explicitly the horizon-indexed overlay rather than the root $\theta$.** Starting with the full tower at the root would trade a canonical binary for an arbitrary-grid quantitative structure and would still need the invariant limit as its anchor — more machinery for less canonicity.

### 4.3 Corollary: the bandit's silent move, correctly adjudicated (a friendly correction to spike §3)

Spike §3 glosses the bandit ratification as "the $\mu$'s are $\theta$." Under the invariance cut that is right only for a *stationary* bandit. Canon's `#example-bandit` is the **nonstationary** bandit — $\mu_i(t+1) = \mu_i(t) + w_i(t)$ — so its $\mu_i(t)$ *drift* and are correctly typed as slow **state**, which is exactly what the segment already declares ($\Omega_t = (\mu_1(t), \ldots, \mu_4(t))$, scope row *exact*); its true $\theta$ is $(q, \sigma^2$, the Gaussian random-walk form$)$. So the typed ontology does something better than ratify the silent move: **it adjudicates the corpus's housing disagreement in both directions at once.** The bandit put drifting law-like content in $\Omega$ — correct, because it drifts; `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate` notated invariant structural parameters $\theta$ as distinct from $\Omega_t$ — correct, because they are invariant. Neither segment was wrong; what was missing was the *rule* (invariance) under which both are right. The unlicensed-move finding in the SP-30 verdict (§4 there) resolves to: the license exists and is the invariance criterion; the bandit exercised it correctly for the drift case.

## 5. The four-quadrant check, re-run typed

### 5.1 Inheritance lemma (why the totality reading's quadrant performance transfers)

The typed target and the hyperstate are the same random object under different bracketing: the totality reading's $\tilde\Omega_t = (\Omega_t, \theta)$ *is* $S_t$ with the product structure forgotten, $\tilde T((\omega', \vartheta') \mid (\omega, \vartheta), a) = \mathbb 1[\vartheta' = \vartheta]\, T_\vartheta(\omega' \mid \omega, a)$ is the $\theta$-diagonal presentation of the pair, and $H(\tilde\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t)$ identically. So every verdict the SP-30 verifier established for the totality reading (quadrant classification, no-downstream-reliance, GA-1-only-strengthens) transfers to the typed predicate without re-proof; the typed reading is a **conservative refinement** — same extension, more structure. What must be checked fresh is only what the *structure* adds: the typed diagnoses, and the sites where canon uses a component rather than the total.

### 5.2 The table

Conditioning below is the order $H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) + H(\theta \mid \Omega_t, \mathcal C_t)$; "warrant" names which term is positive; "profile" is the new temporal column the typed version adds.

| System | $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t)$ | $H(\theta \mid \Omega_t, \mathcal C_t)$ | Verdict | Typed diagnosis; temporal profile of the warrant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalman (hidden state, known law) | $\gt 0$ (steady-state $P^- \succ 0$) | $= 0$ | **in** ✓ | state-type ignorance; **standing** warrant (fresh process noise renews it every step) |
| Tabular RL (observed state, unknown kernel) | $= 0$ ($o_t = \Omega_t$) | $\gt 0$ for all finite $t$ | **in** ✓ (the B-3 repair target) | law-type ignorance; **decaying** warrant — $H(\theta \mid \mathcal C_t) \downarrow 0$ on the visited support for identifiable $\theta$ |
| Observed state, known law, deterministic | $= 0$ | $= 0$ | **out** ✓ | nothing learnable; pure computation remains |
| Observed state, known law, stochastic | $= 0$ | $= 0$ | **out** ✓ (the non-obvious corner) | only chance-type residual remains, and chance is by construction not a component of $S_t$ — the typed form makes the verifier's "nothing learnable" gloss *definitional* rather than argued |
| **(new row) Drifting law** (mood regime; nonstationary bandit) | $\gt 0$ (slow-state stratum never pinned) | $\gt 0 \to 0$ (drift-process parameters learnable) | **in** | mixed; the slow stratum's warrant is **standing** (evidence depreciates at $1/\tau_{\mathrm{env}}$), the drift-parameter warrant decays — the bare $2\times2$ has no cell for this, the generic realistic case |
| **(new row) Compute-bound** (perfect perception, known intractable law — the 193847 flag) | $= 0$ | $= 0$ | **out** — honestly | the typed predicate does **not** subsume the computational-opacity flag; see §5.3 |

### 5.3 What the typed predicate honestly does *not* fix — and a structural warning

**The compute-bound row is a B-3-shaped misclassification one level up, and the typed predicate leaves it standing.** A cellular-automaton world read losslessly, rule known, prediction intractable: $H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) = 0$, out of scope — yet the agent's mismatch machinery is operationally alive (its *estimation term* is large; §6). This is structurally the same pattern B-3 exposed: the predicate excludes a system whose adaptive machinery is non-vacuous. The typed ontology's own §6 typology explains *why* it cannot fix it: compute-shortfall is a **ratio between agent and environment**, not a property of the coupling, so no entropy functional of $S_t$ — typed or not — can see it. Two candidate postures: (a) accept, and declare the adaptive scope *epistemic by design*, with compute-shortfall handled where AAT already prices it (tempo race, `#der-deliberation-cost`, the estimation term) — this keeps the predicate a coupling-classifier, which is what a scope over $(\text{Agent}, \Omega)$ pairs arguably should be; (b) extend the predicate with a compute-relative effective-entropy (entropy relative to a bounded-inference $\sigma$-algebra), which is a real research object, not a paragraph. My recommendation is (a) now, with the exclusion *stated* (one honest sentence in the scope segment: prediction-intractability with full knowledge is out of *adaptive* scope and in scope for the tempo machinery) and (b) recorded as the successor question to the 193847 flag. The SP-30 verifier's §8 note ("totality-with-computability… deserves its own scrutiny") lands the same way: not claimable as a corollary of either reading.

**THREAD-F sharpened, not resolved.** The temporal-quantifier gap (the predicate carries no $\forall t / \exists t$) survives typing, but the typed version explains its texture: the untyped predicate conflates two *temporal profiles* — law-warrants are typically **transient** (decaying as $\theta$ is identified; the agent that fully learns a static MDP exits scope and honestly becomes a planner) while state-warrants are typically **standing** (renewed by fresh state noise), and $\theta$-drift converts law-type warrants into standing ones. Any eventual THREAD-F fix (running condition, $\inf_t$, tie to GA-2's $\rho \gt 0$) should be written per-component; the typed structure is what makes that possible to say.

## 6. The four-term mismatch decomposition — the typology is the term structure

*Status: derived in this pass, single-agent, unverified; consistent with, and refining, spike §4's asymmetry claim (whose independent verification belongs to the 01-agent). If it survives review it is segment-bound per math-lives-in-segments — natural landing: a corollary block in `#result-mismatch-decomposition` plus one Discussion paragraph, with `#internal-external-decomposition` inheriting the refinement.*

Fix the typed conditioning objects, nested by construction: $\sigma(\mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}) \subseteq \sigma(\theta, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}) \subseteq \sigma(S_t, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1})$. Define the prediction ladder

$$\bar o_t^{S} = \mathbb E[o_t \mid S_t, a_{t-1}], \qquad \bar o_t^{\theta} = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \theta, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}], \qquad \hat o_t^{\mathrm B} = \mathbb E[o_t \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}],$$

where conditional-GA-1 (§3.3) lets $\bar o_t^S$ drop the chronica from its conditioning. Telescope $\delta_t = (o_t - \bar o_t^S) + (\bar o_t^S - \bar o_t^\theta) + (\bar o_t^\theta - \hat o_t^{\mathrm B}) + (\hat o_t^{\mathrm B} - \hat o_t)$. The middle two differences are successive projections across the nested $\sigma$-fields (tower property makes each conditionally mean-zero given the coarser field), the first is conditionally mean-zero given the finest field by conditional-GA-1, and the last is measurable in the coarsest — so all six cross-terms vanish by exactly the orthogonality pattern of the existing three-term derivation, run one level deeper:

$$\mathbb E[\Vert\delta_t\Vert^2] \;=\; \underbrace{\mathbb E[\Vert\hat o_t - \hat o_t^{\mathrm B}\Vert^2]}_{\text{(i) estimation}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathbb E\big[\operatorname{Var}\big(\bar o_t^{\theta} \mid \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}\big)\big]}_{\text{(ii) law floor}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathbb E\big[\operatorname{Var}\big(\bar o_t^{S} \mid \theta, \mathcal C_{t-1}, a_{t-1}\big)\big]}_{\text{(iii) state floor at known law}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathbb E\big[\operatorname{Var}\big(o_t \mid S_t, a_{t-1}\big)\big]}_{\text{(iv) chance}}$$

with (ii)+(iii) recovering the current result's middle term by the conditional-variance (total-variance) identity whenever the current result's conditioning is read with $\theta$ housed — the typed form is a strict refinement, not a rival.

**Three consequences.**

1. **The spike-§6 typology is not a table; it is this theorem's term structure.** Term (i) is where bounded compute bites (the gap to the Bayes predictor — the compute-shortfall row, repair: tempo); term (ii) is law-ignorance (repair: learn — and it *decays* in $t$ for identifiable invariant $\theta$, the zero-depreciation property of §4.1 showing up as a vanishing floor); term (iii) is state-ignorance (repair: observe/act — the CIY door, standing under fresh state noise); term (iv) is chance (repair: accept or re-instrument). The plain-language four-way typology and the framework's first exact result are the same object seen at two registers. I did not expect this going in; it is the strongest single argument this pass found for the typed ontology.

### 7.1 `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate`'s bit-accounting: the typed version exposes a $2 \times 2$ the current prose diagonalizes

Current text: exploration earns *environment-bits* (reduces $H(\Omega_t \mid M_t)$); deliberation earns *inference-bits* (reduces $H(\theta \mid M_t, \mathcal C_t)$). The SP-30 verifier noted that under the totality reading the two channels overlap on one random object and need a distinguishing clause. The typed reading does better than add the clause — it shows the current pairing conflates two orthogonal axes:

- **Target axis** (which component of $S_t$): state vs. law.
- **Source axis** (which resource): *evidence* — grow $\mathcal C_t$, reducing $H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t)$ — vs. *computation* — close the extraction gap $H(S_t \mid M_t) - H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \geq 0$ at fixed chronica.

Exploration is the evidence column (either target: a probe can be state-informative or law-informative); deliberation is the computation column (either target: re-filtering history for state, inference on accumulated evidence for law). ### 8.1 Where the typed version is genuinely worse

1. **It is more to hold.** The bare reading is one sentence a reader can absorb in passing; the typed reading asks the root of Part I to carry three named slots plus a wrapper object, and its full justification needs measure-theoretic footing that currently appears nowhere in the corpus. Even with the appendix-segment mitigation, the front door gets heavier — and Part I's front door is exactly where the comprehension-lift (spike §9) wants *less* inscrutability, not more. The counter-argument is the spike's own thesis (the typology is the mental model, and mental models want named nouns), but the counter-argument has to be *earned in the writing*; stated as ontology without the §6-table payoff visible early, three slots are worse pedagogy than one.
2. **More canon surface to audit.** Every $H(\Omega_t \mid \cdot)$ in the corpus must be classified state-component vs. total. This pass found the classification easy at every site checked (scope predicate: total; mismatch step 1: total; ambiguity denominator: state; exploit-explore: componentwise) — but "easy at every site checked" is four sites, and the sweep is real work the bare reading never generates.
3. **New edge cases exist because the boundary exists.** §9 below. The bare reading cannot have type-boundary ambiguities because it has no type boundary; several of §9's items are questions the typed ontology *creates*.
4. **Entropy formalities become obligatory.** The non-degeneracy restatement (§2.3) is owed under either reading, but the typed reading cannot defer it: $H(\theta \mid \cdot)$ on a kernel space has no reference measure, so the naive $H \gt 0$ form is not even sloppy-but-fine there — it is undefined. The typed version forces the repair immediately; the bare version could have limped along.

### 8.2 Where the comparison is a wash dressed as a cost

**The prior.** $H(\theta \mid \mathcal C_t)$ requires $\pi_0$, and a frequentist reader will balk: for fixed-unknown $\theta$ with no prior, the entropy form is meaningless and the predicate needs the identifiability-style surrogate ("$\theta$ not determined by $\mathcal C_t$" — which the non-degeneracy form of §2.3 already covers). But the bare totality reading has *exactly the same dependence* — a hyperstate's initial distribution includes the law-content coordinates — merely folded where nobody will ask about it. The typed version pays in visibility for what the bare version pays in concealment. Per the corpus's existing God's-eye convention (the scope predicate is evaluated from the modeler's viewpoint — `#scope-adaptive-system` Working Notes), $\pi_0$ is the modeler's reference measure, and saying so in one sentence is the honest form of a commitment both readings make.

### 8.3 Net recommendation

Typed at the root, with the construction quarantined: the root definitions name the three slots and the invariance cut in prose (a paragraph each), one appendix-grade segment carries §2–§3's measure theory, and the bare totality reading survives as a stated corollary ("in particular, the scope predicate may be read as residual uncertainty about the totality $S_t$") for readers who want the one-sentence form. If Joseph's root-ontology call comes back minimal-surface, totality-with-a-typed-footnote is *defensible* — the quadrant fix lands either way — but two findings of this pass push typed independently of taste: the atoms' frame-relativity under bare totality is a standing defensive rewrite in two constitutive definitions (§7.2), and the drift question has no bare-totality answer at all (§4), while the corpus (mood machinery, nonstationary bandit) is already living in the drift regime.

## 9. Where the type boundary is genuinely ambiguous

Named honestly, since the assignment asked; none is a blocker, all should be visible in the eventual segment's Epistemic Status or Working Notes.

1. **Other agents.** A peer agent's slowly-updating policy is $\theta$-drift (slow state under §4's cut); its architecture is plausibly invariant (law); its goals sit anywhere on the spectrum. The self-similar cut handles this *formally* (stratify by actual variation rate), but the modeling judgment about where a peer's structure stops varying is genuinely case-by-case — the typed ontology gives the axis, not the answer. (Part III's agent-opacity machinery is where this lands; not developed here.)
2. **Off-support law-content.** $\theta$ restricted to never-visited state-action pairs is invariant, unknown, and *unidentifiable under any policy the agent will actually run* — so $H(\theta \mid \mathcal C_t)$ carries a floor that never decays, and a system can sit in scope forever on the strength of uncertainty it can never act on. Whether the scope predicate should count unreachable uncertainty (current answer: it does, under both readings) or wants a reachability-restricted $\theta$ is a real question with teeth in continual-learning settings. Flag, don't fix.
3. **Chance vs. unlearned law under bounded compute.** The $\varepsilon$-slot is well-defined relative to *unbounded* inference (§3.3's construction is information-theoretic). A bounded agent cannot certify which bucket a residual is in — deterministic pseudo-randomness is chance to every agent below the compute threshold and law above it — so the chance/law boundary is agent-relative in practice even though it is absolute in the ontology. This is the 193847 flag again (§5.3), now visible as a *boundary smear* rather than a missing quadrant: the typed ontology states the clean cut and should say explicitly that bounded agents see a blurred version of it.
4. **Policy-created persistence.** An agent that writes durable structure into its environment (files, institutions, wear) creates content that is persistent *going forward* but not run-invariant (it was once otherwise). The invariance cut books it as state — correct, and consistent with the environment-memory closure (§3.2) — but readers with a "laws of the artifact" intuition will type it as law; the effectively-law overlay (§4.2) is the reconciliation, and the eventual prose should spend a sentence on it.
5. **The $\theta$-symbol collision inside canon.** `#der-mood-timescale`'s regime latent $\theta_t$ is, under this ontology, slow *state*; `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate`'s $\theta$ is genuine law. Same glyph, opposite types, both currently in canon. A naming-cycle item (the mood latent could become $r_t$ or similar), noted for `TERMINOLOGY-TODO` routing — cheap, but exactly the kind of collision the typed ontology makes visible and the untyped corpus could not even state.
