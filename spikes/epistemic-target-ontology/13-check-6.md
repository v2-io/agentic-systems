## The two cold-reader checks

### A Kalman filter

Take (b) as stated: $x_{t+1} = A x_t + w_t$, $o_t = C x_t + v_t$, Gaussian noises independent of everything, law known, posterior $\mathcal N(\hat x_t, P_t)$. For $j \geq 1$, $o_{t+j} = C A^j x_t + (\text{noise independent of } x_t, \mathcal C_t)$, so $(x_t, o_{t+1}, \ldots)$ is jointly Gaussian given $\mathcal C_t$ with cross-covariance $P_t (A^j)^\top C^\top$, and a jointly Gaussian pair has positive mutual information exactly when the cross-covariance is nonzero. The criterion $C A^j P_t \neq 0$ for some $j \geq 1$ follows. The one-step part: given $\mathcal C_t$, $o_{t+1}$ has covariance $C A P_t A^\top C^\top + N$ with $N = C Q C^\top + R$, and covariance $N$ given $x_t$, which gives $\tfrac12 \log\det(I + C A P_t A^\top C^\top N^{-1})$. For the scalar random walk with unit variances, $P_\infty = (\sqrt5 - 1)/2$, the one-step part is $\tfrac12 \log(1 + P_\infty/2) = 0.1346$ nats, and the whole-future value is $\tfrac12 \log(P_\infty / P_{\text{smooth}})$ with $P_{\text{smooth}} = 1/\sqrt5$ (forward variance $P_\infty$ combined with the backward variance $P_\infty + 1$), which is $0.1618$; a 400-observation Gaussian conditioning gives the same to four places. $A = 0$ is out. The renewal of (g′), with the filter restarted from $\tilde P_t = 0$, runs $0, 0.112, 0.131, 0.134, 0.135, \ldots$ and $\sum_k (u_k - r_k) = 0.1618$.

`#example-kalman` agrees with this on scope: $S_t = x_t$, in at every $t$ under either sensor, standing warrant. Its other numbers I recomputed are right ($P^- = 0.640$, $K_{ss} = 0.390$ at $r_H = 1$; $P^- = 1.630$, $K_{ss} = 0.153$ at $r_L = 9$; $V(K) = 0.982, 0.640, 0.940$ at $K = 0.15, 0.39, 0.81$; $P(\lvert e^- \rvert \gt 1.4) = 0.080$; CIY$(H) = 0.161$ nats at $P^- = 4.25$). Its gain paragraph carries a residue of the index fix (F3).

### Standard RL

(c) as stated: $o_t = \Omega_t$ on a finite state space, rows $T(\cdot \mid s, a)$ under independent Dirichlet priors. The information one draw carries about its row is $H(\mathbb E\,\text{row}) - \mathbb E\, H(\text{row})$, positive by strict concavity whenever the row's posterior is not a point mass, so the learner is in at every finite $t$. The $+\infty$ value under recurrence: on the event of infinitely many visits the empirical frequencies converge to the row, so the joint law of (row, stream) charges the graph of the limit map, which the product of the marginals does not charge, since the posterior on the row is atomless; the mutual information is a divergence of a non-absolutely-continuous pair and is $+\infty$. Full-support rows give every transition positive probability, so "always take $a$ at $s$" returns to $s$ infinitely often almost surely. One-step decay against $(K - 1)/(2n)$ for $K = 3$: $0.0297$ against $0.0333$ at $n = 30$, $0.00329$ against $0.00333$ at $n = 300$, $0.000333$ at $n = 3000$. The deterministic-successor exit is pointwise, as stated.

What an RL reader will bring that (c) does not address:

- **Reward.** Standard RL emits a reward, usually depending on $(s_{t-1}, a_{t-1}, s_t)$. Under #def-observation-function's $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$ the reward fits once the transition draws it into the observed state ($\Omega_t = (s_t, \rho_t)$ with $\rho_t$ the reward), so its law is part of $\theta_T$ and (c)'s argument applies to the joint row of successor and reward. A bandit, with one state and an unknown reward law, is then in on its reward law alone. I checked both; they are in.
- **No prior.** A Q-learner has no Dirichlet. By (h′), with finitely many states, actions and reward values, membership depends on $\pi_0$ only through its support, so every $\pi_0$ with full support on the rows classifies a learner not given its transition probabilities the same way as the Dirichlet does: in. The Dirichlet in (c) is one witness, and it is the modeler's reference measure, not the learner's prior. The segment states the ingredients (the Definition's "a tabular learner not given its transition probabilities has a non-degenerate measure over them", and (h′)) but does not connect them at (c), which is where an RL reader will look. With real-valued rewards (h′) does not apply, and (h) makes membership the same for every $\pi_0$ equivalent to the declared one.

Membership is as the segment says on both readings. F5 proposes one or two sentences so an RL reader does not have to reconstruct this.

## Findings

### F1. "Fresh noise" is used in a stronger sense than any definition or global assumption states

**Where.** (g′): "Given $(S_k, \mathcal C_k, a_k)$, $o_{k+1}$ is independent of $S_t$ (the Markov commitment, with fresh noise)". (h): "Given $(S_t, \mathcal C_t)$, the future stream is independent of $\Omega_1$ (the Markov commitment, …)". The Epistemic Status's non-vacuity statement, which runs through (g′) and the data-processing step $u_k \leq$ (ii′), which needs $o_{k+1}$ independent of $S_k$ given $(S_{k+1}, \mathcal C_k, a_k)$.

**What is stated.** #def-action-transition commits to $\Omega$ being Markov in its own evolution. #def-observation-function writes $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$ and defines the kernel as the conditional law of $o_t$ given $(\Omega_t, a_{t-1})$; nothing says $\varepsilon_t$ is independent of earlier states or earlier noise. GA-1 in `NOTATION.md` says $\varepsilon_t$ is conditionally independent of $\mathcal C_{t-1}$ given $(S_t, a_{t-1})$: independence from the chronica, not from earlier states.

**A world that satisfies all of that and breaks the three items.** Known law, $\Omega_t = (t, z_t)$ with $z_1$ a uniform bit and $z_t = 0$ for $t \geq 2$, so nothing is invariant and $\theta$ is a point mass. Observations are constant except $o_K = \varepsilon_K$ with $\varepsilon_K := z_1$, for some $K \geq 3$. The Markov commitment holds, since the transition is deterministic in $\Omega$. GA-1 as worded holds, since $\mathcal C_{K-1}$ is constant. Then:

- At $t = 1$ the value is $I(S_1; F_1 \mid \mathcal C_1) = \log 2$, while every one-step part is zero: $u_{K-1} = I(S_{K-1}; o_K \mid \mathcal C_{K-1}) = 0$ because $S_{K-1} = (K-1, 0)$ is known. Every renewal is zero. So value $+ \sum r_k = \log 2 \neq 0 = \sum u_k$, and (g′)'s identity fails. Its first step fails at $k = K - 1$: $I(S_1; o_K \mid \mathcal C_{K-1}) = \log 2$ while $u_{K-1} - r_{K-1} = 0$.
- (h)'s first step fails: given $(S_2, \mathcal C_2)$, $o_K = z_1$ is a function of $\Omega_1$.
- The non-vacuity statement fails: the loop is in at $t = 1$ under every continuation, and (ii′) at $o_K$ is $I(o_K; S_K \mid \mathcal C_{K-1}) = 0$, as at every other step. No state-and-law term is ever positive.

The world is pathological, a channel with memory whose memory is not in $\Omega$, and that is the point. The items are exact for worlds in which the channel's memory, like the transition's, is kept in $\Omega$. They are not exact from the definitions as written.

**Repair (one sentence in the Definition, after the Markov commitment).** "The declared world is generated by its kernels: given $(S_t, a_t)$, the next state is drawn from $\theta_T$ and the next observation from $\theta_h$ at that state, independently of everything before (earlier states, earlier noise and the chronica); a channel with memory keeps its memory in $\Omega_t$, as the transition does by #def-action-transition. This is what 'fresh noise' means below." With it, the step in (g′) follows: $(\Omega_{k+1}, o_{k+1})$ is independent of $(S_t, \mathcal C_k)$ given $(S_k, a_k)$, hence $o_{k+1}$ is independent of $S_t$ given $(S_k, \mathcal C_k, a_k)$; (h)'s step and the data-processing step follow the same way. The sentence implies GA-1 as worded. GA-1's `NOTATION.md` row could be strengthened to match, or left as the weaker property the mismatch decomposition needs, with this sentence carrying the rest. I have not checked whether other segments that cite "fresh noise" use the stronger property. The squared and information-form decompositions use only the chronica form.

**Where the sentence belongs.** #def-observation-function already relies on this reading. Its sentence "two pairs ($h$, distribution of $\varepsilon_t$) that induce the same kernel produce the same observations under every policy, so no history can tell them apart" fails without it. A channel whose noise repeats, $\varepsilon_t = \varepsilon_{t-1}$ with standard normal marginal, has the same one-step kernel as a fresh one, and $o_t - o_{t-1}$ then shows $h$ at two states without noise, so histories do tell the two apart. The natural home for the repair is #def-observation-function, as the observation-side twin of #def-action-transition's Markov commitment, with this segment citing it where it says "fresh noise".

**Strengthen-first note.** I looked for a route to (g′) from GA-1 as worded plus the Markov commitment and found none. The counterexample satisfies both. The repair is a statement of what the declared world is, not a narrowing of any item's conclusion.

### F2. Non-vacuity reaches the agent's own budget only through its own conduct

The Epistemic Status says: "If the loop is in at $t$, then under some continuation the state-and-law information terms (ii′) … sum to at least that continuation's value, which is positive …, so at some later observation the mismatch budget's state-and-law term is positive." The derivation is right (given F1's sentence): $u_k \leq$ (ii′) at $o_{k+1}$ by data processing over $S_k \to S_{k+1} \to o_{k+1}$ given $(\mathcal C_k, a_k)$, and (g′) gives $\sum_{k \geq t}$ (ii′)$_{k+1} \geq I_{\pi'}(S_t; F_t \mid \mathcal C_t) + \sum_k r_k$. But the closing clause drops "under that continuation", and the mismatch budget of #deriv-mismatch-budget-attribution is computed under the policy in force, $\mathbb P_\pi$. A reader of the Epistemic Status, which is where results that cite this scope will look, gets a promise about the running agent's budget that the derivation does not make.

**Exact statement for the agent's own budget.** Apply the same inequality with $\pi'$ the agent's own continuation: the (ii′) terms after $t$ in the budget the agent runs sum to at least $I_\pi(S_t; F_t \mid \mathcal C_t) + \sum_k r_k$. So the running budget has a positive state-and-law term after $t$ whenever the agent's own conduct can still reveal something. Membership alone does not give this. An invariant hidden bit $b$, shown only when the agent takes an action it never takes (say $o_{t+1} = b$ after "look", else $0$), is in at every $t$, and under the agent's policy every (ii′) is zero.

**Proposed replacement for the closing clause.** "…, so at some later observation the state-and-law term of the budget computed under that continuation is positive. The budget the agent runs is computed under its own policy, where the same sum is at least its own continuation's value: positive when the agent's own conduct can still reveal something, and possibly zero at every step when only other conduct would (an invariant hidden bit shown only by an action the agent never takes)." The "Whose uncertainty" paragraph already says the conduct half in words. This puts it where the guarantee is stated.

### F4. (g)'s clocked example is in one step earlier than stated

"a state drawn afresh at every step, carrying a clock and the previous draw, whose previous draw the channel reports from some time on, is out before that time and in from it." If the channel reports from time $K$, so $o_K = x_{K-1}$, then at $t = K - 1$ the target $S_{K-1}$ contains $x_{K-1}$ and $o_K$ reveals it: the loop is in at $K - 1$. At $t \leq K - 2$ every reported draw is fresh after $t$, so the loop is out. The text puts the switch at $K$. The point the example makes (leaving need not be final) is unaffected. Suggested wording: "…whose previous draw the channel reports from time $K$ on, is out before $K - 1$ and in from $K - 1$, the step whose draw the first report shows." Check-5's C4 confirmed the current wording; this item disagrees with that confirmation on this point only.

## What holds

Re-derived from the page, under F1's sentence where "fresh noise" is used:

- (a), (d), (e), (f); condition 1 implies condition 2; the three ways out cover every case where condition 1 fails, because either the posterior on $S_t$ is a point mass or it is not and the stream is conditionally independent of it.
- Averaged-in iff pointwise-in at some positive-probability history (discrete histories), with the continuation chosen per history.
- (b) and (c), above.
- (g): the law part's finality, and the whole-target bound $I(S_s; F_s \mid \mathcal C_s) \leq I(S_t; F_s \mid \mathcal C_s) + \sum_{k \geq s} r_k$. Data processing gives $I(S_s; o_{k+1} \mid S_t, \mathcal C_k, a_k) \leq r_k$, because $o_{k+1}$ is independent of $S_s$ given $(S_k, S_t, \mathcal C_k, a_k)$. The first term is at most the value at $t$ of the composite continuation.
- (g′): the two-order chain rule, the stream chain rule with vanishing action terms, and the identity in $[0, \infty]$. The law never renews. With a finite value, the non-renewal part tends to zero. The linear-Gaussian renewal formula.
- (h): an equivalent change of $\pi_0$ reweights the law of $(S_t, \mathcal C_t)$ to an equivalent one and leaves the conditional kernel of $F_t$ alone, so conditional independence is preserved.
- (h′), spot-checked: the three support facts, the finite-block reduction ("constant a.e. under the posterior" is conditional independence at a positive-probability history), and the clock-and-rationals counterexample.
- The housing result: the twin is Markov with a policy-free kernel because the policy factors cancel from the posterior. It agrees with the original as a $\pi_0$-mixture. It is case (d). Mutual singularity holds under a fixed law with unbounded visits.
- Discussion and Epistemic Status numbers: fresh uniform row $\log 2 - \tfrac12 = 0.1931$; sign flip one-step part $\tfrac12 \log(1 + 1/(t+1))$, like $1/(2t)$, with infinite value; doubling map $\log 2$ per step, no renewal; the part-of-target counterexample, $\infty$ against $\tfrac12 \log \tfrac32 = 0.2027$.
