## 1. Calibration first: what already works, and works well

The comprehension lift is not starting from zero. Several artifacts in the framing layer are already at or near the respectful-pedagogy bar, and the lift should treat them as the pattern to replicate, not raw material to rework:

- **`INTRODUCTION.md` is the strongest single framing artifact in the repo.** The four anchor results stated up front in plain language, the de-flinched contribution statement, the cascade-is-the-argument framing, and the moral-terminus-as-physics-with-a-shell paragraph are all doing exactly what the placement principle asks. Its main gap is an omission (§2.3 below), not a defect.
- **The six chapter-intro segments are the house style at its best.** `#the-reality-model-intro` (held up by four independent auditors as the model bridge), `#persistence-and-limits-intro` (the bathtub scaffold, the structural-vs-task-adequacy split stated before the math), `#causal-access-intro` (the tragedy-of-agency and "acts but never wonders"), `#strategy-structure-intro` (the derived-vs-chosen honesty about the DAG commitment), `#cooperative-adversarial-intro` (consultant-vs-employee; the recipient-side regime taxonomy as "what the scalar collapses"). These already do mental-model-first with isomorphic analogs. The problem is coverage, not quality: they exist for 6 of ~14 chapter-grade units, and the largest units are the uncovered ones (§2.1).
- **The Findings Briefs, where written, are genuinely Feynman-grade.** `#result-persistence-condition`'s Brief and the measuring-stick Briefs in `#result-certificate-existence` / `#disc-stability-certificate` would each carry a casual reader through the qualitative claim unaided. The failure is in the *surfacing pipeline*, not the prose (§2.4).
- **The snow-driving worked anchor and the scope-of-work cascade figure are the right artifacts in the right places** — with the material caveat that neither currently renders into the built monograph (§2.5).

One meta-observation worth holding: the framework already *owns* the principle the typed-ignorance lens instantiates. `#disc-anti-collapse` names the discipline — *individuate causes at the grain the remedy cares about* — and the four-way ignorance typology is exactly that discipline applied at the root ontology (each ignorance type routes to a different repair: observe / learn / accept / speed up). This matters for adoption: the lens is not an imported reframe competing with the theory's self-understanding; it is the theory's own stated style, applied one level deeper than it has yet been applied. That is the strongest argument that the lens belongs early.

## 2. (a) Where mental-model-shaping weight is disproportionate to structural importance

### 2.1 The front door of Part I is missing — the highest-leverage single gap

The volume introduction is strong, and then the reader falls off a cliff. Part I — the mathematically closed core, the part carrying the most reader-weight — opens with a two-sentence scope italic (Joseph's own inline comment at `OUTLINE.md` line 9 already flags it: "two sentences, the first being an incomplete sentence and the second one being a mislead"), and Chapter 1 (The Coupled Loop) has no chapter-intro segment at all. So the actual reading sequence is: excellent volume intro → nothing → a table of seven definition rows → `#def-agent-environment`. That first segment is good, but it is a definition doing a definition's job; nobody hands the reader the *model of Part I as a whole* — what the machinery is for, what question each chapter answers, what single picture to hang $\delta$, $\eta^\ast$, $\mathcal T$, $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ on. The mental-model vacuum sits precisely where the placement principle says the weight belongs. Every downstream "dense wall of math" complaint compounds from this missing page. (The other `--GAP--` intro rows — Strategy Dynamics, Orient Cascade, Composition Machinery, Unity, Strategic Composition — are real but lower-leverage; those chapters at least inherit momentum from Part-level prefaces.)

### 2.2 Meta-Architecture I leads Part II with weight its tier and its audience-position don't support

This is my clearest "prominent material that maybe shouldn't lead" finding. Part II's first chapter is six discussion-grade meta-segments about facets of the stability certificate, prefaced by paragraphs that are dense with slugs and forward references to results the reader has not met (Sylvester rank-collapse, Mori–Zwanzig projection-residue, composition floors from Part III, mechanism-design translations from the appendices). The introduced-before-used discipline motivated the placement, and the *content* is real — but chapter position is the loudest mental-model signal a book sends, and this placement tells the reader "the most important thing about actuated agents is a cross-sectional taxonomy of the theory you haven't read yet." Three specific mismatches:

1. **Tier vs position.** Every segment in the chapter is `status: discussion-grade`, `stage: draft` — recognition-tier by the framework's own honest marking — yet it occupies the position normally reserved for a Part's strongest content. The reader's forming model over-weights the meta-patterns relative to, say, the diagnostic split or the orient cascade, which are the actual Part II headline and arrive chapters later.
2. **It violates its own ordering discipline.** The certificate spine is the one major framing unit that is *precise-structure-first*: the measuring-stick Layer-0 exists (it is sitting in `#disc-stability-certificate`'s Findings Brief, and it is excellent) but the chapter preamble leads with "operator-sector contraction in some metric and exponential stability are interchangeable witnesses."
3. **Audience position.** A reader one Part in has exactly one certificate instance in hand (the persistence condition). Pattern-recognition across instances lands when the reader has instances; four of the pattern's charter instances live in Part III and the appendices.

Repair direction (my judgment, held loosely): keep a *short* Meta-Architecture chapter at the head of Part II — one page, leading with the measuring-stick paragraph promoted from the Brief, then a compact "you will meet four facets of this one object; here is the map" table — and move the six full meta-segments to a Meta-Architecture appendix cluster (or to Part III's opening, where M4 already lives and where the reader has instances). The vocabulary stays introduced-before-used; the weight moves to where the reader can bear it.

### 2.3 Results that deserve early model-shaping weight but are buried

- **The loop as Level-2 causal engine.** This is arguably the framework's single most *surprising-and-teachable* finding — "the feedback loop the agent already runs is itself the experiment; embodiment is the mathematical prerequisite for understanding" — and it is not among `INTRODUCTION.md`'s four anchors. It first appears ~50 pages in (Part II Ch. 2), exactly the burial pattern the comprehension lift names. It is also the framework's best answer to "why does this theory need causality at all." Candidate fifth anchor in the volume introduction; candidate early mention in the Part I front door (it is the payoff the cascade is climbing toward).
- **Persistence's information burn rate** ($\dot R \geq n\alpha/2$, `#deriv-persistence-cost`). "Survival is not a state you achieve; it is a sustained burn rate of information, and the moment the channel closes you drift at the world's rate" is a first-order mental-model shaper — it converts the persistence condition from a static inequality into a metabolism. It currently lives in an appendix segment plus one mid-intro paragraph. It deserves a sentence in the volume introduction and a prominent place in the Part I front door.
- **The mismatch decomposition** (`#result-mismatch-decomposition`). Currently framed as "seemingly mechanical" — a modest Pythagorean refinement. Under the typed-ignorance lens it is nothing less than the theory's **ignorance ledger stated as a theorem** (§3.1), i.e. the formal object the candidate top-level mental model rests on. If the ontology is adopted, this segment's weight should rise sharply: it becomes the *first place the reader sees the typology cash out as mathematics*, and its "first named result of the volume" position becomes a feature to advertise rather than a fact to state.
- **The containment dichotomy** is already anchor #2 in the introduction — correctly. Noting it as a *positive* calibration: the 2026-05-16 Model-S landing did get its weight moved to the front. That is the precedent the two items above should follow.

## 3. (b) Where the typed-ignorance / law / tempo-race lens yields fruit — and where it doesn't

### 3.1 Strong fruit

1. **The scope predicate becomes teachable.** "A system is in scope while *something learnable remains unknown*" ($H(S_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$, $S_t = (\Omega_t, \theta)$) is strictly easier to state, teach, and defend than "residual state uncertainty," *and* it is the B-3 repair. The excluded corners become narratable in one breath: omniscience (nothing unknown) and pure known-law chance (nothing *learnable* remains) both exit scope because adaptation is about the learnable. The two-wall geometry the auditors liked survives; the walls get honest names.
2. **The mismatch decomposition is the typology stated as a theorem.** The three terms map onto the ignorance types with almost no forcing: estimation term ↔ compute-shortfall (the gap to the Bayes predictor is where bounded inference bites — `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate` already accounts deliberation as reducing $H(\theta \mid M_t, \mathcal C_t)$); state-uncertainty floor ↔ state ignorance (movable only by acting); channel noise ↔ chance. Law-ignorance is the subtle row — per the §4 GA-1-asymmetry claim it flows through the state floor ($\theta_T$) or silently breaks the theorem ($\theta_h$) — and *that is a point in the lens's favor*: the one place the mapping is non-obvious is exactly the place the spike found a live well-posedness gap. If §4 verifies, the decomposition's Discussion earns a typed reading ("three accounts, four ignorance types, and here is where law hides in the accounting"), and the segment graduates from "seemingly mechanical" to the ledger the whole of Part I is balancing. The overfitting/apophenia discussion also sharpens: *overfitting is trying to learn the chance row* — spending law-learning effort on the one account that cannot pay it back.
3. **Model-class fitness is law-representability.** `#the-reality-model-intro`'s "wrong *kind* of model" and `#result-structural-adaptation-necessity`'s change-classes-not-parameters both become one sentence under the lens: $\mathcal F(\mathcal M)$ measures whether the environment's law is *reachable by the agent's model class at all*, and structural adaptation is what an agent does when the law has moved (or always was) outside its representational reach. This also cleanly separates two things the current prose lets blur: the world's *state* drifting (tracked by the ordinary cycle) vs the world's *law* drifting ($\theta_t$ with its own timescale — feeding mood, temporal nesting, structural adaptation). The timescale-tower reading in the spike's §3 is the honest form, and it is *already* how the mood/nesting machinery works; the lens just names it.
4. **The tempo race gives Part I's dynamics their plainest statement.** "A prediction is only knowledge if it arrives before the thing it predicts" compresses `#der-deliberation-cost`, the adaptive-tempo construct, and the persistence condition's qualitative content into one carryable sentence — and it is isomorphic, not evocative (the persistence condition literally is the statement that the correction process outruns the drift process; the deliberation threshold literally prices thinking time against event arrival). It also gives the compute-shortfall row its formal home and, as the spike notes, houses the long-unresolved computational-opacity flag and the sandbox-ceiling connection (fork-based prediction as the attempted end-run that the transportability ceiling closes).
5. **Explore / exploit / deliberate becomes "which ignorance are you buying down this cycle."** The bit-channel accounting in `#disc-exploit-explore-deliberate` already distinguishes environment-bits from inference-bits; the typology completes it — observe buys state, learn buys law, deliberate buys compute, and nothing buys chance — turning a three-way activity split into a four-row budget with one row marked *do not spend here*. That is a genuinely better teaching object than the current framing, and it is the kind of re-emphasis-where-it-yields-fruit the placement principle asks for.
6. **D&C aside, the "law is the bridge" sentence stands on secular legs.** "Records are of the past; survival is about the future; law is the only bridge between them" needs no theological substrate to be true or teachable — it is the Mill–Ramsey–Lewis compression reading plus the chronica's definition, and it answers, in one line, the reader question "why is a theory of adaptation so invested in compression and invariants."

### 3.2 Where the lens does *not* obviously yield fruit — and one competition judgment

- **Part III composition and adversarial machinery.** Closure defect, signed coupling, tempo advantage, unity dimensions — these are about *relations between agents*, and restating them in ignorance-type vocabulary is possible but forced (agent opacity $H_b$ is "state-ignorance about another agent's future action," true but not illuminating; the effects spiral gains nothing from the typology). The lens is a Part-I/Part-II-epistemics lens. Do not stretch it across Part III framing; the signed-coupling and recipient-regime mental models there are already good and native.
- **The chance row is thin in current AAT.** $\varepsilon$ is channel noise plus whatever GA-1 makes of it; the framework has no developed aleatoric theory, and a top-level mental model that gives chance equal billing risks promising one. The honest presentation is asymmetric: state and law are where the theory lives; chance is a named floor; compute is a ratio the tempo machinery prices. The four rows are equally *real* but not equally *developed*, and the Layer-0 should say so (this is exactly the "properly marked" condition on weak-tier material).
- **The binary state/law cut invites a sophisticated objection the presentation must pre-empt.** $\theta$ drifts; the cut is the bottom of a timescale tower, not a metaphysical partition. The spike's §3 already holds this honestly. The Layer-0 must carry the "law is the slow end of state, and that's a feature — it is what the mood and nesting layers are *for*" clause, or the strongest readers will spend their first objection on it.
- **Competition for the top slot.** My judgment: the typed-ignorance ontology is *one of two* top-level mental models, not the sole one, and forcing it to be the single master frame would repeat the single-optimization-principle move the framework criticizes in active inference. The two models are complementary and should be presented as a pair: the **ledger** (what kinds of behind-ness exist, and which repair each routes to — typed ignorance) and the **race** (whether you can stay ahead at all — persistence/tempo, the bathtub). The ledger says what you owe; the race says whether you can keep paying. Both are isomorphic to formal objects (the ledger to $S_t = (\Omega_t,\theta)$ + the mismatch decomposition; the race to $\alpha \gt \rho/R$ + $\mathcal T$). The certificate/measuring-stick model is real but is a *theorist's* cross-sectional model — right for the Meta-Architecture layer, wrong as the reader's first picture. The scope cascade is the *organizational* model — right for the introduction's architecture section, already well placed. Four models, four altitudes, no conflict — but ledger + race are the two that belong on the first page of Part I.

## 5. (d) Layer-0 sketches — marked as sketches, not proposed verbatim

**Sketch A — Part I front door, the ledger (opening paragraphs).** *You can be behind reality in exactly four ways. You may not know where things are — the state of the world has moved since you last looked. You may not know how things work — the rules that carry the world's past into its future. Some of what happens next may be genuinely undecided — no amount of looking or learning buys it. And you may know where things are and how they work, yet be unable to run the rules fast enough — reality computes itself at its own pace and does not wait. The four are different debts with different remedies: the first is repaid by observing, the second by learning, the third only by acceptance or by changing the channel, and the fourth by speed. Part I is the mathematics of an agent holding all four accounts at once — what each costs, which floors each sets, and the single inequality that decides whether the whole account stays solvent as the world drifts.* — Isomorphism check: state ↔ $\Omega_t$; law ↔ $\theta$ (marked candidate-ontology until the root call); chance ↔ the channel floor under GA-1; speed ↔ the tempo/deliberation machinery; "floors" ↔ terms (ii)/(iii) of the mismatch decomposition; "solvent" ↔ bounded mismatch under $\alpha \gt \rho/R$. Caveat to carry: the account metaphor implies fungibility across rows that the theory partly denies (that non-fungibility is the *point*); a following sentence should say so — "the theory's first theorem about the ledger is that the accounts do not transfer."

**Sketch B — the bridge sentence (front door or `#def-chronica` Discussion).** *Everything the agent will ever know arrives as a record of what it has already seen and done. Records are of the past; survival is about the future; and the only bridge between them is law — whatever in the world holds still long enough to be carried forward. An adaptive system is therefore, before it is anything else, a law-learner in a race: it must compress its past into rules faster than the world invalidates them.* — Isomorphism: chronica-as-only-raw-material is `#form-agent-model`'s completeness clause; "holds still long enough" is the timescale tower stated gently; the race clause is the persistence condition. The "law" noun is the candidate terminology from `00-spike.md` §5 and should not appear in canon prose before the LEXICON decision.

**Sketch C — mismatch decomposition Brief (candidate replacement/augment).** *When a prediction misses, the miss has exactly three sources, and they add like the sides of a right triangle: you haven't yet extracted everything your own records imply (fixable by better modeling and more thinking); your records genuinely don't pin down where the world is (fixable only by acting to make your records better); and the world hadn't decided yet (fixable by nothing). The theorem is that the three accounts are cleanly separate — so effort spent on the wrong account is not merely wasted, it is overfitting: the model bends itself to explain what was never explainable.* — Isomorphism: the right-triangle clause is the Pythagorean orthogonality (cross-terms vanish); the three accounts are terms (i)/(ii)/(iii); "acting to make records better" is the active-sensing/CIY door named in the segment. If §4 verifies, a fourth sentence can honestly add where law hides: *the rules of the world, when unknown, are paid for through the second account — unless they contaminate the instrument itself, in which case the triangle's right angle is no longer guaranteed* ($\theta_T$ through the floor; $\theta_h$ breaking GA-1). That sentence is gated.

**Sketch D — the tempo race, one-liner for `#der-deliberation-cost` / tempo framing.** *A prediction is only knowledge if it arrives before the thing it predicts; knowing the recipe is not the same as having the meal cooked, and if the world cooks faster than you do, your recipe feeds no one.* — First clause is isomorphic (the deliberation threshold and $\nu_{\text{agent}}$ vs $\nu_{\text{env}}$); the recipe clause is evocative rather than isomorphic and should be dropped or tightened if used beyond a Working Note.

**Sketch E — Meta-Architecture I compressed head.** Not new prose: promote the existing `#disc-stability-certificate` Findings Brief (measuring-stick paragraph) to the chapter preamble essentially verbatim, with the already-queued sharpening (PROPOSED.md Tier 1, 2026-05-28: the stick is *chosen so* corrections provably shrink it — or the graduated-bathtub extension for continuity with the Ch. 4 analog). The Layer-0 exists; it is filed in the wrong drawer.
