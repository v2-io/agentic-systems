# Spike: how generic is belief-splintering? — a regime trichotomy, and splintering as a symmetry phenomenon

*2026-08-24, third spike of the cycle. Joseph-granted chase of the genericity question left open by `spike-decomposition-uniqueness-distributional-2026-08-24.md` §6: the distributional no-go proved *existence* of goal-coupled kernels with incomparable minimal belief-candidate quotients ("splintering"); this spike asks how generic that phenomenon is. Same role separation as the sibling: **read-only on canon**; integration thoughts appended to the cluster's `…-integration-suggestions.md`. Terminology note: I use "belief carvings" for the lumpable coarsenings of the goal-orbit partition, avoiding "conventions" — canon's convention hierarchy (`#def-value-object` C1–C3) is an unrelated sense and the collision would be a naming-SOP violation waiting to happen.*

## 0. Verdict in four lines

1. **Splintering is not generic — but neither is belief.** For Lebesgue-a.e. goal-coupled kernel, the *only* lumpable coarsening of the goal-orbit partition is the trivial one-block partition: **generic goal-coupling annihilates the belief object entirely** (§2). The dramatic question ("are there many belief carvings?") has the prior question ("is there any?") answered *no* almost everywhere. Splintering lives on a null stratum — but so does everything interesting.
2. **On the structured strata, splintering is *forced by symmetry*** (§3): any kernel automorphism that permutes goal-orbits maps minimal belief carvings to minimal belief carvings, so a symmetry that *moves* one produces incomparable minimals automatically. The 8-state witness from the sibling spike is exactly this — it carries an orbit-swap automorphism conjugating carve $A$ into carve $B$. Real architectures are precisely the non-generic, symmetry-rich points (weight tying, exchangeable components, modular repetition), so the practically relevant regime is the one where splintering is structural, not coincidental.
3. **Symmetry-forced splintering is a discrete gauge; coincidence splintering can be genuinely worse.** When the connecting automorphism exists, the incomparable carvings are isomorphic as quotient dynamics — choosing one breaks a symmetry but costs nothing dynamical. Off the symmetric locus, I construct an explicit perturbed witness (§4) whose two minimal carvings have **dynamically inequivalent quotients** — spectral gap $0.2$ versus $0$: under carve $A$ the agent's beliefs carry persistent, learnable structure; under carve $B$ the belief process is i.i.d. and there is *nothing to learn*. Same agent, same kernel. Under goal-coupling, the choice of belief carve can determine whether the agent has anything to learn at all.
4. The honest hierarchy, then: **disciplined** ($\kappa \equiv 0$): canonical belief, full-size. **Generic coupled:** canonical belief, trivial — annihilation. **Symmetric coupled:** splintered belief, carvings isomorphic (gauge). **Tuned coupled** (deeper null stratum, positive-dimensional): splintered belief, carvings inequivalent — the belief object is not just non-canonical but *non-canonically informative*. Each regime is separated from the next by explicit closed conditions.

## 1. Setup, inherited

Finite $\mathcal X = \mathcal M \times \mathcal G$ (the finite case is where genericity questions are cleanly posable; everything is per-event, one event wlog). Kernel $P$; goal-orbit partition $\Omega$ with blocks $O_m = \{m\} \times \mathcal G$. A **belief carving** is a strongly lumpable coarsening of $\Omega$ (blocks are unions of orbits; the quotient is then a goal-blind factor — the epistemic-closure condition (K2) of the sibling spike). The poset of belief carvings under refinement always contains the one-block partition (trivially lumpable — mass into the whole space is $1$); the question is its *minimal* (finest) elements. The disciplined case ($\Omega$ itself lumpable) has $\Omega$ as the unique minimum — Theorem D. Coupled means $\Omega$ is not lumpable.

Lumpability of a coarsening $\pi$ decomposes into two families of linear constraints on $P$, and keeping them separate is what makes every result below elementary:

- **(within-orbit / leak)** for each orbit $O_m$ and each pair of its states, the row-difference vector $d^{(m)}$ must sum to zero over every $\pi$-block — the goal-leak must *cancel inside each block*;
- **(cross-orbit / base)** any two states in the same $\pi$-block (possibly from different orbits) must have equal mass into every $\pi$-block.

## 2. Generic annihilation

**Proposition G1.** For every coarsening $\pi$ of $\Omega$ with at least two blocks, the set of kernels lumpable w.r.t. $\pi$ is a proper affine-linear slice of the kernel polytope. *Why proper:* some block contains two states (blocks are orbit-unions and $\lvert\mathcal G\rvert \geq 2$), and "these two states have equal mass into block 1" is one affine condition not implied by row-normalization when a second block exists; kernels violating it are trivially exhibited. There are finitely many $\pi$, so the union over all nontrivial $\pi$ is closed, Lebesgue-null, and nowhere dense.

**Corollary G2 (annihilation).** For a.e. kernel on $\mathcal M \times \mathcal G$: no coarsening of $\Omega$ with $\geq 2$ blocks is lumpable — in particular $\Omega$ itself is not (a.e. kernels are coupled, as expected), and the belief-carving poset is $\{$one-block$\}$. **The largest goal-blind factor of a generic coupled agent is a point.** Exact belief, under generic coupling, does not degrade — it vanishes.

Two honest glosses. First, this is an *exactness* artifact in part: the interesting real-world statement is surely the $\varepsilon$-version (how much approximate belief survives generic coupling — flagged open, §6). Second, the result still earns its keep as orientation: it says the entire subject of belief carvings for coupled agents is a *structured-kernel* subject — the question "which strata support belief objects, and what happens on them" replaces "what happens generically," because generically nothing happens. Real architectures are not generic kernels; they are heavily tied, repeated, and symmetric — which is exactly what puts them on the strata where the rest of this spike lives.

## 3. On the strata: splintering is a symmetry phenomenon

**Lemma S1 (symmetry acts on carvings).** Let $\sigma : \mathcal X \to \mathcal X$ be a kernel automorphism ($P(\sigma x, \sigma S) = P(x, S)$) that permutes goal-orbits ($\sigma(O_m) = O_{\tau(m)}$). Then $\sigma$ maps belief carvings to belief carvings, preserves refinement, and hence preserves minimality. *One line:* lumpability constraints are stated in terms of $P$ and block membership, both of which $\sigma$ transports.

**Proposition S2 (symmetry forces splintering).** If some minimal belief carving $\pi$ has $\sigma\pi \neq \pi$, then $\pi$ and $\sigma\pi$ are incomparable minimal carvings — splintering — and moreover $\sigma$ descends to an **isomorphism of the two quotient dynamics**. *Incomparability:* two distinct minimal elements of a poset cannot be comparable. *The descent:* $\sigma$ carries $\pi$-blocks to $\sigma\pi$-blocks and intertwines the quotient kernels by the automorphism property.

**The sibling witness is the worked instance.** The 8-state witness (distributional spike §4.2) carries the automorphism $\sigma$ = swap orbits $O_2 \leftrightarrow O_3$ (identity elsewhere): the uniform rows are swap-invariant, and $O_1$'s leak vector $d = t(1, -1, -1, 1)$ has equal $O_2$- and $O_3$-components, so $\sigma$ preserves $P$ — and $\sigma A = \sigma\{O_1 O_2 \mid O_3 O_4\} = \{O_1 O_3 \mid O_2 O_4\} = B$. What the sibling presented as a found coincidence is in fact symmetry-forced: the witness could not have had a unique minimal carve, because its symmetry moves any candidate. Retrospectively, the construction recipe *was* "build a leak aligned with a symmetry" — the forcing computations of that spike's §4.1 were unknowingly computing the condition for the symmetry to exist.

**Consequence for the interpretation.** Symmetry-forced splintering is the *mild* form: the incomparable carvings are relabelings of each other (S2's descent), so the "choice of what to count as belief" is a **discrete gauge** — it breaks a symmetry of the dynamics but costs nothing dynamically, exactly parallel to the continuous goal-fiber gauge of the pathwise theorem. An agent (or analyst) may pick either carve; all quotient-level quantities agree. If this were the only form of splintering, the sibling spike's "what counts as belief is a convention" would deserve softening to "…a convention among equivalent presentations."

It is not the only form.

## 4. Inequivalent carvings — the perturbed witness

Off the symmetric locus, within the stratum where both carve-constraint sets still hold, the two quotient dynamics are free to differ. Constructing this requires keeping (leak) satisfied for both carves while detuning the (base) masses asymmetrically:

**Witness.** $\mathcal M = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, $\mathcal G = \{g_1, g_2\}$, goal frozen; rows specified by orbit-mass vectors (uniform split inside each target orbit); leak only in $O_1$, $d = t(1, -1, -1, 1)$ with $t = 1/16$; base orbit-mass vectors

$$q_1 = (.3, .3, .2, .2), \quad q_2 = (.4, .2, .1, .3), \quad q_3 = (.2, .2, .3, .3), \quad q_4 = (.1, .3, .4, .2),$$

with $O_1$'s two rows $q_1 \pm d$ and all other rows the plain $q_m$.

*Checks (all one-line sums).* **Coupled:** $O_1$'s rows differ orbit-wise ($t \neq 0$). **$A = \{O_1 O_2 \mid O_3 O_4\}$ lumpable:** leak sums vanish per $A$-block ($t - t$, $-t + t$); base masses into $\{O_1 O_2\}$: $q_1: .6$, $q_2: .6$; $q_3: .4$, $q_4: .4$. **$B = \{O_1 O_3 \mid O_2 O_4\}$ lumpable:** leak sums vanish per $B$-block ($t - t$, $-t + t$); base masses into $\{O_1 O_3\}$: $q_1: .5$, $q_3: .5$; $q_2: .5$, $q_4: .5$. **Both minimal:** every partition strictly between $\Omega$ and $A$ (resp. $B$) isolates an orbit whose leak component is $\pm t \neq 0$. **Positivity:** $q_1 \pm d \geq 0$ entrywise.

**The quotient dynamics.** Carve $A$: $K_A = \begin{pmatrix} .6 & .4 \\ .4 & .6 \end{pmatrix}$ — spectral gap eigenvalue $\lambda_A = 0.2$. Carve $B$: $K_B = \begin{pmatrix} .5 & .5 \\ .5 & .5 \end{pmatrix}$ — $\lambda_B = 0$. **Not isomorphic** (different spectra), and the difference is not bookkeeping: under carve $A$ the belief process has persistent, autocorrelated structure — the agent's coarse belief today predicts tomorrow's, there is something to track and learn; under carve $B$ the belief process is exactly i.i.d. — the maximal goal-blind summary retains *zero* predictive information. **Same agent, same kernel: whether the agent has anything to learn is carve-relative.** No automorphism can connect $A$ and $B$ here (it would have to intertwine kernels with different spectra), so this splintering is genuinely of the second kind.

**Robustness within the stratum.** The witness has free parameters (the $q_m$ subject to four base equalities; $t$ in an open interval), and minimality, positivity, and spectral inequivalence are open conditions — so inequivalent splintering is a **positive-dimensional family**, null in the ambient kernel space but robust to any perturbation that respects the two carves' constraint sets. And *within* the stratum where two incomparable minimals exist without a connecting symmetry, spectral *equality* is a further closed condition — so inequivalence is generic *there*. The hierarchy of §0.4 has each regime carved from the previous by explicit closed conditions: $\kappa = 0$ (disciplined) ⊃-boundary → generic annihilation → symmetric splintering (gauge) → tuned splintering (inequivalent).

## 5. What this does to the earlier claims — self-corrections included

- **The sibling spike's headline sharpens in both directions.** "Under goal-coupling, what counts as belief is a convention" was existence-grade. Now: generically there is nothing to have a convention *about* (annihilation); on symmetric strata the convention is a costless discrete gauge; and on tuned strata the convention is *load-bearing* — carvings can disagree about the learnability of the world. The interesting claim survives, relocated to the tuned stratum and strengthened there.
- **A self-correction on my own §4.2 presentation:** the original witness's two carvings were presented as an unstructured coincidence; S2 shows they were symmetry-forced and *isomorphic* — that witness alone never licensed "substantively different belief objects" (its two carvings retain relabeled-identical information). The perturbed witness is the first construction that does license it. Had the genericity chase not run, the sibling's interpretive paragraph would have quietly over-read its own example. (It hedged — "the two conventions retain different information about the world" was asserted of the general phenomenon, exemplified only by the symmetric witness where it is false as stated. The verifier of that spike should treat this as a finding surfaced by the author.)
- **The κ-companion gap measure** (pathwise Cor 5 discussion, already once corrected to per-maximal-element): the perturbed witness shows the per-element values can differ *dynamically*, not just in cardinality — any eventual formalization should report the spread across minimal carvings, and the spread itself is a meaningful architecture diagnostic (zero spread ⟺ splintering is pure gauge ⟸ symmetry).

## 6. Open after this spike

- **The $\varepsilon$-programme is now clearly the load-bearing continuation.** Exact results say: belief for coupled agents is a null-stratum phenomenon. Real relevance requires the approximate versions: does generic coupling leave an $O(\kappa)$-approximate belief object (softening annihilation)? Do near-symmetric architectures have near-splintered carve *basins* separated by $O(\varepsilon)$? The exact trichotomy is the skeleton; nothing approximate is derived here.
- **Full classification of the symmetric case:** when the automorphism group acts transitively on minimal carvings, all carvings are one gauge orbit; the mixed case (several symmetry-orbits of minimals) combines both forms and is untouched.
- **Infinite/continuous state spaces:** genericity was argued in the finite case only (Lebesgue on a polytope); the right general statement (prevalence? Baire category on kernel spaces?) is not attempted.
- **Whether inequivalent splintering can reach *disconnected learnability in both directions*** — carve $A$ learnable on feature 1 only, carve $B$ on feature 2 only, with no carve seeing both — would be the strongest form ("complementarity" of belief carvings, in the quantum-flavored sense of incompatible maximal observables). The linear algebra looks constructible with $\lvert\mathcal M\rvert = 6$; not attempted, and I flag the quantum analogy as evocative-only, not isomorphic, per the framework's analog-discipline.
- **Prior art:** lumpability-lattice folklore as before; the symmetry-action-on-lumpable-partitions observation feels like it should exist in the Markov-chain literature (automorphisms and lumpings are both classical); queued for the novelty-search agent with the rest of the cluster.
