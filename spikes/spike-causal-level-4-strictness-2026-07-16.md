# Spike: A Strictness Witness for Mechanism Counterfactuals — the Level-4 Question Resolved in Both Directions

**Status**: Resolved — succeed-beyond-claim on the separation half, plus the honest closure half. Landed in `#deriv-mechanism-counterfactual-separation`; `#disc-structural-imagination` updated per integration-is-replacement.

**Date**: 2026-07-16 (commissioned by Joseph same day, following the `disc-structural-imagination` landing that refused the Level-4 claim for want of a witness).

**Prior state**: `spikes/.integrated/spike-causal-level-4.md` + `spike-causal-level-4-formal.md` (2026-03-13/14) defined the structural do-operator $do_s(f_X := g)$ and the structural counterfactual $Y_{f_X := g}$ (Approach 1), asserted without derivation that Level-3 agreement leaves structural equations underdetermined, and produced no strictness witness. The 2026-07-16 landing (`#disc-structural-imagination`) honestly refused the hierarchy claim on that evidence. This spike was commissioned to attempt the strong claim: *make it true, or prove the no-go*.

**Verdict**: **Both, and they are compatible.** (1) There is a two-line strictness witness: two SCMs agreeing on *all* Level-3 quantities (identical joint law of the full potential-outcome process) that assign probabilities $1$ vs $0$ to the same mechanism-replacement counterfactual query. So *noise-preserving mechanism counterfactuals are not Level-3 quantities* — a strict separation in exactly the Causal Hierarchy Theorem's sense of separation. (2) The closure direction also holds, but is weaker than a collapse: any *fixed finite family* of contemplated replacements internalizes into an enlarged SCM (Pearl's own policy-variable move) where the queries become Level 3 — yet the enlarged model's Level-3 content is *not recoverable* from the base model's Level-3 content (that is what the witness shows), so internalization is re-representation, not reduction. The refusal in the morning landing was right about the evidence then and wrong about the world: the witness exists and is elementary.

## 1. Setup and definitions

Fix a recursive (acyclic) SCM $M = \langle U, V, F, P(U) \rangle$ with finite endogenous set $V$; for $v_i \in V$, $f_i : \mathrm{dom}(pa_i) \times \mathrm{dom}(U_i) \to \mathrm{dom}(v_i)$.

**Level-3 content.** Following the symbolic formalization in Bareinboim-Correa-Ibeling-Icard (2022), Level-3 quantities are probabilities of Boolean combinations of events $Y_x = y$, where $Y_x(u)$ is the potential outcome of $Y$ under the *hard* intervention $do(X = x)$, all worlds sharing the exogenous draw $u$. The complete Level-3 content of $M$ is therefore the joint law of the **potential-outcome process** $\mathrm{PO}(M) = \{ V_w(\cdot) : w \text{ ranges over hard interventions on subsets of } V \}$ as a family of random variables on $(U, P(U))$. Write $M \equiv_3 M'$ ("counterfactually equivalent") when $\mathrm{PO}(M)$ and $\mathrm{PO}(M')$ have the same joint distribution. $\equiv_3$ implies agreement on every Level-1, Level-2, and Level-3 query.

**Noise-preserving mechanism replacement** (the archived spike's Approach-1 operator, retained with its name). For $g : \mathrm{dom}(pa_i) \times \mathrm{dom}(U) \to \mathrm{dom}(v_i)$, let $M[f_i := g]$ be $M$ with the $i$-th equation replaced and $U, P(U)$ *unchanged* — the replaced mechanism reads the same background the original did. $do(X = x)$ is the constant special case (Pearl 2009 §7.2.4 local surgery; the archived spike verified the section quotes against the book in the 2026-03 session — flagged for a relata re-verification since the holding has no attached document).

**Mechanism-counterfactual (candidate Level-4) queries.** Cross-world probabilities whose worlds are drawn from the submodels of *both* $M$ and $M[f_i := g]$, sharing $u$: e.g. $P(Y_{f_i := g} = y \mid E)$ with evidence $E$ from the actual world, or $P(Y_{f_i := g} = Y_x)$. Well-defined by the obvious extension of Pearl's three-step (abduce in $M$; surgically form $M[f_i := g]$, possibly composed with hard interventions; predict) — the archived spike already established well-definedness; what it lacked was the separation.

## 2. Theorem 1 (strictness witness): mechanism counterfactuals are not Level-3 quantities

**Claim.** There exist SCMs $M_A \equiv_3 M_B$ and a mechanism-counterfactual query $q$ with $q(M_A) = 1$ and $q(M_B) = 0$.

**Construction.** Binary variables. The two models share the same signature $\langle U, V, P(U) \rangle$ — exogenous $U_X, U_Y$ independent, each uniform on $\{0,1\}$ — so a replacement written over $(pa, u)$ is a single unambiguous query against both. Endogenous $X = U_X$ in both models, and

$$M_A:\; Y = X \oplus U_Y, \qquad M_B:\; Y = X \oplus U_Y \oplus 1.$$

**$M_A \equiv_3 M_B$.** The full potential-outcome process is generated by: $X$'s response (identically $U_X$ in both); $Y$'s response function $x \mapsto Y_x(u)$, which is $(U_Y,\, 1 \oplus U_Y)$ in $M_A$ and $(1 \oplus U_Y,\, U_Y)$ in $M_B$ as $(Y_{x=0}, Y_{x=1})$ pairs; and the trivial responses under interventions on $Y$ itself. The map $\varphi(u_X, u_Y) = (u_X, 1 \oplus u_Y)$ is a $P(U)$-preserving bijection with $\mathrm{PO}(M_B) = \mathrm{PO}(M_A) \circ \varphi$: both models induce the *same* joint law — $(Y_{x=0}, Y_{x=1})$ uniform on $\{(0,1), (1,0)\}$, independent of $X = U_X$, actual $Y = Y_X$ consistent. Every Level-1/2/3 quantity therefore agrees. (Spot-checks: $P(Y=1) = \tfrac12$ both; $P(Y_{x=1} = 1 \mid X=0, Y=1)$ — abduction gives $U_Y = 1$ in $A$, $U_Y = 0$ in $B$, prediction gives $0$ in both.)

**The separating query.** Replace $Y$'s mechanism by $g(x, u_Y) = u_Y$ — "cut the $X$-dependence; let $Y$ read its background directly." Take

$$q = P\bigl(Y_{f_Y := g} = Y_{x=0}\bigr).$$

In $M_A$: $Y_{f_Y := g} = U_Y = Y_{x=0}$ almost surely, so $q(M_A) = 1$. In $M_B$: $Y_{f_Y := g} = U_Y \neq 1 \oplus U_Y = Y_{x=0}$ almost surely, so $q(M_B) = 0$. $\blacksquare$

**Evidence-conditioned form** (the practically-relevant shape — "given what I saw under the actual law, what would this same situation yield under a different law?"): $P(Y_{f_Y := g} = 1 \mid X = 0, Y = 1)$ is $1$ in $M_A$ (abduction: $U_Y = 1$) and $0$ in $M_B$ (abduction: $U_Y = 0$). Maximal disagreement under identical evidence and identical full-counterfactual profiles.

**Remark (where the extra information lives).** Both models *agree* on the post-replacement interventional distribution — $Y_{f_Y := g} \sim \mathrm{Bern}(\tfrac12)$ in each. The separation lives exclusively in the *cross-world coupling* between actual-law worlds and replaced-law worlds through the shared $u$. Level 3 fixes the joint law of all hard-intervention worlds; it does not fix how those worlds are *co-registered against alternative laws on the same background*. That co-registration is precisely what "same situation, different mechanism" queries consume, and it is invisible to every experiment and every standard counterfactual.

**Remark (relation to the archived spike's Approach-1 assertion).** The old claim "agreement on all standard counterfactuals leaves structural equations underdetermined" is *true but was not the needed statement* — underdetermination of the equations (a representation fact, long known via canonical response-function representations, Balke-Pearl 1994) does not by itself yield a *query* the models disagree on; the refusal was right that a witness was owed. The witness shows the underdetermination is *query-relevant*: it changes answers to well-posed mechanism counterfactuals, not merely the bookkeeping.

## 3. Theorem 2 (reducibility boundary): replacements *specified over Level-3-exposed channels* stay inside Level 3

A replacement query is only as well-posed as the language its new mechanism is written in, and the boundary turns exactly on that language. Say a replacement is **L3-specified** when the new mechanism is given as $v_i := h(pa_i,\, f_i(pa_i, \cdot),\, \varepsilon)$ — a fixed measurable function of the parents, the *original mechanism's output*, and fresh noise $\varepsilon \perp U$ with a given law. (Special cases: deterministic parent-only re-wirings $h(pa_i)$; independent randomized devices $h(\varepsilon)$; and all shift-scale soft interventions $a(pa_i) f_i + b(pa_i)$, which reuse $U$ but only through the output channel.) Say it is **latent-specified** when the new mechanism is given as a function of $u$ under a particular exogenous parameterization, as in Theorem 1's $g(x, u_Y) = u_Y$.

**Claim.** Every cross-world query over submodels of $M$ and $M[f_i := h]$ with $h$ L3-specified is determined by the Level-3 content of $M$ (together with the law of $\varepsilon$). In particular, L3-specified replacements can never separate a $\equiv_3$ pair.

**Derivation.** Recursivity gives a topological order. In $M[f_i := h]$ (optionally composed with hard interventions $w$), compute the modified world's trajectory variable-by-variable: every variable upstream of $v_i$ retains its original potential response $V_w(u)$; then $v_i^{h} = h(pa_i^{h},\, f_i(pa_i^{h}, u),\, \varepsilon)$, where $f_i(pa_i^{h}, u)$ is the original potential outcome of $v_i$ under the hard intervention setting $pa_i$ to the already-computed values — an element of $\mathrm{PO}(M)$; every variable downstream is $V_{w \cup \{v_i = v_i^h\}}(u)$, again an element of $\mathrm{PO}(M)$ evaluated at a computed index. By induction the entire modified-world trajectory is a measurable functional of $\mathrm{PO}(M)$ and $\varepsilon$, hence its joint law with all original-model worlds is a functional of the Level-3 content and the law of $\varepsilon$. $\blacksquare$

**Remark (the two theorems together — why there is no contradiction, and what it reveals).** In $M_A$, Theorem 1's $g(x, u_Y) = u_Y$ *happens to factor* as $f^A_Y(x, u_Y) \oplus x$ — an L3-specification; in $M_B$ the same latent-specified $g$ factors differently, as $f^B_Y(x, u_Y) \oplus x \oplus 1$. The latent specification determines an L3-specification only *relative to a model*, and $\equiv_3$-equivalent models translate it inconsistently. So the strictly-beyond-Level-3 query class is not "replacements whose formula mentions $u$" but **replacements whose meaning is anchored to the latent parameterization itself** — "hold *this very background* fixed and change the law over it." Whenever the imagined mechanism can be re-expressed in terms of parents, original outputs, and independent devices, the query collapses into Level 3; when it cannot, its answer is not determined by any experimentable or counterfactual content and is only defined relative to a *posited* representation of the background. An exact characterization of the boundary (which latent-specified replacements admit some model-invariant L3-specification) is open and recorded as such.

## 4. Theorem 3 (closure/internalization): fixed replacement families reduce to Level 3 of an enlarged model — and why this does not collapse the separation

**Claim.** For any finite family $\mathcal G = \{g_1, \dots, g_k\}$ of candidate replacements for $f_i$, define $M^{\dagger}$ by adding a selector variable $S \in \{0, 1, \dots, k\}$ (exogenously constant at $0$) and the mechanism $f_i^{\dagger}(pa_i, s, u) = f_i(pa_i, u)$ if $s = 0$, else $g_s(pa_i, u)$. Then every mechanism-counterfactual query over $M$ and $\{M[f_i := g_j]\}$ equals a *hard-intervention* (Level-2/3) query in $M^{\dagger}$: $Y_{f_i := g_j} = Y_{S = j}$. (This is Pearl's own policy-variable construction, 2009 §3.2.2, applied reflexively.)

**Why the hierarchy survives.** The internalization map $M \mapsto M^{\dagger}$ requires knowing $F$ and the $u$-couplings — by Theorem 1, strictly more than $M$'s Level-3 content ($M_A^{\dagger}$ and $M_B^{\dagger}$ are Level-3-*distinguishable* even though $M_A \equiv_3 M_B$). So "Level 4 reduces to Level 3 of a bigger model" has exactly the status of "Level 3 reduces to Level 1 over the potential-outcome event space" — true, syntactic, and irrelevant to the informational hierarchy, which is defined relative to a fixed variable set. The Causal Hierarchy Theorem's levels are all "Level 1 over successively richer event spaces"; strictness has always meant *the richer content is not determined by the poorer*, and that is what Theorem 1 establishes one rung further. Additionally, internalization only ever captures a *fixed, anticipated* family $\mathcal G$; the open-ended query class over all replacements is not internalizable into any single finite enlargement.

## 5. What this teaches about imagination (the commissioned stake)

Joseph's framing: push the claim because either terminus teaches us about imagination and the levels. Both halves landed, so the teaching is a *split*:

- **Imagination as navigation** (Level-3-internal): imagining re-wirings, alternative inputs, independent new devices, dose-shifting policies — anything whose imagined mechanism is *specified over channels the world already exposes* (parents, actual mechanism outputs, fresh randomness). Theorem 2 says all of this is computable from counterfactual-grade knowledge; an agent with full Level-3 access can do this "imagination" without any commitment beyond it.
- **Imagination as positing** (strictly beyond Level 3): imagining *this very situation under a different law* — a mechanism change anchored to the latent background itself. Theorem 1 says no amount of associational, experimental, or counterfactual knowledge determines the answer; the co-registration of actual background against counterfactual law is underdetermined by *all experimentable content*, and an imagining agent must **posit** it (commit to one representation $F$ among the $\equiv_3$-class). This is why structural imagination in `#disc-structural-imagination` correctly lives in a *workspace with a grounding tag*: the tag marks content that observation cannot, even in principle at Level 3, supply — not merely content that observation has not yet supplied. Hallucination-as-level-confusion sharpens accordingly: promoting a posited co-registration into $M_t$ as if evidence-grade is not just premature, it is a promotion of the *provably* unverifiable.
- **The hierarchy reading**: "Level 4" is legitimate in the same relative sense the CHT levels are — strictly more informative *relative to the model's variable set*, always representable as lower-level over an enlarged set. The morning refusal conflated the syntactic collapse (real, harmless) with the informational collapse (false).

## 6. Prior art (verification state marked per claim)

- **Pearl 2009 §7.2.4** (mechanism replacement as local surgery, within-SCM) and **§3.2.2** (policy/selector variables): machinery adopted with citation; quotes carried from the 2026-03 spike; relata holding `pearl-2009-causality` has *no attached document* — primary re-verification owed before segment promotion past draft.
- **Bareinboim, Correa, Ibeling & Icard 2022** (CHT; relata `bareinboim-correa-ibeling-icard-2022-pearl-hierarchy`): **primary-verified this cycle via `relata inspect`** — their Definition 7 (Layer-3 valuation) is exactly the family of joint distributions over hard-intervention counterfactual events $P^{\mathcal M}(\mathbf{y_x}, \dots, \mathbf{z_w})$, $\mathbf X, \mathbf W \subseteq \mathbf V$, and Definition 9 fixes the PCH as $\mathcal L_1/\mathcal L_2/\mathcal L_3$ over those semantics; so $\equiv_3$ above coincides with their $\sim_3$, and nested counterfactuals (their §"nested", e.g. $Y_{x, M_{x'}}$) are events over the same potential-outcome process, hence covered by the witness's agreement. Our Theorem 1 is a separation *above* their Layer 3 in their own sense (existence of a disagreeing pair); no genericity claim is made (unverified whether generic — plausibly yes, not derived).
- **Canonical response-function representations** (Balke & Pearl 1994): source of the folklore fact that $\equiv_3$ does not pin $F$. Adopted as background; the query-relevance step (Theorem 1) is, to the extent of this spike's search, not in the literature in this form.
- **Soft-intervention literature** (Correa & Bareinboim $\sigma$-calculus, 2020; [Saha et al. 2025, "Cyclic Counterfactuals under Shift-Scale Interventions"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25005)): closest adjacent work; **novelty gate checked this cycle** — the full HTML text was fetched and their "soft interventions are strictly more expressive in defining counterfactual worlds" is a *query-language expressiveness* claim (context-dependent policies that constant do-interventions cannot express), explicitly *not* a model-distinguishability theorem; no result of Theorem-1 form appears there. Their shift-scale class is L3-specified in Theorem 2's sense (reuses noise only through the original mechanism's output), hence L3-reducible in the acyclic case — consistent with their claim being about policy expressiveness rather than information beyond Layer 3. Also adjacent: [Balgi, Peña & Daoud 2024, counterfactually-equivalent SCMs via normalizing flows](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v246/balgi24b.html) (works *with* $\equiv_3$-classes; does not, per abstract, state the separation); Rubenstein et al. 2017 / Beckers & Halpern 2019 (abstraction — transformations between SCMs, orthogonal question); [the one "fourth rung" hit in the sweep](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/fourth-rung-adding-identity-pearls-ladder-causality-its-srikanth-iyer) (LinkedIn essay, "identity" rung for federated learning — unrelated).
- Searched, not found: any published strictness witness for mechanism-replacement counterfactuals over full-$\equiv_3$ pairs, or any published "Level 4" with CHT-style separation. ([CHT technical report](https://causalai.net/r60.pdf); [counterfactual non-identifiability of learned SCMs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.09031).)
